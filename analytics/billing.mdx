The **Revenue** category in the **Analytics** hub is the financial snapshot of your organization. It shows what you billed, what you collected, and what is still outstanding, broken down by where the revenue came from (bookings, memberships, passes, drop-ins, or other), by revenue account, by plan, and by payment method. Owners and managers use it to track how the business is doing.

## When to use it

Use it for the higher-level "how are we doing this month?" view, without exporting raw charges. Typical questions it answers:

- **Which membership plans bring in the most revenue?** - a tennis and padel gym sees membership revenue growing while area bookings stay flat, then checks the **By plan** tab to find the tier behind it.
- **Are we collecting what we bill?** - a muay-thai gym watches the collection rate and the overdue invoice count, and works through the unpaid charges when overdue invoices climb.
- **Did collection keep up with a billing spike?** - a large gym reads paid versus unpaid by day to catch a stretch of annual renewals where collection lagged.
- **What did each payment method take in?** - a front desk reads **Collected by method** on the **Payments Report** at the end of the day, and the **By Items** tab at month end to see every sale grouped under the card terminal, cash, or bank transfer that collected it.
- **Is revenue landing in the right accounts?** - a reformer studio maps class and retail revenue to its revenue accounts and exports the month for its bookkeeper.
- **How much did coaching earn apart from area hire?** - a padel gym files coach time on a **Coaches** account and reads the **Coaching** tab of the **Revenue Report** for each coach's hours, billing, and pay.

For day-to-day invoice and payment work, use [Sales > Finance > Invoices](/sales/invoices) or [Sales > Finance > Payments](/sales/payments) instead. See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for more.

## What "revenue" means here

- **Revenue** counts each sale (charge) on its own date, whether or not it has been paid yet. It is reported **excluding tax**, because tax you collect is owed onward rather than earned.
- The part of a sale paid with wallet credit (a voucher, a comp, or re-spent refund credit) is not new money in, so it is left out. So is anything refunded back out to a card.
- **Paid** is the part of those charges that payments have since covered. The difference is what is still **outstanding**.
- **Collected** is different: the payments received in the period, including tax, on the day they were paid, less refunds, whatever they paid for. Paying from the Wallet is not counted, because that money was collected when the Wallet was topped up.

So a booking charged in January but paid in March counts toward January's revenue on every report, and shows as paid once the payment lands, while the payment itself counts in March's **Collected**. Each money figure says whether it includes or excludes tax. See [Analytics definitions](/analytics/definitions#money).

## Reports in this category

Open **Analytics** and pick the **Revenue** category to reach four reports:

- **Revenue** - Cards plus a stacked revenue-versus-paid trend by source.
- **Revenue Report** - A month-by-month table with **By Source**, **By Account**, and **By Plan** tabs, a **By Items** list grouped by payment method, and a **Coaching** tab when coach time has its own revenue account.
- **Payments Report** - Billed, paid, unpaid, and collected, with what each payment method collected and a payments-by-day chart.
- **Invoices Dashboard** - Invoice counts and amounts by status, with collection rate and payment methods.

For every charge of a short period, who owes you and for how long, and your tax return, see the [Finance](/analytics/finance) category.

## Filters

- **Period** - Defaults to the last 30 days. The **Revenue Report** offers whole months and opens on **This month**.
- **Group by** and **Compare with** - on **Revenue** and the **Invoices Dashboard**; the **Payments Report** offers **Compare with**. See [Analytics overview](/analytics/overview#compare-with-another-period).
- **Billing entity** (under **Filters**) - narrows the figures to one company's money. It appears on all four reports when your organization has two or more [billing entities](/settings/billing-entities).
- **Revenue account** (under **Filters**) - narrows the figures to the money filed to one [revenue account](/settings/billing-general#revenue-accounts), for example coach time apart from area hire. A charge stored with no account counts as your default account. It appears on all four reports when your organization has two or more active revenue accounts. The drill-down and the **Export** follow it.
- **Area type** (under **Filters**) - on **Revenue**, the **Payments Report**, and the **Revenue Report** (except its **By Plan** and **Coaching** tabs), narrows the figures to the sales of one area type's bookings.
- **Source** (under **Filters**) - on **Revenue**, shows one source only: bookings, memberships, passes, drop-ins, or other.
- **Plan kind** (under **Filters**) - on the **Revenue Report**'s **By Plan** tab, shows only memberships, passes, or drop-ins.
- **Club** - Reports follow the club selected in the breadcrumb at the top of the admin; **All clubs** shows the whole organization.

## Revenue dashboard

The **Revenue dashboard** (**Analytics** > **Revenue** > **Revenue**) is the headline view.

Cards, four across the top:

- **Total Revenue** (Excluding tax) - Revenue from the charges in the period, whether paid or not, net of refunds.
- **Tax on Revenue** - The tax on that revenue.
- **Collected** (Including tax) - Payments received in the period, on the day they were paid, less refunds. Payments have no source, so while a **Source** filter is picked the card shows no figure.
- **Average Charge Value** (Excluding tax) - Revenue divided by the charges that earned some; a charge paid in full from the Wallet or refunded in full is not counted.

### Revenue trends chart

The **Revenue Trends** chart stacks revenue versus paid amounts, one stack per source:

- **Bookings** - Class, area, and instructor bookings.
- **Memberships** - Plans that bill again (recurring memberships).
- **Passes** - Plans sold once, such as a class card or a season pass.
- **Drop-Ins** - Single-visit plans.
- **Other** - Everything else (POS, retail, manual charges, and so on).

Within each source, the **paid** portion is drawn solid and the **unpaid** portion semi-transparent on top, so a tall translucent bar means you billed but did not collect. The time bucket adapts to your date range: daily for short ranges, weekly for medium ranges, monthly for long ones.

## Revenue report

The **Revenue Report** (**Analytics** > **Revenue** > **Revenue Report**) has up to five tabs. The first three are month-by-month tables; click any month cell to open the charges behind that figure.

- **By Source** - Revenue grouped by **Revenue Source** (for example Bookings, Memberships, Passes, Drop-Ins, Products, and Other).
- **By Account** - Revenue grouped by **Revenue Account**, showing each account's name and code. A charge stored with no revenue account counts under your default account. Use this for bookkeeping and for mapping revenue to your chart of accounts.
- **By Plan** - Revenue grouped by **Plan**, with each plan's billing frequency shown. Narrow it with **Plan kind**. Use this to see which membership plans drive the most revenue.
- **By Items** - Every sold line item (bookings, memberships, packages, products) as its own row with its type, contact, date, and amount, grouped under the payment method that collected it, with a subtotal per method and a **Grand Total**. Items not yet paid fall under **Unpaid / Outstanding**, and items marked paid with no payment record under **Manual / Other**. When a package was sold in the period, a **List value** column and a **Package discount** line show what bundling took off.
- **Coaching** - Each coach's **Hours**, the coach money they **Billed**, what of it is **Paid**, and, once you pay coaches through [pay rates](/staff/pay-rates), their **Pay** and what the **Club keeps**, with a **Total** row. The tab appears only when an [instructor type](/settings/instructor-types) posts coach time to its own revenue account (see below).

The **Export** button downloads the tab you are viewing, as Excel or CSV.

### Coach revenue on its own account

An area booked with a coach is two sales: the area half posts to the [area type's](/settings/area-types) revenue account, and the coach half to the [instructor type's](/settings/instructor-types) revenue account. This holds however the booking was paid for or changed afterwards. To report coaching apart from area hire, create a **Coaches** revenue account and pick it as the **Revenue Account** of your coach instructor type. Nothing else needs to change. The **By Account** tab then shows the two lines side by side, the **Revenue account** filter reads either one on its own, the **Coaching** tab appears, and [Home](/home/metrics#coaching) shows a **Coaching** panel.

A season pass is one sale on its plan's revenue account, whether or not its slot comes with a coach.

## Payments report

The **Payments Report** (**Analytics** > **Revenue** > **Payments Report**) focuses on collection: of what you charged in the period, how much has been paid, and what money came in. It is the report behind the **Payments** chart on Home.

Cards:

- **Total Billed** - Everything charged in the period, failed charges included, because a failed charge is still owed.
- **Paid** - The part of those charges that payments have covered.
- **Unpaid** - The rest. **Paid** plus **Unpaid** equals **Total Billed**.
- **Collected** - Payments received in the period, whatever they paid for, including tax, less refunds.

**Paid** and **Unpaid** split the period's billing by today's payment state, so an older period has had longer to be paid. Only **Total Billed** and **Collected** are compared with another period.

**Collected by method** lists what each payment method collected, with its share of **Collected**, so you can match the till and the card statement. The **Payments by day** chart stacks paid and unpaid amounts for each day's charges, so you can spot days where billing outran collection.

## Invoices dashboard

The **Invoices dashboard** (**Analytics** > **Revenue** > **Invoices Dashboard**) is the invoice-level view.

Count cards: **Total Invoices**, **Paid Invoices**, **Pending Invoices** (not yet paid in full, overdue ones included), and **Overdue Invoices** (past their due date today and not yet paid in full).

Amount cards: **Total Invoice Amount**, **Paid Amount**, **Pending Amount** (what is still owed; a partly paid invoice counts only its remaining balance), and **Collection Rate** (paid as a percentage of billed). Pending and overdue are today's state, so they show no change when you compare.

Charts:

- **Invoice trends** - Invoice count and amount over time.
- **Payment methods** - A pie of amount collected per payment method.
- **Invoice status breakdown** - **Paid**, **Not yet due**, **Overdue Invoices**, and **Cancelled** invoice counts.

Use collection rate and the overdue count together to decide when to chase unpaid [charges](/sales/charges) or review failed renewals on [Membership billing](/sales/membership-billing).

## Exporting reports

The **Revenue Report** has an **Export** button that downloads the tab you are viewing (source, revenue account, plan, items, or coaching) as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, honoring your filters. For what each member owes and for how long, export [Amount Due](/analytics/finance#amount-due) under **Finance**. For bundled accounting exports such as the QuickBooks bundle, use [Settings > Data Tools > Export](/settings/export).

## Worked examples

**Racket sports - membership versus booking revenue.** A tennis and padel club opens the **Revenue dashboard** and sees the **Memberships** bars growing while **Bookings** stay flat. They switch to the **Revenue report** **By Plan** tab to confirm which membership tier is driving the growth, then export it for the owner.

**Martial arts - collecting on overdue invoices.** A muay-thai gym checks the **Invoices dashboard**, sees a rising **Overdue invoices** count and a collection rate under 90%, and opens [Charges](/sales/charges) to charge saved cards for the overdue balances and follow up with members who have none.

**Padel - coaching apart from area hire.** A padel gym keeps area rentals on **Revenue** (4000) and coach time on **Coaches** (4100). The **By Account** tab shows both lines each month, the **Coaching** tab shows each coach's hours and what the gym keeps after their pay, and picking **Coaches** in the **Revenue account** filter on the **Payments Report** shows how much coaching was billed and how much is still unpaid.

**Pilates / Yoga - reconciling the month.** A reformer studio uses the **Revenue report** **By Account** tab to map class and retail revenue to the right revenue accounts, drills into a month cell to verify a large figure, and exports it for their bookkeeper.

**Gym / Fitness - watching cash collection.** A large fitness club uses the **Payments report** to track paid versus unpaid by day across the month, catching a stretch where billed amounts spiked (annual renewals) but collection lagged, then follows up on the unpaid invoices.

**Climbing - is the intro offer paying off?** A climbing gym compares the **Revenue dashboard** **Drop-Ins** and **Other** stacks against **Memberships**, confirming that day-pass and gear revenue rose alongside the promotion rather than cannibalizing memberships.

## Tips & best practices

- Set the **Date range** before reading any number. Reports default to 30 days, so a surprising "zero" usually means the period or the selected club is excluding data.
- Use the **Revenue dashboard** for the trend and the **Revenue report** for exact monthly figures; drill into a cell whenever a number looks off.
- Reconcile the chart of accounts with the **By Account** tab monthly, so exports to your accounting system are already mapped correctly.
- Revenue, paid, and unpaid are built on the charges dated in your period; **Collected** follows the payments. To follow individual payments, use [Sales > Finance > Payments](/sales/payments).
- Pair the **Invoices Dashboard** collection rate with [Amount Due](/analytics/finance#amount-due) to see both the total and who owes it.

## Troubleshooting

- **Collected is lower than Total Revenue.** Some charges in the period are not paid yet, or were paid from the Wallet. Look at the translucent (unpaid) portions of the revenue trend bars to find the gap. Remember that **Collected** includes tax and **Total Revenue** does not, and that **Collected** also counts payments for charges from earlier periods.
- **Collected shows a dash.** A **Source** filter is picked on **Revenue**; payments have no source.
- **A number is zero when you know you transacted.** Check the date range first (it defaults to 30 days), then the club selected in the breadcrumb and the billing entity and revenue account filters.
- **Refunds seem to change the totals.** Money refunded back out to a card comes off both the revenue and the payments figures for that sale. A sale paid with wallet credit never counts as revenue in the first place.
- **Revenue by account has an Unassigned row.** A charge stored with no revenue account counts under your default account, so **Unassigned** only appears when no account is marked default. Mark one as **Default Revenue Account** in [Settings > Billing > General](/settings/billing-general#revenue-accounts).
- **Coach time shows under the area's account.** The coach half of a booking posts to the instructor type's revenue account. Check that the coach's instructor type has its **Revenue Account** set; **None (use default)** files coach time on the default account.
- **There is no Revenue account filter.** It only appears once your organization has two or more active revenue accounts.
- **There is no Coaching tab.** No instructor type posts to its own revenue account; see [Coach revenue on its own account](#coach-revenue-on-its-own-account).
- **The Export button is disabled.** There are no rows for the current filters. Widen the date range or clear a filter, then export.

## Who can see this

The Revenue reports need the `analytics.read.club` permission (broader analytics permissions satisfy it). Admins, managers, and the Sales role have it by default; front desk and instructors do not. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
