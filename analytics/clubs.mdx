The **Clubs** category in **Analytics** rolls up activity, revenue, and occupancy for each of your locations. It answers two questions: what is happening in the gym right now, and how have the numbers trended over a period you choose. Both dashboards read live from your operational data, so bookings, check-ins, and payments show up as they land. Owners and managers use it to run each location and compare them.

## When to use it

Use it to watch the day as it happens, compare your locations, and decide where you have spare or missing capacity. Typical questions it answers:

- **Which location's areas are underused?** - an operator with two padel venues compares **Area Occupancy** per venue over 90 days, then checks the day-of-week pattern to see when the quieter site fills.
- **Why is revenue low today when bookings look normal?** - a CrossFit box sees check-ins tracking as usual on the hourly chart while revenue stays flat, a sign that today's visits are free or covered by memberships rather than sold.
- **Is there demand for another class slot?** - a yoga studio reads **Class Occupancy** alongside the weekday pattern before adding a second morning reformer class.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to read each of these.

## The two dashboards

Open **Analytics** and pick the **Clubs** category to reach two dashboards:

- **Today's Performance** - A same-day view of bookings, revenue, visitors, and check-ins, broken down by hour. It refreshes on its own every five minutes.
- **Performance Trends** - The same figures plus occupancy over a period you pick, with daily trends and day-of-week patterns.

Both dashboards follow the club selected in the breadcrumb at the top of the admin. Leave it on **All clubs** to see the whole organization, or pick one location to scope every card and chart to it. Numbers are counted in your organization's time zone (set under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**), so "today" means today where the gym is, not where you are.

## Today's Performance

This is the live operations view. It shows today's date and the current hour at the top, and re-queries every five minutes so you can leave it open on a screen at the front desk. It has no date filter, because it is always scoped to today.

### KPI cards

The top rows summarize the day so far:

- **Bookings Today** - Bookings that start today and are not cancelled. The area and instructor holds the platform creates for each class are not counted, so only real client bookings count.
- **Revenue Today** - Revenue from today's sales, excluding tax, whether paid yet or not, net of refunds. The part of a sale settled from Wallet funds no one paid in, such as a voucher, is not counted.
- **Visitors** - Each person once, with a check-in or a booking (not cancelled) today.
- **Check-ins** - Check-ins for today.
- **Bookings Confirmed** - Today's bookings that stand: still confirmed, checked in, or a no-show.
- **Bookings Cancelled** - Today's bookings that were cancelled.
- **Memberships Created** - New memberships sold today.
- **Revenue from Bookings** - The part of today's revenue that came from bookings.

### Hourly Trends chart

A combined chart plots **bookings** (by the hour they start), **check-ins**, and **revenue** hour by hour across the day. A dashed **Now** line marks the current hour. Hours that have passed are drawn solid and hours still ahead faded and dashed, so bookings already on the schedule for later today do not read as activity that already happened. Use it to see when your day peaks, and hover any hour for exact figures.

<Note>
The Today view focuses on activity and revenue by hour. For occupancy, use **Performance Trends** below or the [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy) report.
</Note>

## Performance Trends

This is the historical view. Set a period in the filter bar (it defaults to the last 30 days) and, optionally, pick a single club in the breadcrumb. **Group by** sets whether the trend charts show days, weeks, or months (**Auto** picks from the length of the period), and **Compare with** shows how each card moved against the previous period or the same period last year.

### KPI cards

- **Total Bookings** - Bookings starting in the period that are not cancelled.
- **Total Revenue** - Revenue for the period, on the same excluding-tax basis as the Today view.
- **Total Memberships** - Memberships sold in the period.
- **Total Check-ins** - Check-ins in the period.
- **Average Booking Value** - Total revenue divided by total bookings.
- **Class Occupancy** - Class places booked as a share of the places there were to fill.
- **Area Occupancy** - Area hours booked as a share of the areas' available hours. Shown for every gym, and hidden only for an independent professional, who has no areas. Reads `-` when there was nothing to fill.
- **Instructor Occupancy** - Instructor hours booked as a share of their open hours.

The occupancy cards divide everything booked by everything available over the whole period, rather than averaging the daily rates, so a quiet day weighs less than a busy one. A card shows `-` when the period had nothing to fill (for example, no class with a capacity limit was held).

### How occupancy is calculated

Occupancy compares what was booked with what was available:

- **Class Occupancy** - Booked places divided by class capacity. Cancelled bookings and classes, and classes with no capacity limit, are left out of both sides.
- **Area Occupancy** - Booked hours divided by the hours both the club and the area were open, less blocked time. Classes and Events running on an area count as booked. It is the same figure as on the [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy#how-occupancy-is-measured) report, on Home, and in the daily email.
- **Instructor Occupancy** - Booked instructor hours divided by the instructor's open hours.

Because availability comes from opening hours, keeping the hours of your clubs, [areas](/settings/areas), and instructors accurate directly affects whether these percentages mean anything.

### Charts

- **Daily Trends** - Bookings, check-ins, and revenue over the period on one chart.
- **Occupancy Trends** - Class, area, and instructor occupancy over time, with revenue overlaid as a dashed line so you can see whether busier resources actually bring in more. When the chart groups days into weeks or months, each point is booked over available for that whole week or month. A line breaks where there was nothing to fill, such as a day the club was closed.
- **Day of Week Patterns** - Average bookings, revenue, and check-ins for each weekday, so you can tell a quiet Tuesday from a packed Saturday.

## Worked examples

### Comparing two padel locations

You run two padel venues and want to know where areas sit empty. Open **Performance Trends**, set the period to **Last 3 months**, and note the **Area Occupancy** card with **All clubs** selected in the breadcrumb. Then switch to each venue in turn. If the downtown site sits at 78% and the suburban site at 41%, the suburban site has slack. Cross-check the **Day of Week Patterns** chart per site, and open [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy) for the hours: if the suburban venue only fills on weekends, the fix is midweek programming or off-peak pricing rather than more areas.

### Spotting a revenue dip early

A CrossFit box keeps **Today's Performance** open at reception. Mid-afternoon the **Revenue Today** card is well below a normal day while **Bookings Today** looks normal. The **Hourly Trends** chart shows check-ins tracking as usual but revenue flat. Revenue counts a sale on its day whether it is paid or not, so the gap is not unpaid bookings: it points to visits covered by memberships, or bookings made free. The bookings **Audit** shows each booking's **Entrance** (direct payment, membership, or external program) and **Payment** state, which confirms it.

### Checking capacity before adding a class

A yoga studio is considering a second morning reformer slot. On **Performance Trends**, with the studio picked in the breadcrumb, it reads the **Class Occupancy** card alongside the **Day of Week Patterns** bars. If mornings already run near capacity and weekday demand is even, the second slot is justified. If occupancy is 55% and concentrated on two days, promote the existing class first.

## Tips and best practices

- **Start with All clubs, then drill in.** Read the organization-wide numbers first, then switch the breadcrumb to a single location to explain an outlier.
- **Turn on Compare with.** On **Performance Trends**, each card then says how it moved against the previous period.
- **Leave Today open at the desk.** It refreshes every five minutes on its own, which makes it a good live board for front-of-house staff.
- **Fix opening hours before trusting occupancy.** Occupancy depends on the opening hours of clubs, areas, and instructors. If it looks wrong, check them under **Settings** > **Clubs** first.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Performance Trends shows nothing | No activity in the selected period or club | Widen the period, or switch the breadcrumb to **All clubs**. |
| Occupancy looks far too low or too high | Opening hours for clubs, areas, or instructors are missing or wrong, so the available hours are miscounted | Correct the opening hours under **Settings** > **Clubs** and on instructor schedules. |
| Revenue is lower than the cash you expected | Revenue excludes tax and refunds, and the part of a sale settled from Wallet funds no one paid in | This is expected. Reconcile money in against **Collected** on [Revenue analytics](/analytics/billing). |
| **Area Occupancy** is missing | Your organization is an independent professional, which has no areas | This is expected. |
| Today's revenue looks stalled while check-ins keep rising | The visits are covered by memberships or passes bought earlier, or the bookings are free | Check the **Entrance** column of **Analytics** > **Bookings** > [**Audit**](/analytics/schedule#booking-audit). |
| A club is missing from the breadcrumb | Your account only has access to certain locations, or the club is inactive | Confirm your access and the club's status under [Reference > Organization and clubs](/reference/organization-clubs). |
| Today's numbers seem an hour or a day off | "Today" uses the organization's time zone, not your device's | Verify the time zone under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**. |
