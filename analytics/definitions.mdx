---
title: 'Analytics definitions'
description: 'What each number on the analytics reports counts, and where it appears.'
---

Every number on the analytics reports is counted by one rule, and every place that shows it explains it with the same sentence: the info icon on the report card, the assistant, the MCP tool `explain_metric` and the Platform API (`GET /v1/platform/reports/definitions`).

## Money

| Metric                     | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Where it appears                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Revenue                    | Excluding tax, counted on the day of the sale whether paid or not, net of refunds; the part settled from Wallet funds no one paid in, such as a voucher, is not counted.                                                                 | Revenue, Clubs › Performance Trends, Clubs, Instructors, My performance |
| Collected                  | Payments received, including tax, on the day they were paid, less refunds; paying from the Wallet is not counted.                                                                                                                        | Revenue, Revenue › Payments Report                                      |
| Tax on revenue             | The tax on the period's revenue: on the day of the sale whether paid or not, net of refunds; the part settled from Wallet funds no one paid in, such as a voucher, is not counted.                                                       | Revenue                                                                 |
| Average charge value       | Revenue divided by the charges that earned some; a charge paid in full from the Wallet or refunded in full is not counted.                                                                                                               | Revenue                                                                 |
| Membership revenue         | Revenue from memberships in the period, renewals and joining fees included, ex-tax and net of refunds.                                                                                                                                   | Members › Memberships, Sales                                            |
| Other revenue              | All other revenue in the period: bookings, Passes, Drop-Ins, products and the rest, ex-tax and net of refunds.                                                                                                                           | Members › Memberships, Sales                                            |
| First membership revenue   | What the membership that converted each lead earned on its first charge and joining fee. A lead converted by a Pass or a visit brings none.                                                                                              | Sales › Leads & Conversion                                              |
| Bookings billed            | Including tax, net of refunds, with the cancellation fees the club kept; bookings covered by a membership are left out.                                                                                                                  | Bookings › Audit, Bookings                                              |
| Bookings paid              | What the club has kept on the period's bookings, including tax; a booking covered by a membership is left out.                                                                                                                           | Bookings                                                                |
| Average booking value      | Billed per paying booking, including tax; cancelled and free bookings, and those covered by a membership, are left out.                                                                                                                  | Bookings                                                                |
| Bookings outstanding       | What is still owed on the period's bookings, including tax; a booking covered by a membership owes nothing.                                                                                                                              | Bookings › Audit, Bookings                                              |
| Unpaid bookings            | Bookings with money still owed; a booking covered by a membership is never unpaid.                                                                                                                                                       | Bookings › Audit                                                        |
| Cancellation charges kept  | What the club collected and kept on the period's cancelled and missed bookings, including tax, net of refunds.                                                                                                                           | Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows                                   |
| Cancellation refunds       | What was given back on the period's cancelled and missed bookings: what went back to the Wallet and refunded payments, including tax.                                                                                                    | Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows                                   |
| Pending invoices           | Invoices from the period that are not yet paid in full, overdue ones included.                                                                                                                                                           | Revenue › Invoices Dashboard                                            |
| Overdue invoices           | Invoices from the period that are past their due date today and not yet paid in full.                                                                                                                                                    | Revenue › Invoices Dashboard                                            |
| Pending amount             | What is still owed on the period's unpaid invoices; a partly paid invoice counts only its remaining balance.                                                                                                                             | Revenue › Invoices Dashboard                                            |
| Area revenue               | Revenue earned on the area's bookings, excluding tax and net of refunds: court bookings, seats on classes held there and its share of Events held there. A booking covered by a membership adds nothing; that money is the membership's. | Bookings › Occupancy                                                    |
| Revenue per available hour | Revenue earned on the area's booked time inside its available hours, divided by those hours. Money on a booking held entirely outside them counts in Revenue, not here.                                                                  | Bookings › Occupancy                                                    |

## Time

| Metric    | What it counts                                                                               | Where it appears                             |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Check-ins | Every check-in, on the day of the booking or class it is for, otherwise the day it was made. | Check-ins, Clubs › Performance Trends, Clubs |

## Places

| Metric                 | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                              | Where it appears                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bookings               | Bookings that start in the period; cancelled bookings do not count.                                                                                                                         | Classes, Clubs › Performance Trends, Clubs, Instructors, My performance, My performance › Classes |
| All bookings           | Every booking that starts in the period, cancelled and pending ones included.                                                                                                               | Bookings › Audit, Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows, Bookings                                 |
| Confirmed bookings     | Bookings that stand: still confirmed, checked in or a no-show.                                                                                                                              | Bookings, Clubs, My performance                                                                   |
| Cancellation rate      | Cancelled bookings over every booking in the period, cancelled ones included.                                                                                                               | Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows, Bookings, My performance                                   |
| Late cancellation rate | Cancellations made with less notice than the Booking Policy needs for a full refund, over every booking in the period. The club's own cancellations and moves to another time are not late. | Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows                                                             |
| Classes                | Classes that start in the period; cancelled classes do not count.                                                                                                                           | Classes, My performance › Classes, My performance › Earnings Overview                             |
| Class fill             | Booked seats over capacity; cancelled bookings and classes do not count, and a class with no capacity limit is skipped.                                                                     | Classes, Clubs › Performance Trends, My performance › Classes                                     |
| Occupancy              | Hours booked on the areas over the hours they were available: the club's hours and each area's own, less blocked time. A blocked hour counts as neither.                                    | Bookings › Occupancy                                                                              |
| Hours available        | The hours both the club and the area were open, less the area's blocks and the club's.                                                                                                      | Bookings › Occupancy                                                                              |
| Hours booked           | Hours of bookings on the area that were not cancelled, classes and Events running on it included, inside its available hours; overlapping bookings count once.                              | Bookings › Occupancy                                                                              |
| Busiest hour           | The hour of the week with the highest occupancy over the period.                                                                                                                            | Bookings › Occupancy                                                                              |

## Customers

| Metric                   | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Where it appears                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| No-show rate             | Bookings marked no-show over bookings that were checked in or marked no-show, up to today.                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Check-ins, Bookings › Cancellations and no-shows |
| Visitors                 | Each person once, with a check-in or a booking that starts in the period; cancelled bookings do not count.                                                                                                                                                                                  | Clubs                                            |
| Checked-in people        | Each person once, with a check-in in the period; a walk-in with no contact record is not counted.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Check-ins                                        |
| All customers            | Members plus Drop-ins counted right now, the number your 1club subscription is billed on; the date range does not change it.                                                                                                                                                                | Members › Member Engagement, Members             |
| Members                  | Contacts with a live membership or Season Pass, across all clubs. The Members dashboard counts them right now, whatever the dates; the Memberships dashboard counts them at the end of its period.                                                                                          | Members, Members › Memberships                   |
| Drop-ins                 | Contacts with a live Pass or Drop-In, or a visit in the last 30 days, counted right now; after 30 days without a visit they are Lapsed.                                                                                                                                                     | Members                                          |
| New customers            | Contacts who became a Member or Drop-in for the first time in the period; a returning customer, or a Drop-in taking a membership, is not new.                                                                                                                                               | Members, Sales › Customer Lifecycle, Sales       |
| Upgrades                 | Moves from Drop-in to Member in the period, each move counted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Sales                                            |
| Lapsed                   | Moves from Member or Drop-in to Lapsed in the period, each move counted: no live plan and no visit for 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                             | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Churned                  | Contacts whose membership ended in the period with no new membership within 14 days, whatever the reason. Pass and Drop-In holders never churn: they lapse.                                                                                                                                 | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Churn rate               | Of the contacts holding a running membership on the first day of the period, the share who churned in it.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Frozen                   | Memberships paused in the period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Plan upgrades            | Members who moved to a dearer membership plan, by monthly price, within 14 days of leaving their previous one.                                                                                                                                                                              | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Plan downgrades          | Members who moved to a cheaper membership plan, by monthly price, within 14 days of leaving their previous one.                                                                                                                                                                             | Sales › Customer Lifecycle                       |
| Memberships sold         | Memberships (plans that bill again) added in the period, less those that only replaced the member's previous plan. Passes and Drop-Ins are not counted.                                                                                                                                     | Members › Memberships, Sales                     |
| Active memberships       | Memberships running at the end of the period, paused ones included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Members › Memberships, Sales                     |
| Paused memberships       | Memberships paused at the end of the period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Members › Memberships, Sales                     |
| Membership cancellations | Membership cancellations recorded in the period, ending at once, at the end of the paid period or at a later period end. One withdrawn since is not counted.                                                                                                                                | Members › Memberships, Sales                     |
| Ended memberships        | Memberships that stopped running in the period, apart from one its holder replaced with another plan. A cancellation counts here when the membership stops, not when it is recorded.                                                                                                        | Members › Memberships                            |
| New leads                | Contacts who entered the sales pipeline in the period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Sales, Sales › Leads & Conversion                |
| Converted leads          | Of the period's new leads, those who have since been given a plan or paid for something.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Sales › Leads & Conversion                       |
| Lead conversion rate     | Converted leads as a share of new leads.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Sales › Leads & Conversion                       |
| Median days to convert   | For the converted leads, the middle value of the days from entering the pipeline to converting.                                                                                                                                                                                             | Sales › Leads & Conversion                       |
| Recent tag changes       | Tags added to or removed from contacts in the last 7 days; the date range does not change it.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Members › Member Engagement                      |
| Recent score changes     | Changes to contacts' engagement scores in the last 7 days; the date range does not change it.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Members › Member Engagement                      |
| Eligible for promotion   | Members who meet every requirement their next level sets (sessions, days at level and skills), counted the way the member's own progress counts them. A level that sets no requirement is not counted.                                                                                      | Members › Progression Report                     |
| Stalled                  | Members not yet eligible who have held their level longer than the track's median time at it, read from the promotions out of that level in the last year (at least 3).                                                                                                                     | Members › Progression Report                     |
| Promoted                 | Members moved up to a new level with an earned date in the period. Stripes, corrections and first enrolments do not count.                                                                                                                                                                  | Members › Progression Report                     |
| Median days at level     | The middle value of the listed members' days at their current level, in calendar days (earned yesterday is 1).                                                                                                                                                                              | Members › Progression Report                     |
| First visits             | Contacts whose first-ever visit at the organization fell in the period: a check-in, or a booking that started and was not cancelled; a no-show is not a visit. Someone holding a plan sold over 30 days earlier, or a visit in the organization's first 30 days of records, is not counted. | Members › First visits                           |
| Returned within 14 days  | Of the first visitors whose 14 days have passed, the share who came again on a later day within 14 days of their first visit.                                                                                                                                                               | Members › First visits                           |
| Returned within 30 days  | Of the first visitors whose 30 days have passed, the share who came again on a later day within 30 days of their first visit.                                                                                                                                                               | Members › First visits                           |
| Bought a plan            | Of the first visitors whose 30 days have passed, the share who bought any plan by the end of those 30 days, including one bought before their first visit.                                                                                                                                  | Members › First visits                           |
| Became members           | Of the first visitors whose 30 days have passed, the share who took a membership by the end of those 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                               | Members › First visits                           |
| Intro offers converted   | Of the intro offers sold in the period whose 30 days have passed, the share whose customer took a further membership or pass within 30 days, or kept the intro membership that long.                                                                                                        | Members › First visits                           |
| No second visit          | The period's first visitors, not archived, who have not come back on a later day so far.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Members › First visits                           |
| Intro ending             | Intro offers that are not memberships, ending within 7 days before or after today, with no further membership or pass after them.                                                                                                                                                           | Members › First visits                           |

## Email

| Metric       | What it counts                                                                                         | Where it appears |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Delivered    | Emails that arrived, including every one later opened, clicked, unsubscribed from or reported as spam. | Marketing        |
| Opened       | Emails opened at least once, the clicked ones included, so opened over delivered is the open rate.     | Marketing        |
| Clicked      | Emails with a link clicked at least once; each one is also counted as opened and delivered.            | Marketing        |
| Unsubscribed | Emails whose recipient unsubscribed through them; counted as delivered, apart from opened and clicked. | Marketing        |
