The **Member Engagement** dashboard (**Analytics** > **Members** > **Member Engagement**) shows how engaged your members are right now and how their scores have moved over time. An engagement score is a 0 to 100 number per contact, written by a background job from their visits alone: how often they come compared with your other members, whether they keep up with the period before, and how recently they came. Owners and managers use it to see who is thriving and who is going quiet.

<Note>
Engagement *analytics* (this page) is the 0 to 100 scoring dashboard. The smart-tag category that groups members by recent check-ins is called **Attendance** and lives under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging). They are related but different systems - the score is a single number; Attendance is a tag.
</Note>

## When to use it

The score turns each member's visits into one number, so you can spot at-risk members early and size a win-back or loyalty offer. Typical questions it answers:

- **Are more members slipping?** - watch whether the **Low** count grows week over week.
- **How big is your win-back audience?** - the **Low** count, which picks up quiet members such as a reformer client still on an active plan who stopped coming, sizes a win-back promotion or automation.
- **How many members could you ask for a referral?** - the **Rockstar** count sizes a loyalty offer or referral ask.
- **What happened with this member?** - pick one member to see how their score moved when you investigate a churn case.

See [Using this for churn-risk and win-back](#using-this-for-churn-risk-and-win-back) and the [worked examples](#worked-examples) for more.

## Score levels

The score maps to one of four levels. The thresholds are fixed in code and are the same everywhere a level appears (the dashboard distribution, the score chip on member pages, and any tag-based automations driven by engagement):

| Level | Score range | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rockstar** | 75 to 100 | Highly engaged, frequent users |
| **High** | 50 to 74 | Well-engaged regulars |
| **Medium** | 25 to 49 | Moderate activity |
| **Low** | 0 to 24 | At-risk, infrequent |

## How scoring is calculated

The score reads **visits** alone: a check-in, or a completed booking. Payments, memberships and tenure play no part, so a member who pays every month but never comes scores low. Every part is read over the **attendance period** you set under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging#engagement-scoring) (30 days by default), and the blend itself is fixed:

| Part | Weight | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frequency** | 50 | Where the member's visits this period sit among your other scored members (their percentile, with ties counted half). A padel gym where four games a month is a lot and a gym where twelve visits is normal both read right. No visit this period scores 0 here. |
| **Trend** | 25 | Visits this period against the period before. Full while they keep up or come more, falling as they drop off. Someone who came last period and stopped scores 0 here. Someone new this period scores full. |
| **Recency** | 25 | Full on the day of a visit, fading to 0 one period after the last visit. |

The three parts add up to a score from 0 to 100, rounded.

<Note>
Only **members holding an active plan** get a score. When a member's plan ends, their score is removed. **While scoring is off under Smart tagging, the dashboard is not listed in Analytics at all.**
</Note>

### When scores are written

Scores are not computed live. They are written by the nightly member tagging job (03:00 UTC), and all at once when you turn **Calculate engagement scores** on. The dashboard reads the stored score, so a visit today shows in the score after the next run. The first score, and each time a score moves by 5 points or more or changes level, is recorded on the member's history, which is what powers the progression chart and the per-member trend.

## Worked examples

These use the default 30-day attendance period.

### A regular (martial arts)

A jiu-jitsu member trained 12 times in the last 30 days, more than 80% of your scored members, came 10 times the month before, and last trained yesterday.

- Frequency: 80th percentile = 0.8 x 50 = **40**.
- Trend: 12 visits against 10, keeping up = **25**.
- Recency: 1 day ago, (1 - 1/30) x 25 = **24**.
- Score = 40 + 25 + 24 = **89 -> Rockstar**.

### A member drifting away (Pilates / yoga)

A reformer client came twice in the last 30 days, which puts them at the 30th percentile, came 8 times the month before, and last visited 15 days ago.

- Frequency: 0.3 x 50 = **15**.
- Trend: 2 visits against 8 = 0.25 x 25 = **6**.
- Recency: 15 days ago, (1 - 15/30) x 25 = **13**.
- Score = 15 + 6 + 13 = **34 -> Medium**, and falling. The [At Risk and Declining tags](/settings/smart-tagging#declining) catch members like this one.

## What is on the page

### Filters

- **Period** - the standard analytics period. It defaults to the last 30 days; widen it to see a longer progression.
- **Group by** - whether the progression chart shows days, weeks, or months.
- **Select Member** - above the chart, pick a single contact to switch it from the organization's average to that member's own progression.

The dashboard always covers the whole organization, whichever club the breadcrumb shows.

### KPI cards

Four cards across the top:

- **All customers** - count of contacts of type member or drop-in in your organization (archived contacts are excluded). This is the same figure as **All customers** on the [Members dashboard](/analytics/members#members-dashboard).
- **Average Engagement Score** - mean across members who have a score, with the count of scored members shown underneath.
- **Recent Tag Changes** - tags added to or removed from contacts in the last 7 days. The period does not change it.
- **Recent Score Changes** - changes to engagement scores in the last 7 days. The period does not change it.

### Score distribution

A row of four counts showing how many members sit in each level: **Rockstar** (green), **High** (blue), **Medium** (amber), **Low** (red). This is your at-a-glance health check.

### Progression chart

**Engagement Progression Over Time** is a line chart of the average engagement score over time. The Y-axis is fixed at 0 to 100 and the time bucket adapts to the range. The line is built from recorded score-change events grouped by day and averaged, so it tracks how scores move as the nightly job re-runs, rather than a snapshot of every member each day.

When you select a single member, the chart becomes **Individual Member Progression**, showing that member's score history, and the legend changes from **Average Engagement Score** to **Engagement Score**. A panel above the chart shows the member's **current score** and any tags. If a member has no score yet, you will see **No score** and the chart will be empty.

## Using this for churn-risk and win-back

- **Spot at-risk members early.** If the **Low** count grows week over week, members are slipping. Cross-check against recent tag changes for context.
- **Build a win-back audience.** Use the **Low** count as the size of a re-engagement campaign, then target those members with a promotion or an automation.
- **Reward Rockstars.** Use the Rockstar count as the audience size for a loyalty offer or referral ask.
- **Track a specific member's journey.** Pick a member in the dropdown to see how their score moved - useful when investigating a churn case or qualifying a referral candidate.
- **Tune the period before you trust it.** The attendance period under Smart tagging sets the window every part is read over. Check that the levels match your instinct about who is engaged, then use the dashboard for tracking.

## Tips and best practices

- **Frequency is relative to your own gym.** A member's frequency is compared with your other scored members, so the score adapts to how often people come to your kind of gym. You never need to tell it what "often" means.
- **Set the attendance period deliberately.** A 30-day period suits most gyms; shorten it if your members visit several times a week and you want the score to react faster.
- **Give new data time.** Scores update on the nightly run. Do not expect a visit made an hour ago to move the dashboard immediately.
- **Payments do not count.** A member who pays every month but never comes scores Low on purpose: they are the ones likely to cancel.

## Troubleshooting

- **Member Engagement is missing from Analytics** - Cause: engagement scoring is off under Smart tagging, so the report is not offered and a bookmarked link does not open. Fix: turn on scoring under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging).
- **A member shows No score** - Symptom: the per-member panel reads "No score". Cause: the member holds no active plan, or the nightly job has not run since they got one. Fix: confirm they have an active plan, then wait for the next nightly run.
- **Average score looks too high** - Symptom: the average seems better than reality. Cause: members without a score are excluded from the average, so a small pool of engaged members can skew it. Fix: read the **scored members** count under the Average card alongside it.
- **Progression chart is flat or sparse** - Symptom: few points on the line. Cause: the line only plots days on which scores actually changed, and a short history has few events. Fix: widen the time range.
- **Score did not update after activity** - Symptom: a member trained or paid but their score is unchanged. Cause: scores are written by the nightly job, not live, and payments never count. Fix: wait for the next run.

## Limitations

- The dashboard is read-only. To change the attendance period, edit [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging) settings.
- The blend (50 / 25 / 25) and the level thresholds (75 / 50 / 25) are fixed - only the attendance period is yours to set.
- There is no "no-score-yet" filter - members without a score are silently excluded from the average and chart.
- Scores need data. Brand-new members may not have a stable score until they have accrued a few weeks of activity and the job has run.
