The **Members** category in **Analytics** describes your people: how the customer base is growing, how your memberships are selling and ending, who is slipping away or about to lapse, what newcomers do after their first visit, and who is ready for their next belt or level. Owners and managers use it to track the health of the member base, and the lists in it double as call lists for the front desk and coaches.

## When to use it

- **Is the base really growing?** - a boxing gym reads **New customers** for the month against **All customers**, with **Compare with** on **Previous period**, to see whether new joins keep up with members who lapse.
- **Who stopped coming?** - a CrossFit box opens **At-risk customers**, sorts by **Monthly value**, and messages the members worth the most before they cancel.
- **Whose membership ends next week?** - a yoga studio opens **Renewals** for the next 7 days and calls everyone under **Ending, no renewal**.
- **Does the intro offer turn people into members?** - a climbing gym reads its intro offer's row on **First visits** to see how many buyers went on to a membership within 30 days.
- **Who is ready for grading?** - a jiu-jitsu academy clicks **Eligible now** on the **Progression Report**, ticks the students, and promotes them together.

## Members dashboard

**Analytics** > **Members** > **Dashboard** opens with a row of cards, a growth chart, and two member lists. It always covers the whole organization, whichever club the breadcrumb shows.

### Filters

- **Period** - sets **New customers** and the growth chart.
- **Compare with** - compares **New customers** with another period. The other counts are as of now, so they show no change.
- **Age band** (under **Filters**) - narrows the dashboard to members in the bands you pick, from **Babies** to **Seniors**, each placed by their date of birth on the period's last day. It is offered only when your gym records dates of birth.

### Cards

- **All customers** - Members plus Drop-ins, counted right now. This is the number your 1club subscription is billed on, and the period does not change it.
- **Members** - Contacts with a live membership or Season Pass, counted right now across all clubs. Click it to open the **Members** tab of the member list.
- **Drop-ins** - Contacts with a live Pass or Drop-In, or a visit in the last 30 days, counted right now. After 30 days without a visit, a Drop-in becomes Lapsed. Click it to open the **Drop-ins** tab.
- **New customers** - Contacts who became a Member or Drop-in for the first time in the period. Someone who comes back after lapsing, or a Drop-in who takes a membership, is not new. Click it to list them, with **Joined as** and **Customer since**.
- **Average engagement** - a gauge of the average engagement score and level. It appears only when engagement scoring is on, and shows current scores, updated every night.

The **Members** and **Drop-ins** cards open the member list only for staff who can read members and memberships, and not while an age band is picked (the member list has no age bands, so it would show more people than the card).

### Customer growth trends

A stacked area chart of **Members** and **Drop-ins** week by week across the period. The series are rebuilt from recorded type changes, so a drop-in who takes a membership moves from one area to the other. Use it to tell real growth from churning in place.

### Top members and low engagement alerts

- **Top Members** - the most engaged members, each with their level and score, for recognition or a referral ask.
- **Low Engagement Alerts** - members scored as low engagement, lowest score first.

Both show current scores, updated every night, and ignore the period. They need engagement scoring on. See [Engagement analytics](/analytics/engagement).

## Memberships

**Analytics** > **Members** > **Memberships** shows how your membership base did over the period. It opens on **This month** and offers **Group by** and **Compare with**, so you can read week on week (this week against the previous period) or year on year (any period against the same one last year). It follows the club in the breadcrumb.

### Cards

| Card                   | What it counts                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Memberships sold**   | Memberships (plans that bill again) added in the period, less those that only replaced the member's previous plan. Passes and Drop-Ins are not counted.       |
| **Active memberships** | Memberships running at the end of the period, paused ones included.                                                                                           |
| **Paused memberships** | Memberships paused at the end of the period. Shows "-" for a period before pause history was recorded.                                                        |
| **Cancellations**      | Membership cancellations recorded in the period, ending at once, at the end of the paid period, or at a later period end. One withdrawn since is not counted. |
| **Members**            | Contacts with a live membership or Season Pass at the end of the period. Shown only with **All clubs**, because a single club has no member count of its own. |
| **Membership revenue** | Revenue from memberships in the period, renewals and joining fees included, excluding tax and net of refunds.                                                 |
| **Other revenue**      | Everything else: bookings, Passes, Drop-Ins, products, and the rest, excluding tax and net of refunds.                                                        |

More paused memberships and more cancellations read red when you compare.

### Charts and table

- **Active memberships** - the count of running memberships over the period. With weeks or months, each point is the count on the last day.
- **Memberships sold and ended** - memberships sold drawn above the line and **Memberships ended** below it, per day, week, or month. A membership counts as ended when it stops running, not when its cancellation is recorded, and one its holder replaced with another plan is not counted.
- **Other revenue** - a table of where the rest of your revenue came from: **Bookings**, **Passes**, **Drop-Ins**, each product category, and **Other**. With a comparison on, it adds the compared period and a **Change** column, and keeps a source that earned last time and nothing now.

Use **Export** to download the report.

## At-risk customers

**Analytics** > **Members** > **At-risk customers** lists the paying customers who are slipping away, so you can reach them before they cancel. It reads two smart tags, which you set up under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging):

- **At Risk** (the **At Risk Tag**) - members who hold a membership but have stopped coming: no visit in the set number of days, nothing booked for the next 3 days, nothing unpaid, and a plan held for at least 2 weeks. A paused membership does not count.
- **Declining** (the **Trend Tags**) - members who came at least a set number of times in the attendance period before, and now come at most a set share of that.

The report appears only when at least one of the two is on, and uses the names and colors you gave the tags. A line above the table spells out the rule with your own numbers, for example "At Risk: no visit for 10 days." (10 days is the default). The list is read from the tags themselves, so it matches the tag on the member's profile and the tag filter on the member list. Tags are refreshed by the nightly smart-tagging run.

### What is on the page

There is no period: the report is as of today. It follows the club in the breadcrumb.

- **Signal** (under **Filters**) - show only **At Risk** or only **Declining**.
- **People at risk** - how many are listed.
- **Monthly value at stake** - what the listed members' plans are worth a month, excluding tax.

The table shows each person's **Member**, **Signal**, **Last visit**, visits in the **Last N days** and the **N days before** (N is your attendance period, 30 days by default), **Tagged on**, **Plan**, and **Monthly value**. It opens sorted by monthly value, largest first; you can also sort by last visit or tagged date.

Tick members (or **Select all**) and click **Message** to write to them in one go. **Export** downloads the list.

## Renewals

**Analytics** > **Members** > **Renewals** lists who is about to lapse and what to do about each. Instead of a period, pick **Ending within**: **7 days**, **14 days**, **30 days** (the default), or **60 days** from today. It follows the club in the breadcrumb. Only customers (Members and Drop-ins) are listed, not staff.

The cards and the table use four groups:

| Group                       | Who is in it                                                                                                                                                                                              | Card also shows             |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Ending, no renewal**      | Memberships that do not renew, ending in the window, with nothing after them                                                                                                                              | What they are worth a month |
| **Cancelled at period end** | Memberships cancelled while still running, ending in the window, with nothing after them                                                                                                                  | What they are worth a month |
| **Pass running out**        | Passes and Drop-Ins ending in the window, or with 2 or fewer sessions or hours left. A Season Pass is judged on its end date only. One whose holder already bought the next of the same plan is left out. | -                           |
| **Renews automatically**    | Memberships that renew by themselves in the window. Folded away by default: their risk is a failing payment, which [Amount Due](/analytics/finance#amount-due) shows.                                     | -                           |

"Nothing after them" means the member holds no other live membership that starts on or after the end day. Each row shows the **Member**, the **Plan**, **Ends on**, what is **Left** (sessions or hours, for a pass), and the **Monthly value**. Sort by end date (soonest first) or by monthly value. Tick rows, a whole group, or **Select all**, then click **Message**. **Export** downloads the list.

## First visits

**Analytics** > **Members** > **First visits** follows the people who came to you for the first time in the period, and shows how each intro offer converts. It opens on **Last 3 months**, compares with the previous period, and follows the club in the breadcrumb (a first visit counts for the club it happened at).

A **first visit** is a contact's first-ever check-in, or first booking that started and was not cancelled, at your organization. A no-show is not a visit. Someone holding a plan sold more than 30 days earlier is an existing customer whose earlier visits were never recorded, so they are not counted. Nor is a visit in your organization's first 30 days of records, when everyone looks new; the page says from which date it counts.

### Filters

- **Age band** - only people in the bands you pick.
- **Program / class type** - only people whose first visit was a class of that program or type. It narrows the first visits, not the intro offers, and the page says so.

### Cards

| Card                        | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First visits**            | People whose first visit fell in the period.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Returned within 14 days** | Of those whose 14 days have passed, the share who came again on a later day within 14 days.                                                                                         |
| **Returned within 30 days** | The same within 30 days.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Bought a plan**           | Of those whose 30 days have passed, the share who bought any plan by then, including one bought before the first visit.                                                             |
| **Became members**          | Of those whose 30 days have passed, the share who took a membership by then.                                                                                                        |
| **Intro offers converted**  | Of the intro offers sold in the period whose 30 days have passed, the share whose customer took a further membership or pass within 30 days, or kept an intro membership that long. |

Each rate is of the people whose window has closed, shown as `X of Y`. People still inside their window are counted apart (**Too recent to tell: N**), so a period still running reads the same rate as one long past.

### Their first 30 days and By source

**Their first 30 days** follows the same group step by step: **First visits**, **Came back**, **Bought a plan**, **Became members**. **By source** shows the same figures for each [lead source](/members/leads#sources).

### Intro offers

An intro offer is a plan you marked with the **Intro offer** switch in its settings, or a [promotion](/sales/promotions) limited to a first purchase on plans. Each gets a row: how many were **Sold** in the period, how many **Converted**, how many are **Too recent**, the **Avg. days to convert**, and the plan **Most went on to**. With no intro offers yet, the section offers **Go to plans**.

### Follow up

Two lists show who to contact:

- **No second visit** - the period's first visitors, not archived, who have not come back on a later day so far.
- **Intro ending** - intro offers that are not memberships, ending within 7 days before or after today, with no further membership or pass after them.

Tick people and click **Message**, or **Export** the list.

## Progression report

**Analytics** > **Members** > **Progression Report** shows, for each enabled [progression track](/settings/progression), where every member stands and who is ready for their next level. It lists your current Members and Drop-ins; lapsed contacts and leads are left out. It follows the club in the breadcrumb and appears only when at least one track is enabled.

### Cards

The four cards are for the track you have open:

- **Eligible now** - members who meet every requirement their next level sets (sessions, days at level, and skills), counted the way their own progress bar counts them. A level that sets no requirement is not counted.
- **Stalled** - members not yet eligible who have held their level longer than the track's median time at it, read from the promotions out of that level in the last year (at least 3).
- **Promoted** - members moved up to a new level with an earned date in the period. Stripes, corrections, and first enrolments do not count. This is the one card compared with another period.
- **Median days at level** - the middle value of the listed members' days at their current level.

Click **Eligible now** or **Stalled** to list only those members with every row ticked, ready to promote or message. Click again to list everyone.

### The table

One tab per track. Each row shows the **Member**, **Current Level**, a **Status** (**Eligible**, **On track**, **Stalled**, or **No requirement**), **Sessions** toward the next level (for example `12 of 20`), **Days at level**, and the **History** of levels reached, drawn as belts in their colors. Click the history to open the member's whole progression. Narrow it with **Status** and **Age band** under **Filters**.

### Acting from the table

- **Promote** - moves the member up one level, earned today, with the same confirmation and optional member notice as the member list. Tick several rows to promote them together. Needs permission to edit members.
- **Message** - writes to the member, or to their parent or guardian when the member is a child in a household.

**Export** downloads the open track.

## Worked examples

### Spotting a retention dip

A boxing gym shows 320 **All customers**, steady for three months. With the period on **Last 30 days** and **Compare with** on **Previous period**, **New customers** reads 25, down from 31, so roughly as many people lapsed as joined. The **Customer growth trends** chart shows Drop-ins shrinking while Members hold. The owner then opens **At-risk customers** to call members before they lapse.

### Running a renewal call list

A yoga studio opens **Renewals** every Monday with **Ending within** on **7 days**. The **Ending, no renewal** card says 6 memberships worth 420 a month. The manager ticks the group, clicks **Message**, and sends a renewal offer.

### Judging an intro offer

A climbing gym sells a "3 visits for 30" pass and marks the plan as an **Intro offer**. On **First visits** for the last quarter, its row shows 80 sold, 22 converted, and 10 too recent, with most buyers going on to the monthly membership. The **Intro ending** list shows who to call this week.

### Grading day at a jiu-jitsu academy

Before a grading, the head coach opens the **Progression Report** on the adult track, clicks **Eligible now**, checks the ticked students, and clicks **Promote** with the member notice on.

## Tips and best practices

- **Compare instead of eyeballing.** Leave **Compare with** on **Previous period** on the **Dashboard** and **Memberships** to see at once whether things moved.
- **Name the tags the way your staff talk.** **At-risk customers** shows the smart tags under the names you gave them.
- **Mark every intro plan.** A plan counts on **First visits** only once its **Intro offer** switch is on.
- **Read rates with their counts.** A 50% return rate of 4 people is not a trend; the `X of Y` under each card says how many were judged.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                                                            | Likely cause                                                                                 | Fix                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **At-risk customers**, **Progression Report**, or **Member Engagement** is missing | The feature behind it is off                                                                 | Turn on the **At Risk Tag** or **Trend Tags** or engagement scoring under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging), or enable a [progression track](/settings/progression). |
| **First visits** is missing                                                        | Your role cannot read member profiles                                                        | Ask an admin for member read access.                                                                                                                                        |
| Customer counts ignore the period or club                                          | **All customers**, **Members**, and **Drop-ins** are live organization-wide counts           | Read **New customers** for period figures, or **Memberships** for one club.                                                                                                 |
| The **Members** card is missing on **Memberships**                                 | A club is selected in the breadcrumb                                                         | Switch to **All clubs**.                                                                                                                                                    |
| The **Age band** filter is missing                                                 | No contact has a date of birth                                                               | Record dates of birth on member profiles.                                                                                                                                   |
| **First visits** counts nobody early in your records                               | Visits in your organization's first 30 days of records are not counted                       | This is expected; the page says from which date it counts.                                                                                                                  |
| An intro offer has no row                                                          | The plan's **Intro offer** switch is off                                                     | Turn it on in the plan's settings.                                                                                                                                          |
| A member who renewed is still on **Renewals**                                      | The new membership starts before the old one ends, or is still waiting for its first payment | Check the member's memberships. An unpaid renewal is the call this report is for.                                                                                           |
| **Paused memberships** shows "-"                                                   | The period ends before pause history was recorded                                            | Pick a later period.                                                                                                                                                        |
