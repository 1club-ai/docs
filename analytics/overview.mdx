The **Analytics** section is a collection of pre-built dashboards and detail reports. Each report is a fixed page with a few KPI cards at the top and one or more charts below. Some dashboards link out to deeper per-metric reports.

There is no report builder or query interface - every report has predefined filters. If you need raw data to slice yourself, use [Settings > Data Tools > Export](/settings/export).

## Overview

- **Category sidebar** on the left groups every report under a heading (Clubs, Members, Check-ins, and so on). A search box at the top of the sidebar filters the list by report name or description. On a phone, **Analytics** opens on a category index instead.
- **KPI cards** give you the headline numbers at a glance (total revenue, average score, total members).
- **Recharts charts** below the cards are interactive: hover for tooltips, click a legend entry to toggle a series on or off, and pan or zoom on time-based charts.
- **Live and historical views** - the Clubs category has a live **Today's Performance** dashboard that auto-refreshes; every other report reads a date range you pick.
- **Club scoping** - most reports follow the club selected in the breadcrumb at the top of the admin; **All clubs** shows the whole organization.
- **Role-scoped access** - what each report shows respects the viewer's permissions and scope (see [Who can see what](#who-can-see-what)).

## What's available

The docs group these categories into a few pages:

- **[Clubs](/analytics/clubs)** - **Today's Performance** (auto-refreshing every five minutes) and **Performance Trends**.
- **[Members](/analytics/members)** - Customer counts and growth, progression, and balances.
- **[Engagement](/analytics/engagement)** - Member engagement scoring (0 to 100 per member) and how scores change over time.
- **[Schedule and instructors](/analytics/schedule)** - The **Check-ins**, **Bookings**, **Classes**, **Instructors**, and **My performance** categories.
- **[Revenue](/analytics/billing)** - Revenue dashboard, revenue report, payments report, and invoices dashboard.
- **[Marketing](/analytics/marketing)** - The **Marketing** and **Website and Apps** categories.
- **Inventory** - **Stock Movements** is a ledger of every receive, sale, adjustment, and waste entry. See [Inventory](/sales/inventory).

## How the dashboards work

Every report shares the same layout:

The **filter bar** at the top holds the date range: one field showing the selected days, with previous and next period arrows. Open it to pick a preset - **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 3 days**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 2 weeks**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 365 days**, **This year**, **Previous year** - or a custom range on the calendar. Reports that trend by month (for example the bookings monthly report and instructor reports) show month presets instead. Some pages add extra filters (billing entity, event type, source) on the same row. There is no club control: the report follows the club in the breadcrumb.

The **KPI cards** sit below the filter bar - usually three or four cards, each with a single number and an icon.

The **charts** are below the cards. They are built with Recharts, so hovering shows tooltips, the legend toggles series on and off, and you can pan or zoom on time-based charts.

Your chosen range is remembered per report (stored in the URL and locally), so returning to a report restores the range you last used there.

## Data freshness

Most dashboards run live queries against your operational data, so as soon as a booking is created or a payment lands, it shows up. Two exceptions:

- The **Clubs** > **Today's Performance** dashboard reads hourly aggregates. It shows the timestamp of the last completed hour at the top of the page and auto-refreshes every five minutes.
- The **Engagement** scoring system writes scores in a background job (it runs nightly at 03:00 UTC as part of member smart-tagging, and on demand from the Smart tagging page). The dashboard reads from that stored score, not the live activity tables, so brand-new activity is reflected only after the next run.

## Time zones

All "today" and "this week" calculations use your organization's time zone (set under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**). A booking that is "tomorrow" in your local time may already be "today" in the org's time zone or vice versa.

## Who can see what

Analytics access follows the role's permissions. The gym-wide reports need `analytics.read` at club scope; what each default role gets:

- **Admin** sees everything.
- **Manager** sees every gym-wide report (`analytics.manage.club`).
- **Front desk** has no analytics permission by default.
- **Instructor** sees only the **My performance** category (`analytics.read.own` for bookings and classes, `billing.read.own` for earnings).

**My performance** also requires the user to be linked to an instructor record, whatever their role. The **Inventory** category's **Stock Movements** report requires `inventory.read` instead of an analytics permission, and the category only appears when inventory is enabled.

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions) for the full breakdown.

## AI business report

Separate from these dashboards, 1Club can generate an **AI business report** (business audit) that scores your gym's online presence - Google profile, reviews, AI discoverability, competitors, and website performance - with recommendations. It appears as a card on **Home** and is documented under [Business audit](/home/ai-visibility-report). It is a point-in-time AI-generated report, not one of the live operational dashboards described above.

## Worked examples

### Monday-morning club check (racket sports)

You run a padel and tennis club and want a 30-second read on the weekend. Open **Clubs** > **Today's Performance** with **All clubs** selected in the breadcrumb to see live check-ins and revenue across both venues, then switch to **Clubs** > **Performance Trends** with the range set to **Last 7 days** to compare the weekend against the week. Drill into **Bookings** > **Utilization** to see which courts sat empty.

### Investigating a revenue dip (gym / fitness)

Revenue looks soft this month. Start on **Revenue** > **Revenue** with the range set to **Last 90 days** to confirm the trend, then open the **Revenue report** and **Payments report** to see whether it is fewer memberships, more failed payments, or lower point-of-sale takings. Cross-check **Members** > **Balance Report** for outstanding balances.

### Sizing a win-back campaign (Pilates / yoga studio)

You want to re-engage lapsed reformer clients. Open **Members** > **Member Engagement**, read the **Low** count in the score distribution, and use that as your audience size. See [Engagement analytics](/analytics/engagement) for how to act on it.

## Tips and best practices

- **Set the time range once** at the top of the page. Charts on the same report share the range, so you do not need to set it per chart.
- **Compare clubs side by side** by running **Clubs** > **Performance Trends** for **All clubs**, then switching the breadcrumb to one club at a time.
- **Use the sidebar search** to jump straight to a report by name instead of scanning categories.
- **Bookmark a filtered report.** Because the time range and club are stored in the URL, a bookmark reopens the exact view you set up.
- **For raw data, export instead.** Analytics pages are for reading; CSV exports under **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Export** are for analysis in Excel, BI tools, or your accountant's software.

## Troubleshooting

- **A category is missing from the sidebar** - the category is feature-gated or permission-gated. **Inventory** only appears when inventory is enabled for your organization, and you only see reports your role's permissions cover. Check with an admin or see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
- **A report is empty for the range you picked** - there is no data in that window. Widen the time range or switch the breadcrumb to **All clubs**. If a whole dashboard is blank, confirm the underlying feature (bookings, billing, marketing) is in use.
- **The Today dashboard looks a few minutes stale** - that is expected. It reads hourly aggregates and refreshes every five minutes; the header shows the last completed hour.
- **Engagement numbers do not match live activity** - engagement scores are written by a background job, not computed live. See [Engagement analytics](/analytics/engagement) for the refresh schedule.
- **Numbers differ from a CSV export** - the dashboards apply their own date and status filters (for example, excluding voided charges). Exports give you the raw rows so you can apply your own rules.
