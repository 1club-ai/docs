The **Analytics** section is a collection of pre-built reports about your gym. Each report is a fixed page with summary cards at the top and charts or tables below, and many open the people, bookings, or charges behind a number. Owners and managers use it to run the business; sales staff read the Sales reports; instructors see their own numbers under **My performance**.

There is no report builder or query interface - every report has predefined filters. If you need raw data to slice yourself, use [Settings > Data Tools > Export](/settings/export).

## When to use it

- **Check the day as it happens** - a CrossFit box keeps **Clubs** > **Today's Performance** open at reception; it refreshes every five minutes.
- **Find who to call** - a yoga studio opens **Members** > **Renewals** on Monday, ticks the members whose membership ends this week with nothing after it, and messages them in one go.
- **Close the month** - a padel gym reads **Finance** > **Tax** for last month to fill in its VAT return, then **Revenue** > **Revenue Report** for the bookkeeper.
- **See whether a change worked** - a climbing gym switches **Compare with** to **Previous period** after a price change and reads which cards turned red.

### Reports that appear only when you use the feature

Some reports are about a feature your organization may have switched off. While it is off, the report is not listed, and a bookmarked link to it does not open:

- **Member Engagement** - needs engagement scoring on under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging).
- **At-risk customers** - needs the **At Risk Tag** or the **Trend Tags** on under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging).
- **Progression Report** - needs at least one enabled [progression track](/settings/progression).
- **Inventory** - the whole category needs inventory turned on.
- **My performance** - needs your user to be linked to an instructor record, whatever your role.

An independent professional's organization shows the **Clubs** category as **Overview** and leaves out the reports about running a venue: **Instructor Pay Report**, **Earnings Statement**, and **My performance**.

## How the reports work

### The filter bar

Every report opens on the same bar:

- **Period** - one field showing the selected days, with arrows to step to the previous or next period. Open it to pick a preset (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Last quarter**, **This year**, **Last year**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 12 months**) or click a first and a last day on the calendar. Most reports open on **Last 30 days**. Reports with a column per month offer only whole-month presets, and reports that list every row (**Check-ins Report**, **Activity**) offer only short ones.
- **Group by** - on reports with trend charts: **Auto**, **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**. **Auto** picks the bucket from the length of the period.
- **Compare with** - see [Compare with another period](#compare-with-another-period).
- **Filters** - opens a drawer with the report's own filters, grouped into **Scope** (billing entity, revenue account), **People** (instructors, age band), **What** (class type, area type, plan kind, entrance method), and **State** (status, payment, owed for). Each filter that narrows the report shows as a chip under the bar; click a chip to change it, or clear them all. The button is missing on a report with nothing to narrow.

On a phone, **Group by** and **Compare with** move into the **Filters** drawer.

A few reports have no period because they show where things stand today: **At-risk customers**, **Renewals** (which looks ahead a number of days instead), **Amount Due**, and **Today's Performance**.

Your period, comparison, and filters are kept in the page address, and the period is remembered per report in this browser, so a bookmark or a shared link reopens the same view.

### Which gym a report covers

There is no club control on the bar. A report follows the club selected in the breadcrumb at the top of the admin; **All clubs** covers the whole organization. Reports built on organization-wide data ignore the breadcrumb: the **Members** dashboard, **Member Engagement**, **Customer Lifecycle**, **Leads & Conversion**, **Email Analytics Dashboard**, **Promotions**, **Web Traffic**, and the instructor's own **My performance** reports. Each report page says where it differs.

### Compare with another period

Reports with summary cards offer **Compare with**: **Previous period** (the default), **Same period last year**, or **None**. Each card then shows its change under the figure, in green when it is good news and red when it is not, so more cancellations, no-shows, or lapsed customers read red. Hover the change to see the period it was compared with.

- A rate moves in percentage points: 60% going to 66% reads **6 pts**, not 10%.
- A period still under way is compared with the same stretch of the one before, so this month to date meets the same days of last month rather than the whole month.
- While the first day of the period is still under way there is nothing whole to compare with, and the control says so.
- Figures that count things as they stand today, such as your current members or the invoices still unpaid, show no change: compared across two date ranges they would mislead.

### Cards, charts, and drill-downs

Hover the info icon on a card for its definition, and hover a chart point or bar for its exact figures. Many figures open what is behind them: a month cell on the **Revenue Report**, an hour on the **Occupancy** grid, a member's name on any list.

### Export

Reports with an **Export** button in the top-right download what is on screen, with your filters applied, as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV (.csv)**. **Amount Due** also exports a **PDF (.pdf)**, and **Tax** exports either its summary or every charge behind it. On a phone the button is in the menu in the top bar. The button is disabled when there are no rows to export.

## Data freshness and time zones

Reports read your live data, so a booking or payment shows up the next time the report loads. Two exceptions:

- **Today's Performance** re-reads every five minutes on its own, so it can trail the latest activity by up to five minutes.
- Engagement scores, smart tags (behind **At-risk customers**), and the lifecycle stages are written by the nightly smart-tagging run and on demand with **Sync now** on [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging), so the reports built on them reflect new activity after the next run.

"Today", "this week", and every day a report counts follow your organization's time zone (**Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**), not your computer's.

## Who can see what

Analytics access follows permissions. The gym-wide reports need the analytics read permission at club scope (`analytics.read.club`); some need more:

| Reports                                         | Also need                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sales** category, **First visits**            | Reading member profiles (`members.read.standard`), because they read organization-wide contact data |
| **Amount Due**                                  | Reading billing (`billing.read.club`)                                                               |
| **Stock Movements**                             | Only the inventory read permission (`inventory.read.club`), not an analytics one                    |
| **My performance** **Bookings** and **Classes** | `analytics.read.own`                                                                                |
| **My performance** earnings tabs                | `billing.read.own`, so you can hide pay from instructors without hiding their schedule figures      |

What each default role gets:

- **Admin** sees everything.
- **Manager** sees every gym-wide report.
- **Sales** sees the gym-wide reports, including the **Sales** category, but not **Amount Due**, because the role has no billing access.
- **Front desk** has no analytics permission by default. It can still open **Inventory** > **Stock Movements** when inventory is on.
- **Instructor** sees only **My performance**.

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions) for the full breakdown.

## Outside Analytics

- [Home](/home/overview) rolls six of these figures into small charts that link here, and can [email you the day](/home/overview#daily-email) with an AI summary.
- The [AI visibility report](/home/ai-visibility-report) scores your online presence. It is a point-in-time report on **Home**, not one of these live reports.

## Worked examples

### Monday-morning check (racket sports)

A padel and tennis club wants a quick read on the weekend. It opens **Clubs** > **Performance Trends** for **Last 7 days** with **Compare with** on **Previous period**, then **Bookings** > **Occupancy** to see which hours of the weekend still had empty areas.

### Investigating a revenue dip (gym / fitness)

Revenue looks soft this month. Start on **Revenue** > **Revenue** for **Last 3 months** to confirm the trend, then open the **Revenue Report** to see whether it is fewer memberships or lower shop takings. **Finance** > **Amount Due** shows who still owes money and for how long.

### Sizing a win-back (Pilates / yoga studio)

A studio wants to re-engage clients who stopped coming. **Members** > **At-risk customers** lists the paying members who stopped coming or come much less, with what each is worth a month. The owner ticks them and clicks **Message**.

## Troubleshooting

- **A category or report is missing** - it is permission-gated or feature-gated. See [Reports that appear only when you use the feature](#reports-that-appear-only-when-you-use-the-feature) and [Who can see what](#who-can-see-what).
- **A report is empty for the period you picked** - widen the period, or switch the breadcrumb to **All clubs**. If a whole report is blank, confirm the feature behind it (bookings, billing, marketing) is in use.
- **A card shows no change while Compare with is on** - it counts things as they stand today, or the compared period has no figure (for example, before the data began to be recorded).
- **Today's Performance looks a few minutes stale** - it refreshes every five minutes; reload the page to see the latest numbers at once.
- **Numbers differ from a CSV export from Settings** - the reports apply their own rules (for example, excluding voided charges). Exports give you the raw rows. See [Analytics definitions](/analytics/definitions) for what each figure counts.
