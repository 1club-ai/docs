The **Sales** category in **Analytics** follows people from first enquiry to loyal member. It has three tabs: the **Sales Dashboard** shows what you sold and how your leads turn into customers, **Customer Lifecycle** shows how customers move between plans and types and who left, and **Leads & Conversion** shows which sources, campaigns and salespeople bring leads that buy. Owners, managers and sales staff use it to judge acquisition and retention.

## When to use it

Use it when you want to know whether the gym is winning new members and keeping them, not just how many people walked in. Typical questions it answers:

- **Which ads bring people who buy?** - a padel gym running [Lead Ads](/settings/integrations/meta#lead-ads) opens **Leads & Conversion**, groups by **Campaign**, and sees that its "Spring trial week" campaign converts twice as many leads as its brand campaign, at a higher first membership revenue.
- **Why do leads say no?** - a boxing gym reads **Top lost reason** by **Salesperson** and finds most of one coach's lost leads say **Price**, so it gives that coach a starter offer to work with.
- **Are members moving up or down?** - a reformer studio compares **Plan upgrades** with **Plan downgrades** on **Customer Lifecycle** after a price change, apart from the new memberships sold.
- **Why are members leaving?** - a CrossFit box reads **Who left** by **Reason** for the last quarter, then by **Plan**, and sees most leavers cancel the off-peak plan over its timetable.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to read each of these.

## How it works

The reports read the history of each contact rather than today's snapshot, so a number reflects what happened in the period you pick.

- A **customer** is a contact who is a **Member** or a **Drop-in**.
- A **lead** enters the pipeline on the [Leads](/members/leads) board. A lead **converts** once, since entering the pipeline, they are given a plan or pay for something. That makes them a customer.
- A conversion is credited to the lead's owner at that moment: the staff member they were assigned to when they converted, whoever rang the sale up and whether they bought at the desk or online. Reassigning the lead later does not move the credit.
- Every change of contact type (for example Drop-in to Member, or Member to Lapsed) is recorded, and the lifecycle movements are counted from those changes.

### Period, comparison and club

Each tab has a date range (default: the last 30 days) and **Compare with**: **None**, **Previous period** or **Same period last year**. With a comparison on, each card shows the change against that period.

Contacts belong to your organization, not to one gym location, so most figures are organization-wide. The gym selected in the breadcrumb narrows only what belongs to a gym:

- On the **Sales Dashboard**, the six membership and revenue cards.
- On **Customer Lifecycle**, **Who left**.

**Leads & Conversion** is always organization-wide. A lead's gym is the gym of the sale that converted them, so you see it by grouping by **Club** instead.

### Figure definitions

Each card has an info icon with its definition. The figures this page covers are defined as follows:

| Figure                       | Definition                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Memberships sold**         | Memberships (plans that bill again) added in the period, less those that only replaced the member's previous plan. Passes and drop-ins are not counted. |
| **Active memberships**       | Memberships running at the end of the period, paused ones included.                                                                                     |
| **Paused memberships**       | Memberships paused at the end of the period.                                                                                                            |
| **Cancellations**            | Membership cancellations recorded in the period, ending at once or at the end of the paid period. One withdrawn since is not counted.                   |
| **Membership revenue**       | Revenue from memberships in the period, renewals and joining fees included, ex-tax and net of refunds.                                                  |
| **Other revenue**            | All other revenue in the period: bookings, passes, drop-ins, products and the rest, ex-tax and net of refunds.                                          |
| **Plan upgrades**            | Members who moved to a dearer membership plan, by monthly price, within 14 days of leaving their previous one.                                          |
| **Plan downgrades**          | Members who moved to a cheaper membership plan, by monthly price, within 14 days of leaving their previous one.                                         |
| **Converted**                | Of the period's new leads, those who have since been given a plan or paid for something.                                                                |
| **Conversion rate**          | Converted leads as a share of new leads.                                                                                                                |
| **Median days to convert**   | For the converted leads, the middle value of the days from entering the pipeline to converting.                                                         |
| **First membership revenue** | What the membership that converted each lead earned on its first charge and joining fee. A lead converted by a pass or a visit brings none.             |

## Sales dashboard

Open **Analytics** > **Sales** > **Sales Dashboard**.

### Memberships and revenue

The first row shows what you sold: **Memberships sold**, **Active memberships**, **Paused memberships**, **Cancellations**, **Membership revenue** and **Other revenue**, as defined [above](#figure-definitions). A member who switches plan is a plan change, not a sale, so it counts under [Plan upgrades and downgrades](#plan-upgrades-and-downgrades) instead. These six cards follow the gym in the breadcrumb.

**Paused memberships** shows "-" for a period that ends before pause history was recorded.

### Customers and leads

- **New customers** - Contacts who became a Member or Drop-in for the first time in the period. Someone who comes back after lapsing, or a Drop-in who takes a membership, is not new. This is the same figure as on the [Members dashboard](/analytics/members#members-dashboard).
- **New leads** - Contacts who entered the pipeline in the period.
- **Upgrades** - Drop-ins who took a membership in the period.

### Lead journey

The **Lead journey** follows one group of people: the leads who entered the pipeline in the period. Each step shows a count and a percentage of the step above.

| Step                     | What it counts                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entered the pipeline** | Leads who entered the pipeline in the period.                                                                               |
| **Became customers**     | Those who have since converted.                                                                                             |
| **Took a membership**    | Those who have since become a Member, whether they bought a membership straight away or were a Drop-in first.               |
| **Reached Active**       | Those still a customer on the day the **Active** lifecycle tag would apply to them, a set number of days after they bought. |

**Reached Active** uses the threshold of the **Active** tag under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging) (46 days unless you changed it). Members who bought more recently than that are too new to judge: a note under the step says how many, and they are left out of its percentage instead of counting as members who fell away.

Below the steps, a line reads **Customers who lapsed in this period and have come back since**, shown as `X of Y` with a percentage. It shows even when no leads entered the pipeline, so a gym that does not use the pipeline still sees its win-backs.

### Lead sources

The **Lead Sources** chart shows, for each source, how many of the period's new leads came from it (**New leads**) and how many of those converted (**Converted**). Leads with no source set get their own **No source** bar.

### Why leads were lost

**Why leads were lost** counts the period's new leads who are in the **Lost** stage now, by the reason given when they were moved there (see [Lost and lost reasons](/members/leads#lost-and-lost-reasons)). A line above the chart says how many of these leads are lost, how many are still being worked and how many converted. A lead moved to Lost without a reason, for example before reasons existed, counts under **Reason not recorded**.

## Customer lifecycle report

Open **Analytics** > **Sales** > **Customer Lifecycle**.

### Movement cards

The cards count the period's movements between contact types and plans:

| Card                | What it counts                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New leads**       | Contacts who entered the pipeline in the period.                                               |
| **New customers**   | Contacts who became a Member or Drop-in for the first time.                                    |
| **Upgrades**        | Moves from Drop-in to Member.                                                                  |
| **Downgrades**      | Moves from Member to Drop-in.                                                                  |
| **Plan upgrades**   | Members who moved to a dearer membership plan.                                                 |
| **Plan downgrades** | Members who moved to a cheaper membership plan.                                                |
| **Lapsed**          | Moves from Member or Drop-in to Lapsed. A customer who becomes Staff is not counted as lapsed. |
| **Won back**        | Moves back to Member or Drop-in by someone who was a customer before.                          |

**New leads** and **New customers** match the same cards on the Sales Dashboard.

### Plan upgrades and downgrades

A plan change is a member who leaves one membership plan and takes a different one within 14 days. It is an upgrade when the new plan costs more a month, and a downgrade when it costs less. Plan changes are kept apart from new sales: they are not counted in **Memberships sold**.

Plan changes are worked out from the memberships themselves, so two cases are not seen:

- A plan changed in place on the same membership, rather than by ending one membership and starting another.
- A plan billed on a custom period, which has no monthly price to compare.

A new membership taken alongside one that keeps renewing is a second plan, not a change.

### Who left

**Who left** lists the members who churned in the period: a membership plan ended and no new membership followed within 14 days. A member who renews a few days late, or moves to another plan the week after, has not left. Pass and drop-in holders lapse rather than churn, so they count under **Lapsed** instead.

Group the list with the toggle:

- **Reason** - the cancellation reason recorded when their membership was cancelled. A membership that ended without one shows under **No reason recorded**.
- **Plan** - the plan they left, or **No plan**.
- **Source** - the [source](/members/leads#sources) they came from, or **No source**.
- **Salesperson** - the staff member credited with them, or **Unassigned**.

<Note>
  Reasons are only recorded for cancellations from 20 October 2026. A period
  before that date shows how many members left but no breakdown, and a period
  that spans it breaks down only the ones who left since.
</Note>

### Where contacts stand now

These two charts are current counts, so the date range does not change them:

- **Contacts by type** - How many contacts are **Contact**, **Leads**, **Drop-ins**, **Members**, and **Lapsed** right now.
- **Customers by lifecycle stage** - Your customers by their lifecycle smart tag (Trial, Probation, and Active by default, then Frozen), named and colored as you set them. Customers with none of these tags are counted under **No lifecycle tag**; they are mostly Drop-ins who visit without a plan, or who are still in the pipeline. This chart only appears when the **Lifecycle** smart-tag category is turned on.

### Retention by start month

A table groups customers by the month they first became one. The **Customers** column is the size of each group, and the **Month 1**, **Month 2**, and later columns show the share of that group still a customer that many months after they started. Darker cells mean higher retention.

The table always covers the 12 months up to the end of the selected range. A cell fills in only once everyone in that group has been a customer for that long, so the most recent rows have fewer filled columns.

## Leads and conversion report

Open **Analytics** > **Sales** > **Leads & Conversion**. It follows one group of people: the leads who entered the pipeline in the period, wherever they are now.

### Cards

- **New leads** - Contacts who entered the pipeline in the period.
- **Converted** - Of those, the ones who have since been given a plan or paid for something.
- **Conversion rate** - **Converted** as a share of **New leads**. It shows "-" when no leads entered the pipeline in the period, rather than 0%.
- **Median days to convert** - For the converted leads, the middle value of the days from entering the pipeline to converting.
- **First membership revenue** - What the membership that converted each lead earned on its first charge and joining fee. A lead converted by a pass or a visit brings none.

### Leads by

The table groups the same leads one way at a time. Pick the grouping with **Leads by**:

| Grouping        | Each row is                                                                                                                   | Leads it cannot place                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Source**      | The lead's [source](/members/leads#sources).                                                                                  | **No source**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Club**        | The gym of the sale that converted them.                                                                                      | **No club yet** for a lead who has not converted. **All clubs** for a lead converted by a plan for your whole organization. **Club not known** for a lead converted by a paid visit or a plan shared on their account, which has no gym of its own. |
| **Salesperson** | The owner the conversion was credited to, or, for a lead not converted yet, whoever owns them now.                            | **Unassigned**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Campaign**    | The campaign of the lead's first tagged arrival: an ad, a campaign link or a [lead ad](/settings/integrations/meta#lead-ads). | **No campaign**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Stage**       | Each open stage of your pipeline, in board order.                                                                             | -                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

For each row the table shows **New leads**, **Converted**, **Conversion rate**, **Median days to convert**, **First membership revenue**, **Lost leads** (how many are in the Lost stage now) and **Top lost reason**.

**Top lost reason** is the reason given most often for that row's lost leads. When two or more reasons tie, all of them are listed, since picking one would misstate it. A row with no lost leads shows "-".

#### Grouping by stage

Leads skip stages and go back, so the stage view reads each stage on its own rather than as a funnel:

- **Reached** - how many of the period's leads were in that stage at some point after entering the pipeline.
- **Converted** - how many converted from that stage, the one they were in when they bought.
- **Lost leads** - how many were moved from that stage to Lost.

A stage's figures are its own, not a share of the stage before. The stage view has no median, revenue or top reason columns.

## Worked examples

**Padel gym comparing ad campaigns.** A padel gym runs two Instagram lead ad campaigns. On **Leads & Conversion** for the last 90 days, grouped by **Campaign**, "Spring trial week" has 60 new leads and a 35% **Conversion rate**, while "Brand awareness" has 90 new leads and 10%. **First membership revenue** is higher on the first too, so they move budget to it.

**Boxing gym learning why leads say no.** The head coach groups **Leads & Conversion** by **Salesperson**. One coach's **Top lost reason** is **Price**, another's is **No response**. The first gets a starter offer to work with, and the gym turns on the "Win back lost leads" [automation](/marketing/automations#suggested-blueprints) for leads lost over price or timing.

**Reformer studio after a price change.** The studio raises its unlimited plan. On **Customer Lifecycle** with **Compare with** set to **Previous period**, **Plan downgrades** rises while **Plan upgrades** holds, and **Memberships sold** on the dashboard is flat, so the change moved existing members down rather than stopping new sales.

**CrossFit box reading why members leave.** The owner opens **Customer Lifecycle** for the last quarter and reads **Who left** by **Reason**, then by **Plan**. Most leavers left the off-peak plan and gave the timetable as their reason, so the box adds an evening slot to that plan.

## Tips and best practices

- **Set a source on every lead** (forms and lead ads can do it for you) so **Lead Sources** and the **Source** grouping stay meaningful and **No source** stays small. See [Leads](/members/leads).
- **Assign every lead to an owner** before they convert. Credit goes to the owner at conversion, so an unassigned lead converts under **Unassigned**.
- **Judge conversion on ranges old enough** for leads to have converted. A range that ended yesterday always looks weak.
- **Read Reached Active with its note.** For a recent range, many members are too new to judge, so the percentage covers only the ones old enough.
- **Keep the Lifecycle smart tags on** if you want the stage chart. Its thresholds also set when **Reached Active** is judged.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                                                 | Cause                                                                                                       | Fix                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The **Sales** category is missing                                       | Your role lacks `analytics.read.club` or `members.read.organization`                                        | Ask an admin to grant both. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions). |
| **New leads** is 0 although the board has leads                         | The range counts leads that entered the pipeline in that range, not leads sitting in it                     | Widen the range, or open the [Leads](/members/leads) board for where leads stand now.  |
| **Conversion rate** shows "-"                                           | No leads entered the pipeline in the period                                                                 | Widen the range.                                                                       |
| Many leads fall under **No club yet**                                   | Those leads have not converted yet, and a lead's gym is the gym of the sale that converted them             | This is expected. The row shrinks as leads convert.                                    |
| Choosing a gym in the breadcrumb does not change **Leads & Conversion** | That report is organization-wide                                                                            | Group by **Club** instead.                                                             |
| **Who left** shows no reasons                                           | The period is before 20 October 2026, when reasons began to be recorded, or the cancellations had no reason | Pick a later period. Recording a reason on every cancellation fills it in.             |
| A plan change does not show under **Plan upgrades**                     | The plan was changed in place on the same membership, or it bills on a custom period                        | This is expected. Only a move from one membership to another is seen.                  |
| **Paused memberships** shows "-"                                        | The period ends before pause history was recorded                                                           | Pick a later period.                                                                   |
| Many leads fall under **No source**                                     | Those leads have no source set                                                                              | Set a source on each lead, or have your forms set one.                                 |
| Recent retention rows have empty cells                                  | Not everyone in that month's group has been a customer long enough yet                                      | This is expected. The cells fill in as the group ages.                                 |
