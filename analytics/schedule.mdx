These reports show who came in, what was booked and paid for, where bookings leak, how classes perform, and what each instructor earned. Owners and managers use them to plan the timetable, chase payments, tighten booking policies, and pay instructors. Instructors use **My performance** to see their own numbers.

## When to use it

- **Which bookings are still unpaid?** - a jiu-jitsu gym opens the booking **Audit**, sets the table's **Payment** filter to **Unpaid**, and reads the total to collect.
- **Who cancels late or does not show?** - a reformer studio opens **Cancellations and no-shows**, switches **Rows by** to **Member**, and messages the members at the top about the cancellation policy.
- **Which classes get booked but not attended?** - a CrossFit box finds a midday class with many bookings but a low attendance rate on the **Classes Dashboard**.
- **What do you owe each instructor this month?** - a climbing gym reads each coach's month in the **Instructor Pay Report**, then exports it for payroll.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for what each gym did next.

| **Bookings**       | [**Dashboard**](#bookings-dashboard) (volume, money, peak times), [**Audit**](#booking-audit) (payment reconciliation per booking), [**By Source**](#bookings-by-source) (channel, API key, and UTM source), [**Occupancy**](/analytics/occupancy) (booked hours over open hours, by hour of the week), [**Monthly Report**](#bookings-monthly-report) (month by month by type), [**Cancellations and no-shows**](#cancellations-and-no-shows) (where bookings leak) |

## Filters

Every report has the shared filter bar (see [Analytics overview](/analytics/overview#the-filter-bar)). What differs per report:

- **Period** - most reports open on **Last 30 days**. The **Check-ins Report** opens on **Today** and accepts up to 31 days. The **Monthly Report** offers whole months and opens on **This year**. The **Instructors Dashboard** opens on **Last 6 months**. The **Instructor Pay Report** offers whole months and opens on **Last month**, as do the **Earnings Statement** and **Earnings by entrance method**.
- **Under Filters** - the **Bookings** dashboard, **Audit**, and **Cancellations and no-shows** offer **Instructors** and **Area type**; **Attendance**, **Classes Dashboard**, **Check-ins Report**, and **Cancellations and no-shows** offer **Program / class type**; **Attendance** and the **Classes Dashboard** offer **Instructors**; the **Check-ins Report** offers **Entry method**.
- **Club** - reports follow the club selected in the breadcrumb; **All clubs** shows the whole organization. **Earnings by entrance method** and the **My performance** reports are not narrowed by club.

**Compare with** is offered on **Attendance**, the **Bookings** dashboard, **Cancellations and no-shows**, **Occupancy**, the **Classes Dashboard**, the **Instructors Dashboard**, and the **My performance** **Bookings**, **Classes**, and **Earnings Overview** tabs.

## Check-ins

The **Check-ins** category covers who actually came in. Check-ins count on the day of the booking or class they are for, otherwise on the day they were made.

### Attendance

**Analytics** > **Check-ins** > **Attendance** has four cards:

- **Total Check-ins** - every check-in in the period.
- **People checked in** - each person once; a walk-in with no contact record is not counted.
- **Check-ins per person** - total check-ins over people checked in.
- **No-Show Rate** - bookings marked no-show over bookings that were checked in or marked no-show.

Below them are **Attendance Trends**, **Peak Times** by hour, and **Attendance by Area Type**.

<Note>
  Only a [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) that requires check-in
  marks a missed booking as a no-show. Without that requirement, missed bookings
  are never marked, so the rate reads 0%.
</Note>

### Check-ins report

**Analytics** > **Check-ins** > **Check-ins Report** lists every individual check-in, grouped by how the person got in: a membership, a pass, a drop-in, each [external program](/settings/external-programs) on its own, or **Unspecified**. Each group has a **Subtotal** and the table ends with **Total Check-ins**. Columns are **Member**, **Entry method**, **Time**, and **Reference**.

Above the table, **By entry method** shows the split, and **Check-ins by hour** (for one day) or **Check-ins by day** (for longer) shows when people came. It opens on **Today** and accepts periods of up to 31 days, so the front desk can reconcile a shift or a day. Narrow it with **Entry method** and **Program / class type** under **Filters**.

## Bookings dashboard

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **Dashboard** is the top-level view of demand and money.

Cards:

- **Total Bookings**, **Confirmed Bookings**, **Cancelled Bookings**, and **Pending Bookings** - volume by status. **Confirmed Bookings** counts every booking that stands (still confirmed, checked in, or a no-show) and notes how many were checked in, so total = confirmed + cancelled + pending. **Cancelled Bookings** also shows the cancellation rate as a share of all bookings.
- **Total Billed** - what the period's bookings billed, including tax and net of refunds, with any cancellation fees the gym kept. The amount excluding tax is shown underneath. Bookings covered by a membership are left out. Paid plus outstanding equals billed, the same total as the **Audit** tab.
- **Total Paid** - how much of that has been collected, including tax.
- **Outstanding** - what is still owed, including tax.
- **Average Booking Value** - billed per paying booking, including tax.

Charts:

- **Booking Trends** - bookings (bars) and total billed (line) over time.
- **Bookings by Type** - bookings per area type, class type, or instructor type.
- **Bookings by Source** - bookings per booking source.
- **Peak Booking Hours** and **Peak Booking Days** - when bookings cluster, by hour of day and day of week.

## Bookings by source

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **By Source** splits booking volume two ways:

- **Bookings by Source** - the booking channel (for example admin, member portal, kiosk). Bookings made through the API are split per API key.
- **Bookings by marketing source (UTM)** - the `utm_source` attached to the booking, so you can attribute bookings to specific marketing links. Sources that are AI assistants are drawn in a second color, and a **From AI assistants** line above the chart totals them.

## Occupancy

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **Occupancy** (formerly **Utilization**) compares booked hours with available hours for your areas and coaches, and booked places with class capacity, with a grid of the week by hour for every area. It has its own page: [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy).

## Booking audit

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **Audit** is a row-level payment reconciliation view. It lists every booking in the period, unpaid ones highlighted, so you can see what has been paid, what is outstanding, and how each booking was paid for. It opens on **Last 30 days**.

Cards: **Outstanding**, **Unpaid bookings**, **Total billed**, and **Total bookings**. They cover the whole period: the table's own filters narrow the table, not these totals.

Columns: **Date**, **Member**, **Type**, **Resource**, **Club**, **Source**, **Status**, **Entrance**, **Amount**, **Outstanding**, **Payment**, and **Method**.

Above the table, three filters narrow the rows, and the export follows them:

- **Type** - grouped under **Area types**, **Class types**, **Instructor types**, **Events**, and **Other**.
- **Status** - Confirmed, Pending, Checked in, No-show, or Cancelled.
- **Payment** - **Unpaid**, **Partially paid**, **Paid**, **Refunded**, **Covered** (settled without a direct charge, for example by a membership), or **No payment required**.

**Entrance** shows how the booking was paid for: **Direct payment**, **Membership** (from a membership allowance), or **External program** (redeemed against an external program, with its own per-visit fee).

Click a row to open the booking.

## Bookings monthly report

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **Monthly Report** is a month-by-month table: one row per month, one column per booking type, plus **Total Bookings** and **Total Billed (Including tax)** and a **Totals** row. Each month's billed figure matches the bookings **Dashboard** for that month. Months with no bookings still get a row of zeros. A **Bookings by Type** stacked chart above the table shows the same breakdown. It offers whole-month periods and opens on **This year**.

## Cancellations and no-shows

**Analytics** > **Bookings** > **Cancellations and no-shows** shows where a period's bookings leak, and who leaks them. It counts the same bookings as the **Dashboard** (by when they start, every status).

### Cards

Each card compares with the previous period, and more of any of them reads red:

| Card                          | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Bookings**            | Every booking that starts in the period, cancelled and pending ones included.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Cancelled Bookings**        | Cancelled bookings, with their share of all bookings.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Late cancellations**        | Cancellations made with less notice than the booking policy needs for a full refund, with their share of all bookings. The gym's own cancellations (a closure, an instructor who cannot teach) and moves to another time are not late. |
| **No-shows**                  | Bookings marked no-show, with their share of the bookings that were attended or missed.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Cancellation charges kept** | What the gym collected and kept on cancelled and missed bookings, including tax, net of refunds, with what is **still owed** on them.                                                                                                  |
| **Refunds given**             | What was given back on cancelled and missed bookings: what went back to the Wallet and refunded payments, including tax.                                                                                                               |

A line under the cards says what **late** means for your gym, for example "Late means less than 24 h before the start (Standard policy)." A booking is judged by its Event's cancellation policy if it has one, otherwise by the terms it accepted when booked, otherwise by the [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) in force now.

No-shows are counted only for bookings under a booking policy that requires check-in. Where no booking is, the card says **No-shows are only tracked under a policy that requires check-in** instead of showing 0%.

### Rows by

The table breaks the same figures down one way at a time. Pick it with **Rows by**:

- **Class type** - each class type.
- **Area** - each area.
- **Time slot** - hours down and weekdays across, each cell shaded by its cancellation rate, so you can see which evening or morning loses most. Hover a cell for its bookings, cancellations, late cancellations, and no-shows.
- **Member** - the members who cancel late or do not show most, listed first.

Each row shows **Bookings**, **Cancelled Bookings**, **Late cancellations**, **No-shows**, **Cancellation charges kept**, and **Refunds given**. Narrow the report with **Area type**, **Program / class type**, and **Instructors** under **Filters**, and **Export** the table as Excel or CSV.

## Classes dashboard

**Analytics** > **Classes** > **Classes Dashboard** covers group classes.

Cards: **Total Classes**, **Total Bookings**, **Total Attendance**, **Average Attendance** (attendees per class), and **Class Occupancy** (booked places over class capacity; classes with no capacity limit are skipped).

Views:

- **Classes and Attendance Trends** - classes (bars) plus bookings and attendance (lines) over time.
- **Attendance by Instructor** - attendance per instructor.
- **Most Popular Classes** - the top classes by bookings, with their attendance and **Attendance Rate**.

A big gap between bookings and attendance points to no-shows; read [Cancellations and no-shows](#cancellations-and-no-shows) and tighten your [booking policies](/settings/booking-policies).

## Instructor analytics

The **Instructors** category is the gym-wide view of every instructor.

### Instructors dashboard

**Analytics** > **Instructors** > **Instructors Dashboard** is a table of **Sessions** and **Revenue** per instructor per month, with **Total Instructors**, **Total Sessions**, and **Total Revenue** cards and a **Totals** row. Each row also shows the instructor's club. A direct booking counts as one session; a co-taught class counts once for each instructor, and its revenue is split between them so organization totals are not double-counted. Sessions covered by a membership or a pass add no revenue here: that money counts when the membership or pass is charged.

### Instructor pay report

**Analytics** > **Instructors** > **Instructor Pay Report** is a month-by-month table of what each instructor earned, with an **Instructor Compensation** total and a **Totals** row. Switch the amount shown per month between **Pay** and **Revenue**. Expand an instructor to see what made up each month, or click a month cell to open the details behind it. Pay comes from [pay rates](/staff/pay-rates).

### Earnings statement

**Analytics** > **Instructors** > **Earnings Statement** is an itemized list of every payment behind one instructor's pay. Pick the instructor under **Filters** and a period; click a line to open the class or booking behind it.

### Earnings by entrance method

**Analytics** > **Instructors** > **Earnings by entrance method** shows, for the instructors you pick, the revenue and coach share split by how members got in. Each pass, program, and direct payment gets its own row, with a line-item count and a total. Narrow it to entrance methods under **Filters**.

### My performance

Any user linked to an instructor record - an instructor, or an owner or manager who also teaches - gets a **My performance** category with their own numbers only. An admin who also teaches sees both it and the gym-wide **Instructors** category. The reports are not narrowed by club.

| Tab                    | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bookings**           | Your bookings outside classes (class bookings are under **Classes**): **Total Bookings**, **Cancellation Rate**, **Confirmed Bookings**, **Total Revenue**, **Average Booking Value**, **Cancelled Bookings**, **Pending Bookings**, and **My Compensation**, plus a **Booking Trends** chart.               |
| **Classes**            | **Total Classes**, **Total Bookings**, **Total Attendance**, **Average Attendance**, **Class Occupancy**, and **My Compensation**, a **Classes and Attendance Trends** chart, and **Most Popular Classes** with their attendance rate. |
| **Earnings Overview**  | **Total Earnings**, **Total Classes**, **Total Attendance**, and **Personal Trainings**, with **Earnings by Day** and **Earnings from Your Work** charts.                                                                              |
| **Earnings Statement** | Every payment behind your pay for the chosen period, line by line. Opens on **Last month**.                                                                                                                                            |
| **By entrance method** | Your pay split by how members got in, in a **My share** column, one row per pass, program, and direct payment. Opens on **Last month**.                                                                                                |

The **Bookings** and **Classes** tabs need `analytics.read.own`; the three earnings tabs need `billing.read.own`, so you can hide pay from instructors without hiding their schedule figures.

## Exporting reports

Reports with an **Export** button in the top-right download what is on screen, with your filters, as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV (.csv)**. In these categories that is the **Check-ins Report**, **Audit**, **Monthly Report**, **Cancellations and no-shows**, **Occupancy**, **Classes Dashboard**, **Instructors Dashboard**, **Instructor Pay Report**, **Earnings Statement**, and **Earnings by entrance method**. Files are named with the report and the period, and the club when the report is narrowed to one. For scheduled or bundled exports, see [Settings > Data Tools > Export](/settings/export).

## Worked examples

**Martial arts - chasing unpaid drop-ins.** A jiu-jitsu gym opens the bookings **Audit** for **Last 30 days**, sets the table's **Payment** filter to **Unpaid** and **Type** to its class types. The **Outstanding** card shows the total owed for the period; each unpaid row opens the booking so the front desk can take payment.

**Pilates / yoga - late cancellations.** A reformer studio opens **Cancellations and no-shows** for the last quarter. **Late cancellations** reads 9% of bookings, up 3 points on the previous quarter. **Rows by** **Time slot** shows Monday 07:00 loses most; **Rows by** **Member** lists five members with three or more late cancellations each. The studio messages them about its 12-hour policy and adds a late cancellation fee.

**Gym / fitness - trimming a class that does not fill.** A CrossFit box checks the **Classes Dashboard**: a midday class shows many bookings but a low **Attendance Rate**, while **Class Occupancy** on the 07:00 class is near 100%. It cuts the midday slot and adds an early class.

**Climbing - paying instructors correctly.** A climbing gym closes the month with the **Instructor Pay Report** on **Pay**, expands each coach to check the sessions behind the figure, and exports it for payroll. For a disputed coach, the **Earnings Statement** shows every line.

**Racket sports - reading demand by source.** A padel gym uses **By Source** and sees a spike in **Bookings by marketing source (UTM)** tied to an intro-offer link, then confirms on the bookings **Dashboard** that the bookings were paid.

## Tips and best practices

- Set the **Period** first. A "zero" often just means the period or the selected club excludes the data.
- Pair [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy) with the **Dashboard**'s peak hours and days before changing the schedule.
- Reconcile with the booking **Audit** weekly, using the **Payment** filter set to **Unpaid**, so outstanding amounts do not pile up.
- Require check-in in your booking policy if you want no-shows counted: without it, **No-Show Rate** reads 0% and **Cancellations and no-shows** says no-shows are not tracked.

## Troubleshooting

- **A report is blank or shows zeros.** The period excludes the data. Check the period, then the club in the breadcrumb.
- **The Export button is disabled.** There are no rows for the current filters. Widen the period or clear a filter.
- **The Audit cards do not change when I filter the table.** The cards cover the whole period; the **Type**, **Status**, and **Payment** filters narrow the table and the export only.
- **No-Show Rate is 0%.** No booking policy requires check-in, so missed bookings are never marked no-show. See [Booking policies](/settings/booking-policies).
- **A cancellation is not counted as late.** It came with enough notice for a full refund, the gym cancelled it, or the booking was moved to another time.
- **The Check-ins Report asks for a shorter range.** It accepts up to 31 days.
- **An instructor shows no compensation.** Confirm they had sessions in the period and that [pay rates](/staff/pay-rates) apply to them; the **Earnings Statement** shows whether any lines exist.
