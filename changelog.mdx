<Update label="2026-08-02" description="Cancellation policies for events" tags={["New","Highlight","Operations"]}>
  ## Cancellation policies for events

  Set refund terms for your events, and let members cancel their own tickets within them.

  Booking policies can now be scoped to events. Pick "Events" when you create a
  policy, add your refund steps, and every event follows those terms: full refund
  up to a week out, half up to two days, nothing after that. Leave the event list
  empty for a policy that covers all your events, or name specific events to give
  a sold-out gala its own non-refundable terms.

  Until you set terms for an event, its tickets stay in your hands: members are
  told to contact you rather than being offered a refund the club never agreed
  to. Once a policy is in place, members see exactly what cancelling returns and
  can release their tickets themselves. Either way, cancelling a multi-ticket
  purchase releases every ticket in it, so the seats go back on sale together.

  Event policies carry cancellation terms only. Booking and check-in settings
  stay with your other policies, since event tickets do not use them.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-30" description="Area blocks" tags={["New","Operations"]}>
  ## Area blocks

  Close a court, or a whole club, for a set period — and have every calendar and booking channel respect it.

  Courts can now be closed for a specific window instead of being switched to
  maintenance indefinitely or blocked out with a placeholder booking.

  Pick a court and a period, add a reason ("Resurfacing", "Camp", "Public
  holiday"), and the block appears on the schedule as a greyed-out block on
  that court's lane. The times default to that day's opening and closing hours,
  so closing for a day takes two clicks. Closing the whole club is a single
  entry rather than one per court, and courts added later are covered by it
  automatically.

  A block stops new bookings everywhere the slot is offered — the admin
  schedule, the member portal and app, the marketplace, and the API — and it
  keeps working with your existing opening hours rather than replacing them.
  Bookings already on the books are not cancelled, so you stay in control of
  who to move. Staff with the right permission can still record a booking
  inside a closed period when they need to, for example when entering a session
  that already happened.

  Partners and integrations can read blocks over the API to stop selling a
  closed court, and write them when migrating a schedule across from another
  system.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-30" description="Scan barcodes at the point of sale" tags={["New","Billing"]}>
  ## Scan barcodes at the point of sale

  Plug a barcode scanner into the till to add items to an order, or scan a member card to see at once whether the member is valid.

  Any keyboard-style USB or Bluetooth scanner gun works at the point of sale.
  There is nothing to install, nothing to pair inside 1club, and no scan mode to
  switch on. The till listens for a scanner the whole time it is open, wherever
  the cashier's hands are.

  Scan a product and it drops into the order, ready to charge. Membership plans,
  passes, and packages can carry a barcode as well, so a stickered day pass rings
  up exactly the way a drink does. A code means one thing inside your
  organization: if you try to save a barcode that is already on another item or on
  a member card, the save is refused and you are told what already holds it.

  Scan a member's card and the till answers the question the desk actually asks:
  may this person be let in right now. You get one of three verdicts, each with its
  reason. Valid means they hold a membership the desk can admit them on. Needs
  attention means they are a current member but nothing they hold is
  straightforwardly usable, for example the sessions are spent or money is owed.
  Not valid means the card resolves to nobody usable. From the same panel you can
  add that member to the order in one click, or open their profile.

  Scanning a card is a lookup, not a check-in. Nothing is written against the
  member, no session is spent, and no attendance is recorded, so admitting someone
  stays a separate and deliberate step.

  [Learn more](/pos/point-of-sale)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-28" description="Multiple websites per organization" tags={["New","Highlight","Content"]}>
  ## Multiple websites per organization

  Run more than one website from a single account, each on its own domain or subdomain.

  An organization is no longer limited to one website. Build a second site for a
  shop, a brand, a location or a language, and point it at its own domain
  (`shop.example.com`) or an entirely separate one — each site gets its own pages,
  design, SEO settings and publish history.

  Everything operational stays shared: the same members, plans, classes,
  instructors and media library sit behind every site, so a visitor who books on
  one is the same member you already have. Only the pages and the design are
  separate.

  Switch between sites from the website editor's header, duplicate an existing
  site to start a variant from a working page tree, and choose which site each
  custom domain opens on the Domains page. One site is your default — it is where
  email links and your `1club.me` address point.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-27" description="Connect AI assistants to your gym with MCP" tags={["New","Highlight","Settings"]}>
  ## Connect AI assistants to your gym with MCP

  Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI assistants can now connect to 1club and work with your real schedule, availability, bookings, and revenue.

  1club now runs Model Context Protocol servers, so an AI assistant can call
  1club directly instead of guessing. Connect Claude or ChatGPT once, and you can
  ask things like "which classes next week still have space?" or "book court 2 on
  Thursday at 18:00" and get an answer from your live data.

  There are two servers, each with its own address. The admin server works with
  one organization's own data and needs an API token, which you create in
  Settings > APIs and MCP. The discovery server searches the public marketplace
  and needs nothing at all. An assistant connected to one never sees the other's
  tools.

  What an assistant can reach today: your classes, your bookable areas and when
  they are free, your gyms, your published website content, and your billing
  transactions. It can also create and cancel bookings when you grant it write
  access.

  You stay in control of how much it can do. The token you give an assistant
  carries scopes, and the assistant can only use tools those scopes allow, so a
  read-only token can answer questions but can never book. Revoking the token
  cuts access off immediately.

  [Learn more](/mcp/overview)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-23" description="Import your product catalog from a spreadsheet" tags={["New","Billing"]}>
  ## Import your product catalog from a spreadsheet

  You can now bulk-import products from a CSV, including price, VAT rate, barcode, category, cost, and opening stock for a chosen location.

  The data import tool now supports products. Export your existing product list
  to CSV and upload it the same way you import members or memberships - the
  importer detects the columns (including Bulgarian headers like "Име артикул",
  "Продажна цена", "ДДС %" and "Количество") and maps them for you.

  Each imported product can carry its own VAT rate. That rate is created once and
  then used at the point of sale, so a product is always taxed at the correct rate
  regardless of its revenue account. Products also gained a cost price (for margin
  reporting) and a product code (SKU) alongside the existing barcode.

  If your file has a quantity column, choose a location during the import and those
  quantities are recorded as opening stock for that location. Products without a
  quantity are imported without stock. Duplicate products (matched by barcode, then
  name) are skipped so you can safely re-run an import.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-22" description="Cash registry & expense management" tags={["New","Highlight","Billing"]}>
  ## Cash registry & expense management

  Track the cash in your drawer, record expenses, and see where the money goes — cash payments are captured automatically.

  You can now manage cash and expenses right inside 1club. Turn on the new
  Cash module in Settings and each club gets a cash drawer that always knows
  how much cash it should hold.

  - Cash payments you take for classes, memberships, and products are added to
    the drawer automatically — no double entry.
  - Record expenses (rent, instructor pay, supplies, water delivery, and more)
    against editable categories, with an optional receipt photo. Non-cash
    expenses like card or bank transfer are tracked for reporting without
    touching the drawer.
  - Add or remove cash for anything else (floats, deposits, pay-outs).
  - Optionally count the drawer at any time — 1club compares your count to the
    expected balance and records any over/short so the books match reality.
  - An overview dashboard shows cash on hand per club, cash in vs out over time,
    and expenses by category.

  Every entry is kept as a permanent record; corrections are made with a
  reversing entry rather than editing history, so the trail is always auditable.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-10" description="Self-service Check-in App" tags={["New","Highlight","Operations"]}>
  ## Self-service Check-in App

  Members can now check themselves into classes or the gym from a dedicated self-service screen at the club.

  A new touch-first Check-in App runs on a tablet or screen at your club, next to
  the existing kiosk. Members find themselves by name (or with their QR code or
  PIN, when enabled) and are checked in instantly - a green screen confirms the
  check-in, an amber screen means they are in but a payment is still due, and a
  red screen sends them to the front desk.

  - Members can hold the QR code from their 1club app up to the tablet's camera
    and are checked in on the spot, without touching the screen.
  - Members with an upcoming booking are checked in the moment they identify
    themselves.
  - Without a booking, members pick from the classes starting in the next hour;
    the club's booking policy decides whether their membership covers it or an
    unpaid payment is recorded for the front desk.
  - Choose the journey per club: identify first, or pick a class first and tap
    your name on the class list.
  - Open Gym mode checks members in on their general membership with no class
    list at all.

  Configure everything under Settings, Member Experience, Check-in App: enable it
  per club, copy the device link, toggle QR and PIN identification and Open Gym
  mode, and drag to reorder the user journey.
</Update>
