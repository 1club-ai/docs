Work is now the first item in the main menu. Everything you have going with the assistant
is listed beside the piece you have open, each marked with where it stands: working, needs
your review, or waiting on a result.

A new piece of work starts on New work. Under the question and a line about your day sit
four topics: Schedule, Members, Payments and Reports. Each shows how many issues wait in
it, coloured by the most urgent. Open one to see them, with a question worth asking, and
start any of them in one tap. Below, three cards bring up the most pressing issues and a
question you ask often.

Using the assistant is now a permission of its own, so you decide who has it, role by
role, in the permissions editor. Owners, managers, front desk and sales keep it;
instructors get it once you grant it to them.
