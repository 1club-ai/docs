    <div className="prose dark:prose-invert lx-prose">

A new built-in automation, Review request, asks members how their visit
went. The morning after a visit, the member gets an email with a one-tap
star rating. Once they have rated it, they are invited to review your
business on Google too, whatever the stars, when your Google profile is
connected.

Members are never asked too often: after their first visit, then every tenth,
and at most once a month. It works whether or not you check members in, and it
sends in each member's own language. You can reword the email in Message
templates.

Review request is ready for every club, on every plan, and starts switched
off. Turn it on from Home or in Automations, where you can also change how
often members are asked, the channels and the Google invitation.
