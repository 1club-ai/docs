The overview metrics are a panel of six small charts on [Home](/home/overview), each with one headline figure, a **Coaching** panel when coach time has its own revenue account, and a full-width **Revenue by month** chart below them. Owners and managers use them for a quick read on the gym's money, activity, and growth before opening a full report.

## When to use it

Typical uses:

- **Morning check** - a climbing gym owner sees the week's bookings, check-ins, and payments in one glance, without opening Analytics.
- **Spot a collection problem** - a Pilates studio sees the red **Unpaid** share in **Payments** growing and chases open invoices before the gap widens.
- **See where bookings come from** - a padel gym reads the source split in **Bookings** to tell whether a busy week came from the member app, the website, or a partner platform.
- **Check how full the gym is** - a tennis gym switches the period to **3m** and watches **Occupancy** week by week before opening more hours.

## How it works

The **Overview** section is shown to staff who can read gym analytics, once your organization has any revenue or members. Before that, Home leaves it out rather than showing empty charts.

The **Period** switch next to the **Overview** title sets the stretch of time every chart in the panel covers: **7d** (the last 7 days, today included), **30d**, or **3m** (the last 90 days). It goes back to **7d** each time you open Home. At 7d and 30d each bar is a day. At 3m each bar is a week, and the tooltip names it "Week of" its first day.

If your organization has more than one club, the club picker next to the greeting narrows **Payments**, **Bookings**, **Occupancy**, **Check-ins**, **Coaching**, and **Revenue by month** to that club. **Customer growth** and **Unique visitors** are always organization-wide and show the caption **All clubs** while a club is picked.

Every chart works the same way:

- **The figure** at the top is the headline number for the period.
- **The chart** below it has no axes. The labels under it give the first and last day or month it covers.
- **Totals** are printed above each bar or point at **7d**. At **30d** and **3m** they move to the tooltip, and hovering shows each series with a **Total**.
- **The legend** names each series, with its total for the period where it has one.
- **The title** links to the full report.

Money is shown in your organization's currency. If a chart has nothing to show for its period, it says so instead of drawing an empty chart.

## The charts

| Chart | Headline figure | What it draws | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Payments** | Amount billed in the period | Bars, paid and unpaid, split by revenue account once a second account has sales | Payments report |
| **Bookings** | Bookings for sessions in the period | Bars stacked by booking source | Bookings dashboard |
| **Occupancy** | How full what you offer was | Occupancy per day or week, for areas, instructors, and classes | Occupancy |
| **Check-ins** | Check-ins in the period | Check-ins per day or week | Check-ins report |
| **Customer growth** | Billable customers now | Members and drop-ins over the period | Members dashboard |
| **Unique visitors** | Unique visitors in the period | Daily visitors line | Web traffic |
| **Coaching** | Coach time billed in the period | Coach money per day or week, and the coaches who sold it | Revenue report, **Coaching** tab |
| **Revenue by month** | Revenue for the months shown | Monthly bars stacked by revenue account | Revenue report |

### Payments

The amount billed over the period, with each bar split into **Paid** (green) and **Unpaid** (red). The legend shows the total paid and unpaid for the period.

Once a second revenue account has sales in the period (area hire and coach time, for example), each bar is stacked by account instead. Each account has its own color, with its paid part solid and what is still owed faded on top. The legend then shows each account's billed amount, and the caption under the headline shows the total paid and unpaid.

The title opens the [Payments report](/analytics/billing#payments-report).

### Bookings

The number of bookings for sessions in the period, today included, with a bar for each day or week, quiet days included. Bookings count on the day the session takes place, not the day they were made, so today's bar includes sessions later today. Each bar is stacked by booking source, in the colors your organization uses for its sources, and the legend shows each source's count for the period. Smaller sources are grouped into **Other (N)**. Cancelled bookings are not counted.

The title opens the [Bookings dashboard](/analytics/schedule#bookings-dashboard). For how sources are defined, see [Bookings by source](/analytics/schedule#bookings-by-source).

### Occupancy

How full what your gym offers was: the open hours of your areas, the hours of the coaches members book, and the places in your classes. The headline is the average of those that apply, each measured on its own, so a studio that only runs classes reads how full its classes were. Each bar is a gauge on a 0 to 100% scale, filled to that day's or week's figure. With more than one measure, each also runs as its own line, and the legend shows **Areas**, **Instructors**, and **Classes** with each one's rate. With a single measure, a caption says how much was booked, such as "120 of 300 h booked" or "18 of 24 spots booked".

Area occupancy is counted the same way as on the [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy#how-occupancy-is-measured) report. The title opens [Occupancy](/analytics/occupancy) in Analytics.

### Check-ins

How many check-ins your clubs recorded in the period, with a bar for each day or week.

The title opens the [Check-ins report](/analytics/schedule#check-ins).

### Customer growth

How many billable customers you have now: members plus drop-ins. The chart shows the same count over the period, stacked as **Members** and **Drop-ins**: one point per day at **7d** and **30d**, and one per week at **3m**.

The title opens the [Members dashboard](/analytics/members#members-dashboard).

### Unique visitors

How many unique visitors your website and member portal had over the period, counted together. The line stays daily at every period, and days with no visitors show as zero.

The title opens **Web Traffic** under **Analytics** > **Website and Apps**. See [Analytics overview](/analytics/overview).

### Coaching

The **Coaching** panel appears below the six charts only when an [instructor type](/settings/instructor-types) posts coach time to its own revenue account (see [Coach revenue on its own account](/analytics/billing#coach-revenue-on-its-own-account)). It follows the **Period** switch and shows:

- What coach time billed in the period, with a caption giving its share of everything billed and the hours coached.
- A bar chart of coach money per day or week.
- **Instructors** - how many coaches sold coach time, and the top five with what each billed.
- Once you pay coaches through [pay rates](/staff/pay-rates), their **Pay** and what the **Club keeps**. Before that, it says **No pay rates yet** with a link to set them up.

The titles open the **Coaching** tab of the [Revenue report](/analytics/billing#revenue-report).

### Revenue by month

Revenue for the months shown, with one bar per month stacked by revenue account. It sits on its own row below the panel and doesn't follow the **Period** switch. The legend lists each account and its total. When you have many accounts, the smaller ones are grouped into **Other (N)** so the chart stays readable.

The chart shows the last 12 months by default. To change that, click the settings icon in the chart's header to open **Configure Revenue Widget**, set **Number of months** (1 to 24), and click **Update**. The setting is saved in this browser only, so it doesn't follow you to another device or apply to your colleagues.

The title opens the [Revenue report](/analytics/billing#revenue-report). Switch to its **By account** tab to see the same breakdown as the chart.

## Troubleshooting

**The Overview section doesn't appear.** You need permission to read gym analytics, and your organization needs at least some revenue or members. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

**A chart shows a dash instead of a number.** The data for that chart failed to load. Reload the page.

**A figure differs from the report it opens.** Most reports open on their own default date range. Set the report to the same period as the chart (for example the last seven days) to compare.

**The numbers don't change during the day.** The charts update when you return to the tab after a while, or when you reload the page.

**Customer growth doesn't change when you pick a club.** Customer growth and Unique visitors are always organization-wide.

**There is no Coaching panel.** No instructor type posts coach time to its own revenue account, so coach time is filed with everything else.
