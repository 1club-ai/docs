**Home** is the page you land on after signing in to the admin. It shows what needs attention today, how the gym is doing at a glance, and what is still left to set up to grow it. Owners, managers, front desk staff, and instructors all use the same page, and each person sees the sections their permissions allow.

## When to use it

Typical uses:

- **Run the front desk** - at a CrossFit box, the front desk opens **Today** before the 7:00 class, opens its roster, and checks members in as they arrive, adding a walk-in without leaving the page.
- **Catch problems early** - a padel gym manager sees two memberships ending next week and an overdue invoice in the **Memberships** column of **Today**, and follows up before the members lapse.
- **Read the week at a glance** - a yoga studio owner checks payments, bookings, occupancy, check-ins, and website visitors in the **Overview** charts, then clicks through to the full report behind any number that looks off.
- **Get the day by email** - a gym owner who is rarely at the desk switches on the **Daily email** for both locations and reads each one's day over breakfast.
- **Keep people in view** - a martial arts school adds a member list of the **Birthday** tag to Home, so coaches see whose birthday is this week.
- **Finish setting up** - a new jiu-jitsu gym uses the **Grow** checklist to publish its website, list itself on the 1club network, and turn on review requests.

## What's on the page

Sections appear top to bottom in this order. A section you can't see is left out entirely, so the page never shows empty placeholders.

| Section | What it shows | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greeting | Your first name, a one-sentence AI summary of the day, and the club picker | Everyone (the summary needs access to **Today** or the **Overview** charts) |
| **Today** | Today's classes, standalone bookings, memberships, and overdue invoices, with the **Daily email** switch | Staff who can read classes, bookings, members, invoices, or gym analytics, and instructors |
| **Member lists** | Your own lists of the members carrying a tag | Staff who can read member profiles |
| **Overview** | Six small charts (payments, bookings, occupancy, check-ins, customer growth, and unique visitors) with a **Period** switch, a **Coaching** panel when coach time has its own revenue account, plus **Revenue by month** | Staff who can read gym analytics, once the gym has revenue or members |
| **Monthly earnings** | What you earned teaching this month | Instructors who can see their own pay |
| **Grow** | The **Online presence** and **Marketing** cards | Staff who can read organization settings, members, or marketing |

The **Overview** charts sit three across on a wide screen, then two, then one as the window narrows. The **Grow** cards sit side by side on wide screens and stack on narrow ones. For what each overview chart measures, see [Home metrics](/home/metrics).

### Greeting and day summary

The line under **Hello** is a one-sentence AI summary of the most relevant thing about today, such as the next class, how busy the day is, or an overdue invoice worth a look. It is scoped to you, so an instructor gets a summary of their own day, and it only mentions money and membership figures you have permission to read.

The line stays blank while the summary loads, and stays blank if AI isn't available for your organization. For the other AI features in the admin, see [AI assistants](/reference/ai-assistants). If there is nothing to show yet and you can see the **Overview** charts, the line reads "Let's set up your account." instead.

### Club picker

If your organization has more than one club, a club picker sits next to the greeting, with **All clubs** and each of your clubs. Picking a club narrows the club view of **Today** and the **Overview** charts to that club, except **Customer growth** and **Unique visitors**, which stay organization-wide. It is the same club choice as the rest of the admin, so picking one here picks it everywhere.

## Today

**Today** shows what is happening at the gym today, in up to three columns side by side: **Classes**, **Bookings**, and **Memberships**. A column you can't read, or one with nothing in it today, is left out and the others share the width. When nothing is left at all, the section reads **Nothing scheduled for today**. The details link next to the section header opens the [Schedule](/schedule/calendar).

Each column has a headline figure for the day, a small chart of the day hour by hour, and a **Next up** (or **Ending soonest**) list of up to five rows. Click a row to open the record behind it, or click **View all (N)** at the bottom of a column for the full list.

| Column | Figure and chart | Rows | Click opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Classes** | Today's classes, with a caption such as "18 of 24 spots booked · 75%" (or how many have checked in once classes have run). The chart stacks **Checked in**, **Not checked in**, and **Open spots** by hour. | Classes still ahead: start time (or **Now** while it runs), class name, and spots taken out of capacity (for example `8/12`), or **Full**. A **View** button opens the roster. | The class |
| **Bookings** | Today's standalone bookings, with the amount charged and how many are still to come. | Bookings still ahead: start time (or **Now**), the area, the member, and the amount charged. | The booking |
| **Memberships** | How many memberships are active, with a caption such as "4 ending in the next 14 days · 2 paused". The chart splits the next 14 days into **Today**, **This week**, and **Next week**. | One **N overdue invoices** row with the total amount, in red, then memberships ending soonest, with the end day, member, and plan. | Memberships, or the overdue invoices (the invoice itself when there is only one) |

The lists drop classes and bookings once they have ended, and show **No more classes today** or **No more bookings today** when the day is done. The figures and charts still count the whole day. Cancelled bookings are not listed.

Each column is built from what you are allowed to read. Front desk staff who can read classes but not invoices, for example, see the timetable and bookings without the overdue invoices. Staff who can read invoices but not memberships see an **Overdue invoices** column instead of **Memberships**.

### Club and My classes

If you teach classes and also have access to the whole gym's day, a **Club** / **My classes** switch appears in the **Today** header.

- **Club** shows the columns described above.
- **My classes** shows only the classes you teach and the bookings assigned to you, including the ones already finished. Booking rows show a status such as **Starts in 1h 30m**, **In progress**, or **Completed**, and class rows show **View** to open the roster. Each row opens your own performance page for that class or booking. This list refreshes every minute.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Instructors who can only see their own schedule get the **My classes** list without a switch.

For who to call about memberships ending soon, open **Analytics** > **Members** > [Renewals](/analytics/members#renewals).

### Class roster

Click **View** on a class row to open its roster in a side panel. You can open rosters from both **Club** and **My classes**. The header keeps the class name and time in view and counts arrivals, for example **6 of 12 checked in**. The roster lists everyone in the class: members who booked, and walk-ins who were checked in without a booking. Members move to the bottom as they check in, so the people still to arrive stay at the top.

Each attendee shows their name, what covers their place (a membership or plan name, or **Drop In**), and an indicator when they owe money or still need to sign a required waiver. Row actions:

- **Check In** - records the member's arrival. Check-in opens one hour before the class starts and stays open until the end of the club's day. Only staff who can record check-ins for the whole organization (admins by default) can check members in outside that window.
- **Cancel check-in** - click the checked-in mark to take a check-in back. If you recorded it yourself, you can undo it for a short time afterwards. Staff with permission to delete check-ins can take one back at any time, but a check-in that took a payment has to be refunded by a manager first.
- **Assess** - opens the member's score card for this session. See [Assessments](/members/assessments).
- **Send Message** - writes to the member.
- **Cancel** - cancels the member's booking. Cancelling a booking that is already checked in voids the visit, so it needs permission to delete check-ins. Bookings made through a partner platform can't be cancelled here.

Use **Search members…** to find someone on a long roster, and **Back to full roster** to clear the search. Use **Add attendee** to sell a place to a walk-in. **Add attendee** is unavailable when the class is full, when the class is cancelled, and outside the check-in window.

Which actions you see depends on your permissions. Instructors get the same roster for the classes they teach.

## Daily email

The daily email sends you a club's day, so you can follow it without opening the admin. It opens with a short AI summary of what stood out and up to three things worth doing. Below that is Home as it read at the end of that day: **Classes**, **Bookings**, and **Memberships** from **Today**, then **Payments**, **Bookings**, **Occupancy**, and **Check-ins** for the last 7 days, with the same figures and charts. Each email links back to Home for its club.

It is off by default, and each person chooses it for themselves:

1. On **Home**, click **Email me daily** in the **Today** section's header.
2. Switch it on for each club you want an email for. You get one email a day per club.
3. Pick when it arrives: **End of day** (at 20:00, about that day) or **Morning** (at 07:00, about the day before), on the club's clock.

A gym that had the daily summary switched on under the earlier club setting keeps sending it each morning to its owners and admins. Once anyone switches the email on or off here for that gym, only the people who chose it get it.

While it is on, the header shows **Daily email** with your timing; click it to change clubs or timing, or switch every club off to stop it.

The switch is shown to staff who can see the **Overview** charts. Each email is checked against your access when it is sent: you need to still be active staff with permission to read gym analytics, and it shows only the parts your permissions let you read.

## Member lists

**Member lists** keep the members carrying one [tag](/settings/smart-tagging) in view on Home, for example birthdays this week, unpaid members, or new members. Each list is yours: nobody else sees it, and you cannot set one up for someone else.

The first time, the section shows one row explaining what a list is for, with **Add list**. To add or change a list:

1. Click **Add list** (or **Edit** in a list's menu).
2. Pick the **Tag**. Only tags that someone carries are offered.
3. Pick how many members to **Show**: 5, 10, or 20.

Each list shows the tag, how many members carry it, and the first members by name, each with their age, phone, and email, and a **!** when they have a contact alert. Click a member to open their profile, or the list's title to open the member list filtered to that tag. **Remove** in a list's menu takes it off Home. If you removed every list, the section keeps only its title with **Add list**.

## Monthly earnings

Instructors who can see their own pay get a **Monthly earnings** section. It shows what you earned teaching this month and never the gym's revenue, so a manager who also teaches sees their own pay here and the gym's numbers in the **Overview** charts.

The card shows the month's total and one bar per day. The solid part of each bar is paid and the pale part on top is still pending, so a bar's height is the work you did and its color is the money collected so far. Today's bar is drawn wider. Between the total and the chart are four figures: **Classes**, **Standalone bookings**, **Days worked**, and **Avg / day**.

Use the arrows to move between months. Click a day's bar to open **Payment details**, which lists the classes and bookings behind that day with each one's amount and whether it is paid, pending, or partly paid.

## Grow

The **Grow** section holds two checklist cards. Each row is one channel or tool, with a status chip when it is live and one next step: open it when it is live, or set it up when it is not. Rows that are not live yet look muted.

### Online presence

**How members find you**

- **AI visibility report** - the score out of 100 from your latest [business audit](/home/ai-visibility-report), and when it was checked. Click **View report** to open it, or the re-run button to check again. If you have never run one, click **Check**, find your gym by name, and click **Analyze**.
- **1club network** - whether your gyms are listed on the [1club network](/marketing/marketplace). With several gyms, the row shows how many are listed; expand it to see each gym's status. Click **Publish** to list the rest.

**Where they book**

- **Website** - your [website](/marketing/website) address once it is published. This row needs access to marketing. Click **Publish** if you have a draft, or **Set up** if you have no website yet.
- **Member portal** - the [member portal](/settings/member-portal) address once it is on, or **Set up** to turn it on.
- **Mobile app** - your members book in the 1club app. The details link opens the app's settings.

If you can see the AI visibility report but not the other channels, the card is titled **AI visibility report** and shows only that row.

### Marketing

**Your reputation**

- **Reviews** - your average rating, the number of reviews, and how many are verified. The details link opens [Reviews](/marketing/reviews).
- **Review request** - whether the built-in [Review request](/marketing/automations#built-in-automations) automation is running. It is on every plan, on for new gyms and off for gyms that existed before it. When a visit is completed, it asks the member to rate it the morning after, then invites them to review you on Google if your profile is connected: after the first visit, then every 10th, at most once a month. While it is on, the row shows an **Active** chip and a link to the automation. While it is off, click **Turn on**: it is switched on and opened so you can see its steps.
  If your gym still runs a review request it set up from a suggested blueprint before the built-in existed, the row shows that one instead. Such an automation asks after a check-in, so when no [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) checks members in automatically, staff who can read organization settings see a note under the row, **Turn on auto check-in so every visit gets asked**, linking to the booking policies.

**Bring members back**

- **Promotions** - opens [Promotions](/sales/promotions).
- **Automations** - how many of your own [automations](/marketing/automations) are running. Built-in system automations are not counted.

Staff who can read marketing but not manage it only see the **Review request** row once requests are already on, since turning them on needs permission to manage marketing.

## 1club AI on Home

On a wide screen, Home opens with the **1club AI** panel docked to its right, so you can ask a question or start a task next to your numbers. The panel suggests prompts that fit the page. If you close it, Home goes back to the floating AI bar for the rest of your session. On smaller screens the assistant opens as a drawer from the AI bar instead.

The panel is the same assistant you can open from any page. See [AI assistants](/reference/ai-assistants) for what it can do.

## Troubleshooting

**A section is missing.** Sections follow your permissions. The **Overview** charts need permission to read gym analytics, and only appear once the organization has revenue or members. **Member lists** need permission to read member profiles. **Monthly earnings** needs your user to be linked to an instructor and allowed to see their own pay. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

**Today looks shorter than expected.** In **Club**, classes and bookings that have already ended are removed from the lists, and a column with nothing in it today is left out. Rows you aren't allowed to read, such as overdue invoices, are left out too. If you picked a club next to the greeting, only that club's classes and bookings show.

**Today starts or ends at the wrong time.** "Today" follows your organization's time zone, not your computer's.

**There is no Email me daily switch.** It is shown to staff who can see the **Overview** charts, next to the **Today** header.

**The daily email stopped arriving.** It stops when you lose permission to read gym analytics or stop being active staff, and it is sent only for the clubs you switched on.

**Numbers don't update.** **My classes** refreshes every minute. **Today** reloads each time you come back to Home, and the rest updates when you return to the tab after a while. Reload the page to force fresh data.
