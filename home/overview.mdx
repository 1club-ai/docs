**Home** is the page you land on after signing in to the admin. It shows what needs attention today, a row of headline numbers, and a short checklist of what helps your gym grow, with the **1club AI** assistant docked beside it. Front desk staff run the day from it, owners and managers read the numbers, and instructors see their own classes and pay.

Home adapts to what you are allowed to see. There is no separate instructor dashboard and nothing to configure: each section appears only for staff who hold the permissions behind it (see [What each role sees](#what-each-role-sees)).

## When it helps

- **Run the morning at the desk** - open a class from **Today**, check members in from its roster, and add a walk-in without leaving the page.
- **Chase money before it slips** - **Today** lists memberships expiring today and overdue invoices, each one click from the record.
- **Read the business in ten seconds** - the overview shows revenue, today's activity, payments, bookings, customer growth, and website visitors side by side.
- **Start asking for reviews** - the **Marketing** card turns on review requests in one click.

## What's on Home

From top to bottom:

1. **Greeting** - "Hello" with your first name, and below it a one-sentence AI summary of your day. An admin in a brand new organization with nothing to show yet sees "Let's set up your account." instead.
2. **Today** - the day's classes, bookings, expiring memberships, and overdue invoices.
3. **Overview** - six metric cells, each a figure over a small chart.
4. **Monthly earnings** - for instructors only: what you earned teaching this month.
5. **Grow** - the **Online presence** and **Marketing** cards.

On wide screens the **1club AI** panel sits docked to the right of Home (see [1club AI on Home](#1club-ai-on-home)).

## Today

**Today** lists everything happening in your gym today, in one list:

| Row | What it shows | Click opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Class** | Class name, spots taken out of capacity (for example `8/12`), and start time | The class page under **Schedule** > **Classes** |
| **Booking** | The member, the area, the start time, and the amount charged | The booking page under **Schedule** > **Bookings** |
| **Expiring** | The member, their plan, and the end date of a membership about to lapse | **Sales** > **Memberships** |
| **Overdue** | The member and the amount owed, in red | The invoice |

Only the first five standalone bookings are listed. A **more bookings** row links to the full list under **Schedule** > **Bookings**. The section header shows how many bookings there are today, and the arrow next to it opens the [calendar](/schedule/calendar). When nothing is planned, Today says "Nothing scheduled for today".

The server builds Today from your permissions, so each block appears only if you may read it. For example, a front desk role that reads the timetable but not invoices sees the classes and bookings without the overdue rows.

### The class roster

Click **View** on a class row to open its roster in a side drawer, without leaving Home. The drawer shows how many attendees have checked in (for example "5 of 12 checked in"). From it you can:

- **Search members** in the roster, and **Add attendee** to add a walk-in (disabled when the class is full).
- **Check In** an attendee, or cancel a check-in by mistake. A check-in can only be taken back for a short window, and one that took a payment needs a manager to refund it first.
- **Assess** an attendee once the class has started, when [assessments](/members/assessments) are set up.
- **Send Message** to an attendee, or **Cancel** their booking.

### Club or My classes

Staff who both teach and run the gym see a **Club** / **My classes** switch on the Today header. **Club** shows the whole gym's day. **My classes** shows only the classes and bookings you teach, each with a status such as "Starts in 1h 30m", "In progress", or "Completed". Your choice is remembered in this browser.

Instructors who only see their own schedule get **My classes** with no switch.

## Overview

The overview is one panel of six cells. Each cell shows a headline figure over a small chart, and its title links to the full report in [Analytics](/analytics/overview).

| Cell | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue overview** | Monthly revenue split by revenue account, for the last 3 months by default |
| **Today's performance** | Today hour by hour: revenue as bars, bookings and check-ins as lines. Hours still to come are drawn dashed |
| **Payments (last 7 days)** | Paid against unpaid amounts for each of the last seven days |
| **Bookings (last 7 days)** | Bookings per day, split by booking source |
| **Customer growth** | Members and drop-ins over time, stacked (together they are your billable customers) |
| **Unique visitors (last 7 days)** | Visitors to your website and apps |

To change how many months **Revenue overview** covers, click the cog in its title row. In the **Configure Revenue Widget** dialog, set **Number of months** (1 to 24). The setting is saved in your browser, so it does not follow you to another device.

The panel shows three cells across on wide screens, two on medium screens, and one on a phone. It appears only to staff who can read analytics, and only once your organization has some revenue or at least one member, so a brand new gym does not see empty charts.

## Monthly earnings

Instructors with pay set up see **Monthly earnings**: a chart of what they earned each day this month, with tiles for **Paid**, **Pending**, **Classes**, **Standalone bookings**, **Days worked**, and **Avg / day**. Click a day to see its **Payment details**.

This section only ever shows the instructor's own pay. An admin who also teaches sees their own earnings here and the gym's revenue in the overview.

## Grow

The **Grow** section holds two checklist cards. Each row shows a status chip when something is live, and offers one next step when it is not.

**Online presence** shows how members find you and where they book:

- **AI visibility report** - a free report on how search engines and AI assistants see your gym. Click **Check** to run it the first time, then **View report** to open it. See [Business audit](/home/ai-visibility-report).
- **Marketplace** - how many of your gyms are listed on the 1club Marketplace, with **Publish** when some are not. See [Marketplace](/marketing/marketplace).
- **Website** - your website's address once it is published, or **Set up** / **Publish**. See [Website](/marketing/website).
- **Member portal** - the portal's address once it is on, or **Set up**. See [Member portal](/settings/member-portal).
- **Mobile app** - a reminder that your members book in the 1club app.

**Marketing** shows your reputation and what brings members back:

- **Reviews** - your average rating and number of reviews. See [Reviews](/marketing/reviews).
- **Review request** - whether members are asked for a review after their visit. When it is off, **Turn on** switches on the review request automation and opens it. If your plan does not include custom automations, the row links to **Automations** instead.
- **Promotions** - discounts and promo codes. See [Promotions](/sales/promotions).
- **Automations** - how many of your own automations are running. See [Automations](/marketing/automations).

<Note>
  Review requests go out two hours after a check-in. If no [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) has auto check-in on and you do not check members in by hand, no request is ever sent. The **Review request** row warns you about this and links to the booking policies.
</Note>

## 1club AI on Home

On a wide screen, Home opens with the **1club AI** panel docked to its right, so you can ask a question or start a task next to your numbers. The panel suggests prompts that fit the page. If you close it, Home goes back to the floating AI bar for the rest of your session. On smaller screens the assistant opens as a drawer from the AI bar instead.

The panel is the same assistant you can open from any page. See [AI assistants](/reference/ai-assistants) for what it can do.

## What each role sees

Every section is gated on a permission, not on a role, so a customized role sees exactly the sections it is entitled to.

| Section | Shown to staff who can |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** (gym-wide) | Read analytics, classes, bookings, members, or billing - any one of them is enough |
| **Today** (**My classes**) | Teach (linked to an instructor) and read their own classes or bookings |
| **Overview** | Read analytics |
| **Monthly earnings** | Teach and read their own billing |
| **Online presence** | Read organization settings (the **AI visibility report** row needs members read access instead) |
| **Marketing** | Read marketing. Turning on review requests needs marketing manage access |

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions) for the default roles.

## When numbers look off

Home fetches its data when you open the page. There is no auto-refresh: reload the page to see fresh figures.

- **Time zone** - "today" and "last 7 days" follow your gym's time zone, not your browser's.
- **Permissions** - a section you cannot read does not appear at all, and a Today block you cannot read arrives empty.
- **No charts at all** - the overview is hidden until your organization has revenue or members, and for staff without analytics access.

To check a figure, click the cell title to open the full report. The report shows the same data with filters and is the source of truth.
