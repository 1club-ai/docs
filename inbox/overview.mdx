The **Inbox** (left rail, route `/messages`) is a unified conversation surface: every email, SMS, and WhatsApp message you send, plus every reply or inbound message that lands in 1Club, shows up here in one chronological list. Open a conversation to read the full thread and reply.

A **message** is human-authored correspondence between a gym and a member, addressed and replyable. That is what distinguishes it from a **notification** ("your booking is confirmed"), which is system-generated, one-directional, and not replyable. The Inbox holds only messages. System events for staff live in **My Notifications**, opened from your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (see [Notifications](/settings/notifications)).

## Overview

- Read outbound and inbound messages as threaded conversations in one list.
- Compose to a single contact, a hand-picked group, or everyone matching a segment (contact type, tags, membership, pipeline stage, or source).
- Send over three channels: **email**, **SMS**, and **WhatsApp**.
- Reply inside a thread.
- Save drafts, attach files to emails, and reuse custom message templates.
- Scope access by role: organization-wide for admins and managers, limited to their own conversations for instructors.

## Channels

Every message carries a `type` that fixes its channel. The Inbox lists all three side by side.

- **Email** - The default for new manual sends. Uses a block editor (subject plus body), supports templates, variables, and file attachments. Delivered through SendGrid.
- **SMS** - Plain text, no subject, no formatting. Sent from your gym's own sender name, or its own toll-free number in the US and Canada, once it is set up under [SMS](/settings/sms). This is a paid-plan channel because it carries a direct carrier cost.
- **WhatsApp** - Sent through a per-organization WhatsApp Business connection (WhatsApp Cloud API). Requires a connected WhatsApp number and a paid plan. A WhatsApp icon in the conversation row marks contacts who can be reached on WhatsApp.

A new manual message goes out as **email** by default; pick **SMS** or **WhatsApp** in the composer once that channel is set up. WhatsApp also arrives in the Inbox as inbound conversations: when you reply inside a WhatsApp thread, the reply stays on WhatsApp. SMS is outbound only. Automations can also send on any of the three channels.

App notifications, which cover the notification center and alerts on a member's devices, exist in the platform too, but they are emitted by transactional triggers (booking confirmed, waitlist promotion), not composed from the Inbox.

## How a message becomes a conversation

Sending creates one **message** record per outbound action and one recipient record per recipient. The Inbox lists only top-level messages: replies (anything with a `replyToMessageId`) roll up into the thread of their original.

A message's content is **canonical and channel-neutral**: it is stored as structured blocks (paragraph, heading, button, image), not as rendered HTML. Each channel renders its own projection of that content. The Inbox renders the blocks natively; email renders them through the transactional layout. So the delivered email is a projection of the message, not the message itself.

Inbound messages, including replies to your outbound sends, arrive as new top-level messages tagged `direction: inbound`. Selecting a draft opens the compose pane; selecting any other conversation opens the thread view with an inline reply box.

## Display sender, display recipient, and send on behalf of

The raw record only shows the staff `senderId` and the resolved recipient contacts, which is not always who a message should *appear* to be from or to. Three concepts fix that:

- **Display sender** - Who an outbound message appears to be *from* to the member. By default there is none, so member surfaces fall back to the club and admin surfaces fall back to the sending staff user. When a message is sent *on behalf of* a specific person, that person's name and avatar are persisted as the display sender and drive both the member inbox and the delivered email's `from` name. Today the one send-on-behalf-of path is the "send from the instructor" option on the review-request automation. `senderId` is unchanged: it stays the accountable staff user for audit and billing.
- **Message target** - Who a member-composed message is addressed to, as the member chose it: the club (the default) or a specific instructor they have a real relationship with. A member never addresses arbitrary contacts.
- **Display recipient** - The mirror of display sender for member-initiated threads. It fixes who the conversation appears to be *with* (the club's name and logo, or the instructor's name and avatar), so a club-targeted message never leaks which staff member happened to receive it.

## AI-assisted replies

Inside a thread, the reply composer shows a **Generate with AI** button. AI-drafted replies are not enabled for organizations at the moment, so the button returns "Failed to generate AI response" instead of a draft. Write the reply yourself, or ask the [AI assistant](/reference/ai-assistants) for help with the wording.

## Permissions and access

The Inbox respects two scopes:

- **Admins and managers** see the organization-wide inbox, compose new messages, use segments, and pick from custom templates.
- **Instructors** see only their own conversations: messages they sent, messages from the contacts they wrote to, and the threads those belong to. They can compose to the members they teach, with no segments and no template picker.

The governing permissions are `messages.read.own` / `messages.read.club` for viewing and `messages.create.own` / `messages.create.club` for composing. A holder of only the `.own` scope can message specific contacts they select, but cannot broadcast to a segment and can only reply to messages they can already read. Templates need `messages.read.club`.

## Plan limits

Each channel is gated differently at send time:

- **Email** requires a verified [sending domain](/settings/email-sending) during trial, and counts against your plan's email quota. Hitting the quota returns `PLAN_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with the daily and monthly figures, surfaced as a dialog rather than a generic toast.
- **SMS** is available on paid plans only. On a trial or locked plan the send returns `PLAN_FEATURE_LOCKED` with an upgrade prompt. Without an approved sender the send returns `SMS_NOT_CONFIGURED`; members in a country your gym has no ready sender for are skipped, and if Twilio refuses every text the send returns `SMS_DELIVERY_FAILED`. See [SMS](/settings/sms).
- **WhatsApp** is also paid-only and additionally requires a connected WhatsApp Business number. A missing connection returns `WHATSAPP_NOT_CONNECTED`; a carrier delivery failure returns `WHATSAPP_DELIVERY_FAILED`.

## Worked examples

**Racket sports - court-closure blast.** A padel club needs to warn members that Court 3 is resurfaced this weekend. Compose an email, switch to **Use segments**, and pick the **Members** type and the "Padel league" tag, write the notice in the block editor, attach the revised weekend schedule as a PDF, and send. Replies from members land back in the Inbox as inbound messages threaded under nothing new, ready for a one-tap reply.

**Martial arts - belt-test reminder over WhatsApp.** A jiu-jitsu gym with a connected WhatsApp number gets an inbound question from a student about the upcoming grading. Open the thread, click **Reply**, answer with the grading date and what to bring, and click **Send Reply**. The reply goes out on WhatsApp because the conversation is a WhatsApp thread.

**Pilates/Yoga - lapsed-client win-back.** A reformer studio switches to **Use segments**, picks the lapsed contact type, and sends a warm email offering a return class. Because the audience is a segment, this requires organization-level compose access, not an instructor's own-scope inbox.

**Gym/Fitness - event promotion.** From a live HIIT open-day event, open the **Message** menu and choose **Announce to all members**. The composer opens with a draft invitation carrying the event details and a link to book, addressed to every member. Adjust the audience if needed and send. **Message attendees** in the same menu writes to everyone booked on the event instead.

## Tips & best practices

- Reserve SMS and WhatsApp for time-sensitive, high-value messages. They cost more and read as more urgent than email.
- Segment with tags rather than blasting your whole contact list. Filtered sends keep engagement high and unsubscribes low.
- Use the "send from the instructor" path for relationship messages so members see a familiar name, not a generic club sender.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SMS send is blocked with an upgrade prompt | SMS is a paid-plan channel; the org is on trial or a locked plan | Upgrade to a paid plan, or send by email instead |
| WhatsApp send fails with "not connected" | No active WhatsApp Business number is connected for the org | Connect a WhatsApp number, then retry the send |
| Email send hits a limit dialog | The plan's email quota (daily or monthly) is exhausted | Wait for the quota to reset or upgrade the plan |
| "All recipients are unsubscribed or suppressed" | Every selected contact has opted out or is on the suppression list | Adjust the recipient list and resend |
| Instructor cannot see a conversation | Instructor inboxes show only the conversations they took part in | Have an admin or manager handle org-wide conversations |
| **Generate with AI** shows "Failed to generate AI response" | AI-drafted replies are not enabled for organizations | Write the reply yourself |
| A member's reply appears on the wrong side of the thread | Reply arrived without sender org-membership context | Reload the thread; the Inbox realigns member vs. staff senders on refetch |
