1club is an AI-native platform for running your business: members, bookings, sales, marketing and staff for every gym in your organization, in one admin at **admin.1club.ai**. Your members use the other side of it: your website, your member portal and the 1club App.

New here? Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to set up your first gym, plan and booking.

## Find your way around

The admin has the same layout on every page.

- **The menu down the left side** - each section is an icon with its name under it. Click one and its pages list in a column beside the menu, so moving between, say, **Plans** and **Memberships** is one click. **Settings** is the last item.
- **Your organization, top left** - your logo opens the organization menu: your **Member portal** and **Website**, and your other organizations if you belong to more than one.
- **Your account, bottom left** - your name or photo opens **Profile Settings**, **My Notifications** (with the unread count), **Setup** (until your setup checklist is done), **Account & Billing**, **Language**, **Theme** and **Logout**. **What's new**, just above it, has product updates and help.
- **The breadcrumb, top of the page** - when you work in more than one gym, its first crumb is the gym you're in. Click it to switch gyms, or choose **All clubs**.
- **Search everything** - in the middle of the page header, or press `Cmd` + `K` (`Ctrl` + `K` on Windows). Find any member, booking, membership, invoice or page, or run a quick action. See [Search and quick actions](/reference/ai-assistants#search-and-quick-actions).
- **The AI assistant** - the 1club icon at the right of the page header, or `Cmd` + `.`. See [AI assistants](/reference/ai-assistants).

On a phone, a tab bar at the bottom holds your first three sections, **Search** and **More**, which opens the full menu.

Some sections and pages appear only when their feature is on: **Point of Sale** and the cash register when you run a till, **Events** unless you switch it off in **Settings** > **Events**, and tournaments, matches and ratings when competition features are on. An independent professional's admin leaves out the pages about running a venue. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions) for what each role sees.

<Note>
  The menu changed in October 2026. If you're used to the old one: plans,
  memberships, products, orders, promotions and Finance are now under **Sales**;
  events, tournaments and matches have their own **Events** section; areas are
  under **Settings** > **Clubs**; and your notifications are in your account
  menu.
</Note>

## What your members use

- **[The 1club App](/settings/mobile-app)** - the free phone app where your members book, pay, check in and follow their progress.
- **[Member portal](/settings/member-portal)** - the web version of the member experience, on your own address.
- **[Website](/marketing/website)** - your gym's website, built and edited in **Marketing**.
- **[Check-in App](/settings/kiosk#check-in-app)** - a self check-in tablet at your door.
- **[Marketplace](/marketing/marketplace)** - your listing on 1club.me, where new people find you.

## Getting help

- **What's new**, above your account menu, lists product updates and opens help.
- **Ask the assistant** - it can answer from these docs and from your own data.
- **Search these docs** for a topic, or check [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) for common issues.
- **Contact your administrator** for questions about your account or access.
