## What is 1Club Admin?

1Club is an AI-native gym management platform. Use the admin to run members, bookings, sales, marketing, and staff for every gym in your organization from one place.

## Quick start guide

### Day 1: Basic setup

1. Complete your profile with accurate information.
2. Explore **Home**: today's classes and bookings, your key numbers, and 1club AI.
3. Review member data and understand current status.
4. Check billing settings and payment methods under **Settings** > **Billing**.

### Week 1: Core operations

1. Set up areas for bookings under **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Areas**.
2. Create membership plans and pricing under **Sales** > **Plans**.
3. Configure class schedules and instructor assignments.
4. Test the booking system with sample data.

### Month 1: Full system

1. Implement automated billing and payment processing.
2. Set up message templates under **Settings** > **Communication** > **Templates**.
3. Review your numbers in **Analytics**.
4. Train staff on system usage and best practices.
