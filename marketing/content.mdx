**Content** is where you write the standalone pieces that sit alongside your website pages: news posts, how-to guides, FAQs, and reusable blocks of copy. Owners and managers write them, and visitors and members read them on your public website and member portal.

## When to use it

Content items let you publish and update copy without touching your website layout. You write an article or an answer once, translate it, and every place that shows it stays in sync.

Typical uses:

- **Post gym news** - a padel gym announces its winter league as a **Post**, and the website's **News** section lists it with its own page.
- **Answer the same questions once** - a yoga studio writes its parking, mat hire, and late-arrival answers as **FAQs**, so the website's **FAQ** section and the member portal show the same answers.
- **Teach members how to use the app** - a climbing gym writes **How-To Guides** for booking a slot and freezing a membership, and members find them in the portal.
- **Reuse a paragraph across pages** - a jiu-jitsu gym keeps its "About us" text as a **Block** and pulls it into several website pages, so one edit updates them all.

## How it works

Every content item has a type, a slug, and a language. The four types and where they show:

| Type | What it is for | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Post** | News and blog entries | The website's **News** section (each post gets its own page at `/news/<slug>`) and the member portal's posts page |
| **How-To Guide** | Instructional articles | The member portal's how-to guides page |
| **Frequently Asked Question** | One question and its answer | The website's **FAQ** section and the member portal's FAQs page |
| **Block** | A reusable chunk of copy | Wherever a website page uses a **Block** section that points at its slug |

Items that share a slug and type are language variants of the same piece. The list shows one row per slug, with the languages it already has.

Signable documents (waivers, terms, privacy policy) are content items too, but you manage them under [Settings > Bookings > Waivers](/settings/waivers), not here.

### Content items vs website pages

If you want a brand-new page on your public site, build it in the [website builder](/marketing/website). If you want a single article that a website section lists (**News**, **FAQ**) or the portal shows, create a content item. Copy you would otherwise retype on several pages, such as legal blurbs or an "About us" paragraph, belongs in a **Block**.

## Where to find it

Open **Marketing** > **Content**. The page groups your items by type (**Posts**, **How-To Guides**, **Frequently Asked Questions**, **Blocks**), with a tab for each type showing how many items it has. Each row shows the title, status, visibility, and languages.

Drag rows by the handle to reorder items within a type. The order you set here is the order they are listed in.

## Creating a content item

1. On **Marketing** > **Content**, click **Create Content** and pick a type. You can also use the **Add** button next to a type's heading (for example **Add Post**).
2. Enter the **Title** and write the body in the block editor. It supports headings, text, images, tables, and more. Images you add to the body are uploaded from your computer and saved to your [Media library](/marketing/media) too.
3. In the **Details** panel, fill in at least **Slug** and **Content Type**.
4. Set **Status** to **Published** when it should go live, then save.

### Details panel

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Slug** | The URL-friendly version of the title, used in links and by **Block** sections. Required. |
| **Content Type** | **Post**, **How-To Guide**, **Frequently Asked Question**, or **Block**. The list also offers **Document**, the type used for signable documents (see below). Required. |
| **Status** | **Draft** or **Published**. Only published items appear on your site and portal. |
| **Visibility** | **Public** (anyone), **Admin & Member** (signed-in members), or **Admin only**. |
| **Language** | Which language this variant is written in. |
| **Featured** | Pins the item to the top of its listing. |
| **Author** | The contact credited as the author, or **No Author**. |
| **Excerpt** | A short description shown in listings. |
| **Images** | Images attached to the item, picked from your [Media library](/marketing/media). |
| **Tags** | Shown publicly with the content. Website sections such as **FAQ** can filter by tag. |
| **SEO** | A collapsible group with **Meta Title** and **Meta Description** (what search engines show) and **Summary (for AI)** and **Keywords (for AI)** (a plain description and keywords that AI assistants and search use to understand the item). |

## Translating and copying

Each row's language menu offers **Translate to** each of your organization's languages that the item does not have yet. Translating copies the item into that language and translates it, then opens the new variant for review. From an item's own page you can also **Copy** it, or switch between its existing language variants.

Your organization's languages are set under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**.
