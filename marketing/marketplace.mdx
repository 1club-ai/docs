<Note>
  The Marketplace is currently in **beta**. Listing is free, and every application is reviewed by the 1club team before it goes live.
</Note>

The 1club marketplace at [1club.me](https://1club.me) is a public directory where people discover, explore, and book gyms. A published listing shows your gym's details, photos, amenities, and location to anyone browsing - no account required to look around. Gym owners submit and manage the listing, and prospective members browse it.

## When to use it

A listing puts your gym in front of people who are looking for somewhere to train but haven't found you yet. Listing is free during the beta, and you decide which of your gyms appear and when.

Typical uses:

- **Get found by people nearby** - a new reformer Pilates studio lists itself so people browsing 1club.me can see its classes and plans and book a trial.
- **List locations one at a time** - an organization with several gyms submits each gym separately, so a location whose profile isn't complete yet stays off until it is.
- **Step off the marketplace for a while** - a gym closing for renovation unpublishes its listing, which takes effect immediately, then resubmits it for review when it reopens.

See [Worked example: listing a Pilates studio](#worked-example-listing-a-pilates-studio) for the full walkthrough.

## Private, link only or listed

Every gym on 1club is in the 1club App for its own members, whether or not it is on the marketplace. What the marketplace decides is who else can find it. Each gym has one of three levels:

| Level | Who can open the gym in the 1club App and on 1club.me |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Only your own people: members with an account at your gym, people you invited by email, contacts whose verified email or phone you already hold, and anyone who opens your gym's invite link. Nobody else can tell the gym exists. Every gym starts here. |
| **Link only** | Anyone with the link to your gym's page. It never appears in search or browse. |
| **Listed** | Everyone: your gym shows in search, browse, the map and 1club.me. |

Your members see your gym first on their home screen in the app, at every level, and can book, buy plans, open doors and message you there.

### Invite people to a private gym

1. Open the gym's QR code: in **Settings** > **Clubs**, open the gym and click **Show and download** under **QR code** in the right-hand panel. You can also click **QR code** on the gym's card in **Marketing** > **Marketplace**.
2. Click **Copy link** to share the link, or **Download SVG** to print the QR code for your reception desk.

Whoever opens the link can book a trial or buy a public plan straight away. On a phone with the 1club App, your gym opens in the app. Everywhere else, the link opens your gym's website with the gym already picked in its booking sections, or, if you don't run a website, your member portal on the gym's first booking page (its classes or its booking calendar, in the order of your portal menu), where they browse, book and sign up without installing anything. Their member record is created at their first booking or purchase, not when they open the link.

Bookings that come from your QR code record **QR** as their marketing source, and a new contact who books or signs up from it is filed under your **QR** contact source, so you can see what the code brings in. A contact who already has a source keeps it.

To send the link to someone already on your member list, open their profile, click **...** and choose **Send invite link**. The message opens already written in your gym's language, with a **Book now** button, and goes out by email or SMS like any other message. A link sent in a message is recorded as a message, never as a QR scan. Automations can send it too: put the `{{clubInviteUrl}}` variable in an automation message, for example in your welcome message.

The code and link you print or copy from **QR code** never expire. A link sent from a member's profile or by an automation works for a year. Click **Reset link** to stop them all at any time: the old links and code stop working, and people who opened them lose access to the gym until they get the new one. Your members keep theirs.

## How it works

There are two sides to the Marketplace:

- **The consumer marketplace** at 1club.me, where the public finds and explores gyms.
- **The admin side** under **Marketing** > **Marketplace**, where you submit your gym to the network and manage its listing.

## The consumer marketplace (1club.me)

Anyone can browse [1club.me](https://1club.me) without an account. Visitors:

- **Search and filter** gyms by sport, location, and availability.
- **Browse gym cards** with the logo, name, location, and photos, and save favorites.
- **Open a gym's profile** to see its description, photo gallery, amenities, sports, coaches, and the plans, classes, and appointments it offers.
- **Get in touch or book** - contact the gym or start a booking from the public profile.

Your published listing is what powers all of this, so the more complete your gym profile is, the better it presents.

## Get your gym listed

You manage your listing from **Marketing** > **Marketplace** in the admin dashboard. You submit a gym to the network through a guided wizard, and the 1club team reviews it before it goes live.

1. Open **Marketing** > **Marketplace** and click **Start now**. If your organization has more than one gym, pick which one to list from the menu.
2. The setup wizard opens in a new tab and walks you through the listing profile in these steps: **club type**, **sports**, **location** (address, photos, phone, and email), **description**, **amenities**, and a final **Confirm & submit** step. Everything already on file for the gym is prefilled.
3. Confirm the final step to submit your application. This sends the gym for review and the 1club team is notified.

Your application lands in an **In review** state. You will see the status on the Marketplace page, and the listing goes live once a reviewer approves it.

<Note>
  A brand-new sign-up that starts from 1club.me creates a free account and must verify its email before the wizard's presets load. If you are already signed in to the admin dashboard, you go straight into the wizard for the gym you picked.
</Note>

## Profile requirements

A gym can only be submitted when its profile is complete. The required fields are:

- **Address**
- **Phone number**
- **Email**
- **Description** (at least some written content)
- **Location** - a city plus a resolved map pin (latitude and longitude)

Photos are optional but strongly recommended - a listing without them looks bare. If something is missing, the Marketplace page shows a warning listing exactly which fields to complete, and the submit step will not go through until they are filled. The wizard is the guided way to complete them.

## Listing statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **In review** | Submitted and waiting for approval by the 1club team. |
| **Listed** | Live on the marketplace and shown in search and browse. |
| **Link only** | Reachable by direct link, but hidden from marketplace search and browsing. |
| **Private** | Not on the marketplace. Your members, and people you invite, still find the gym in the 1club App. A gym that has never been listed is private. |

## Managing a listing

Before any gym is listed, the Marketplace page shows an introduction with **Start now**, **QR code** and **Learn more**. Once a gym has applied, the page lists each gym in your organization under **Your listings** as a card with its current status. Which actions you see depend on where the gym is:

- **In review** - **Withdraw application** pulls the application back. This takes effect immediately, no review needed; the gym returns to unlisted and you can resubmit later. **QR code** opens the gym's invite link and QR code.
- **Listed** or **Link only** - **Unpublish** takes the listing off the marketplace immediately, and **View Listing** opens the public page on 1club.me. **QR code** opens a code to print for your reception desk: members scan it to open your gym and book straight away on their phone, with no app to install, and what they book is recorded as **QR**. **Download SVG** saves it. If you later unpublish, codes you already printed keep working: they open your website, or your member portal if you have no website.
- **Private** (unpublished or never listed) - **QR code** opens the gym's invite link and QR code, and **Submit application** opens the wizard to complete the profile and send it for review.

To relist a gym after unpublishing, submit it again and it goes back through review. Resubmitting is treated as a fresh application - any earlier review decision is cleared.

### Changes to a live listing

Edits to a published gym's marketplace profile are reviewed before they replace what is live. While they wait, the gym's card shows **Changes awaiting review** and the listing keeps showing the approved information. If the 1club team declines them, the card shows **Changes not approved**. On the gym's own details page in Settings you can edit and save to submit again, or **Discard changes** to go back to what is published. Unpublishing a gym with changes awaiting review applies those changes right away, after a confirmation.

If a live gym loses a required profile field (address, phone number, email, description, or location), its details page in Settings shows **Marketplace profile incomplete** with what is missing. A listing removed because it no longer meets the requirements shows **Removed from the marketplace**: add the missing fields, then submit the gym again.

<Note>
  Viewing the Marketplace page needs marketing read access, but submitting, withdrawing, and unpublishing require the organization-management permission (`settings.manage.organization`). Read-only staff see the listings and their status but not the action buttons.
</Note>

## Worked example: listing a Pilates studio

A single-location reformer Pilates studio wants to appear on 1club.me:

1. The owner opens **Marketing** > **Marketplace**. Because there is only one gym, **Start now** launches the wizard directly.
2. In the wizard, the club type is Pilates/Yoga and the sports include pilates and reformer. Location is confirmed with a map pin, and the owner adds interior photos and the studio phone and email.
3. The description highlights small-group reformer classes and the amenities step lists showers, mat storage, and free parking.
4. On **Confirm & submit**, the studio moves to **In review**. The owner sees the "In review" status on the Marketplace card, with a **Withdraw application** option if plans change.
5. Once the 1club team approves it, the card flips to **Listed**, and **View Listing** opens the studio's public page at 1club.me, where prospective clients can browse classes and plans and book a trial.

## Tips & best practices

- **Finish the profile before you submit.** Address, phone, email, a real description, and a located map pin are all required; the submit step blocks until they are present.
- **Add photos even though they are optional.** Listings with a gallery get far more attention than bare ones.
- **Write the description for prospects, not staff.** Say what makes the gym worth visiting, in plain language.
- **List accurate amenities and sports.** They power the marketplace filters visitors use to find you.
- **Manage each gym separately.** In a multi-gym organization, submit and publish gyms independently as each one's profile becomes ready.
- **Use link only for soft launches.** A link-only listing is reachable by direct link but stays out of search until you are ready to be found.
- **Stay private if you only serve your own members.** Members-only gyms, kids academies and corporate gyms don't need a listing: your members already have the app, and the invite link brings in new people you choose.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A member can't find my private gym in the 1club App.

**Cause**: The app shows a private gym only to people the gym let in. Someone who has never booked with you, isn't on your member list with a verified email or phone, and hasn't opened your invite link sees nothing.

**Fix**: Send them your gym's invite link from the gym's **QR code** (**Copy link**), or check that the email or phone on their member record matches the one they sign in with.

**Issue**: An invite link I shared stopped working.

**Cause**: Someone reset the gym's invite link, or the link is more than a year old.

**Fix**: Open the gym's **QR code** again and share the current link.

**Issue**: The **Submit application** step fails and says the profile is incomplete.

**Cause**: One or more required fields (address, phone, email, description, or location) is missing.

**Fix**: Complete the fields named in the message. The wizard walks through each one; the location step needs both a city and a resolved map pin.

**Issue**: I do not see **Start now**, **Submit**, or **Unpublish** buttons.

**Cause**: Your account has marketing read access but not the organization-management permission.

**Fix**: Ask an owner or admin to grant `settings.manage.organization`, or have them perform the listing action.

**Issue**: My gym was published but disappeared from the marketplace.

**Cause**: A profile edit may have left a required field empty. A listing that becomes incomplete is pulled from the public network automatically.

**Fix**: Reopen **Marketing** > **Marketplace**, complete the fields flagged on the gym's card, and resubmit for review.

**Issue**: I unpublished a gym and now want it back.

**Cause**: Unpublishing is immediate and final for that listing; relisting goes back through review.

**Fix**: Click **Submit application** on the gym's card to resubmit. It re-enters **In review** as a fresh application.

**Issue**: My organization has several gyms but only one submitted itself.

**Cause**: Each gym is listed independently.

**Fix**: On the Marketplace page, use **Submit application** on each additional gym's card to send it through the wizard and review.
