The **Marketing** section is where you get found, turn visitors into contacts, and keep members coming back. It groups several standalone tools: your public website and the content and media behind it, the forms people sign up through, automations that follow up for you, your reviews, and your marketplace listing. Each tool works on its own, so you can adopt them one at a time.

Open **Marketing** from the left rail. It opens on **Website**, and the section column lists the other tabs.

## What's in here

The **Marketing** tabs, in order:

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **[Website](/marketing/website)** | Build, translate, and publish your public website with the section-based builder and AI assistant. |
| **[Automations](/marketing/automations)** | Trigger-driven workflows that message members, manage tags, and notify staff - including reminders and review requests. |
| **[Content](/marketing/content)** | Posts, how-to guides, FAQs, and reusable blocks shown on your website and member portal. |
| **[Media](/marketing/media)** | The shared library of images and videos used across your website, content, and emails. |
| **[Forms](/marketing/forms)** | Public signup pages that onboard new members, and questionnaires or health declarations for people you already know. |
| **[Reviews](/marketing/reviews)** | Member and Google reviews, with your coaching, facilities, and response-rate scores. |
| **[Marketplace](/marketing/marketplace)** (beta) | List your gym on the public 1club.me marketplace so new members can discover it. |

You only see the tabs your role allows. **Forms** is also open to roles that can read settings but not Marketing, so those roles see Marketing with **Forms** as its only tab.

## How the pieces fit together

**Website**, **Content**, and **Media** form your CMS. Media holds the images and videos, Content holds the posts, guides, FAQs, and blocks, and Website is where you assemble pages and publish them. Website sections pull live data too (plans, classes, instructors, reviews), so the site stays current without extra edits.

**Forms** and the website's contact form are how new people get onto your list. A signup form creates a contact, collects their documents, and can sell them a plan. A contact-form submission creates a lead you work on the [Leads](/members/leads) board.

**Automations** sit on top of the rest. An automation reacts to a trigger (a booking, a membership change, a failed payment, a birthday, a tag change) and sends a message, applies a tag, notifies staff, or mails a personal link to a form. The review-request automation feeds new reviews into **Reviews**.

**Reviews** and **Marketplace** shape how people who don't know you yet see your gym. Your 1club reviews show on your website and your marketplace listing; Google reviews show on your own website and portal only.

## Getting started

1. Build your website from a template on **Website** and publish your first release.
2. Create a signup form on **Forms** and link it from your website and social profiles.
3. Turn on the built-in booking reminder on **Automations**, and accept the review-request blueprint.
4. Write a few FAQs on **Content** for the questions your front desk answers every day.
5. Check **Reviews** monthly to see your scores and response rate.
