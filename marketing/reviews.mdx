**Reviews** lists every review members leave on 1club after a visit, beside your Google rating for each gym that has its Google listing linked. Above the list it shows your ratings, how many members you actually ask, and what reviewers say about your facilities, staff, amenities, value and access. Owners and managers read and reply to reviews, and members write, edit, and decide the visibility of their own.

## When to use it

Reviews bring in first visits: your rating and what reviewers say help new people find and pick you on 1club.me, in AI assistants and on Google. The page shows whether you are collecting enough of them, and what to fix before a member leaves.

Typical uses:

- **Check that you are asking enough people** - a padel gym with a low **Response rate** turns on the built-in review request, then watches the rate and the **Google conversion** card climb.
- **Find what lets the visit down** - a climbing gym sees under **What reviewers say** that most answers on **Amenities** are **Poor**, and reads the private notes to learn it is the showers.
- **Get found for what you do well** - a yoga studio sees it needs a few more answers before **Friendly staff** becomes a filter people search by on 1club.me.
- **Answer in public** - a jiu-jitsu gym replies to a three-star review about a crowded class, and the reply shows under the review on its network listing and website.
- **Flag an abusive review** - a yoga studio reports a review that names a member of staff, and 1club looks at it.

## How it works

### Who controls a review

A review belongs to the member who wrote it. Your gym cannot edit its text or rating, delete it, or hide it: the member chooses whether it is public when they submit it, and can change that later along with the rest of their review. This keeps ratings honest, because a gym cannot quietly remove the unfavorable ones.

What you can do is [reply in public](#replying-to-a-review) and [report a review](#reporting-a-review) that breaks policy.

### 1club reviews

Members leave these from the review link emailed after a visit or from the member portal's rate-your-visit dialog. They are tied to a member account, so the **Author** column shows the member's name and email. A review is **Verified** when it is tied to a visit 1club has a booking for.

A review has:

- **Stars** - the overall rating, 1 to 5. This is what your 1club rating averages.
- **An instructor rating** - for a session with a coach, the member can rate the coach they trained with.
- **Up to three review questions** - see below.
- **A comment** - the member's own words.
- **A private note** - optional, written beside the public review, and only your gym sees it.

### Review questions

After the stars, a review asks the member to rate up to three of five categories, with one tap each:

| Question                                                 | Answers                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Facilities and equipment**                             | Great, OK, Poor             |
| **Staff friendliness**                                   | Great, OK, Poor             |
| **Amenities** (changing rooms, showers, lockers, a café) | Great, OK, Poor, Didn't use |
| **Value for money**                                      | Great, OK, Poor             |
| **Parking and accessibility**                            | Great, OK, Poor             |

Every gym is asked the same five in the same words, so answers stay comparable; you can't add or edit them. Each review asks the three your gym has the fewest answers to, so answers spread across all five. Everyone is asked the same way whatever rating they gave. "Didn't use" is stored but never counts for or against you.

Once a question has **five** answers from reviews that count towards your rating (public, backed by a visit), your gym's page on 1club.me shows the leading answer with the count behind it, for example "Staff friendliness: Great, 41 of 46". It reads the latest 50 answers per question, so it changes only when someone reviews.

When at least **70%** of those answers agree on **Great**, your gym earns that question's trait, and people can find you by it: in 1club.me search filters, through AI assistants, and on city pages such as "padel clubs in Sofia with friendly staff". The five traits are **Well-kept facilities**, **Friendly staff**, **Great amenities**, **Good value** and **Easy parking and access**. A trait is never negative.

### Your Google rating

Link a gym to its Google listing on its page under **Settings** > **Clubs** with **Connect Google Place**. 1club then keeps that gym's **Google rating**: the star rating and the number of reviews as Google publishes them, with a link to the reviews on Google Maps.

1club never copies the reviews themselves - no text, author name or photo. The Google rating shows on the **Google rating** card and in a website [Reviews section](/marketing/website#content-and-media) set to **Google reviews**, always linking out to Google, and it never counts toward your 1club rating. To respect Google's terms, 1club refreshes the rating about weekly and never keeps it longer than 30 days. Read and reply to Google reviews on Google itself.

## What the page shows

Open **Marketing** > **Reviews**. The page opens with stat cards, then **What reviewers say**, then the list.

### Stat cards

Every card narrows with the list's filters, so filtering to one club or one instructor gives you their figures. A rating card shows a dash (`-`) when there is nothing to count, never a zero; **Recent Reviews** shows 0.

- **1club rating** - your average star rating out of 5 from reviews members left after a visit, with the number of reviews (and how many are verified, when not all are). This is the rating your network listing shows.
- **Google rating** - your rating on Google as Google publishes it, for gyms with a Google place linked. It is never mixed into your 1club rating.
- **Response rate** - 1club reviews against visits that actually happened in the last 12 months. It turns orange below 2%. A low rate means members are not being asked, not that they are unhappy.
- **Recent Reviews** - reviews in the last 30 days.
- **Google conversion** - shown once members have been invited to post on Google: the share who clicked through, with the counts. A click is all 1club can see; whether they post depends on their Google account.

### What reviewers say

A card with your review-question answers for one gym. With one gym it shows straight away; with several, filter the page to a club. It appears once someone has answered.

- On the left, each question with its leading answer, the count behind it (for example "41 of 46") and a score bar. **Not on your page yet** marks a question that doesn't have five counted answers.
- On the right, **Found in search for** lists the five traits: earned, how close you are (for example "62% · needs 70%"), or **Needs more answers**.

Answers from reviews a member kept private count here, for your own insight, but never on your public page.

### Review list

| Column           | What it shows                                                                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Author**       | The member's name and email                                                            |
| **Club**         | The gym the review is about; some show an area underneath                              |
| **Instructor**   | The instructor the review is about, if any                                             |
| **Content**      | The title and the start of the comment, with the private note marked when there is one |
| **Rating**       | 1 to 5 stars                                                                           |
| **Verified**     | **Verified** or **Unverified**                                                         |
| **Public**       | **Public** or **Private**, as the member chose                                         |
| **Published At** | The publish date, or the creation date                                                 |

Narrow the list with the filters drawer: **Club**, **Instructor**, **Rating** (exactly 1 to 5 stars) and **Source**. The search box matches review content. Filters and search are kept in the page address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared.

### A review's side panel

Click a review to open it. The panel shows who wrote it, the visit it is about, the stars and the instructor rating, every word of the comment, the **Review questions** they answered, the **Private note from the reviewer**, and when they **Clicked through to Google**. For a 1club review it also holds the [public reply](#replying-to-a-review).

## Replying to a review

You can answer a 1club review in public, once per review.

1. Click the review to open its side panel.
2. Under **Public reply**, type into **Write a public reply** (up to 2,000 characters).
3. Click **Post reply**.

The reply shows under the review, in your gym's name and with its date, wherever the review is published: the 1club network, your website and the member app. The member is messaged about your first reply, once. Saving again replaces the reply without messaging them again. To take it down, remove it and confirm **Remove this reply?**: it comes off your public pages, and the member keeps the message they already received.

Replying doesn't count as the member changing their review, so it never reopens a report 1club has ruled on.

## Reporting a review

Each row's action menu has **Report to 1club**.

1. Open the row's action menu and choose **Report to 1club**.
2. In **Why are you reporting it?**, explain how the review breaks policy (for example abusive language, naming a member of staff, or describing a different venue).
3. Click **Send report**.

The review stays visible while 1club decides; reporting is not a way to take one down. While the report is open, the action reads **Reported** and is disabled. Once 1club has ruled, you can report the review again if the member edits it.

## Collecting reviews

You collect reviews with [automations](/marketing/automations#built-in-automations). Every gym has the built-in **Review request**, on every plan. The morning after a visit, it emails the member a one-tap star rating; once they have rated it, it invites them to review you on Google too, when your Google profile is connected. It asks after the first visit, then every 10th, and at most once a month, whether or not you check members in, in each member's own language.

Turn it on from the intro on this page (**Ask members for reviews**), from the **Marketing** card on [Home](/home/overview), or in **Marketing** > **Automations**. There you can also change how often members are asked, the channels (email or WhatsApp), the instructor message style, and the Google invitation. Reword the email by customizing the **Review Request** [template](/settings/templates).

Visits booked on the 1club network or in the 1club App are asked by the **Network review request**, which 1club runs for every listed gym.

## Worked examples

### Padel club lifting its response rate

A padel club sees a **Response rate** of 1%, in orange, and realises nobody is being asked. It clicks **Ask members for reviews**, which switches on the built-in review request. Over the next month the rate climbs, and the **Google conversion** card shows how many reviewers clicked through to Google.

### Climbing gym fixing its amenities

Under **What reviewers say**, a climbing gym's **Amenities** answers lead with **Poor**. Filtering the list and opening a few reviews shows private notes about cold showers. The gym fixes the boiler, and over the following weeks the leading answer moves to **Great**.

### Yoga studio close to a trait

A yoga studio's **Friendly staff** trait reads "62% · needs 70%". Most of its reviewers answered **Great**, but it has only a few answers so far. Keeping review requests on is all it takes: more answers bring it past the line, and the studio starts showing up in 1club.me searches for friendly staff.

### Jiu-jitsu gym replying to a fair complaint

A member gives three stars because the Tuesday class was crowded. The owner opens the review, writes a short public reply thanking them and saying a second Tuesday class starts next month, and clicks **Post reply**. The member gets a message, and the reply sits under the review on the gym's network listing.

## Tips & best practices

- **Watch the response rate** - a low rate is a sign you are not asking, not a bad score. Turn on the built-in review request.
- **Read the private notes** - members say there what they won't say in public.
- **Reply to the critical ones** - a short, specific public reply reads better to the next visitor than silence.
- **Report, don't argue** - for a review that breaks policy, use **Report to 1club** with a clear reason. For one you simply disagree with, the better answer is more reviews from the members you serve well.
- **Leave the Google invitation open to everyone** - inviting only high ratings goes against Google's policy.

## Permissions

Viewing the list, cards and insights needs `marketing.read.club`. Replying and reporting need `marketing.update.club`. Turning on review requests needs `marketing.manage.club`.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: Google reviews are not appearing in the list.

**Solution**: That is expected. 1club keeps only your Google rating and review count, never the reviews themselves, so the list holds your 1club reviews alone. Read and reply to Google reviews on Google.

**Issue**: The **Google rating** card shows a dash.

**Solution**: The gym has no Google place linked, or it has not synced yet. Connect the gym to its Google listing on its page under **Settings** > **Clubs**.

**Issue**: A review is missing from the website but shows in the list.

**Solution**: The member set it to **Private**. Only the member can change that.

**Issue**: I want to remove or edit a review.

**Solution**: Gyms cannot edit, hide, or delete reviews. Reply in public, or, if it breaks policy, choose **Report to 1club** and explain why.

**Issue**: There is no **Public reply** on a review.

**Solution**: You don't have permission to reply. Replying needs `marketing.update.club`.

**Issue**: **What reviewers say** doesn't show.

**Solution**: Nobody has answered a review question yet, or you have more than one gym and the page isn't filtered to a club.

**Issue**: The **Google conversion** card is not showing.

**Solution**: It appears once at least one member has been invited to post on Google. Turn on the review request with **Enable Google review conversion**, and link the gym's Google place.

**Issue**: A card shows a dash instead of a figure.

**Solution**: There is nothing to count for the current filters. A dash means no data, not a score of zero.
