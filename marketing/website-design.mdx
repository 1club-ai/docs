Website design is how your website looks rather than what it says. The **Design** panel sets the look of the whole site - colors, fonts, corners, shadows, spacing and motion - and each section's **Design** tab sets how that one section sits on the page. Owners and managers use them to make the site look like their gym, not like every other site built on 1club.

## When to use it

A template gives you a finished design, and your Brand Book gives it your colors. Use the design controls when you want more than that, or less work than custom CSS.

Typical uses:

- **Try a different feel without rebuilding** - a boxing gym built from **Forge** tries the **Velocity** look, keeps its pages and copy, and only the fonts, colors and shapes change.
- **Make one section stand out** - a padel gym gives its closing call to action the **Dark band** background and a **Curve** top edge, so it reads as the end of the page.
- **Run a seasonal offer only for a while** - a yoga studio adds an offer section that shows only to **Visitors not signed in**, between two dates.
- **Keep the brand while changing the shape** - a CrossFit box squares off every corner and sets headings in capitals, and leaves its brand color alone.

## How it works

The look is built in layers, and each layer only changes what you set:

1. **Your Brand Book** - the logo, colors and fonts under **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Brand Book**. Every website starts from them.
2. **The website's Design panel** - overrides for this website only. Anything you leave **Inherited** follows the Brand Book, so the panel reads as "our brand, with these exceptions". Clearing a setting hands it back to the Brand Book.
3. **A section's Design tab** - how one section looks, inside the site's design.

Design changes go into the draft like every other edit. You see them in the preview, publish them when they are right, and can restore an earlier release if they are not.

Visitors who ask their phone or computer for less on-screen movement never see animation, whatever you pick.

## The Design panel

Open **Marketing** > **Website**, then **Configuration** > **Design**. The panel is grouped into **Template looks**, **Colours**, **Dark mode colours**, **Typography**, **Shape and surface** and **Motion**. Settings that inherit show **Inherited (...)** with the value they fall back to.

### Template looks

Every [website template](/marketing/website#start-from-a-template) offers its look: **Sage**, **Align**, **Forge**, **Kaizen**, **Highball**, **Baseline**, **Rally**, **Kitchen**, **Velocity** and **Essentials**. Click one and its colors, fonts, shape and motion fill the panel, so you can adjust it before saving ("Forge, but keep our heading font").

When you save a look, 1club asks **Use the ... look?**. With **Restyle all sections** ticked (the default), every section on every page also takes that template's layout and background. Words and photos stay. Untick it to change only the site-wide design.

Picking a look replaces the previous one. It keeps a brand color you chose unless that color would disappear on the new look's background, in which case this website takes the template's color instead; your Brand Book itself is never changed. Turn on **Always keep my brand colors** in the [Brand Book](/settings/organization#brand-book) if your colors must stay, even where they fade.

### Colours

Seven colors, each inheriting until you set it:

| Setting                | What it colors                                    | Inherits from                                  |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Brand colour**       | Buttons, links, the announcement bar, brand tints | Your Brand Book's primary color                |
| **Accent colour**      | Highlights and secondary emphasis                 | Your Brand Book's accent, else the brand color |
| **Page background**    | The page itself                                   | Your Brand Book's background, else white       |
| **Body text**          | Text on the page                                  | Your Brand Book's text color, else near-black  |
| **Cards**              | Card and panel surfaces                           | A light mix of the background and text         |
| **Secondary text**     | Captions, labels, quieter text                    | The body text at reduced strength              |
| **Lines and dividers** | Borders and rules                                 | The body text at low strength                  |

**Dark mode colours** offers the same seven for the dark theme. It shows only when the website allows dark mode (**Theme support** under **Configuration** > **Edit website**).

Every shaded section on the site - subtle bands, brand tints, the dark band - is mixed from these, so one color change re-tones the whole site.

### Typography

- **Body font** and **Heading font** - chosen from the fonts the site can load, each shown in its own typeface. The heading font falls back to the body font.
- **Heading size** - **Compact**, **Standard** or **Editorial**. Scales every heading, the hero's included, against your body text.
- **Heading weight** - **Regular**, **Medium**, **Semibold** or **Bold**.
- **Heading spacing** - **Tight**, **Normal** or **Wide** letter spacing.
- **Uppercase headings** - sets every heading in capitals without retyping them.

### Shape and surface

- **Corner radius** - **Sharp**, **Soft** or **Round**, for every corner on the site at once: section bands, photo frames, cards, form fields and buttons.
- **Card corners**, **Image corners** and **Input corners** - override the corner radius for one kind of element, for contrast.
- **Button shape** - **Pill**, **Rounded** or **Square**, and **Uppercase button text**. Applied to every button on every page, so they always match.
- **Shadow** - **None**, **Soft** or **Deep**.
- **Card style** - **As designed**, **Flat**, **Outlined**, **Raised** or **Glass**, for every card on the site.
- **Texture** - **None**, **Grain** (a fine grain over tinted sections) or **Lines** (faint guide lines and corner marks, a technical look that suits dark sites).
- **Line weight** - **Hairline**, **Standard** or **Bold**.
- **Spacing** - **Compact**, **Standard** or **Airy**: the room between sections and inside them. A section's own **Vertical spacing** still wins.

### Motion

- **Animation** - **None**, **Subtle** or **Lively**: how much sections move as they scroll into view.
- **Section entrance** - **Fade**, **Rise up**, **Zoom in**, **Slide in** or **Into focus**.
- **Cards arrive one by one** - cards in a section arrive one after another instead of all at once.
- **Count numbers up** - figures in a **Stats** section count up as visitors reach them.
- **Page transition** - **None**, **Fade** or **Rise up** between pages.
- **Card hover** - **None**, **Lift**, **Zoom photo** or **Glow** (in your brand color).
- **Button hover** - **Standard**, **Fill sweep** or **Lift**.

Photos load over a soft blurred preview on every site.

## A section's Design tab

Select a section in the editor and open its **Design** tab. Its settings are grouped into **Layout**, **Style** and **Visibility**, and behave the same on every content section. Which layouts a section offers depends on its type (for example **Comparison table** for **Plans**, **Masonry** for **Gallery**); the rest are shared:

| Setting                                      | Options                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Label above the title**                    | A short line over the section title, for example "Our coaches"                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Title size**                               | **Standard**, **Large** or **Display**                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Background**                               | **Page background**, **Subtle contrast**, **Raised card**, **Brand tint**, **Solid brand colour**, **Dark band** (light text on a dark surface tinted towards your brand), **Brand gradient** or **Frosted glass** |
| **Background photo** and **Photo darkening** | A photo behind the section, with a slider for how dark the layer over it is (45% by default)                                                                                                                       |
| **Width**                                    | **Page width**, **Edge to edge** or **Narrow, for reading**                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Vertical spacing**                         | **Standard**, **None**, **Tight** or **Generous**. Set two neighbouring sections to **None** to join them into one block                                                                                           |
| **Alignment**                                | **Left** or **Centered** heading and buttons                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Frame**                                    | **Band** (full width) or **Floating panel** (an inset, rounded island)                                                                                                                                             |
| **Overlap**                                  | **None**, or **Rise over the section above**                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Top edge**                                 | **Straight**, **Curve**, **Angle** or **Fade**                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Entrance**                                 | **Site default**, **None**, or one of the entrances above, for this section only                                                                                                                                   |
| **Hide on**                                  | **Never**, **Phones** or **Larger screens**                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Show to**                                  | **Everyone**, **Visitors not signed in** or **Signed-in members**                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Show from** / **Show until**               | Dates between which the section is shown                                                                                                                                                                           |

Sections that show photos (areas, instructors, galleries, calls to action, locations) also offer **Image shape**: **Natural size**, **Square**, **Landscape (4:3)**, **Wide (16:9)** or **Portrait (3:4)**, so a row of mismatched uploads still lines up. Buttons in a section can be **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Outlined**, **Text only** or **Arrow link**, and some sections offer a **Large** button size.

To set one word of a section title or the hero headline in your accent color, put it between asterisks: `Move *differently*`.

### Hero

The hero has the most design choices. Its **Style** is **Full-screen photo**, **Text and photo**, **Centered, photo below** or **Listing header** (a tiled gallery under the headline). The picker also offers starting points: **Photo banner**, **Full-screen photo**, **Introduction**, **Editorial** and **Listing header**.

With a full-screen photo:

- **Hero height** - **Short banner**, **Medium**, **Tall** or **Full screen**.
- **Text position** - **Centred**, **Bottom left** or **Bottom centre**.
- **Photo darkening** - a slider for the layer over the photo (70% by default), so a good photo can actually be seen.
- **Photo motion** - **Still** or **Slow zoom**. Several background photos crossfade as a slideshow; a video plays on its own.
- **Info card** - a frosted card over the hero showing the **Next class**, whether you are **Open now**, or your own **Custom** title, text and link.

On every hero:

- **Tagline** above the headline, or **Reviews tagline** to show your rating there.
- **Social proof** - a short line with **Proof faces** beside it, for example "Join 400 members".
- **Hero figures** - up to three figures as **Tiles** or **One line**. A figure can be a live figure that reads your data.

### Header and footer

The header and footer are edited like a section, from the top and bottom of the **Sections** list.

**Header:**

- **Header style** - **Full-width bar**, **Floating pill** or **Islands** (logo, menu and buttons as separate floating pieces).
- **On scroll** - **Stays as it is**, **Shrinks** or **Hides while scrolling down**.
- **Position** - **Stays on scroll**, or **Scrolls away** with the page, which suits a banner hero.
- **Menu alignment** - **Next to the logo**, **Centered** or **Next to the buttons**.
- **Dropdown menus** - **List of links** or **Photo tiles**, which show each sub-item's image and description.
- **Phone menu** - **Side drawer** or **Full screen**.
- A second button, such as "Request a tour", with its own **Button label** and **Button link**.
- **Navigation** - items with an optional set of **Sub-items** that open as a dropdown.

**Footer:**

- **Closing headline** and up to three buttons.
- **Link groups** and **Links**.
- **Club details** - **Every club**, **First club only**, **Selected only** or **Hidden**.
- **Documents** - which legal documents it links to.
- **Signup button label** and **Signup form**.
- **Newsletter signup** - an email box on every page, with its own title. A new address becomes a contact opted in to marketing email and enters the pipeline as a website lead. An address you already have, or one that unsubscribed, is left as it is.
- **Copyright line** and **Footer background** (**Subtle tint**, **Stronger tint** or **Dark band**).

## Ask the assistant

The website builder in the editor, and any AI assistant connected to 1club, can read and change the design directly: "make this feel more premium", "use a serif for headings", "square everything off", "give the pricing section a dark band". It leaves your brand color alone unless you ask, and its changes land in the draft. See [AI website builder](/marketing/website#ai-website-builder).

## Permissions

Viewing the Design panel needs `marketing.read.club`. Changing the design, or a section's **Design** tab, needs `marketing.manage.club`.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                                       | Likely cause                                                                        | Fix                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A design change doesn't show on the live site                 | Design changes are part of the draft                                                | Publish the website                                                           |
| The site still uses your old brand color after picking a look | The look kept your color, or your Brand Book has **Always keep my brand colors** on | Set **Brand colour** in the Design panel, or turn that Brand Book switch off  |
| Picking a look changed every section's background             | **Restyle all sections** was ticked                                                 | Restore the previous release, or reapply the look with it unticked            |
| **Dark mode colours** is missing                              | The website is set to **Light only**                                                | Change **Theme support** under **Edit website**                               |
| Sections don't animate                                        | **Animation** is **None**, or the visitor's device asks for less motion             | Set **Animation** to **Subtle** or **Lively**; the device setting always wins |
| A section shows in the preview but not live                   | Its **Hide on**, **Show to** or date settings exclude the current visitor           | Check the section's **Visibility** group                                      |
