The Website builder runs your gym's public site without leaving the admin. A site is made of pages, and each page is made of sections you can add, reorder, and configure. Most gyms run one site, and you can add more. Owners and managers build and publish the site, and visitors and members browse, book, and get in touch through it.

<Note>
  The public website is a **plan add-on**. It's included on the Grow plan,
  available as a paid add-on on the Run plan, and unavailable on the Free plan.
  Manage it under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription**. While the Website
  add-on is inactive, your public site is replaced by a simple unbranded holding
  page rather than returning an error.
</Note>

## When to use it

The site stays accurate without extra work: many sections pull live data from the platform (your plans, classes, instructors, locations, reviews), so they change as your operations change. Edits live in a draft, you publish them as a release, and you can restore any previous release.

Typical uses:

- **Let visitors book from your site** - a padel gym adds the **Book online** section so visitors see live availability, pick a slot, and pay without leaving the site.
- **Keep your timetable and prices current** - a yoga studio uses the **Classes** and **Plans** sections instead of pasting its schedule, so the site always shows what is set up in 1Club.
- **Turn visitors into leads** - the contact form creates a lead contact for a new visitor and an inbound message your team replies to from the inbox.
- **Redesign safely** - publish a release before a big round of edits, then restore it if the new version doesn't work out.

## How the site is structured

A site has three layers:

- **Site** - holds its web address, design, features, SEO defaults, custom CSS, and the list of pages. An organization can have several sites (see [More than one website](#more-than-one-website)).
- **Page** - a route on your site (`/`, `/about`, `/classes`, etc.). Each page has its own SEO metadata and an ordered list of sections.
- **Section** - a building block on a page (hero, plans, instructors, contact form, gallery). Every section has a **type** that determines what it renders, plus configurable fields.

Every page also includes the site's shared **header** and **footer** sections, so navigation and footer links do not have to be set page-by-page.

## Pages

Pages are free-form. You decide what to add. A new site from the template starts with Home, Classes, Book, Instructors, Blog, About, and Contact, and you can add up to 30 pages per site.

Each page has:

- **Title** shown in browser tabs and (by default) as the SEO title.
- **Slug** URL segment. The home page lives at `/`; everything else lives at `/<slug>`.
- **SEO** **Meta title**, **Meta description**, and **Social image URL**. The meta title and description can be translated per language; the image is shared across languages.
- **Hide from search engines** keeps the page out of Google and your sitemap. It stays reachable to anyone with the link. Use it for thank-you and campaign landing pages.
- **Sections** the content of the page, in order.

To add a page, open the website editor and use **Add new page**. To remove one, open its **Page Details** tab and choose **Delete page**; links to it will need to be updated manually.

## Sections

Sections are the building blocks. Pick from the catalog when you add a section to a page: it is grouped into categories, has a **Search sections** box, and marks some types **Recommended**. If the site has no booking section yet, the picker suggests adding **Book online**. Each section has its own properties panel.

### Layout

- **Header** - your site's top bar: site name, logo, primary navigation, optional Login/Sign-up buttons. Navigation supports flat items and nested dropdowns.
- **Footer** - bottom of every page: link groups, contact information, social icons, and legal links.

### Hero and calls to action

- **Hero** banner with a headline, subheadline, one or more CTA buttons, and optional imagery. Four layout variants: **Full-screen photo** (full-width image or video behind the headline), **Text and photo** (copy on the left, image on the right), **Centered, photo below** (copy centered, full-width image below), and **Listing header** (Airbnb-listing style: headline left, CTA right, image gallery below). The hero accepts a list of background images: **Full-screen photo** and **Text and photo** crossfade through them as a slideshow; **Listing header** renders them as a tiled gallery. An optional tagline or reviews tagline (for example "4.8 on Google") can sit above the headline.
- **CTAs** focused call-to-action section with title, supporting text, one or more buttons, and an optional image.
- **Quote** centred testimonial or pull quote attributed to a staff member.
- **Stats** row of headline numbers ("10 years", "500 members", "4.9 stars") for social proof.

### Live data sections

These pull from the platform automatically. There is nothing to sync; they always reflect what is currently set up in 1Club.

- **Plans** your published [membership plans](/sales/plans), rendered as pricing cards with a CTA to sign up.
- **Classes** class schedule, optionally limited to selected class types.
- **Events** your live [events](/events/events), as a featured-events rail (default) or a full list; configurable title, subtitle, and card limit. Self-hides when there is nothing to show. New template sites include it on the home page.
- **Calendar** unified booking calendar combining areas and instructors; visitors can click through to book.
- **Book online** visitors see live availability, pick a slot, and pay without leaving your site. The older **Book** section is no longer offered for new sections.
- **Event** spotlight a single [event](/events/events).
- **Tournaments** your tournaments.
- **Instructors** staff directory. Choose **Compact grid**, **Photo cards**, or **Full details**.
- **Instructor** spotlight a single instructor, picked from your instructors list.
- **Areas** bookable spaces or facilities, each linking to its booking page.
- **Clubs** location cards with map for organizations with multiple gyms.
- **Club** spotlight a single gym with its address, hours, and map.
- **Location** alternative compact location card.
- **Products** items from your product catalog (day passes, merch).
- **Reviews** Google reviews (from the gym's Google connection, set on each club under **Settings** > **Clubs**) or 1Club [reviews](/marketing/reviews), displayed as cards or a carousel.
- **News** list of posts from your CMS (slug `/news/<post-slug>` for each).
- **FAQ** accordion of questions and answers. Filter by tag if you keep separate FAQ topics.

### Content sections

- **Gallery** grid of images or videos picked from your [Media library](/marketing/media).
- **Block** a reusable content block (rich text managed under [Marketing > Content](/marketing/content)) referenced by slug. Use this for legal text, "About us" paragraphs, or any chunk of copy you want to reuse on multiple pages.
- **Columns** multi-column layout that nests other sections side-by-side. Use it to put a hero next to a contact form, or stats next to a quote.

### Forms

- **Contact form** lead-capture form. You choose the fields to show (name and email are always on; phone, date of birth, gender, category, subject, message are optional). It also supports any custom fields you have defined under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**. Each submission creates a **lead** contact in your CRM and an inbound message you can reply to from the inbox.

### Section anatomy

Under the hood every section is the same simple shape: a **type**, a **config**, and optional **translations**.

- **Type** decides what the section renders (`hero`, `plans`, `gallery`, and so on) and which fields it exposes.
- **Config** holds the section's settings - text, numbers, dropdowns, image references, and structured lists.
- **Translations** store per-locale overrides for the translatable text, keyed by field. Complex lists (nav items, CTA buttons, footer link groups) carry stable per-item IDs, so translations survive when you reorder items.

Two config fields appear on most content sections and are worth knowing:

- **Background tone** (**Page background**, **Subtle contrast**, **Raised card**, **Brand tint**, **Dark band**) sets the section's background. **Brand tint** tints it with a subtle wash of your brand color; use it to make one section stand out without hardcoding colors.
- **Size** (`content`, `full`, or `narrow`) controls width: `content` constrains to a centered max-width, `full` runs edge to edge, and `narrow` keeps it tighter still.

Layout section types (`header`, `footer`) live once at the site level and appear on every page. Content section types live in each page's ordered list. The editor keeps these separate for you - you will not see a hero offered as a header, or a footer offered inside a page. This same config schema drives both the manual property panels and the AI builder, so anything you can set by hand, the assistant can set too.

## The editor

Open **Marketing** > **Website** to launch the editor. **Website** is the first Marketing tab and the page Marketing opens on. The editor drops the page header to give you room, but keeps the Marketing column beside it, so the other Marketing tabs stay one click away. It has two regions:

- **Live preview** your site renders in the main area and updates as you edit. Switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** sizes. Clicking a section in the preview selects it in the editor.
- **Editor sidebar (right)** with three tabs: **Sections** lists every section on the current page, in order, with each section's fields (text, numbers, dropdowns, images, lists); **Page Details** holds the page's title, slug, and SEO; **Agent** is the AI website builder.

Each section's ⋮ menu offers its **ID** (an anchor you can link to), **Translations**, **Duplicate**, **Move up**, **Move down**, and **Delete**.

The **Configuration** menu in the editor header holds the site-wide settings: **Edit website** (name, web address, default website, online switch), **Features** (such as user authentication, the AI assistant, a booking card, an announcement bar, and legal documents with cookie consent), **Design** (presets and theme choices), **SEO**, **CSS**, **Take website offline** / **Bring website online**, and **Delete website**.

Behavior you can rely on:

- **Auto-save.** Edits save automatically a moment after you stop typing.
- **Image picking.** Image fields open the [Media library](/marketing/media). Upload new images from the same dialog; they are stored in your media library for reuse.
- **Publish state.** **Publish** is enabled only when your draft has changes that aren't live yet.

The **Agent** tab is the AI website builder, covered in full below.

## AI website builder

The AI website builder is an assistant that edits your site by chatting with it. Ask it to "add a pricing page", "make the hero more energetic", "add an FAQ about parking", or "translate the whole site into Spanish", and it makes the changes for you the same way you would in the editor.

What it can do:

- **Build and edit pages and sections.** It adds pages, adds or removes sections at a chosen position, and merges new settings into an existing section.
- **Write copy grounded in your data.** It reads your real organization facts - name, description, clubs, cities, amenities, sports, plans, branding - and writes headlines and body copy from them rather than inventing awards or history.
- **Pick images from your library.** It searches your [Media library](/marketing/media) by name (for example "pool" or "reformer") and sets the match on a hero or CTA. It never uploads on your behalf, so it only uses images you already have.
- **Manage layout, content, and CSS.** It can update the header and footer, create or edit reusable content items (blocks, FAQs, posts), and adjust custom CSS using theme tokens (never hardcoded colors).
- **Translate.** It fills missing translations per section across your locales.
- **Undo.** It keeps a short history of its own changes so you can revert the last one.

What it will not do:

- **It only edits the draft.** Like every edit in the builder, the assistant's changes stay in the draft. Nothing goes live until *you* publish a release.
- **It cannot publish, reset, or delete the site.** Those actions are yours alone, from the UI.
- **It respects the layout-versus-content rule.** It will not put a header inside a page or a hero into the site layout, and it always sets required fields (for example, a hero's variant).

### Starter content generation

When you first create a site from a template, the builder seeds it with copy written from your organization's stored facts: a home hero headline, a hero subheadline, and an About-page intro, generated in your default language. If generation is unavailable, the site falls back to sensible default copy. Either way, a brand-new site reads like *your* site from day one instead of showing generic placeholders you have to overwrite.

## Theming and branding

The website uses your **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Branding** as the source of truth (logo, favicon, primary color, fonts). The site adds a few extras on top:

- **Header / dark color overrides** for tuning contrast.
- **Theme support** light only, dark only, or both (with a user-facing theme switcher). Pick the **default theme** when you allow both.
- **Custom CSS** site-level CSS for fine-tuning, under **Configuration** > **CSS**. Lives on the release, so it is versioned with the rest of the site. It is limited to 50KB and can't use `@import`.

## Navigation

The header section configures your top navigation:

- **Nav items** label + URL. Items can have **children** to render as a dropdown menu.
- **Site name / logo** sourced from branding by default; override per site if needed.
- **Login / Sign-up buttons** appear automatically when [authentication](/settings/overview) is enabled for the site.

The footer section configures footer links, grouped into categories (e.g. _Company_, _Programs_, _Legal_) plus contact info, social links, and legal links.

## Multi-language

A site can be published in several languages.

- The languages come from your organization's supported languages and default language (see [Organization](/settings/organization)).
- Each translatable field on a section or page (headline, subtitle, CTA label, meta title) has per-locale values.
- To translate a section, open its ⋮ menu, choose **Translations**, and use **Auto Translate** to fill it from the default language with AI. You can also ask the **Agent** to translate the site. Review the suggested translations before publishing.
- Visitors see a language switcher in the header when more than one language is published.

## SEO and metadata

Per page: meta title (defaults to the page title), meta description, social image, and **Hide from search engines**. The meta title and description can be translated per locale.

**Configuration** > **SEO** scores the site, lists what to fix in the draft, and can measure the published site with Google Lighthouse (**Measure again** reruns it). **Fix my SEO** writes every missing site and page description with AI from your own gym details and page content, without replacing anything you wrote yourself.

Site-wide, the site automatically serves a `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`. Canonical URLs are generated from your domain and page slugs.

## Analytics and tracking

Tracking is set up under **Settings** > **Integrations**: [Google Tag Manager](/settings/integrations/google-tag-manager) and the [Meta Pixel](/settings/integrations/meta).

Tracking can be gated behind cookie consent categories. _Necessary_ scripts run always; _analytics_ and _marketing_ scripts only run after the visitor accepts the matching category in the cookie banner.

## Cookies and legal

Built into the site:

- **Cookie banner** with category-level consent (necessary / analytics / marketing).
- **Legal documents** privacy policy, terms, and cookie policy. Write them as documents under [Settings > Bookings > Waivers](/settings/waivers); the site links to them by slug.

## Domain and hosting

- Every site has a free 1club address built from its **Web address**, set under **Configuration** > **Edit website**. Changing it moves the site: the old address stops working immediately and nothing redirects from it.
- You can attach a **custom domain** under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Domains**; point your DNS to 1Club following the on-screen instructions.
- HTTPS is provisioned automatically.

### Holding page

The public website is a plan add-on. If the add-on becomes inactive while you still have published content or a custom domain pointed at 1Club, visitors do not see an error or a 404. Instead, 1Club serves a generic, unbranded **holding page** that says the site is temporarily unavailable. It protects your public reputation, avoids leaking 1Club branding, and disappears the moment the add-on is active again - your live site returns exactly as it was, from the last active release. Reactivate the add-on under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription** to bring the site back.

## Publishing and releases

Edits do not go live until you publish.

1. **Draft** everything you change in the editor is stored as the working draft.
2. **Publish** updates the live release in place with your draft. Use this for typo fixes, small copy tweaks, or routine content updates. No new version is created.
3. **Releases** open it from the arrow next to **Publish**. It shows the **Draft** and the release history, with the live one marked **Live**. **Publish** there creates a new versioned release, which is what you want before bigger changes you may want to undo cleanly. **Restore** makes an earlier release the live one again.

To stop the site being visible without losing anything, use **Configuration** > **Take website offline**: visitors get a placeholder, while every page, version, and setting is kept and you can still edit and preview. **Bring website online** reverses it.

**Delete website** removes the site, its pages, and its published versions after you type a confirmation phrase. A copy is kept for a retention period, during which you can **Restore** it. Deleting needs the `marketing.delete.organization` permission.

### More than one website

Use **Add website** in the site picker to create another site, for example a shop. A second website shares your members, plans, and classes; only the pages and design are separate. Under **Configuration** > **Edit website**, **Default website** picks the one the organization's own address, its booking and invoice emails, and the member portal's legal pages point at. Exactly one website is the default.

## Forms and leads

Contact-form submissions are first-class CRM events. Each submission:

- Creates a **contact** in 1Club, entered into the pipeline as a lead, including any custom fields the form collected.
- Posts an **inbound message** to your staff inbox, so someone can reply.
- Is de-duplicated by email: if a contact with that email already exists, the message is attached to that contact without changing their name or other details, and no new lead is created.

From there you can convert the lead into a member, work it on the [Leads](/members/leads) board, tag them for [smart segmentation](/settings/smart-tagging), or send them a [promotion](/sales/promotions).

## Tips

Start from defaults, then prune. Add the prebuilt pages, delete the ones you do not need, and edit the rest. Faster than building from scratch.

Use live-data sections for anything that changes. Do not paste your class schedule into a hero; use the **Classes** or **Calendar** section so updates flow automatically.

Lead with one strong CTA per page. Hero CTA plus one or two supporting CTAs is plenty; more dilutes the action you want visitors to take.

Use Columns to break monotony. Pair a stats block with a quote, or a hero with a contact form, instead of stacking full-width sections everywhere.

Publish a release before big changes. Create a release from **Releases** first; if the next round of edits goes sideways you can restore it instantly.

Translate after you stabilize copy. Running **Auto Translate** early means redoing it when you keep tweaking the English. Lock the source copy, then translate.

Wire the contact form to a tag. Tag new leads from the website so [automations](/marketing/automations) and [promotions](/sales/promotions) can find them.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edits don't show on the live site | Changes are saved to the draft, not published | Click **Publish**, or create a new release from **Releases** |
| **Publish** is disabled | There are no unpublished changes | Nothing to do: the live site already matches the draft |
| **Publish** opens "Website add-on required" | Publishing and restoring require an active **Website** add-on | Activate or renew the add-on under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription**, then publish again |
| Visitors see a placeholder and the editor says the site is offline | The site was taken offline | Use **Configuration** > **Bring website online** |
| Public site shows a plain "temporarily unavailable" page | The Website add-on is inactive, so the holding page is served | Reactivate the add-on under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription**; your last active release returns automatically |
| A live-data section is empty or missing | There is no data to show yet (no published plans, no live events) | Publish the underlying records; sections like **Events** self-hide until there is something to show |
| New languages show English text | Translations were not filled after you changed the source copy | Run **Auto Translate** on the section, review the results, then publish |
| A plans link points somewhere odd | Plans always link to `/plans`; there is no `/pricing` page | Leave plan CTAs pointing at `/plans` and do not create a `/pricing` route |
