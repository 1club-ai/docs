The Website builder runs your gym's public website without leaving the admin. You start from a template made for your kind of business, and 1club fills it with your own classes, plans, coaches, areas, prices, reviews and photos. Owners and managers build and publish the site, and visitors and members browse, book, pay and get in touch through it.

<Note>
  The public website is a **plan add-on**. It's included on the Grow plan,
  available as a paid add-on on the Run plan, and unavailable on the Free plan.
  Manage it under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription**. You can
  build and preview a site without it, but publishing needs it. While the add-on
  is inactive, your public site is replaced by a simple unbranded holding page
  rather than returning an error.
</Note>

## When to use it

The site stays accurate without extra work: most sections read live data from 1club (plans, classes, instructors, clubs, areas, events, reviews), so they change when your operations change. Edits live in a draft, you publish them as a release, and you can restore any earlier release.

Typical uses:

- **Take bookings on your own domain** - a padel gym adds the **Book online** section so visitors see live availability, pick a slot and pay without leaving the site.
- **Look like a premium club on day one** - a yoga studio picks the **Sage** template, and AI rewrites its sample copy around the studio's own classes and prices before anyone sees it.
- **Tell everyone about a closure** - a climbing gym turns on the announcement bar for "The hall is closed this Sunday", and the line sits above the navigation on every page.
- **Run a second site** - a gym that sells kit opens a separate shop website with its own pages and design, sharing the same members and products.

## How it works

A website has three layers:

- **Website** - its name, web address, design, features, SEO defaults, custom CSS and its pages. An organization can have several websites (see [More than one website](#more-than-one-website)).
- **Page** - a route on the site (`/`, `/about`, `/plans`). Each page has its own SEO settings and an ordered list of sections. A website holds up to 30 pages.
- **Section** - a building block on a page: a hero, your plans, a timetable, a contact form. Every section has a **type** that decides what it shows, a **Content** tab and a **Design** tab.

Every page also shows the site's shared **header** and **footer**, so navigation and footer links are set once.

Everything you change - pages, sections, design, CSS - goes into the **draft**. Visitors only see a change once you publish. The exceptions are the website's name, web address, club and its **Website is online** switch, which apply as soon as you save them.

The look of the site is covered on its own page: see [Website design](/marketing/website-design) for the **Design** panel, template looks, and each section's **Design** tab.

## Start from a template

Every website starts from a template. Open **Marketing** > **Website**: with no website yet, click **Browse templates**. With a website already, open the website picker in the editor header and choose **Add website**. Both open **Website templates**.

Each template is a complete site designed for one kind of business, with its pages, sections and design:

| Template       | Made for             | Style                                            |
| -------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sage**       | Yoga studios         | Calm, cream and sage, soft photos                |
| **Align**      | Pilates studios      | Editorial, linen and olive, elegant serif        |
| **Forge**      | Gyms                 | Black and white, grey ground, dark grainy photos |
| **Kaizen**     | Martial arts schools | Off-white, fighting red, bold headings           |
| **Highball**   | Climbing gyms        | Dark, hot orange, condensed capitals             |
| **Baseline**   | Tennis               | Bright, white, ball lime and court blue          |
| **Rally**      | Padel                | Light, court green, rounded and friendly         |
| **Kitchen**    | Pickleball           | Dark, gold and salmon, huge capitals             |
| **Velocity**   | Sports centers       | Dark, lime accent, bold capitals                 |
| **Essentials** | Any business         | Neutral, your brand colors                       |

Each card shows the template's home page; hover it to scroll down the design. If you run more than one gym, a **Club** picker above the gallery builds the website for one gym: the site is bound to that gym and its copy talks about that gym only. Leave it empty for a website about your whole organization.

### What happens when you pick one

Click a template's cover or **Use template**. 1club builds the website while you watch (**Building your website**), which usually takes under a minute, then opens it in the editor:

1. The template's pages, sections and design are laid down.
2. Where the template shows a sample list beside live data (sample programs, sample courts), your own programs or areas replace it when you have any.
3. AI rewrites every line of sample copy for your business, from your gym details, in your website's languages. It writes in the voice set in your [Brand Book](/settings/organization#brand-book) if you have one.
4. Your own photos replace the template's stock photos where you have them.

**Claims your data can't back up stay off the site.** A rewritten line with a number, an opening time or a length of time that your data doesn't contain is left out, and so is a section that ends up empty. If the AI writer can't run, the site is built with plain wording instead, and nothing about the template's sample business reaches it.

The new website is **offline** and named after the template. The first time you publish it, 1club asks whether to **Publish and bring it online** or **Publish and keep it offline**. Your first website takes your organization's web address; a later one gets its own address, which you can change under **Edit website**.

### Trying another template

To compare templates, choose **Add website** again and pick another. If a website you built from a template hasn't been touched since, 1club asks what should happen to it:

- **Replace with ...** removes the untouched website once the new one is built. The new one becomes your default website and takes your organization's address. Only staff who can delete websites are asked; others simply get the new site added.
- **Keep ... too** keeps both.

Picking the same template again for the same gym reopens the website you already built instead of building another. Any edit, save or publish marks a website as touched, so your own work is never replaced.

### After the website is built

The site is now an ordinary website: change anything in the editor, or ask the AI website builder. The builder knows what is left to do - stock photos still in place, sample copy, sections hidden for lack of data, links to pages that don't exist - and can work through it with you. Ask it "What's left to finish on my website?".

To take another template's look without changing your pages and content, use the template looks in the **Design** panel (see [Website design](/marketing/website-design#template-looks)).

## The editor

Open **Marketing** > **Website**. The editor has two regions:

- **Live preview** - your site renders in the main area and updates as you edit. Switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile**. Click a section in the preview to select it.
- **Editor sidebar (right)** - the **Sections** tab lists every section on the current page, in order. The **Page Details** tab holds the page's title, slug and SEO.

The header carries the website picker, the page picker, **Preview**, **Publish** with **Releases** beside it, and **Configuration**.

Selecting a section opens its two tabs:

- **Content** - what it shows: titles, text, images, buttons, which records it lists.
- **Design** - how it looks: layout, background, spacing, width, entrance and when it shows. See [A section's Design tab](/marketing/website-design#a-sections-design-tab).

Each section's ⋮ menu offers its **ID** (an anchor you can link to), **Translations**, **Duplicate**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete**.

Behavior you can rely on:

- **Auto-save.** Edits save to the draft a moment after you stop typing.
- **Image picking.** Image fields open the [Media library](/marketing/media). Images you upload there are kept in the library for reuse.
- **Text.** Text-heavy fields such as content blocks use the same editor as the rest of 1club: one toolbar for text style, bold, italic, links, lists, alignment and an **Insert** menu. Pasting from Google Docs, Word or a web page keeps headings, lists, bold, italic and links.
- **Publish state.** **Publish** is enabled only when the draft has changes that aren't live yet.
- **Offline notice.** While the website is offline, the editor says so above the preview: you are the only one who can see it.

## Pages

To add a page, use **Add new page** in the page picker. Each page has:

- **Title** - shown in browser tabs and, by default, as the search title.
- **Slug** - the URL segment. The home page lives at `/`; every other page at `/<slug>`.
- **SEO** - **Meta title**, **Meta description** and **Social image URL**. The meta title and description can be translated per language; the image is shared by all languages.
- **Hide from search engines** - keeps the page out of Google and your sitemap. It stays reachable to anyone with the link. Use it for thank-you and campaign landing pages.

To remove a page, open its **Page Details** tab and choose **Delete page**. Links to it from other pages need updating by hand.

## Sections

Add a section with **Add section** (or the insert control between two sections). The picker groups section types, has a **Search sections** box that understands everyday words ("timetable", "reservations", "testimonials"), and marks **Book online** as **Recommended**. Many types offer named starting points, such as **Inline nudge** or **Photo banner**, so the section arrives already set up. If your website has no booking section yet, the editor suggests adding **Book online** to the page.

### Bookings and schedule

- **Book online** - the full booking experience on your own site: areas, sessions with a coach and group activities, with live availability, real prices and checkout. Visitors pay by card, from their wallet or against their membership, and guests can book without an account where your [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) allows it. Members see their own bookings on the same calendar. A multi-location gym gets a location picker, a gym with one area sees a week at a glance, and on a phone the calendar becomes a list of times. Narrow it to one sport or one club if you want.
- **Calendar** - a live availability calendar for just the **Area types** or **Instructor types** you pick, for a page about one facility or one coach. **Area type grouping** shows one calendar or a calendar per type.
- **Classes** - the class timetable, optionally limited to class types, programs or one club. **View** is **Day by day** or **Week grid**. The timetable remembers the club a visitor picked.
- **Events** - your live [events](/events/events), as featured events or **All events**. Hides itself when there is nothing to show.
- **Event** - one event's spotlight with its cover, date, place and price.

The older **Book** section is no longer offered for new sections; existing ones keep working.

### Club and team

- **Clubs** - your locations as cards, each linking to its own page.
- **Club** - one location with its photo, details and map.
- **Location** - your locations with the full address, phone and email spelled out.
- **Programs** - your programs (Kids BJJ, Adult Muay Thai) as cards, linking to the timetable filtered to each.
- **Areas** - your bookable areas, each linking to its page and calendar.
- **Instructors** - your instructors, as a compact grid, photo cards, portrait cards or full details, optionally filtered by instructor type. **Type tags** let visitors pick a type.
- **Instructor** - one instructor with bio, photo gallery and booking calendar.

Clubs, programs, areas, instructors and news can also show as a swipeable carousel, or as any of the shared card layouts (photo tiles, title on photo, bento grid, index rows, stacking cards, interactive showcase).

### Plans and shop

- **Plans** - your published [plans](/sales/plans) with prices. **Layout** is **Cards**, **Pricing tiers**, **Comparison table**, **Price list** or **Price breakdown cards**; **List features on each card** adds each plan's included features. Limit it to plan categories or one club. Every plan also has its own page on your site, with what is included and the other plans in its category.
- **Products** - items from your product catalog.

### Content and media

- **Hero** - the opening banner of a page: headline, subheadline, up to three buttons, and a background photo, slideshow or video. Its options are covered in [Website design](/marketing/website-design#hero).
- **Highlights** - photo cards for experiences, spaces and amenities, with a figure and points beside each where you want them.
- **Gallery** - photos and videos from your [Media library](/marketing/media), as a mosaic, carousel, masonry, or a statement with photos.
- **Scrolling band** - short phrases that scroll by between two sections, as a flat band or angled tape.
- **Logos** - press, partner or sponsor logos in a row or scrolling, grey until hovered.
- **News** - your latest posts from [Content](/marketing/content), each with its own page at `/news/<slug>`.
- **Stats** - a row of figures ("10 years", "500 members"). A figure can be a **Live figure** that reads your data: number of areas, instructors, programs or reviews, or your average rating.
- **League table** - your teams or players in order, with their scores. You keep it up to date.
- **Quote** - a large pull-quote attributed to a member of staff.
- **Reviews** - your **Reviews on 1club** or **Google reviews**, as cards, a carousel, a scrolling strip, one large quote, scrolling columns or pinned notes. Pin chosen reviews with **Pinned reviews**. See [Reviews](/marketing/reviews).
- **FAQ** - questions and answers from [Content](/marketing/content), filtered by tags, as a list or **A tab per tag**.

### Contact and conversion

- **CTAs** - a call to action with up to three buttons. Starting points: **Closing ask**, **Showcase**, **Inline nudge**, **Offer card**, **Photo poster** and **Two large links**.
- **Contact form** - see [Contact and tour forms](#contact-and-tour-forms).

### Advanced

- **Columns** - places other sections side by side.
- **HTML Block** - shows a **Block** from [Content](/marketing/content) by its slug, so one piece of copy can appear on several pages.
- **Page navigation** - a row of links to the sections on the page that sticks under the header and highlights the one being read. It builds itself from your section titles.
- **Tournaments** - your tournaments with their draw or standings.

A live-data section with nothing to show is left off the public site. The editor's preview still draws it so you can see where it sits.

### Contact and tour forms

The **Contact form** section turns visitors into leads. Choose:

- **Form purpose** - **Contact** for a general enquiry, or **Tour request**. A tour request asks which club the visitor wants to see (when you have more than one), a preferred day and a time of day. It files the message under that club with the subject "Tour request" and tags the contact so staff can find everyone waiting to be shown around. Nothing is reserved: someone on your team follows up.
- **Form fields** - for a contact form, **Category**, **Subject** and **Message**.
- **Contact fields** - **Phone**, **Date of birth** and **Gender**, beside the name and email that are always asked.
- **Custom fields** - any [custom fields](/settings/contacts#custom-fields) you defined for contacts.
- **Layout** - **Form only**, or **Details beside the form**, which shows the club's address, phone, email, opening hours and whether it is open now next to the form.

Each submission:

- Creates a **contact** entered into the pipeline as a lead, with any custom fields it collected.
- Posts an **inbound message** to your [Inbox](/inbox/messages), so someone can reply.
- Is matched by email: if a contact with that email already exists, the message is attached to them without changing their details, and no new lead is created.

From there you can work the lead on the [Leads](/members/leads) board, tag it for [smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging), or start an [automation](/marketing/automations).

## Features

**Configuration** > **Features** holds what the site does beyond showing its pages. Each feature is a switch; its settings appear once it is on. Changing them needs permission to manage marketing.

| Feature                              | What it does                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **User authentication**              | Visitors can sign in and see member-only pages. Adds **Login** and sign-up buttons to the header.                |
| **AI Assistant**                     | Adds a chat launcher that answers visitors' questions.                                                           |
| **Legal documents & cookie consent** | Shows the cookie consent banner and links to your policy documents.                                              |
| **Announcement bar**                 | One line above the navigation, on every page.                                                                    |
| **Booking card**                     | A floating card that invites visitors to book, on every page once they start scrolling. Desktop and tablet only. |
| **Phone action bar**                 | Up to three buttons pinned to the bottom of the screen on phones, on every page.                                 |
| **Back to top button**               | A round button that takes visitors back to the top of long pages.                                                |
| **Popup**                            | An offer or announcement in a dialog, shown once in a while.                                                     |

### Announcement bar

Use it for the one thing a visitor has to know before anything else: a closure, a deadline, a launch.

- **Announce** - **A sentence** you write, or **A post**, **A class** or **An event**. A post, class or event gives the bar its title and a "Read more" link to its page, and the bar retires itself once the class or event is over. A record that is no longer public (an unpublished post, a cancelled class) empties the bar rather than breaking it.
- **Announcement text** - the sentence itself, or an optional lead-in before the record's title, for example "This Saturday:". Keep it to one line; a longer one is cut with an ellipsis.
- **Bar style** - **Still**, or **Scrolling** to run a longer line across the bar as a ticker.

There is nothing to style: the bar uses your brand color. Visitors can close it, and it stays closed for them until you change what it says.

### Booking card

Shows a card with a heading, a subtitle and a button, plus a row of instructor photos.

- **Card heading**, **Card subtitle**, **Card button text** and **Card button link**. The link defaults to `/book`, and the card hides itself on the page it links to.
- **Instructor photos** - switch off for a card that is the invitation alone. **Instructors on the card** picks up to four, in order; leave it empty for your first four public instructors.

### Popup

- **Popup title**, **Popup text**, **Popup image**, **Button label** and **Button link**.
- **Opens** - **After a delay** (8 seconds by default), **On scroll** (after 50% of the page by default) or **When leaving the page**.
- **Show again after (days)** - how long before the same visitor sees it again, 7 days by default.
- **Shown to** - **Everyone**, **Visitors not signed in** or **Signed-in members**.

The popup never shows in the editor's preview.

### Legal documents and cookie consent

With the feature on, pick the **Privacy policy** and **Cookie policy** documents the banner and footer link to. **Publish starter policies** creates a basic privacy and cookie policy in each of your languages, ready to edit; a document you already have is never overwritten. Write and edit the documents under [Waivers](/settings/waivers). Only published, public documents can be linked.

The banner records consent by category: _necessary_ scripts always run, while _analytics_ and _marketing_ scripts run only after the visitor accepts that category. Tracking itself is set up under **Settings** > **Integrations**: [Google Tag Manager](/settings/integrations/google-tag-manager) and the [Meta pixel](/settings/integrations/meta).

## Website settings

**Configuration** > **Edit website** has three groups:

- **General** - **Website name** (only you see it), **Web address** (the website's free 1club address), **Club** (when the website is for one location, it uses that club's logo) and **Default website**.
- **Appearance** - **Theme support**: **Light only**, **Dark only**, or **Light and dark (user can switch)**. With both, pick the **Default theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System preference**.
- **Visibility** - **Website is online**. Switch it off and visitors get a placeholder instead; every page, version and setting is kept and you can still edit and preview it.

<Warning>
  Changing the **Web address** moves the website. The old address stops working
  immediately and nothing redirects from it. If the address you type is taken,
  1club saves the closest free one and tells you which.
</Warning>

## SEO

**Configuration** > **SEO** opens on an analysis rather than a form:

1. **Measured on the published site** - Google Lighthouse measures your live site and reports **SEO**, **Speed**, **Accessibility** and **Best practices** scores, with the issues it found. **Measure again** reruns it. It needs a published, online website.
2. **Content SEO** - 1club checks the draft and lists what to fix before you publish: no site description, pages with no search title or description, two pages with the same one, a description too short or too long for a search result, pages not translated for search, a page hidden from search. **Open page** jumps to the page a finding is about.
3. **Fix my SEO** - writes every missing site and page description with AI, from your gym details and the content already on each page. Nothing you wrote yourself is replaced, and everything lands in the draft for you to review before publishing.
4. **Site-wide SEO** - defaults for every page; a page's own value always wins:
   - **Site description** - one sentence about the business, used where a page has no description of its own.
   - **Default social image** - shown when a shared page has no image of its own.
   - **Google Search Console token** and **Bing Webmaster Tools token** - paste only the token from the HTML or meta tag method, so you can verify the site with Google and Bing.

The site serves a `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` on its own. The sitemap lists your pages plus every club, plan, area, coach, news post, class and event page the site serves, and leaves out pages hidden from search. News posts and events describe themselves to Google in a form it reads, so an event can show its date, place and price in search results.

## Multi-language

A website can be published in several languages.

- The languages come from your organization's languages under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details**.
- Each translatable field on a section or page (headline, subtitle, button label, meta title) has a value per language.
- To translate a section, open its ⋮ menu, choose **Translations**, and use **Auto Translate** to fill it from the default language with AI. You can also ask the website builder to translate the whole site. Review the result before publishing.
- Visitors see a language switcher in the header when more than one language is published.

## AI website builder

The website builder is an assistant that edits your site from a chat. On the website pages the copilot on the right answers as the website builder: open it with the 1club icon at the right of the editor panel's tabs, or press **Cmd + .** (**Ctrl + .** on Windows). Ask it to "add a pricing page", "make this feel more premium", "use a serif for headings" or "translate the whole site into Spanish". The copilot takes the editor panel's place while it is open.

What it can do:

- **Build and edit pages and sections**, including the header and footer.
- **Change the design** - colors, fonts, shape and motion, or a template's whole look. It leaves your brand color alone unless you ask.
- **Rewrite copy** from your real gym details (name, description, clubs, plans, classes, branding), rather than inventing awards or history.
- **Pick images from your library** by name, for example "pool" or "reformer". It never uploads images for you.
- **Edit content items** (blocks, FAQs, posts) and custom CSS.
- **Translate** sections into your other languages.
- **Tell you what is left** - sample copy, stock photos, sections hidden for lack of data, broken links.

What it will not do:

- **It only edits the draft.** Nothing goes live until you publish.
- **It cannot publish, take the site offline, or delete it.** Those actions are yours.

The same tools are open to any AI assistant connected through the [1club MCP server](/mcp/overview) and to the [Platform API](/api-reference/introduction), so you can build and edit the site from Claude or ChatGPT.

## Domain and hosting

- Every website has a free 1club address built from its **Web address**.
- Attach a **custom domain** under **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Domains** with the purpose **Website (CMS)**, and point your DNS to 1club following the on-screen instructions. HTTPS is set up automatically.
- With more than one website, choose which one a domain opens with **Change website** on the Domains page.

### Holding page

If the Website add-on becomes inactive while you have published content or a custom domain pointed at 1club, visitors don't see an error. 1club serves a plain, unbranded **holding page** saying the site is temporarily unavailable. Reactivate the add-on under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Subscription** and your last published release returns exactly as it was.

## Publishing and releases

Edits do not go live until you publish.

1. **Draft** - everything you change in the editor is stored as the working draft.
2. **Publish** - updates the live release in place with your draft. Use it for typo fixes and routine updates. No new version is created.
3. **Releases** - open it from the arrow next to **Publish**. It shows the **Draft** and the release history, with the live one marked **Live**. **Publish** there creates a new versioned release, which is what you want before bigger changes. **Restore** makes an earlier release live again.

**Take website offline** hides the site without losing anything: visitors get a placeholder, and every page, version and setting is kept. **Bring website online** reverses it.

**Delete website** shows what goes (pages, published versions, the live site) and asks you to type a confirmation phrase and an optional reason. A copy is kept for a retention period, shown in the dialog, during which you can **Restore** it from the Website page. Deleting needs the `marketing.delete.organization` permission. Any custom domain stays attached, so you can point it at another website.

### More than one website

An organization can run several websites, for a shop, a brand, a location or a language. Each has its own pages, design, SEO settings and publish history. Members, plans, classes, instructors and the media library are shared, so a visitor who books on one is the same member you already have.

- **Add website** in the website picker opens the template gallery.
- The picker marks the **Default** website and any that are **Offline**.
- Under **Edit website**, **Default website** picks the one your organization's own address, booking and invoice emails, and the member portal's legal pages point at. Exactly one website is the default.

## Tips

- **Use live-data sections for anything that changes.** Don't paste your timetable into a hero; use **Classes**, **Calendar** or **Book online** so updates flow through.
- **Read the template's result before you publish.** The AI rewrite keeps to your data, but a soft claim can still slip through. The builder's "what's left" list is the fastest check.
- **One strong call to action per page.** A hero button plus one or two supporting ones is plenty.
- **Publish a versioned release before big changes**, so you can restore it if the next round goes wrong.
- **Translate after the copy settles.** Running **Auto Translate** early means redoing it.
- **Run Fix my SEO before your first publish**, then add the Search Console token so you can see what people search to find you.

| Visitors see a placeholder and the editor says the site is offline | The site was taken offline                                                                            | Use **Configuration** > **Bring website online**                                                |
| Public site shows a plain "temporarily unavailable" page           | The Website add-on is inactive                                                                        | Reactivate the add-on; your last release returns automatically                                  |
| A section shows in the editor but not on the live site             | It is a live-data section with nothing to show, or its **Design** tab limits when or to whom it shows | Add the underlying records, or check **Hide on**, **Show to**, **Show from** and **Show until** |
| A line of the template's copy disappeared                          | The rewrite refused a claim your data can't back up                                                   | Write the line yourself, or add the data it needs                                               |
| The announcement bar disappeared on its own                        | The class or event it announced is over, or the post is no longer public                              | Point it at something current, or write a sentence                                              |
| New languages show the default language                            | Translations weren't filled after the copy changed                                                    | Run **Auto Translate** on the section, review, then publish                                     |
| Lighthouse scores don't load                                       | The website isn't published, is offline, or Google timed out                                          | Publish and bring it online, then **Measure again**                                             |
