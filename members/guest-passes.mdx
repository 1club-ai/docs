A guest pass lets a member bring one non-member for one visit at no charge: a class, or a day at the gym. You decide how many passes a plan includes, members do the inviting themselves, and every guest becomes a contact you can follow up with.

## When to use it

- You sell a membership that includes "bring a friend" visits each year.
- You want guests to sign your waiver before they arrive, not at the desk.
- You want to hand a member a few extra passes as a goodwill gesture.

## How passes are granted

Open a plan in **Sales > Plans** and set **Guest passes per year**. See [Plans](/sales/plans).

- A member on that plan receives the passes when their membership becomes active.
- They receive the same number again once every 52 weeks, as long as they hold an active membership on a plan with guest passes.
- A plan with **0** guest passes grants none.
- A pass is good for one visit and expires 52 weeks after it was granted. Unused passes do not roll over.

Passes belong to the member, not to one membership. Changing plan neither adds nor removes passes. The next grant, 52 weeks after the last one, uses the plan the member holds at that time.

When a member's last membership with guest passes ends, their unused passes are revoked. A visit already reserved for a day on or before the end date still goes ahead.

## How a member brings a guest

Members see their passes on their membership in the member portal and in the app: how many are left, and until when.

1. The member chooses **Bring a guest**.
2. They pick a **Class** or a **Day visit**, and the day.
3. They enter the guest's name and email.

The guest then receives an email saying who invited them, where and when, with a link to sign each document you require for entry. See [Document signatures](/members/document-signatures).

The guest is added to your contacts, or matched to an existing contact with the same email. A guest can't be the member themselves or someone who already has an active membership.

A class visit is a regular booking in the guest's name, free of charge. Until the visit, the member can cancel it from the same place, and the pass is theirs to use again. The same happens when the booking is cancelled any other way, for example when you cancel the class: the member gets the pass back at once.

<Note>
  Guests can't book for themselves with a guest pass. The member who holds the pass makes the reservation.
</Note>

## Checking a guest in

When you open a guest at the desk, in the check-in dialog or after scanning, a notice shows **Guest of** and the member's name, the class and time or the day they are expected, and whether the guest has signed your entry documents.

- **Class visit** - check the guest in on their booking, like any other booking. See [Check-ins](/schedule/check-ins).
- **Day visit** - choose **Check in guest** in the notice. The button appears on the day of the visit.

Checking the guest in uses the pass. A guest who does not turn up uses it too.

## Issuing and revoking passes

Open the member's profile and go to the **Memberships** tab. The **Guest passes** block lists every pass with its status: available, reserved, used, revoked, or expired.

- **Issue** - give the member extra passes. Enter how many and the day they expire. The expiry defaults to 52 weeks from today.
- **Revoke** - take back a pass that has not been used, with a reason. Revoking a reserved pass also cancels the guest's class booking.
- **Cancel reservation** - cancel a guest's visit on the member's behalf. The pass becomes available again.

Viewing passes needs permission to view member profiles. Issuing, revoking, and cancelling need permission to update memberships. See [Users](/settings/admin-users).
