Instructors working in the instructor view see members by first name and last initial ("Maria P.") with their photo, and never their email, phone, date of birth or address. That applies everywhere they meet a member: the **Members** roster, the calendar, a booking, a class roster, a check-in, and the client picker when they add a booking. Owners and admins decide which members an instructor can see, and whether they see membership status.

## When to use it

- **A new coach learning names** - a jiu-jitsu instructor checks the roster before their first evening class to recognise the regulars by face.
- **Keeping member data private** - a gym with freelance coaches lets them book and run sessions for anyone, while emails and phone numbers stay with the desk.
- **Checking a plan without the front desk** - a padel coach whose role can view memberships sees at a glance whose membership ends next week, without access to contact details or billing.
- **Coaches who only see their own clients** - a personal-training studio narrows each trainer to the members they actually teach.

## How it works

What an instructor sees of a member is decided by the **Members** module's **View** level in their role:

| Level        | Members they can see                                                        | Details                                                                 |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Own**      | Members they teach: anyone booked with them or attending a class they teach | First name, last initial and photo                                      |
| **Limited**  | Every member in your organization                                           | First name, last initial and photo                                      |
| **Standard** | Every member                                                                | The full profile with contact details, and the full **Members** section |

The default **Instructor** role holds **Limited**, so a coach can find and book any member by name without seeing their contact details. Set **Own** to narrow a coach to the people they work with, or **Standard** to give them full member details again. Change it under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

The same level decides who the client picker offers when the instructor adds a booking, so it always matches their roster. When two members would read the same ("Maria P."), 1club tells them apart automatically.

Nothing changes for admins, managers or front desk, who hold **Standard**.

## The Members roster

The roster appears in the menu as **Members** for a staff user who:

- has an instructor profile,
- works in the instructor view, meaning their access is limited to their own classes and bookings, and
- holds Members **View** at **Own** or **Limited**.

Staff who can open the full **Members** section never see it, so nobody gets two **Members** entries.

The list opens at `/instructor/members`, 25 members per page, and can be searched by first name.

- **Member** - avatar, first name, and last initial.
- **Membership**, **Status**, and **Next billing / Ends** - each live membership's plan, its status, and **Next billing** or **Expires** with a date, or **Ongoing**. A member without one shows **No active membership**.

The three membership columns appear only when the instructor's role can also view memberships (**Memberships** > **View**). The default Instructor role can't, so out of the box the roster shows names only.

The roster is read-only. Rows don't open a profile and have no actions. An instructor without Members **View** who opens the page sees "You don't have access to the member list."

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                           | Cause                                                                                                                                       | Fix                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An instructor has no **Members** item in the menu | They have no instructor profile, their role has no Members **View**, or they hold **Standard** and see the full **Members** section instead | Check their role under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**  |
| The roster shows only a few members               | The instructor's Members **View** is at **Own**                                                                                             | Expected. Set it to **Limited** to list every member                      |
| An instructor can't find a member to book         | Their level is **Own** and the member has never booked with them                                                                            | Set Members **View** to **Limited**                                       |
| No membership columns                             | The role can't view memberships                                                                                                             | Grant **Memberships** > **View** if instructors should see plans          |
| A coach needs a member's phone number             | Instructors at **Own** or **Limited** never see contact details                                                                             | Grant Members **View** at **Standard**, or have the desk reach the member |
