Your sales pipeline is the people you are working to turn into customers. It lives on the **Leads** tab of **Members**: each lead is a contact with a stage, a source, and a staff member assigned to follow up. You see them as a board with a column per stage, or as a list. Owners, managers, sales and front desk staff use it to follow up on enquiries and trials until people buy.

## When to use it

- **Turn trial enquiries into members** - a padel gym adds everyone who asks about a trial as a lead in **New lead**, moves them to **Trial booked** when they book, and lets them leave the board on their own when they buy a membership.
- **Let forms, the website and your ads fill the pipeline** - an open-day signup form adds every submitter to **New lead** under the **Event** source, enquiries from your website's contact form arrive under **Website**, and people who fill in your Facebook or Instagram instant form arrive through [Lead Ads](/settings/integrations/meta#lead-ads) with the campaign behind them.
- **Share out follow-ups** - a manager selects ten new leads on the list, assigns them to a coach, and an automation notifies that coach.
- **Learn why leads say no** - staff pick a reason each time they move a lead to **Lost**, so you can see on [Sales analytics](/analytics/sales) whether you lose people on price, timing or distance.
- **Keep quiet leads from cluttering the board** - leads with no activity for 30 days move to **Lost** overnight, and can come back later.

## How it works

The pipeline is part of the contact record, not a separate record. A contact is in the pipeline when they have a stage. The same stage, source, and assignee show on the board, the list, the contact's profile, and in automations.

- **A lead is a contact in the pipeline who has never been a customer.** Being in the pipeline is what makes a contact's type **Lead**. You don't set the type by hand.
- **Only someone who is not a customer right now can enter.** A plain contact, a lead, a lapsed member, or a drop-in can be added. A drop-in or lapsed member you are selling a membership to stays in their own type, and their card shows it.
- **Leads convert by themselves.** On their first paid purchase after entering the pipeline (a membership or a pass), a lead becomes a customer and leaves the board. A free or unpaid plan does not convert anyone. There is nothing to close by hand.
- **There is no Won, and Lost is a stage.** The one **Lost** stage is for leads who said no or went quiet. Moving a lead there asks why (see [Lost and lost reasons](#lost-and-lost-reasons)), and it is reversible, because a "not now" in March can be a member in May. A lost lead is still a lead.
- **A conversion is credited to the lead's owner.** The staff member a lead is assigned to when they convert gets the credit in the sales reports, whether they bought at the desk or online (see [Lead owners](#lead-owners)).
- **A source is first-touch.** The source records where a contact first came from, and entering the pipeline again does not overwrite it.

<Note>
  Pipeline used to be a separate **Deals** feature under **Marketing**, with deal values, win probabilities, close dates, and custom deal fields. Deals were replaced by the pipeline on the contact. Each contact's latest open deal became their stage, source, and assignee (a lost deal moved to the stage now called **Lost**), and deal notes became contact notes. Old **Pipeline** and **Deals** links open the Leads tab.
</Note>

## The Leads tab

Go to **Members** and open the **Leads** tab. The tab is always shown, even when the pipeline is empty, because an empty board is where you add your first lead. Its badge counts everyone in the pipeline.

Use the icons in the toolbar to switch between **Board** and **List**. The page remembers your choice in this browser.

### Board

The board has one column per stage: your open stages in order, then **Lost** last. Each column header shows the stage name and the number of leads in it. Each card shows:

- the contact's name,
- their source, as a colored chip,
- their type, when they are not a lead (for example a drop-in or a lapsed member),
- their next booking, when they have one,
- who they are assigned to, or **Unassigned**,
- why they were lost, on a card in **Lost** (for example **Lost: Price**).

To move a lead, drag the card to another column. Moving cards and the card menu need members update access. The three-dot menu on a card offers **View profile**, **Mark as lost**, and **Remove from pipeline**. Removing a contact from the pipeline keeps the contact and takes them off the board.

Click a card to open the lead: their contact details, their stage, source, and assignee under **Pipeline**, and their notes, which you can add to on the spot. **View profile** opens their full [profile](/members/member-profiles).

When a column holds more leads than it shows, click **Show N more** at its foot. The list opens, filtered to that stage.

### List

The list shows the same leads as rows, with **Stage**, **Source**, and **Assigned to** right after the name. Columns a lead does not have yet, such as type, memberships, and levels, are left out. Click a row to open the contact's profile.

### Filters

The board filters by **Source** and **Assigned to**, and you can search it by name. The list adds a **Stage** filter, alongside the usual member filters. Filters stay in the URL, so you can bookmark "my leads from Instagram" or share it.

## Lost and lost reasons

Dragging a lead to **Lost**, or choosing **Mark as lost**, asks **Why was this lead lost?** Pick one reason from the list every gym shares:

- **Price**
- **Not the right time**
- **Chose another club**
- **Too far away**
- **No response**
- **Not interested**
- **Not a fit**
- **Other** - with a **Note**, which is required for this reason

A bulk **Move to stage** to Lost asks once for everyone you selected. The reason shows on the lead's card and in their **Pipeline** block as **Reason lost**, and stays in their history with the staff member the lead was assigned to at the time.

The reason belongs to the move. Moving the lead out of Lost clears it, and a later move back asks again. A lead moved to Lost by the inactivity rule is recorded as **No response**. One moved there by an automation step may carry the step's reason. One moved by the platform API, or before reasons existed, shows **Reason not recorded**.

A lost lead can always come back: move them to an open stage, or let them raise their hand again. A new form submission or lead ad from someone in Lost puts them back in the stage it sends leads to.

## Lead owners

Each lead can be assigned to a staff member who follows them up: their **owner**, shown as **Assigned to**. Any staff member can own a lead. Staff with the **Sales** [role](/settings/admin-users#the-roles) are listed first in the picker, because following up leads is their job.

When a lead converts, the sale is credited to whoever owned them at that moment, whoever rang the sale up and whether they bought at the desk or online. Reassigning the lead later does not move the credit. The [sales reports](/analytics/sales) group conversions and lost leads by **Salesperson** from this credit.

## Where leads came from

Besides their [source](#sources), 1club records each tagged arrival that led somewhere: the campaign and UTM tags of the link they came from (`utm_source`, medium, campaign, content and term), an ad click id (`fbclid`, `gclid`, `ttclid`), the page they landed on and the site that sent them. It is recorded when someone arrives that way and then:

- signs up, including with Google, Facebook or Apple,
- submits a [form](/marketing/forms),
- writes through your website's contact form or signs up to the newsletter,
- books, places an order, or buys a membership online,
- or comes in through a [lead ad](/settings/integrations/meta#lead-ads), with the campaign, ad set, ad and form behind it.

A contact with no source yet is filed under the channel they came from, such as **Instagram**, **Facebook** or **Google**. A source a form sets, or staff set, comes first. The earliest arrival is their first touch, and its campaign is the one the **Campaign** grouping of [Leads & Conversion](/analytics/sales#leads-by) reads. A visit with no tag, click id or outside referrer records nothing.

## Adding leads

- **Add lead** - on the Leads tab, the outlined button at the top right is **Add Lead**. It is also in the **+** menu on every Members tab. Fill in the contact details and, under **Pipeline**, the stage, source, and assignee. The stage starts at your default stage.
- **From a board column** - click **+** on a column header to add a lead that starts in that stage.
- **From a contact's profile** - the **Pipeline** block on the contact card has **Add to pipeline** for anyone who is not a customer. For someone already in it, change the stage, source, or assignee in place.
- **From a form** - turn on **Add submitters to the pipeline** on a [form](/marketing/forms) and choose a stage and a source. People who submit it become leads, unless they are already customers. A signup form can't do this, because whoever signs up becomes a customer.
- **From your website** - enquiries from the contact form on your [website](/marketing/website) arrive under the **Website** source.
- **From Facebook and Instagram lead ads** - turn on [Lead Ads](/settings/integrations/meta#lead-ads) on the Meta integration, and everyone who fills in your instant form arrives in the stage you choose.
- **From an automation or the API** - the **Move to pipeline stage** automation step and the platform API add and move contacts too.

## Bulk moves and assignment

Select leads on the list with the checkboxes. The bulk action bar adds **Move to stage** and **Assign to** to the usual member actions. The confirmation counts the contacts you selected.

## Automations

[Automations](/marketing/automations) can work the pipeline for you:

- **Trigger** - "When a contact's pipeline stage changes", narrowed with **From stage (any of)**, **To stage (any of)** and, for moves to Lost, **Lost reason (any of)**. A lead moved to **Lost** by the inactivity rule fires it too.
- **Blueprints** - **Re-engage stale leads** follows up on leads who went quiet, and **Win back lost leads** checks in a month after a lead was lost over price or timing. See [Suggested blueprints](/marketing/automations#suggested-blueprints).
- **Steps** - **Move to pipeline stage** and **Assign to staff member**.
- **Entry conditions** - **In pipeline stage** and **Came from source**.
- **Recipient** - **Assigned staff member** notifies whoever the contact is assigned to.

## Pipeline settings

Stages and sources are set under **Settings** > **Members** > **Pipeline**. The page has two sections, **Stages** and **Sources**. Changing them needs organization settings manage access.

### Stages

Every organization starts with four stages: **New lead**, **Contacted**, **Trial booked**, and **Lost**. Click **Add stage** to add one, or click a stage to edit it. Drag the handle to reorder stages. The board shows the open stages in this order from left to right, with **Lost** always last.

Each stage shows chips for its kind (**Open** or **Lost**), **Default** on the default stage, **Lost after N days** when the Lost stage has an inactivity rule, and how many contacts are in it. A stage has:

- **Name** and **Color** - the column label and its accent color.
- **Default stage** (open stages only) - optional, and at most one open stage has it. With a default, everyone who has never been a customer (other than the children in a household) enters the pipeline there as they are added, and turning it on asks whether the contacts you already have come in too. Without one, nobody enters on their own.
- **Move leads here after this many days without activity** (Lost only) - leads with no activity for that many days (1 to 365) move to Lost overnight, with the reason **No response**. Leave it empty to move leads to Lost only by hand.

Activity means a stage move, a booking, a form submission, or a message exchanged with the person (one they sent, or one staff wrote to them). Messages sent by an [automation](/marketing/automations), including messages to a whole segment, don't count, so they don't keep a lead warm.

You can't delete the **Lost** stage, your last open stage, or a stage that still has contacts in it. Move its contacts to another stage first.

### Sources

Sources record where a contact came from. Every organization starts with **Walk-in**, **Website**, **Referral**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **Google**, **Event**, and **Other**. A source for any other channel, such as TikTok, is added the first time it brings someone. Click **Add source** to add one. Each source has a **Name**, a **Color** (used for the chip on the board), and an **Active** switch. A switched-off source shows an **Inactive** chip. Drag the handle to reorder.

Deleting a source leaves the contacts filed under it with no source. To keep their history, switch the source off instead.

## Reporting

[Sales analytics](/analytics/sales) reports on your pipeline. The **Sales Dashboard** shows new leads, a **Lead journey** through to customers, **Lead Sources**, and **Why leads were lost**. **Leads & Conversion** shows how many of a period's leads converted, how fast and for how much, by source, gym, salesperson, campaign or stage, with how many were lost and the top reason.

## Who can do what

| Action | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| See the Leads tab, the board, and the list | Members read |
| Move, assign, add, or remove leads | Members update |
| Add a lead | Members create |
| Change stages and sources | Organization settings manage |

Members permissions apply to the whole organization, not to a single **Club**. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add to pipeline** is missing on a contact | The contact is a customer right now (a member with a live plan) | Only non-customers can enter the pipeline |
| A lead disappeared from the board | They made their first paid purchase and converted, or someone removed them | Open the contact. A converted lead is now a member or drop-in |
| A lost lead shows **Reason not recorded** | They were moved to Lost by the platform API, or before reasons existed | Move them out and back to record a reason |
| You can't drag cards | Your role lacks members update access | Ask an admin to grant it |
| You can't delete a stage | It is the Lost stage, your last open stage, or still has contacts | Move its contacts to another stage |
| A form doesn't add leads | **Add submitters to the pipeline** is off, or it is a signup form | Turn it on in the form's settings. Signup forms can't add leads |
| Leads never go to Lost on their own | The Lost stage has no inactivity rule | Set **Move leads here after this many days without activity** on the Lost stage |
