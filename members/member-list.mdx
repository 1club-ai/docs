The Member list is the contacts grid: every person tracked in your org - members, drop-ins, leads, and lapsed contacts - in one searchable list, sorted by created date by default. Front desk staff use it to find a person and open their profile, and managers use it to add members and work through groups of contacts at once.

## When to use it

Open the Member list when you need to find someone, pull out a group of contacts, or act on many of them together. Tabs split the list by contact type, the filter drawer narrows by tag, plan, status, or progression level, and bulk actions invite, merge, or archive contacts. It is also where you add new members, leads, children, memberships, and accounts, and where your sales pipeline lives on the **Leads** tab.

Typical uses:

- **Nudge members who never set up their portal** - filter by plan and **Unregistered** status, then invite everyone in one batch.
- **Sign up a family** - add a parent at a martial-arts gym, then add each child as a dependent on the household.
- **Clear out cold leads** - move the leads from a padel open day that never converted to **Cold** in one go.
- **Chase payments before a bill run** - check the **Unpaid** tab for anyone carrying a pending or failed payment.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps.

## Opening the list

Go to **Members** in the main menu. The Member list is the default page, at the canonical path `/members/list`.

A member belongs to your whole organization, not to one of its **Clubs**. Staff at every location see the same list, and where a member trains lives on what they do: each membership, booking, check-in, and order records its own **Club**.

## Tabs

Tabs filter the grid. **All** and **Leads** are always present. The others only show when their bucket has at least one contact, so a new org may see fewer tabs than a busy one. In order:

- **All** - every non-archived contact.
- **Members** - contacts with `type = member`. Default tab when present.
- **Drop-ins** - one-off visitors (`type = drop_in`).
- **Leads** - your sales pipeline: everyone with a pipeline stage, as a board or a list. See [Leads and pipeline](/members/leads).
- **Unpaid** - contacts carrying the system **Unpaid** smart tag (pending or failed payment). Only shown when that tag exists in your org.
- **Accounts** - contacts grouped under household or company accounts, shown grouped by account. This tab is read-only (no row selection).
- **Lapsed** - members whose membership ended without renewal (`type = lapsed`).

**Members**, **Drop-ins**, and **Lapsed** describe membership history, so they only appear for staff who can read memberships. Staff without that access land on **All**.

Each tab shows a count badge from the server. The page remembers the last tab you used per browser. The former **Households** tab was renamed to **Accounts**, so old `?tab=households` links redirect there automatically.

## Columns

The grid shows, by default:

- **Name** with avatar, plus email below.
- **Phone**, with a WhatsApp badge when the number is WhatsApp-verified.
- **Age** computed from date of birth.
- **User status** - whether the contact has a member-portal account. If they have no account but do have an email address or a phone number, this cell shows an **Invite** button. Otherwise it shows a status chip (`unregistered`, `invited`, `incomplete`, `active`, `inactive`, or `suspended`).
- **Type** chip (member, lead, drop-in, etc.).
- **Memberships** - active membership plan names (shows "No memberships" when none).
- **Accounts** - household and company accounts the contact belongs to, each a link, with the account type underneath.
- **Progression** - skill and level chips per enabled track. Only shown when progression tracks are configured.
- **Rating** - when ratings are enabled, the contact's strongest sport rating, with the rest in a tooltip.
- **Engagement** - latest engagement score chip (see [Engagement analytics](/analytics/engagement)). Only shown when at least one contact in the current view has a score.
- **Tags** - manual and smart tags as chips.

On the **Leads** tab, **Stage**, **Source**, and **Assigned to** follow the name, and the columns a lead does not have yet (type, memberships, progression, accounts, and user status) are left out.

Hidden by default: **Invitation status** and **Created at**. Any custom contact fields your org has defined are added as extra columns, also hidden by default. Toggle visibility from the column menu. On the Accounts tab, the per-row Accounts column is hidden because the grouping header already shows it.

## Search and filter

Use the search bar to match by name, email, or phone. The filter drawer (top right) holds:

- **Tags** - any combination of manual and smart tags. Only tags currently applied to at least one non-archived contact appear.
- **Status** - the contact's user-account status.
- **Plan** - one or more active plans. Only plans that currently have at least one active membership are listed.
- **Level** - progression levels, grouped by track when more than one track is enabled.
- **Stage**, **Source**, and **Assigned to** - on the **Leads** tab only. The board view filters by source and assignee, since its columns already are the stages.

The drawer loads its in-use option lists the first time you open it, so early on it may briefly show every option before narrowing to those actually in use. Filter selections live in the URL, so a filtered view can be shared as a link or bookmarked.

## Tags

Members carry two kinds of tags: **manual tags** that you add per contact, and **smart tags** that the system applies automatically based on rules. Both appear as chips and both work as filters here, in automation entry conditions, and in promotion segments.

Each tag can have an optional color, set as a hex code in **Settings** > **Members** > **Smart Tags**. Text color is auto-picked for contrast.

Smart tags ship with a default rule set covering **Attendance** (All-Star, Regular, Casual, Low Activity, Ghost), **Financial** (Unpaid, Failed, Overdue, Lapsed), **Lifecycle**, **Loyalty** (Legend, Champion, Pro, Rising Star, Beginner), **Membership**, and **New contact**. See [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging) for the full list and rule editor.

## Adding a member

Click **Add member** at the top right to open the create dialog. It contains the contact form plus an optional membership section:

1. Fill in the contact details. **First name** is always required. **Last name**, **email**, **phone**, **date of birth**, and **gender** are required only if your org marks them required under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**. You can also set a profile image, tags, address, and any custom fields. There is no **Club** to pick: a member belongs to the organization, and each membership or booking records its own.
2. To sell a plan at the same time, click **Add membership** and pick a plan in the membership section. Leave it off to create the contact without a membership.
3. Save. The contact is created and you land on their [profile](/members/member-profiles).

The contact's `type` is derived from what they actually hold - attaching a membership makes them a member, and a bare contact with no membership or booking stays a plain contact. A contact becomes a lead only by entering the pipeline. You don't set the type by hand.

On the **Leads** tab the outlined button becomes **Add Lead**, which adds the contact straight into the pipeline with a stage, source, and assignee.

### The create (+) menu

The primary **+** button next to **Add member** opens a menu of create actions:

- **Add Member** - the dialog above.
- **Add Lead** - the same contact form plus a **Pipeline** section (stage, source, assignee). See [Leads and pipeline](/members/leads#adding-leads).
- **Add Child** - the same contact form with an account picker, preset to the Dependent role. Use it to add a child to a household, for example a parent's second kid joining the same kids climbing program.
- **Add Membership** - open the membership dialog standalone and pick the contact inside it. Only shown to staff who can create memberships.
- **Add Account** - create a household or company account, then jump straight to it.

### Header quick links

The three-dot overflow menu (next to the **+** button) links to related pages. Each entry only shows if you can open the page behind it:

- **Memberships** - opens [**Sales** > **Memberships**](/sales/memberships).
- **Cards** - the [access cards](/members/member-cards) registry.
- **Matches** - opens [**Events** > **Matches**](/events/matches). Only when ratings are enabled.
- **Accounts** - the [accounts](/members/accounts) list.
- **Contact analysis** - a check of your roster for missing details and possible duplicates.
- **Signatures** - the [document signatures](/members/document-signatures) ledger.
- **Archive** - opens **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Archive**.

## Per-row actions

Click a row to open the member's [profile](/members/member-profiles). The row action menu also offers:

- **View** - open the profile (needs `members.read.organization`).
- **Edit** - open the contact edit dialog (needs `members.update.organization`).
- **Archive** - soft-delete the contact (needs `members.update.organization`).
- **Merge** - open the merge dialog with this contact on one side, then pick the duplicate (needs `members.delete.organization`).

Members permissions are organization-wide: there is no per-**Club** access to the member list.

## Bulk actions

Select rows with the checkboxes. The bulk action bar appears with the count of selected contacts and its actions:

- **Move to stage** and **Assign to** - on the **Leads** tab only. See [Leads and pipeline](/members/leads#bulk-moves-and-assignment).

- **Invite** - opens the invite dialog, where you choose how the invitation travels, then sends it to every selected contact who is eligible (reachable on that channel, and either unregistered or holding an expired invite). The info icon shows how many of your selection are eligible. Invitations go out in a single request that the server governs against your gym's daily invite budget; it reports back how many were invited, how many each channel carried, and how many failed, were throttled, skipped, could not be reached on your chosen channel, or were ineligible, so a problem with one contact never hides another. Invite is offered for any selection. When nobody selected can be invited, the dialog says why. It needs `members.update.organization`.
- **Merge** - appears when exactly two contacts are selected. Opens the merge dialog for the pair. It needs `members.delete.organization`.
- **Archive** - archives each selected contact. Contact records are never hard-deleted, so financial history and audit trails stay intact. Archiving runs one request per contact and reports partial success if some fail. This action needs `members.update.organization`.

Archived contacts disappear from the list. To get back to them, use **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Archive**.

## Choosing how an invitation reaches a member

Every invitation asks how it should travel, whether you are inviting one member
or fifty. There is no gym-wide default, because the reason to send one member a
WhatsApp invitation is usually that member: no email address on file, or a number
they actually answer.

| Choice                    | What happens                                                                           |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Whatever reaches them** | Email if the member has an address, otherwise WhatsApp, otherwise a text message.      |
| **Email**                 | Only email. A member with no address is reported instead of being invited another way. |
| **WhatsApp**              | Only WhatsApp, from your own connected number if you have one, otherwise from ours.    |
| **Text message**          | Only SMS.                                                                              |

A named channel is a requirement, not a preference. If it cannot carry the
invitation, that member is reported rather than quietly emailed instead, so you
can see who was missed. The result tells you which channels actually carried the
batch, which is how you spot a WhatsApp choice that is silently going out as
email because WhatsApp is not connected yet.

Two things narrow who a phone channel can reach:

- **WhatsApp needs the member's consent.** Sending from our number requires them
  to have opted in, and a member who has replied `STOP` is never messaged on
  either phone channel.
- **A number has to identify the member.** If the contact already has a portal
  account and the number on their contact has not been verified on it, the
  invitation is refused. Otherwise a link sent to a stale, shared, or mistyped
  number could be used by whoever holds it to get into that member's account.
  Send it by email instead, or have the member verify the number first.

Staff invitations always go by email. A staff role cannot be handed to whoever
holds a phone number.

For the whole picture - what members can sign in with, social sign-in, verification,
and what happens when someone returns - see [Member authentication](/members/member-authentication).

### What the member does next

How they accept follows how you sent it:

- **Invited by email** - they open the link and set a password.
- **Invited by WhatsApp or text** - they open the link and enter a code sent to
  that number. This is how the invitation proves the number belongs to them, and
  it is required even when their account also has an email address. Their email
  address is not marked as confirmed by accepting this way.

## Worked examples

### Find everyone on a plan who hasn't set up their portal

You just launched online booking and want to nudge members who never activated their account.

1. Open the filter drawer, set **Plan** to your membership plan, and set **Status** to **Unregistered**.
2. Select all rows and click **Invite**, pick how the invitation should travel, and confirm. Everyone the chosen channel can reach gets a portal invitation in one batch.

### Onboard a family at a martial arts gym

A parent signs up two kids for the kids jiu-jitsu program.

1. Click **Add member** and create the parent as the account owner (or **Add account** to create the household first).
2. Use the **+** menu > **Add dependent** twice, picking the household each time, to add both children with the Dependent role.
3. Attach the kids program plan to each child in the membership section or from their profile.

### Clean up stale leads after a padel open day

Your open day generated a batch of leads that never converted.

1. Open the **Leads** tab and switch to **List**.
2. Filter by **Source** (for example **Event**), or by created date if you show the Created at column.
3. Select the ones that went quiet and use **Move to stage** > **Cold**. They stay in the pipeline and can warm up later. To drop someone from the pipeline entirely, use **Remove from pipeline** on their card instead.

### Spot unpaid members before a billing run

1. Open the **Unpaid** tab (visible when the system Unpaid tag exists).
2. Review who is carrying a pending or failed payment, then follow up before the next [bill run](/sales/bill-runs).

## Tips & best practices

- **Share filtered views as links.** Because tab, search, and filter state live in the URL, you can bookmark "active members on the coaching plan" or paste it to a colleague.
- **Use the inline Invite button for one-offs.** You don't need to select a row - the User status column shows an **Invite** button for any unregistered contact you can reach. It opens the same dialog, so a single invitation gets the same channel choice as a batch.
- **Turn on Created at when auditing signups.** It's hidden by default; toggle it from the column menu when you need to sort or eyeball recent joins.
- **Let the type calculate itself.** Attach a membership rather than hand-setting `type = member`; the system keeps the type in step with what the contact actually holds.
- **Archive, don't fear data loss.** Archiving is a soft delete that preserves charges and history, so it's safe to tidy the list aggressively.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A tab I expect (Members, Drop-ins, Lapsed, Unpaid) isn't showing.

**Solution**: Tabs other than All and Leads only appear when their bucket has at least one contact, and Unpaid also requires the system Unpaid smart tag to exist. Members, Drop-ins, and Lapsed also need permission to read memberships. Add a matching contact or enable the tag, then reload.

**Issue**: The invite dialog says nobody in the selection can be invited.

**Solution**: Only contacts who are unregistered or holding an expired invite, and reachable on the channel you pick, can be invited. Contacts who are already active, still have a pending invite, or have no email address and no phone number are skipped. Picking **WhatsApp** or **Text message** also narrows eligibility to contacts who have a phone number.

**Issue**: Some invitations weren't sent, or the result says "throttled".

**Solution**: Invites are capped by your gym's daily invite budget. The result breaks the batch into invited, failed, throttled, skipped, could-not-be-reached, and ineligible counts. Throttled invitations exceeded the budget - retry later or contact support to raise the limit. An invitation that could not be delivered does not count against the budget.

**Issue**: A plan or tag is missing from the filter drawer.

**Solution**: The drawer only lists options currently in use - plans with at least one active membership and tags applied to at least one non-archived contact. If nothing matches yet, the option won't appear.

**Issue**: I archived a contact by mistake.

**Solution**: Archiving is reversible. Go to **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Archive** to find and restore the contact; their history is intact.
