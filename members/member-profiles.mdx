The member profile is the page for one contact: it pulls together their identity, balances, memberships, history, and integration links into one view. A **contact** is your organization's own copy of a person's record - name, email, phone, plus their memberships, invoices, and signatures. Front desk staff open it when a member is at the desk, and managers use it to fix records, set up access, and follow up on money owed.

## When to use it

The profile answers the questions staff ask most at the desk - does this person owe anything, have they checked in today, how active are they - without opening a tab. From the same page you add a booking, membership, check-in, or voucher redemption, keep internal notes, promote the member on their progression track, and manage their portal account, pipeline stage, cards, and household or company links.

Typical uses:

- **A padel member who owes money checks in** - the **Due now** tile shows last week's unpaid area fee, so you record the visit and take payment in one place.
- **Getting a new jiu-jitsu member onto the portal** - confirm their email and send the invitation from **User Account** in **Contact Information**.
- **Bringing a member to the app** - send them your gym's booking link with **Send invite link**, so they open your gym in the 1club App.
- **A door PIN for a climber** - add a PIN from the **Cards** tile when access control is on.
- **Belt progression for a martial-arts student** - see how close they are to their next belt on the **Progression Levels** tile, and promote them from it.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps.

## Page layout

You land on the member profile after clicking a row in the [Member list](/members/member-list). The URL is `/members/list/{contactId}`.

From top to bottom:

- **Header** - the member's avatar (click it to upload a photo), name, type chip, and account status chip. Below the name: their email and phone (with a WhatsApp badge when the number is WhatsApp-verified, and a **No SMS** chip when text messages are stopped) and their age (hover it for the date of birth). Their tags sit beside the name, with a button to add a manual tag. When they belong to a household or company, the [account](#accounts) shows on the right with its members' avatars.
- **Notices** - anything to resolve before serving the member. See [Notices above the info strip](#notices-above-the-info-strip).
- **Info strip** - tiles summarizing what the member owes, pays, holds and does.
- **Tabs** - the member's history, opening on **Overview**.

Click the member's **name**, or their account status chip, to open **Contact Information**: everything the header doesn't show, in one panel. Click the **type chip** to see the type-change history.

## Info strip

The tiles at the top summarize the member's current state. Which ones show depends on what your gym uses:

- **Due now** - what this member owes that has already fallen due, plus any shortfall on a required wallet deposit. It turns red when anything is due. Underneath, **Upcoming** shows unpaid charges that are not due yet, such as an area booked for tomorrow or a membership that starts next month. Upcoming charges are never added to **Due now** - see [What counts as due now](#what-counts-as-due-now). A **Wallet** line shows any wallet credit; when a deposit is required it reads **Deposit** (held against required) with the **Available** balance instead. Staff who can read billing can click it to open **Wallet history**, where they top up, refund, or adjust the balance. What is due and the credit are never netted: a member who owes 100 and holds 100 in credit still shows 100 due now. Use the redeem icon on this tile to redeem a voucher.
- **Total paid** - lifetime amount paid, with an "of X billed" line.
- **Payment method** - the saved card charged first, with its expiry, or **None**. Shown when your gym takes card payments through Stripe or the member has a card saved. Click it to see and add saved [payment methods](#payment-methods).
- **Cards** - the member's main access card or PIN, when access control is on. Click it to see all their [cards](#cards-and-pin); the plus icon adds one.
- **Attendance** - a sparkline of recent visits. The dot lights up when the member has already checked in today. Use the overflow icon to change the window: **4 weeks**, **12 weeks** (the default), **6 months**, **12 months**, or **Since first visit**. Your choice is remembered for the next profile you open.
- **Engagement Scores** - the member's [engagement score](/settings/smart-tagging#engagement-scoring) and level, when your gym calculates scores and the member has one.
- **Progression Levels** / **Assessments** / **Progress** - the member's current levels when progression tracks are enabled, and their overall assessment average (for example "Tennis juniors: 3.5/5") once your organization has an assessment template and you may view assessments club-wide. The tile is titled **Progress** when both are on. Under each level it shows the member's progress toward the next one - sessions, days at the level and skills learned - or **Eligible for promotion** once every requirement is met (see [Promotion requirements](/settings/progression#promotion-requirements)). Click a level to open the member's progression, with **Promote**, the history, and the curriculum to tick off. The plus icon offers **Assign Level** and **Add assessment**; clicking the average opens the member's [assessment history](/members/assessments#reviewing-a-members-assessments). When ratings are enabled, the tile also shows the member's ratings by sport.

### What counts as due now

**Due now** adds up the member's unpaid [charges](/sales/charges) that have fallen due. A charge that is not due yet counts as **Upcoming** instead:

- **Bookings** - a booking's charge (an area, an instructor session, a class, an event, or one session of a recurring booking) is upcoming until the visit starts. It falls due when the visit starts or the member checks in, whichever comes first. If the booking is cancelled or marked as a no-show, any cancellation charge is due at once.
- **Everything else** - other charges fall due on their date. A membership's charge, its renewals, and its signup fee are dated at the start of the period they pay for, so a membership that starts next month stays upcoming until then. Products and walk-in visits are due when sold.

The same **Due now** figure appears wherever staff see what a member owes: the red marker next to their name in the bookings, check-ins, and attendance lists, the card that opens when you hover over it (which also shows **Upcoming**), the point of sale, and booking details. The marker only shows while something is due now.

### Notices above the info strip

Two kinds of notice can appear between the header and the info strip:

- **Alerts** (red, or orange for warnings only) - what to resolve with the member before serving them: a required document still unsigned, a missing required field, a wallet below its required deposit, gear out or overdue. An archived contact shows an **Archived** row with **Unarchive**, and a suspended one an **Access suspended** row with **Reinstate**. What the member owes is not repeated here, because the **Due now** tile already shows it.
- **Missing contact details** (blue) - a row such as **No phone number** or **No email address** for a contact detail your gym requires that the record lacks, with a **Fix** button that opens the contact form. The notice names the phone number or email only when that field is required under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**, on the staff form or at signup. Out of the box only email is required, so a contact without a phone number is not flagged unless you make the phone field required.

## Header actions

For an active contact the header has an **Add** (+) menu and a more-actions menu (**...**).

The **Add** menu creates, depending on your permissions and the features you use:

- **Booking** - opens the schedule pre-filtered to this contact.
- **Membership** - opens the membership dialog with the contact pre-selected.
- **Checkin** - records a visit (against an existing booking or membership, or a new booking or membership in one step).
- **Wallet** (or **Deposit** when a deposit is required) - tops up the member's wallet.
- **Voucher** - redeems a voucher against this contact's account.
- **Payment** - takes a payment toward what the member owes, upcoming charges included, so a member can pay ahead. Disabled while the member owes nothing.
- **Card** - adds an access card when access control is on, or a card for an external program such as MultiSport. See [Access cards](/members/member-cards).
- **Payment card** - saves a payment card to charge, when your gym takes card payments through Stripe.
- **Assessment** - adds an assessment card, when assessments are set up.
- **Rental** - lends gear, when Rentals is on.
- **Account** - creates a household or company account with this member as its owner. Needs Members **Create**.
- **Message** - opens a new message pre-addressed to the contact in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

The more-actions menu holds, depending on the contact and your permissions:

- **Edit** - opens the contact form. See [Editing the contact](#editing-the-contact).
- **Statement** - what the member was charged and paid over a period, as a PDF or by email. Needs Billing **View**. See [A member's statement](/sales/statements#a-members-statement).
- **Suspend** (or **Reinstate**) - pauses the member's access without changing anything. Only shown when the contact has an active or suspended portal account.
- **Stop SMS** (or **Allow SMS again**) - when the contact has a phone number.
- **Send invite link** - opens a new message to the member with a link to book at your gym in the 1club App or on the web. See [Invite a member to the app](#invite-a-member-to-the-app).
- **Archive** - removes the member from active use.

**Suspend**, **Reinstate**, and the SMS actions need Members **Edit**. See [Suspend and archive](/members/suspend-and-archive) for which of Suspend and Archive to reach for.

If the contact is archived, the header collapses to a single **Unarchive** action (Members **Edit**). An **archived member** is retained for financial and legal history but is hidden from listings, has its memberships and future bookings cancelled, and cannot sign in until you unarchive them.

A **suspended member** keeps everything - record, plan, bookings, staff role - but cannot sign in to your gym, so cannot book for themselves, until someone reinstates them. Staff can still book and check them in. The profile shows an **Access suspended** notice with a **Reinstate** button while they are suspended.

Both are scoped to your gym: the same person can stay an active member elsewhere.

### Invite a member to the app

**Send invite link** opens a new message addressed to the member, already filled in with a subject ("Book at" and your gym's name), a short note, and a **Book now** button. The button opens your gym's own link - the one its QR code holds - so the member lands on your gym in the [1club App](/settings/mobile-app), or on the web without installing anything. The message is in your organization's language, and you choose the channel and can edit it before sending, like any message.

It is offered when the member has an email address or a phone number, to staff who can send messages. It is a booking link, not a login invitation: to give a member a portal login, use **Invite** in [User account](#user-account).

## Tabs

Tabs load their own data on demand, so opening the profile stays fast even for contacts with long histories.

| Tab | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Overview** | The member's **Activity** (their bookings and memberships, each with its money), their **Notes**, and the automations that have run for them |
| **Bookings** | All bookings, including cancelled. Click through to the booking detail |
| **Memberships** | Every membership ever assigned. Edit, pause, resume, renew, or cancel from here. Shown to staff who can read memberships |
| **Charges** | What this contact owes, with the payments and invoices against it |
| **Check-ins** | Visit history |
| **Rentals** | Gear the member has out, shown only when Rentals is on. The badge turns red while anything is overdue |
| **Documents** | Signed documents (waivers, contracts), form responses, signatures awaiting approval, and required documents the member has not signed yet, with **Send for signature**. See [Document signatures](/members/document-signatures#signatures-awaiting-approval) |
| **Matches** | Match history, shown only when ratings are enabled |
| **Notifications** | Which notifications the member receives, with any overrides of your defaults |

**Bookings**, **Memberships**, **Charges**, **Check-ins**, **Documents**, and **Rentals** show a count badge when they have something to count. The **Bookings** badge leaves out cancelled bookings, the **Memberships** badge counts live memberships, and the **Documents** badge includes required documents still unsigned. The **Bookings** tab has **Active**, **Cancelled**, and **All** pills, the **Memberships** tab has **Active**, **Inactive**, and **All** pills, and both open on **Active**.

### Notes

The **Notes** panel on the **Overview** tab is a free-form running log. Click **Add**, type the note, and save it. Each note is timestamped; editing a note updates its timestamp. Use the pencil and trash icons on a note to edit or delete it. Adding, editing, and deleting notes needs Members **Edit**; without it the panel is read-only. Notes are internal - members never see them.

The automations panel beside it, for staff who can view marketing, lists the [automations](/marketing/automations) that have run for this member.

## Editing the contact

Choose **Edit** in the more-actions menu, or in **Contact Information**. The form covers name, contact details, date of birth, profile image, address, and any custom fields configured under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**. The email can't be changed for a member with a linked user account. If you save an email address another contact already uses, the form offers to open or merge with that contact - see [Duplicates and merging](/members/merge-duplicates).

The contact's **type** (member, lead, drop-in, lapsed, contact, staff) is not edited here. It follows what the contact holds and does, and every change is logged. Click the type chip in the header to open its type-change history.

## Contact Information

Click the member's name, or their account status chip, to open **Contact Information**. From top to bottom it shows:

- **Accounts** - see [Accounts](#accounts).
- The contact's details: source, date of birth with age, gender, address, tags, custom fields that have a value, and the **Chosen program** the member picked at signup (see [Programs](/settings/progression#programs)).
- **Pipeline** - see [Pipeline](#pipeline).
- **User Account** - see [User account](#user-account).
- **Cards** and **Door access from plans** - see [Cards and PIN](#cards-and-pin).
- **Reviews**, **Integrations**, and **Payment Methods** - see below.
- **Edit**, to open the contact form.

### Accounts

The **Account** block lists every household and company the contact belongs to, with the account type on each. Click an account name to open the [account page](/members/accounts).

- When the contact belongs to **no account**, the block shows **Add**, which creates a new household or company account with this contact as its owner.
- When the contact **is on an account**, each account shows up to four of the other members with their roles. The account's **Add** button attaches a new person, and the trash icon detaches a member, after you confirm, without deleting their contact record.

The header shows the same account beside the member's name (a household first, when they have several), with its members' avatars. Hover an avatar to see that person's first name and role, and click it to open their profile.

### Pipeline

The **Pipeline** block shows the contact's stage, source, and assigned staff member, each changed in place. For a contact who is not a customer and not yet in the pipeline, it offers **Add to pipeline**; for one in the pipeline, **Remove from pipeline** and, in an open stage, **Mark as lost**. A lost lead shows why ("Lost: Price"). Changing the pipeline needs Members **Edit**. See [Leads and pipeline](/members/leads).

### User account

The **User Account** block shows whether the contact has a member-portal login. Possible states:

- **Unregistered** - no portal account exists. When the contact has an email address or a phone number, an **Invite** button appears for staff with Members **Edit**. It asks how the invitation should travel - see [Choosing how an invitation reaches a member](/members/member-list#choosing-how-an-invitation-reaches-a-member).
- The account exists (statuses such as **Active**, **Invited**, **Incomplete**, **Suspended**, **Expired**, or **Deleted**). The block shows the status chip, when they joined, the member's **Sign-in methods**, and the invitation expiry date while an invitation is outstanding. An **Incomplete** account lists what the member has not completed yet, and a **Deleted** one says the person deleted their account (you can invite them again).

The block's menu offers, depending on state:

- **Reset password** - sends a password reset email. Available for an active account that is not your own, to staff who manage organization settings or staff.
- **Send Invitation** / **Resend invitation** - sends or re-sends a portal invitation while one is pending or has expired.
- **Delete** - deletes an outstanding invitation, or removes the portal login link, for anyone but yourself.

### Cards and PIN

When [access control](/settings/access-control) is enabled, the **Cards** block lists every access card, PIN, or QR code this contact holds, with a star on their main card. The **Cards** tile in the info strip opens the same list. From here you can:

- **Issue card** - scan a card on the reader (or type its code, or enter a PIN) to give it to this member. A card already on file as club stock is issued the same way.
- **Remove from member** - takes a card off the member. It stops working right away.

A **PIN** is a number the member types at a kiosk or door. See [Access cards](/members/member-cards) for card types and how issuing works.

Below it, **Door access from plans** lists which doors the member's plans open and when, or why a plan opens none. It needs Operations **View**. See [Door access from plans](/settings/access-control-settings#seeing-which-doors-a-members-plans-open).

### Reviews

When the member has left reviews and you can view marketing, a **Reviews** block shows how many, with **View all** to open them in [Reviews](/marketing/reviews).

### Integrations

The **Integrations** block shows:

- **Stripe Customer** - when the contact has a Stripe customer: the customer ID, email, and creation date. **Sync** pushes the contact's details to Stripe.
- **WebLock** - when the WebLock integration is active: the WebLock user ID, or a prompt to sync. **Sync** re-pushes every active access grant for this contact to WebLock and reports how many grants were pushed.

**Sync** needs Members **Edit**.

### Payment methods

Saved cards and bank accounts on file appear in **Payment Methods**, also opened from the **Payment method** tile. Use **+** (**Add card**) to save a new card. They are the same payment methods available when adding payments to invoices or membership charges.

## Worked examples

### Front-desk check-in for a padel member who owes money

1. Open the member from the [Member list](/members/member-list).
2. The **Due now** tile shows 30 in red - an area fee from last week.
3. Use **Add** > **Checkin** to record today's visit against their membership.
4. On the same profile, open the **Charges** tab and take payment for the outstanding 30, or redeem a voucher from the **Due now** tile if they have credit.

### Onboard a new jiu-jitsu member to the portal

1. Create or open the contact and click **Edit** to confirm the email is correct.
2. Click the member's name to open **Contact Information**, and under **User Account** click **Invite**. The block switches to show the invitation and its expiry.
3. If the member does not accept in time, use the block's menu to **Resend invitation**.

### Give a climbing member a door PIN

1. Make sure access control is enabled for your org, with PIN as an identification method.
2. Use the plus icon on the **Cards** tile (or **Add** > **Card**), choose **PIN code** as the card type, and enter the number. Leading zeros count.
3. Turn on **Make this their main card** if the PIN should be the member's main card, and save.

### Track belt progression for a martial arts student

1. Enable a progression track so the **Progression Levels** tile appears in the info strip.
2. Use the plus icon on the tile and **Assign Level** to record the student's current belt. Their first level sends no notice.
3. The tile then shows their sessions, days and skills toward the next belt. When it reads **Eligible for promotion**, click the belt and use **Promote**: the student gets a promotion notice.

## Tips & best practices

- Read the info strip before every front-desk interaction: it answers "do they owe anything?", "have they checked in today?", and "how active are they?" without opening a tab.
- Keep sensitive context (injuries, disputes, access notes) in the **Notes** panel, not in message threads - notes stay internal.
- Prefer **Archive** over deletion when a member leaves: archiving preserves their financial and legal history and can be reversed with **Unarchive**.
- Redeem vouchers and add wallet credit from the **Due now** tile so the credit balance updates in place.
- When a family shares billing, create the household from the paying member's profile so they are set as the owner automatically.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Invite** button under **User Account** | The contact has neither an email address nor a phone number, or you lack Members **Edit** | Click **Edit** and add an email or phone number, or ask an admin for the permission |
| **Due now** shows nothing, but the member has an unpaid booking | The booking has not started yet, so its charge is upcoming | Check **Upcoming** under **Due now**. To collect it ahead of time, use **Add** > **Payment** |
| No "No phone number" notice for a contact without a phone | Your gym does not require a phone number | Make the phone field required under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts** |
| **Reset password** missing from the **User Account** menu | The account is not active, you can manage neither organization settings nor staff, or it is your own account | Have someone with staff-management access send the reset |
| No **Cards** tile | Access control is not enabled for the org | Enable it under [access control settings](/settings/access-control) |
| **PIN code** missing from the card types | PIN is not an identification method enabled for the club | Enable it under [access control settings](/settings/access-control) |
| Stripe block missing | The contact has no Stripe customer yet | Expected. The block appears once the contact has a Stripe customer |
| WebLock block missing | The WebLock integration is not active | Connect the integration under **Settings** > **Integrations** |
| Only an **Unarchive** button in the header | The contact is archived | Unarchive to restore active use (needs Members **Edit**) |
| No **Send invite link** in the more-actions menu | The contact has neither an email address nor a phone number, or you can't send messages | Add an email or phone number |
| No **Engagement Scores** tile | Your gym doesn't calculate engagement scores, or the member has no active plan | Turn on **Engagement Scoring** under [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging#engagement-scoring) |
