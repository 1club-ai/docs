The member profile is the page for one contact: it pulls together their identity, balances, memberships, history, and integration links into one view. A **contact** is your organization's own copy of a person's record - name, email, phone, plus their memberships, invoices, and signatures. Front desk staff open it when a member is at the desk, and managers use it to fix records, set up access, and follow up on money owed.

## When to use it

The profile answers the questions staff ask most at the desk - does this person owe anything, have they checked in today, how active are they - without opening a tab. From the same page you add a booking, membership, check-in, or voucher redemption, keep internal notes, and manage the member's portal account, pipeline stage, cards, and household or company links.

Typical uses:

- **A padel member who owes money checks in** - the **Due from member** tile shows the outstanding court fee, so you record the visit and take payment in one place.
- **Getting a new jiu-jitsu member onto the portal** - confirm their email and send the invitation from the **User Account** block.
- **A door PIN for a climber** - add a PIN from the **Cards** block when access control is on.
- **Belt progression for a martial-arts student** - assign their current belt from the **Level** tile so it stays visible to the front desk.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps.

## Page layout

You land on the member profile after clicking a row in the [Member list](/members/member-list). The URL is `/members/list/{contactId}`.

The page has two columns:

- **Left sidebar** - the contact card: avatar, name, email, phone, accounts, custom fields, the **Pipeline** block, the user-account section, cards (when access control is on), integration links (Stripe, WebLock), payment methods, and a notes panel.
- **Main column** - an info strip across the top, then the underlined tabs underneath.

## Info strip

Tiles at the top of the main column summarize the contact's current state. Three tiles always show, and a fourth appears when progression tracking or assessments are enabled:

- **Due from member** - the amount this member currently owes. It turns red when the balance is negative (money is owed). A **Credit balance** sub-line shows any wallet credit on the account. Due and credit are shown separately and never netted against each other: a member who owes 100 and holds 100 in credit still shows 100 due. Use the redeem icon in this tile to redeem a voucher against the account.
- **Total paid** - lifetime amount paid, with an "of X billed" sub-line so you can see paid versus billed at a glance.
- **Attendance** - a sparkline of recent check-ins. The dot lights up when the member has already checked in today. Use the overflow icon to change the window: **Last 4 weeks**, **Last 12 weeks**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 12 months**, or **All time**. Your choice is remembered for the next profile you open.
- **Level** / **Progress** - current progression level, shown when progression tracks are enabled, and the member's overall assessment average (for example "Tennis juniors: 3.5/5") once your organization has an assessment template and you may view assessments club-wide. The tile is titled **Progress** when both are on. When the next level sets a number of sessions, the tile also shows the member's count toward it (for example "13 / 20 sessions to Blue belt") or **Eligible for promotion** - see [Sessions to the next level](/settings/progression#sessions-to-the-next-level). The plus icon assigns a level or adds an assessment; clicking the average opens the member's full [assessment history](/members/assessments#reviewing-a-members-assessments).

## Header actions

For an active contact the header has an **Add** menu, an **Edit** action, and a three-dot overflow menu holding **Suspend** (or **Reinstate**), **Stop SMS** (or **Allow SMS again**) when the contact has a phone number, and **Archive**. The **Add** menu creates, depending on your permissions and the features you use:

- **Booking** - opens the schedule pre-filtered to this contact.
- **Membership** - opens the membership dialog with the contact pre-selected.
- **Check-in** - records a visit (against an existing booking or membership, or a new booking or membership in one step).
- **Wallet** (or **Deposit** when a deposit is required) - tops up the member's wallet.
- **Voucher** - redeems a voucher against this contact's account.
- **Payment** - takes a payment toward what the member owes. Disabled while nothing is due.
- **Card** - issues an access card, when access control is on. See [Access cards](/members/member-cards).
- **Payment card** - saves a payment card to charge.
- **Assessment** - adds an assessment card, when assessments are set up.
- **Rental** - lends gear, when Rentals is on.
- **Message** - opens a new message pre-addressed to the contact in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

**Edit** opens the contact form. **Suspend** pauses the member without changing anything, and **Archive** removes them from active use. See [Suspend and archive](/members/suspend-and-archive) for which to reach for.

If the contact is archived, the header collapses to a single **Unarchive** action (visible to staff with the `members.update.organization` permission). An **archived member** is retained for financial and legal history but is hidden from listings, has its memberships and future bookings cancelled, and cannot log in until an admin unarchives it.

A **suspended member** keeps everything - record, plan, bookings, staff role - but cannot book, check in, or sign in until someone reinstates them. The profile shows a banner with a **Reinstate** button while they are suspended.

Both are scoped to your gym: the same person can stay an active member elsewhere.

## Tabs

Each tab shows a count badge and loads its own data on demand, so opening the profile stays fast even for contacts with long histories.

| Tab | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Activity** | Engagement gauge and activity timeline |
| **Bookings** | All bookings, including cancelled. Click through to the booking detail |
| **Memberships** | Every membership ever assigned. Edit, pause, resume, renew, or cancel from here. Shown to staff who can read memberships |
| **Check-ins** | Visit history |
| **Charges** | What this contact owes, with the payments and invoices against it |
| **Documents** | Signed documents (waivers, contracts), form responses, and documents the member still owes |
| **Notifications** | Which notifications the member receives, with any overrides of your defaults |
| **Matches** | Match history, shown only when ratings are enabled |
| **Rentals** | Gear the member has out, shown only when Rentals is on. The badge turns red while anything is overdue |

### Activity tab

The **Activity** tab stacks two elements:

- **Engagement gauge** - the member's engagement score and level. See [Engagement analytics](/analytics/engagement) for how the score is computed.
- **Activity timeline** - a chronological feed of the member's bookings, check-ins, messages, and membership activity.

## Editing the contact

Click **Edit** in the header. The form covers name, contact details, date of birth, profile image, address, and any custom fields configured under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**. A contact has no **Club** of their own: they belong to your whole organization, and each membership or booking records its own.

The contact's **type** (member, lead, drop-in, lapsed, contact, staff) is not edited here. It follows what the contact holds and does, and every change is logged. Click the type chip in the page header to open its type-change history.

## Pipeline

The **Pipeline** block on the contact card shows the contact's stage, source, and assigned staff member, each changed in place. For a contact who is not a customer and not yet in the pipeline, it offers **Add to pipeline**. See [Leads and pipeline](/members/leads).

## Notes

The notes panel at the bottom of the sidebar is a free-form running log. Type a note and press the add button (or Ctrl+Enter) to save it. Each note is timestamped; editing a note updates its timestamp. Use the pencil and trash icons on a note to edit or delete it. Notes are internal - members never see them.

## User account

The **User Account** block in the sidebar shows whether the contact has a member-portal login. Possible states:

- **Unregistered** - no portal account exists. When the contact has an email, an **Invite** button appears to send a portal invitation.
- The account exists (statuses such as **Active**, **Inactive**, **Suspended**, or an invitation still pending). The block shows the status chip, and the invitation expiry date while an invitation is outstanding.

The overflow menu on this block offers, depending on state:

- **Reset password** - available for an active account when you are an admin and the account is not your own.
- **Send invitation** / **Resend invitation** - re-sends a portal invitation while one is outstanding.
- **Delete** - deletes an outstanding invitation, or removes the portal login link, for anyone but yourself.

## Cards and PIN

When [access control](/settings/access-control) is enabled, the **Cards** block lists every access card, PIN, or QR code this contact holds, with a star on their main card. From here you can:

- **Issue card** - scan a card on the reader (or type its code, or enter a PIN) to give it to this member. A card already on file as club stock is issued the same way.
- **Remove from member** - takes a card off the member. It stops working right away, and scanning it again later issues it fresh.

A **PIN** is a number the member types at a kiosk or door. See [Access cards](/members/member-cards) for card types and how issuing works.

## Accounts panel

The Accounts block in the sidebar shows every household and company the contact belongs to, with the contact's role on each. Click an account name to open the [account detail page](/members/accounts).

- When the contact belongs to **no account**, the block shows an **Add** button that creates a new account (household or company) with this contact locked in as the owner.
- When the contact **is on an account**, each account row shows the other members with their roles. The row's **Add** button attaches a new person to that account, and the trash icon detaches a member without deleting their contact record.

## Integrations

When the contact has been pushed to external systems, the sidebar shows:

- **Stripe customer** - customer ID, email, and creation date. **Sync** re-pulls Stripe's record.
- **WebLock** - the WebLock user ID, or a prompt to sync. **Sync** re-pushes every active access grant for this contact to WebLock and reports how many grants were pushed.

These blocks only appear when the corresponding integration is installed and connected for your org.

## Payment methods

Saved cards and bank accounts on file appear in the sidebar. They are the same payment methods available when adding payments to invoices or membership charges.

## Worked examples

### Front-desk check-in for a padel member who owes money

1. Open the member from the [Member list](/members/member-list).
2. The **Due from member** tile shows 30 in red - a court fee from last week.
3. Use **Add** > **Check-in** to record today's visit against their membership.
4. On the same profile, open the **Charges** tab and take payment for the outstanding 30, or redeem a voucher from the **Due from member** tile if they have credit.

### Onboard a new jiu-jitsu member to the portal

1. Create or open the contact and click **Edit** to confirm the email is correct.
2. In the **User Account** block, click **Invite**. The block switches to show the invitation and its expiry.
3. If the member does not accept in time, use the overflow menu to **Resend invitation**.

### Give a climbing member a door PIN

1. Make sure access control is enabled for your org, with PIN as an identification method.
2. In the **Cards** block, click **Issue card**, choose **PIN code** as the card type, and enter the number. Leading zeros count.
3. Turn on **Make this their main card** if the PIN should be the member's main card, and save.

### Track belt progression for a martial arts student

1. Enable progression tracks so the **Level** tile appears in the info strip.
2. Use the plus icon on the **Level** tile to assign the student's current belt.
3. The **Activity** tab's timeline and the info strip keep the level visible for front-desk staff.

## Tips & best practices

- Read the info strip before every front-desk interaction: it answers "do they owe anything?", "have they checked in today?", and "how active are they?" without opening a tab.
- Keep sensitive context (injuries, disputes, access notes) in the **Notes** panel, not in message threads - notes stay internal.
- Prefer **Archive** over deletion when a member leaves: archiving preserves their financial and legal history and can be reversed with **Unarchive**.
- Redeem vouchers and add wallet credit from the **Due from member** tile so the credit balance updates in place.
- When a family shares billing, create the household from the paying member's profile so they are set as the owner automatically.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Invite** button in the User Account block | The contact has no email address | Click **Edit** and add an email, then reopen the block |
| **Reset password** missing from the overflow menu | The account is not active, you are not an admin, or it is your own account | Have an admin reset it; you cannot reset your own password from another user's profile |
| **Cards** block not visible | Access control is not enabled for the org | Enable it under [access control settings](/settings/access-control) |
| **PIN code** missing from the card types | PIN is not an identification method enabled for the club | Enable it under [access control settings](/settings/access-control) |
| Stripe or WebLock block missing | The integration is not installed or connected, or the contact has not been pushed yet | Connect the integration, then use **Sync** on the block once it appears |
| Only an **Unarchive** button in the header | The contact is archived | Unarchive to restore active use (requires `members.update.organization`) |
