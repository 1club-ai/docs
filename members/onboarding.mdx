Member onboarding is the path from a brand new person to a fully set-up member: they complete a signup form, accept any required documents, and land in your member list with the right plan attached. New members, and parents signing up their children, go through it on your website and member portal. You design the form itself under [Forms](/marketing/forms); this page explains the experience a new member goes through and the settings that shape it.

## When to use it

A short signup that collects only what you need turns interest into a paying member in one sitting, with documents signed and the plan attached. The settings on this page decide how short that path is: which fields are asked, whether family members can join in the same flow, and whether a visitor can book before they sign up.

Typical uses:

- **Prospects from an ad** - someone who clicks a trial-plan ad picks the plan, fills in a few details, signs the waiver, and pays in one short flow.
- **Parents enrolling kids** - a parent registers two children for a junior martial-arts program without becoming a member themselves, and each child gets their own record with a date of birth.
- **A first-time drop-in** - a visitor books a class from your website with just their name and email, without creating an account first.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the settings behind each of these.

## The signup wizard

New members complete signup through a step-by-step wizard. Depending on how the form is set up, the steps are **Plan**, **Your details**, **Agreements**, **Family agreements**, **Questions**, **Video**, **Review**, and **Payment**. Steps the form doesn't need are skipped. The wizard guides them and validates as they go:

- **Step intros** explain each step. For example, the details step shows "We'll use your email to create your account.", and the review step shows "Check that everything's correct before you submit."
- **Inline validation on Next** - if a required field is missing, the wizard scrolls to and focuses the first invalid field instead of failing silently.
- **Editable review** - each section on the review step has an **Edit** link that jumps back to that step.
- **Already-registered email** - if the email is taken, the form shows "This email is already registered." with a "Sign in here" link, rather than a generic error.
- **Who is joining** - a form's **Who is this form for?** setting decides whether it is for **Individuals**, **Children (registered by a parent)**, or **Individuals & children**. On a form for both, the details step asks **Who's joining?** with **Just me**, **Only my kids**, or **Me and my kids**, so a parent can enroll children without becoming a member themselves.

Drafts are saved in the member's browser as they go, so a half-finished signup survives a page refresh. Passwords and drawn signatures are never persisted in the draft.

### Combine plan and details into one step

Each signup form has a **Combine plan & user details into one step** toggle (off by default). Its help reads: "Plan choice and sign-up details share one step instead of two." Turn it on to shorten the wizard to a single first step that shows plan selection and account details together.

## Configuring which fields appear

Which standard fields the signup form asks for is set per organization under **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts**, in **Standard Fields**. Each field (**Last name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Date of birth**, **Gender**, and **Profile photo**) has an **Admin** column, for when staff add or edit a member, and a **Member** column, for when the member signs up or completes their own profile. In the **Member** column you choose one of:

- **Required** - the field is shown and must be filled in to submit.
- **Optional** - the field is shown but can be left empty.
- **Don't show** - the field is not shown at all. **Last name** and **Email** can't be hidden.

The **Applies to** column decides who the field is asked of at signup: the **Account holder**, each **Child** on the household step, or **Both**.

Out of the box, signup asks for last name and email, and doesn't show phone, date of birth, gender, or profile photo.

The server enforces these choices on submission - a hidden field cannot be back-filled by editing the request, and a required field that is empty rejects the signup.

## Family members on signup

The same **Settings** > **Members** > **Contacts** page has a **Show family members on signup** toggle ("Members can add family members while signing up."). Each form also has its own **Allow adding family members during signup** setting.

- **On** - the signup form shows a "Family members" section where the primary signer can add additional members (children, partner, etc.) in the same flow. Each family member submitted carries its own date of birth.
- **Off** - the family-members section is hidden. The server rejects any request that includes family members with HTTP 400, which protects you against forged requests that try to enrol additional people while the feature is disabled.

When **Date of birth** is set to **Required** and applies to children, the requirement applies to **every** family member as well - submitting a family member without a date of birth fails the signup. The public website and member portal validate this before the request is sent, so the user sees an inline message rather than a server error.

## Verification

- **Signer verification** - a form with **Confirm the signer's email or phone** turned on sends someone who signs without being signed in a code to their email, then WhatsApp, then a text message. If no code reaches them, they can still finish, and their signature waits on their profile for staff to approve. See [Signatures awaiting approval](/members/document-signatures#signatures-awaiting-approval).
- **Email and phone** - members confirm their email address and phone number from their portal profile. See [Member authentication](/members/member-authentication#verifying-an-email-address-and-a-phone-number).
- **Browser check (Turnstile)** - signup is protected by a Cloudflare Turnstile check. If something blocks it, the member sees an actionable message rather than a silent failure: "We couldn't verify your browser. This can happen with an ad blocker, a VPN, or a network filter (sometimes set for the whole device). Please try again, or continue with Apple or Google instead."

## Guest bookings

When a [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) has **Allow guest bookings** turned on, a logged-out visitor can book from your member portal or website without an account or password. What happens depends on the email they give:

- **An email you don't have on file** - they book with their name, email, and whatever else you require at signup. No code is asked, and no account is created: they stay a contact until they sign up themselves.
- **An email that matches a contact** - they must enter a one-time code sent to that email. The booking attaches to that contact, and they are signed in as a passwordless member.
- **An email that already has an account** - they are asked to sign in instead.

Guest bookings are not available on the 1club network marketplace, which always needs an account to book.

## Worked examples

- **Ad landing page** - share a signup form link that offers a single trial plan, with Phone optional and documents required, so prospects convert in one short flow.
- **Junior program** - set the form's **Who is this form for?** to **Children (registered by a parent)**, with Date of birth required for children, so each junior is enrolled with their own record.
- **Walk-in trial** - turn on guest bookings so a first-time visitor can book and pay for a drop-in without creating an account, then sign up later.

## Tips & best practices

- **Keep required fields minimal.** Every required field is a drop-off point; mark only what you truly need at signup and collect the rest later.
- **Attach documents through rules, not the form.** The wizard automatically shows any [document rules](/settings/waivers) that target new members, so a waiver added once applies to every form.
- **Test each form end to end** from a real signup before sharing the link.
