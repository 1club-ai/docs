## Get started with 1Club admin

This guide walks you through the first steps: sign up or sign in, complete your profile, add a club and a plan, and create your first member and booking.

## Step 1: Sign up or sign in

1. Open the admin at **https://admin.1club.ai**. Every organization uses the same address.
2. Click **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Continue with Facebook**, or type your email and click **Continue**. If you were invited, sign in with the email the invitation went to. See [Signing in](/reference/sign-in) for all supported methods.
3. If you belong to multiple organizations, you land in the one you used last. To switch, open the organization menu at the top of the left rail and pick another organization.

### New to 1Club?

If there is no account for your email, the sign-up form opens. After you create your account, a short setup wizard creates your organization and first club:

1. **Choose your club type** - a preset that sets up sports, amenities, and booking policies for you. You can change everything later.
2. **Sports** - confirm the sports you offer.
3. **Tell us about your club** - find your gym on Google, or enter its address manually.
4. **Almost there** - review your plan and confirm a few details. You start on Grow, free for 30 days, with no credit card. See [Platform subscription](/settings/platform-subscription).

Along the way you confirm your email address with a 6-digit code we send you. If the wizard finds that your gym is already on 1club, it stops you from creating a second copy and asks how you're connected to it. Choose **I work here** to send the gym a **Request access** so they can invite you, or **I'm a member here** to go to the gym's member sign-in.

If you started from the [marketplace](/marketing/marketplace) to list your gym, a different wizard collects your club type, sports, location, description, and amenities, then submits the listing for review.

## Step 2: Complete your profile

1. Open the account menu at the bottom of the left rail and choose **Profile Settings**.
2. Update your name and contact information. See [Profile](/settings/profile).
3. Choose **My Notifications** in the same menu if you want to adjust email or app notification preferences.

## Step 3: Set up your organization and gym

1. Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > **Details** and check your organization name and slug.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Clubs** and add at least one club (for example, your main location) with its name, address, and contact details.
3. Optionally configure **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Area types** and **Instructor types** so you can define areas and assign instructors later.

## Step 4: Configure billing (optional but recommended)

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and connect **Stripe** so you can collect payments.
2. Go to **Sales** > **Plans** and create at least one membership plan (name, price, billing interval). See [Plans](/sales/plans).
3. Optionally set up **Settings** > **Billing** > **General** (currency, tax rates, payment methods) and **Settings** > **Billing** > **Automated Billing** for bill runs.

## Step 5: Add your first member

1. Go to **Members** and add a new member (or use the member portal signup if enabled).
2. Sell them a membership from their profile. See [Memberships](/sales/memberships).
3. Optionally issue an access card if you use RFID or other access credentials. See [Member cards](/members/member-cards).

## Step 6: Create a booking or class

1. Go to **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Areas** and make sure you have at least one area (for example, a court or room). See [Areas](/settings/areas).
2. Go to **Schedule** and create a booking for the member and area. See [Calendar](/schedule/calendar).
3. Or open **Schedule** > **Classes** to create a class and assign an instructor; members can then book into the class. See [Classes](/schedule/classes).

## Next steps

- Set up the [Inbox](/inbox/overview) and [templates](/settings/templates) for member communication.
- Configure **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Branding** and the [Member portal](/settings/member-portal) so members can log in and book online.
- Read [Welcome to 1club](/) for a tour of every admin section.
