1Club has AI woven through the product. In the admin, staff get a chat copilot that can take real actions on their behalf: check members in, create bookings, change settings, draft messages, and answer questions about your data. On the member side, your portal and public website can carry an assistant that answers members' questions and books classes for them. This page maps out every AI surface and what each one lets you do.

## When it helps

- **Doing a task in one sentence** - type "check in Sarah for the 6pm class" and the copilot routes it to the booking assistant, does it, and confirms exactly what happened.
- **Asking about your numbers** - ask for peak hours, month-over-month comparisons, or revenue for a period in plain language.
- **Replying to the inbox faster** - generate a reply grounded in the message and your organization's data, check its tone and confidence score, and edit it before sending.
- **Letting members help themselves** - turn on the member assistant for your portal or website, so members can ask about classes, availability, and plans, and create or cancel their own bookings.

## The admin AI copilot

Open the AI panel from anywhere in the admin to chat with your copilot. Ask it something in plain language and it figures out what you want, then either answers or performs the action. Behind the panel is a set of specialized assistants, and a router reads your message and hands it to the right one automatically.

You don't have to think about which assistant handles what. Type "check in Sarah for the 6pm class" and it routes to booking; type "how do I set up pricing rules?" and it routes to help. When the copilot isn't confident which assistant you need, it asks a quick clarifying question instead of guessing.

### The agent picker

Next to the chat input is an **agent picker**. Leave it on the default and the router chooses for you. Or pick a specific assistant to keep the whole conversation focused on one job (for example, force every message to the configuration assistant while you set up your gym).

### Actions and confirmations

When an assistant completes something with a real effect - a booking created, a setting changed, a message drafted - the reply confirms exactly what happened. Assistants also surface clickable suggestion chips below their answers, so you can jump to a relevant page or continue with a follow-up in one tap.

## AI day summary on Home

The line under the greeting on [Home](/home/overview) is a one-sentence AI summary of the most relevant thing about today, such as the next class, how busy the day is, or an overdue invoice. It is scoped to the person reading it, so an instructor gets a summary of their own day, and it only mentions money and membership figures they are allowed to see.

## AI reply suggestions in the inbox

Inside a message thread, the compose pane has a **Generate AI reply** action. It drafts a response grounded in the original message plus your organization's data (retrieved with pgvector similarity search), and lands the suggestion in the editor for you to edit before sending. Each suggestion carries a tone and a confidence score so staff can review before anything goes out. The feature works on replies, not brand-new sends. See [Messaging](/inbox/overview).

## Suggestions: what needs your attention

The copilot's suggestion chips start with what it found in your gym today. Each chip states a finding with a real number - "3 invoices overdue · €375.00", "4 memberships end this week", "Spin Express today 18:30 is 4 of 12 booked" - and, underneath, what the copilot will do about it in one word: **Remind** for invoices, payments and renewals, **Invite** for members and classes.

### What it looks for

| Finding | What it means | What the copilot drafts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overdue invoices | Unpaid invoices past their due date, with what is still owed (the same figure as the Invoices dashboard) | One payment reminder to those members |
| Failed payments | Charges that failed in the last 7 days | A message asking them to update their card or pay at the desk |
| Unpaid charges | Charges from the last 7 days that are due and still unpaid, such as a court booking that has started and wasn't paid. A booking's charge is due once the visit starts | A reminder asking them to settle online or at the desk |
| Memberships ending | Active memberships ending within 7 days, without auto-renew, where the member hasn't bought what comes next | A renewal reminder |
| Regulars who stopped coming | Members with an active membership who came at least 4 times in the 8 weeks before the last 3, and not since, with nothing booked | A "we miss you" message |
| New members who never came in | Members who joined 5 to 30 days ago and have never booked or checked in | A first-visit welcome |
| A class short of bookings | A recurring class in the next 36 hours that is under 40% full, with regulars who haven't booked it | An invitation to those regulars |

### How it works

- **Click a finding and the facts appear at once.** The chat shows the members, invoices or regulars behind the chip straight away, as a table with each member's photo or initials, then the copilot writes the draft. The table shows the first five, with a count of the rest. The numbers always come from your data, never from the AI.
- **Each row opens its record.** Click a row in that table to open the invoice, the membership or the member. A charge opens what it was for, such as its booking, as it does from the Charges list.
- **Nothing is sent.** Every follow-up is saved as a draft in the inbox for you to review and send. **Review draft** opens it. For payment and charge findings, **View charges** opens the full Charges list.
- **Handled findings leave the list.** Once the copilot has drafted something for a finding, its chip disappears and the next finding takes its place. The chip comes back only if the finding changes (for example, a new membership starts ending).
- **Not now.** Hover any chip, a finding, a setup step or a question, and use its ✕ to hide it for a week in your browser.
- **Questions fill the rest.** Up to three findings show at a time; the remaining chips are next steps for setting up, or questions you can ask: about your numbers, who is booked tomorrow, what is still free to book, which memberships end this week, or how to use a feature. The questions change each time you start a new conversation, so you see more of what the copilot can do.

Findings show on the screens they belong to: Home shows them all, **Members** shows memberships, regulars and new members, **Schedule** shows classes, and **Billing** shows invoices, payments and unpaid charges. Urgent ones (overdue invoices, failed payments and unpaid charges) follow you to other screens when you open the copilot. Each finding needs the permission to see its data, so someone without billing access never sees invoice, payment or charge findings. Findings refresh every 15 minutes, and straight away after the copilot drafts or changes something.

## AI business audit

1Club can generate an AI-powered audit of your gym's online presence. The report scores your public business profile, review sentiment, website performance, and AI discoverability, then gives you recommendations to improve. A report is generated automatically after onboarding, and you can regenerate it from the admin. See [Business audit](/home/ai-visibility-report).

## Member-facing assistant

Your members can have their own AI assistant, available in the member portal and on your public website. It answers questions about classes, availability, instructors, plans, and gym locations, and it can act on the member's behalf - creating and cancelling their bookings, and looking up their profile, bookings, and memberships.

The member assistant is off by default and enabled per surface:

- **Portal** - turn on the assistant for your member portal.
- **Website** - turn on the assistant for your public website (the site must be active).

Member chat is rate-limited so a single account can't fire off unlimited requests.

## Configure agents and review history

Go to **Settings** > **Artificial Intelligence** to manage the AI experience:

- **AI assistants (MCP)** connects outside AI clients to your organization. See [MCP](/mcp/overview).
- **Agents** (`/settings/ai/agents`) lists the available admin assistants with descriptions and example prompts, so your team knows what the copilot can do.
- **Conversation History** (`/settings/ai/conversations`) lets you browse past AI chat sessions and read the full back-and-forth of each one.

## Availability

AI features depend on your organization's plan and configuration. The admin copilot and AI reply suggestions need AI enabled for your organization; the member-facing assistant is off until you turn it on for the portal or website; and the day summary on **Home** stays blank when AI can't run. If an AI surface isn't showing up, check your plan and the relevant settings first.
