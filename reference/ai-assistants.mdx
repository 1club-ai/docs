1club has AI woven through the product. In the admin, staff get a chat copilot that can take real actions on their behalf: check members in, create bookings, change settings, draft messages, and answer questions about your data. On the member side, your portal and public website can carry an assistant that answers members' questions and books classes for them. This page maps out every AI surface and what each one lets you do.

## When it helps

- **Doing a task in one sentence** - type "check in Sarah for the 6pm class" and the copilot does it and confirms exactly what happened.
- **Asking about your numbers** - ask for peak hours, month-over-month comparisons, or revenue for a period in plain language.
- **Replying to the inbox faster** - generate a reply grounded in the message and your organization's data, check its tone and confidence score, and edit it before sending.
- **Letting members help themselves** - turn on the member assistant for your portal or website, so members can ask about classes, availability, and plans, and create or cancel their own bookings.

## The admin AI copilot

Open the AI panel from anywhere in the admin to chat with your copilot. Ask it something in plain language and it either answers or performs the action. It is one assistant that holds every tool you are allowed to use, so a request that mixes jobs, such as "find Sarah, book her into the 6pm class, and draft her a welcome message", works in a single conversation. The same assistant is available in ChatGPT and Claude through [MCP](/mcp/connect).

### Opening and hiding it

- **On Home**, the copilot is docked on the right side of the dashboard and stays there, whether you keep it open or minimized elsewhere.
- **On every other page**, click the 1club icon at the right end of the page's header to open the copilot on the right, and click it again to put it away. A small dot on the icon means the copilot found something on that page, such as overdue invoices. On the Schedule, which has no page header, it sits at the right end of the row above the calendar, and on the point of sale at the right of the cart panel's header. In the website editor it sits at the right of the editor panel's tabs, and the copilot takes the panel's place while it is open.
- **From the keyboard**, press **Cmd + .** on a Mac or **Ctrl + .** on Windows to show or hide it on any page.
- Open or minimized, it stays the way you left it as you move between pages and after a reload. Close it with the **X** at the top of the panel.
- When a page has its own side panel, such as a member's details or the point of sale cart, the copilot sits beside it. It keeps the same width on every page.

On a phone, tap the 1club icon in the page's header to open the copilot full screen.

### Full screen and your conversations

Click the **Full screen** button at the top right of the copilot panel to give it the whole page. The conversation you were in carries over, even while a reply is still being written, and the page's address names it, so you can bookmark or reopen it later.

Each conversation is a piece of **work**. On the left you see **Your work**, newest first, grouped as **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**:

- Each piece of work is named in a few words after its first reply. Until then it shows your first question.
- An amber dot marks work that is waiting for you to **Confirm** or choose **Not now** on something the copilot was about to do.
- **Search your work** finds one by its name or its first question.
- **New work** starts a fresh one. Its page asks **What would you like to work on?**, under [For you today](#for-you-today).
- Click a piece of work to carry on where you left it. Your browser's Back and Forward buttons move between the work you opened.

Click **Exit full screen** at the top right to go back to the page you came from, with the copilot as you left it. On a phone, the list sits behind the list button at the top left. You only ever see your own work.

### What it can do

- **Bookings and check-ins** - check members in, create and cancel bookings, look up member schedules, and create memberships.
- **Members and messages** - find a member by name, email, or phone, and draft a message to them. The draft is saved for you to review and send from the inbox - the assistant composes it, you send it.
- **Settings and setup** - change organization settings and create or edit entities such as areas, area types, policies, templates, and plans, and draft gym events.
- **Data and insights** - answer questions about bookings, memberships, check-ins, contacts, and revenue, including trends, peak hours, and month-over-month comparisons.
- **Help and docs** - answer how-to and what-is questions grounded in the 1club documentation and your live settings, and point you to the right page.
- **Websites** - read your own website and your competitors' (see [Reading websites](#reading-websites)).

### Guided setups

Some jobs in 1club take several steps in the right order. For these the copilot follows a built-in guide, a playbook, so you can ask for the outcome in your own words and it does the steps for you:

1. It reads what you already have first, so it reuses an existing tag or promotion instead of making a near-duplicate.
2. It asks only for what it can't work out, in one question, and says what it chose for the rest.
3. Anything with a real effect waits for your **Confirm**, shown with a preview of what will be created.
4. It finishes the follow-ups you might not think of, such as bookings people already hold.

The copilot follows these guides today:

| Guide | Ask something like | What it sets up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standing discount for a group** | "Our league team gets 15% off every court booking. Can you make that automatic?" | A tag for the group and an auto-apply discount for that tag, the people tagged, and the discount offered on their upcoming unpaid bookings. See [a standing discount for a tagged group](/sales/promotions#example-a-standing-discount-for-a-tagged-group). |
| **Gym events** | "Plan a padel social for Saturday evening." | A draft event with a title, description, time, place and photo, for you to refine and publish. See [Events](/events/events). |

Once a group rate is running, ask in the same way to keep it up: "Lou has left the team, take him off the rate", "What would Sara pay for Court 4 on Saturday?", or "Book Dan on Court 1 tomorrow at 7pm, he's paying at the desk". The copilot books and quotes with the discount already off, and records it against the promotion.

### Reading websites

The copilot and the [website builder](#website-builder) can read a website for you: its pages, prices and services, contact details, and its brand (colours, fonts and logo). Try:

- "Take the colours and fonts from our old website."
- "Write our About page from what is on our current site."
- "Compare our monthly prices with our competitors'."

It only reads websites you trust it with: your own (the website on your organization and your gyms), the gyms on your [Competitors](/marketing/competitors) list, and up to 10 sites you name in the conversation. A link or an instruction on a page it reads never sends it anywhere else, it follows each site's robots.txt, and when it doesn't know which page has the answer, it searches inside those sites only, never the rest of the web.

A competitor's website is for comparing and for ideas: the copilot never copies a competitor's text, photos or logo onto your site. Your own old website it can reuse freely.

### Members, bookings and classes as cards

When the answer is about specific members, bookings or classes, the assistant shows them as cards instead of writing them out: a list of members with their photo and last visit, a member with their memberships, bookings with their status and amount, or classes with how full they are and who is waiting. The cards are loaded from your club's records with your permissions, so they always show what the admin shows. Click a row to open that member, booking or class. A long list shows its first rows, with **Show all** to see the rest.

Above the answer, a line such as **Worked through 3 steps** says how the assistant got there. Click it to see each step, for example looking up members or reading a report.

### Permissions and confirmations

The assistant can only do what you can do. Each tool is checked against your permissions the same way the admin screens are, so an instructor's assistant can't change settings or see figures the instructor can't see.

When an action has a real effect - a booking created, a setting changed, a message drafted - the reply confirms exactly what happened. Creating a promotion, deleting something or cancelling a booking is never done in one step: the assistant says what it is about to do and shows a **Confirm** button with **Not now** beside it. Nothing changes until you press **Confirm**; **Not now** leaves everything as it is. Suggestion chips below its answers let you jump to a relevant page or continue with a follow-up in one tap.

### Website builder

The website editor has its own assistant for designing and updating your public website by chat - adding pages and sections, populating them from your organization data, editing the header and footer, and adjusting styling. See [Website](/marketing/website).

## Search and quick actions

Press **Cmd + K** on a Mac or **Ctrl + K** on Windows from any page to open search, or click **Search everything** in the middle of the page's header. On a phone, tap **Search** in the tab bar at the bottom.

Before you type, search shows the members, bookings, and pages you opened recently, and quick actions: **New booking**, **Add a member**, **Sell a membership**, and **Check in a member**. Each quick action appears only if you have the permission its page asks for.

Type to search members, bookings, memberships, invoices, plans, gyms, areas, and every admin page, setting, and report. Matching works across scripts and accents, so a name typed in Latin letters finds one written in Cyrillic.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Left / Right arrows** | Switch between **All**, **Members**, **Bookings**, **Memberships**, **Invoices**, **Plans**, **Pages**, and **Actions** (when nothing is typed) |
| **/** | Show filters for the current type, such as **Today** or **Upcoming** bookings, **Ending soon** memberships, or **Unpaid** and **Overdue** invoices |
| **Up / Down arrows** and **Enter** | Move through the results and open one |
| **Tab** | Ask the copilot instead. What you typed, or the highlighted member or booking, goes with your question |
| **Esc** | Close |

On a phone, the filters are a row of chips and the sparkle button next to the search field asks the copilot.

## AI day summary on Home

The line under the greeting on [Home](/home/overview) is a one-sentence AI summary of the most relevant thing about today, such as the next class, how busy the day is, or an overdue invoice. It is scoped to the person reading it, so an instructor gets a summary of their own day, and it only mentions money and membership figures they are allowed to see.

## AI reply suggestions in the inbox

Inside a message thread, the compose pane has a **Generate AI reply** action. It drafts a response grounded in the original message plus your organization's data (retrieved with pgvector similarity search), and lands the suggestion in the editor for you to edit before sending. Each suggestion carries a tone and a confidence score so staff can review before anything goes out. The feature works on replies, not brand-new sends. See [Messaging](/inbox/overview).

## Suggestions: what needs your attention

The copilot's suggestion chips start with what it found in your gym today. Each chip states a finding with a real number - "3 invoices overdue · €375.00", "4 memberships end this week", "Spin Express today 18:30 is 4 of 12 booked" - and, underneath, what the copilot will do about it in one word: **Remind**, **Settle**, **Invite** or **Set**.

### What it looks for

| Finding | What it means | What the copilot does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overdue invoices | Unpaid invoices past their due date, with what is still owed (the same figure as the Invoices dashboard) | **Remind**: one payment reminder to those members |
| Failed payments | Charges that failed in the last 7 days | **Settle**: sorts them by why they failed, for you to settle (see [Settling unpaid charges](#settling-unpaid-charges)) |
| Unpaid charges | Charges from the last 7 days that are due and still unpaid | **Settle**: sorts them by why they are unpaid |
| Memberships ending | Active memberships ending within 7 days, without auto-renew, where the member hasn't bought what comes next | **Remind**: a renewal reminder |
| Regulars who stopped coming | Members with an active membership who came at least 4 times in the 8 weeks before the last 3, and not since, with nothing booked | **Invite**: a "we miss you" message |
| New members who never came in | Members who joined 5 to 30 days ago and have never booked or checked in | **Invite**: a first-visit welcome |
| A class short of bookings | A recurring class in the next 36 hours that is under 40% full, with regulars who haven't booked it | **Invite**: an invitation to those regulars |
| Kids' classes with no age group | Class types booked almost only by children in the last 8 weeks, but with no age group, so the 1club App suggests them to adults | **Set**: works out the age groups to set |
| Setup | A new gym's setup checklist, while it is still setting up | **Continue setup**: guides you through the steps, doing what it can for you |

### How it works

- **Click a finding and it opens as its own piece of work.** The members, invoices or charges behind it appear straight away as cards, then the copilot writes the draft. The numbers always come from your data, never from the AI. See [For you today](#for-you-today).
- **Each row opens its record.** Click a row to open the invoice, the charge, the membership or the member.
- **Nothing is sent.** Every follow-up is saved as a draft in the inbox for you to review and send. **Review draft** opens it.
- **Handled findings leave the list.** Once someone on your team takes a finding, its chip leaves the list and the next finding takes its place. The chip comes back only if the finding changes (for example, a new membership starts ending).
- **All clear.** When nothing needs attention, the strip says what it checked, for example "All clear: invoices, payments and renewals look fine".
- **Questions fill the rest.** Up to three findings show at a time; the remaining chips are questions you can ask about your numbers, or next steps for setting up.

Findings show on the screens they belong to: Home shows all but the age-group finding, **Members** shows memberships, regulars and new members, **Schedule** shows classes and age groups, and the finance pages under **Sales** show invoices, payments and unpaid charges. Urgent ones (overdue invoices, failed payments and unpaid charges) follow you to other screens when you open the copilot. Each finding needs the permission to see its data, so someone without billing access never sees invoice or payment findings. Findings refresh every 15 minutes, and straight away after the copilot drafts or changes something.

## For you today

**For you today** sits at the top of your work in the [full-screen assistant](#full-screen-and-your-conversations). It opens with how many things need you today, then lists every finding you may see, with who on your team is already on which, and how many are handled ("2 of 5 handled"). When nothing needs you, it says so.

Each finding is one of:

- **Not taken yet** - click it to take it.
- **In your work** - you took it. Click it to carry on.
- **... is on it** - a colleague took it, for example "Maya is on it". You don't see their work, but you won't remind the same members twice.
- **Resolved** or **Done** - the members paid, renewed, came in or booked, so nothing is left to do.

For a new gym that is still setting up, setup is the first piece of work: the copilot starts it for whoever may set the gym up, says where the gym stands, recommends one step at a time, and does the steps it can from what the gym already has.

### A piece of work for each finding

Take a finding and it opens as its own piece of work: the members, invoices or charges behind it as cards, then a draft written for exactly those people. Your team sees that you are on it, so nobody reminds the same members twice.

Not today? Click **Snooze** and pick **Tomorrow**, **Monday** or **Next week** (the copilot suggests one). A snooze is yours alone and follows you to any device; the finding comes back at that time.

### Settling unpaid charges

Unpaid and failed charges come grouped by why they are unpaid, because the reason decides what to do:

| Group | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Played, not paid** | The visit happened and no payment was recorded. |
| **Player's share** | A player's share of someone else's booking. |
| **Late cancellation** | Cancelled too close to the start. |
| **No-show** | Marked as a no-show. |
| **Membership** | A membership charge still open. |
| **Insufficient funds** | The card was short. A retry after payday often works. |
| **Bank wants approval** | The bank asked the member to approve the payment. |
| **Card expired or lost** | The saved card can no longer be charged. |
| **Declined** | The bank declined it without saying why. |
| **Other** | No visit or membership explains it. |

Each charge shows the facts behind it, such as when they checked in, how long before the start they cancelled, or their next visit. Settle a whole group from its card, or one charge from its row:

- **Record desk payment** - the member paid at the desk; pick the payment method.
- **Charge saved card** - charge the member's saved card again.
- **Waive** - drop a late cancellation or no-show charge. A reason is required.
- **Collect at next visit** - leave it for the desk, which sees it at the member's next booked visit.

The card asks you to confirm once, naming the amount and what will happen. You only see the buttons your permissions allow, and only the person who took the finding can press them. A card payment the provider hasn't confirmed yet shows as **Processing**, never as failed, so nobody takes the money twice.

### Checking back

Once you send the message, the work shows who it reached. Your next briefing checks back on the same members: "2 of 3 paid since your reminder", "1 of 4 came back since your message", with a follow-up for the rest in one click. For charges you settled, it counts what each one still owes. When nobody is left, the work is done and the copilot suggests what to take next.

## AI business audit

1club can generate an AI-powered audit of your gym's online presence. The report scores your public business profile, review sentiment, website performance, and AI discoverability, then gives you recommendations to improve. A report is generated automatically after onboarding, and you can regenerate it from the admin. See [Business audit](/home/ai-visibility-report).

## Member-facing assistant

Your members can have their own AI assistant, available in the member portal and on your public website. It answers questions about classes, availability, instructors, plans, and gym locations, and it can act on the member's behalf - creating and cancelling their bookings, and looking up their profile, bookings, and memberships.

The member assistant is off by default and enabled per surface:

- **Portal** - turn on the assistant for your member portal.
- **Website** - turn on the assistant for your public website (the site must be active).

Member chat is rate-limited so a single account can't fire off unlimited requests.

## AI settings and history

Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **AI assistants (MCP)** to connect outside AI clients, such as ChatGPT or Claude, to your organization, and to see and revoke your connections. See [MCP](/mcp/overview) and [Connect an assistant](/mcp/connect). Who may connect one is set by the **AI assistants (MCP)** permission; see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions#ai-assistants-mcp).

Your past conversations with the copilot live in the copilot itself: open it [full screen](#full-screen-and-your-conversations) to browse, search and continue your work.

## Availability

AI features depend on your organization's plan and configuration. The admin copilot and AI reply suggestions need AI enabled for your organization; the member-facing assistant is off until you turn it on for the portal or website; and the day summary on **Home** stays blank when AI can't run. If an AI surface isn't showing up, check your plan and the relevant settings first.
