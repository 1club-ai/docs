1club decides what an admin user can do from **permissions**, not from their job title. A **role** is a default bundle of permissions, and each staff member holds a role **in each gym they work in**: the same person can be an instructor in one gym and a manager in another. Every operation in the admin is gated on a specific permission, so two people with different roles can do the same thing whenever they hold the same permission.

This page covers the roles, how a person gets a role in each gym, the permission model behind them, exactly what each permission allows, and how to change a role's permissions in **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**.

## Overview

- **Built-in roles** - Administrator, Manager, Front Desk, Sales, Instructor, and Member. You cannot create another named role.
- **A role per gym** - an administrator reaches every gym, including gyms you add later. Everyone else holds a role in each gym they are assigned to, set in **Staff** > **Team** > **Club access**. A gym with no assignment is closed to them.
- **Permissions decide access** - a permission is a module and an action (`booking.read`), with a **level** where the action really behaves differently at different levels (`booking.read.own`).
- **Actions** - View, Create, Edit, Delete, and in a few modules Export, plus **Full access**, which grants all of a module's actions at once.
- **Levels** - Own, Standard, and Extended (and Limited, for viewing members only). A level never opens another gym: which gyms a person reaches comes from their assignments.
- **Customizable per role** - an admin edits which permissions each role grants in **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**. A change applies to that role in every gym.
- **Owner and super admin** - two capabilities outside the role list that bypass permission checks by design.

## Roles

The **Member** role has no admin access at all; members use the member portal and the 1club App.

| Role | Typical use | Default access |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Administrator** | Owner or main administrator | Full access to every module in every gym, including settings and the permission editor. Cannot be customized. |
| **Manager** | Gym or department manager | Runs the gym: members and memberships, the timetable, bookings, check-ins, attendance, assessments, marketing, analytics, inventory, rentals, cash, and the till. Manages staff. Views and takes payments, voids charges, and refunds payments, but does not configure billing. No settings access. |
| **Front Desk** | Receptionist or front-of-house staff | Works the desk: looks members up, signs them up to a plan, books them in and cancels bookings, checks them in, scores their training, runs the till, lends gear, handles the inbox. Reads the timetable and catalog without editing them. No staff, marketing, or settings access. |
| **Sales** | Salesperson or membership advisor | Works the [pipeline](/members/leads) and turns leads into members: adds and edits contacts (a lead, its stage, source and owner), books the trial and moves it when asked, writes to the lead, signs them up to a membership and takes its payment as the desk does, and reads the Sales analytics. Holds neither the books nor the till (no billing or point of sale), no staff or settings access, and deletes nothing by default. Sales staff are listed first when a lead is assigned, and a conversion is credited to the lead's owner. |
| **Instructor** | Coach or teacher | Own world only: reads their own timetable, runs the bookings and attendance on it, sees every member by first name and last initial (no contact details), assesses the members of the sessions they teach, reads their own earnings and stats, sends their own messages. |
| **Member** | End member | No admin access. Uses the member portal and the 1club App. |

<Note>
You may see **staff** on some screens and reports. That is a label for any organization user who can reach the admin (Administrator, Manager, Front Desk, Sales, or Instructor), not another stored role.
</Note>

### Owner

An organization can mark one or more administrators as **owner**. Owners bypass permission checks entirely, and only an owner can delete the organization. Owner is a flag on the user's membership, not a role. See [Users](/settings/admin-users).

### Super admin

Super admin is a 1club platform capability used by the 1club team for support and provisioning. It bypasses organization permission checks and is not something you assign to your own staff.

## A role in each gym

Which gyms a person can work in, and what they can do there, are two separate questions:

- **Club access** decides the gyms. An administrator reaches every gym, now and in future. Everyone else reaches only the gyms they are assigned to, each with its own role. Adding a new gym does not give existing staff access to it.
- **The role's permissions** decide what they can do in each of those gyms.

Assign roles per gym from **Staff** > **Team**: open the person's **Club access** and pick **Instructor**, **Front Desk**, **Sales**, **Manager** or **No access** for each gym. When you invite someone, you choose their gyms in the invitation. See [Users](/settings/admin-users#roles-per-club).

### Records that belong to every gym

Some records belong to the whole organization rather than one gym: member profiles, marketing, the website, staff. For those, a person's permissions are the combination of all their gym roles. A manager in one gym who is an instructor in another reaches shared records as a manager; someone who is an instructor in every gym reaches them as an instructor. Changing every one of a manager's gyms to Instructor therefore also removes their manager access to shared records. Removing every assignment removes their staff access altogether.

Member profiles are shared across the organization, so staff in any of their gyms find the same member. A member's activity is not: bookings, attendance, assessments and payments stay visible only for the gyms the staff member can work in.

### Working in one gym or all of them

What a person can do also follows the gym selected in the breadcrumb at the top of the page:

- **With one gym selected**, that gym's role alone decides, for its own records and the shared ones. A manager in Gym 1 who is front desk in Gym 2 works as front desk while Gym 2 is selected, and reaches Analytics, staff administration and settings only from Gym 1 or **All clubs**.
- **With All clubs selected** (offered to anyone who works in more than one gym), each gym's records answer to the role there, so that same person's Analytics shows Gym 1's figures and none of Gym 2's. Shared records answer to the combined roles.
- Marketing, the website and the Permissions page belong to no gym, so they always use the combined roles.

### Who may change club access

- **Administrators and owners** assign any role in any gym.
- **Managers** (with Staff: Full access) assign lower roles, and may promote someone to Manager, in the gyms they manage. They cannot change their own access, another manager's, an administrator's, or anyone's access in a gym they don't manage.

Changes to club access and to a role's permissions take effect the next time the person's session refreshes, within about 15 minutes, or at once if they sign out and back in.

## How a permission is built

A permission names a **module** and an **action**, and a **level** where the action has more than one: `booking.read.own` is "view bookings, the ones that are mine".

- **Module** - the area of the product, such as Members, Bookings, or Billing.
- **Action** - what you may do there: View, Create, Edit, Delete, Export, or Full access.
- **Level** - how far the action goes, only where it really behaves differently: Own, Standard, or Extended.

An operation always requires the **narrowest** permission that actually fits it. A screen that only lists things requires View. A button that changes something requires Create, Edit, or Delete.

### Actions

| Action | Permission key | What it means |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **View** | `read` | See the data. Every list, detail page, and picker in the module. |
| **Create** | `create` | Add new records. |
| **Edit** | `update` | Change records that already exist. |
| **Delete** | `delete` | Remove records, and in several modules cancel them (see the module reference below). |
| **Export** | `export` | Download the module's records, where the module offers it (Members, Bookings, Billing). |
| **Full access** | `manage` | Everything the module offers. Granting Full access grants the other actions together, plus any configuration the module keeps behind it. On Bookings it is also what lets staff override booking rules, and on Classes what lets them override scheduling conflicts. |

Not every module offers every action. The editor only shows an action when granting it changes something real, so Marketing offers View and Full access while Members offers View, Create, Edit, Delete and Export. The [module reference](#what-each-permission-allows) lists what each one actually has.

### Levels

| Level | Reaches |
|-------|---------|
| **Own** | The person's own records: bookings assigned to them, bookings on a class they teach, their own messages, their own earnings. It does not mean "records I created". |
| **Limited** | Members View only: every member, by first name and last initial with their photo, and no contact details. |
| **Standard** | Every record of that kind in the gyms the person can work in, within the operation's ordinary limits. |
| **Extended** | The extra behavior a few operations support, such as correcting attendance on a past day or deleting a payment record. |

A higher level covers the lower ones: Extended covers Standard, and Standard covers Own. So a user holding "View bookings (Standard)" passes any check for "View bookings (Own)".

Levels describe what someone can do to records they can already reach. They never reach another gym: a manager whose role holds Extended in Gym 1 still has no access to Gym 2 unless they are assigned there.

Levels are why an Instructor stays in their own world. Their permissions are Own, so they run their own classes, bookings, and check-ins but cannot reach billing, staff, or settings, even where the action matches.

<Note>
Before October 2026 the levels were called **Organization**, **Club** and **Own**, and described geography. Organization became **Extended** and Club became **Standard**; which gyms a person reaches moved to their club access. Saved customizations carried over unchanged.
</Note>

### How the hierarchy plays out

Both hierarchies apply at the same time, so one grant can satisfy several checks:

- Full access covers the other actions on the same module.
- Extended covers Standard, and Standard covers Own.

A Manager holding **Bookings: Full access** therefore passes a check for "View bookings (Own)" without holding it literally. That is the point of the model: you grant the least a person needs, and broader holders pass automatically.

<Warning>
**Extended** is what unlocks the sensitive operations, so grant it deliberately. By default only an administrator or owner holds them: deleting the website, deleting a payment record, deleting a booking outright, removing attendance from a past day, checking someone in outside the check-in window or recovering a no-show, booking into a session that has already ended, and changing the entrance method after the booking's day. Granting the matching action at **Extended** delegates it, with no separate checkbox saying so.
</Warning>

## What each permission allows

The editor groups the modules under **Members**, **Schedule**, **Sales**, **Marketing** and **Organization**. Each module below lists the actions it offers and what they unlock in the product.

Your own profile and notification preferences need no permission: every staff member can always change their own. Setting notification defaults for a whole role (**Settings** > **Organization** > **Notifications**) is organization configuration and needs settings access, so it is administrator and owner only.

### Members

The people and their records: contacts, members, tags, custom fields, and billing accounts.

A member belongs to the organization, not to one of its gyms - the membership, booking, check-in, and order each carry their own club. So staff in any of their gyms find the same member, instead of creating a duplicate. What they see of the member's activity still stops at the gyms they work in.

Members **View** has three levels, and only the top one is the member record:

| Level | What it shows | Default holders |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Own** | The members you teach (your bookings and the attendees of your classes), names only. | Nobody by default. |
| **Limited** | Every member, names only: first name, last initial and photo, no contact details. | Instructors. |
| **Standard** | The full member profile with contact details, and every page built on it. | Front Desk, Sales, Manager, Administrator. |

Own and Limited power the instructor's **Members** page and member picker. To let a coach see full member details, give the Instructor role Members: **View (Standard)**.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | At **Standard**: the member list, a member's profile, and every member picker in the admin. Below Standard: names only, as above. |
| **Create** | Add a member or contact. Also creates billing accounts, tag definitions, custom-field definitions, and recorded matches. |
| **Edit** | Amend an existing record: details, notes, household links, PIN, tags, email preferences, invitations, member type, progression, and unarchiving. |
| **Delete** | Delete a member or a billing account, archive a member, purge the archive, delete a tag or custom-field definition, delete a progression history entry, **and merge two profiles**. |
| **Export** | Export members and contacts from [Export](/settings/export). |
| **Full access** | All of the above. |

Two things are worth knowing here:

- **Merging is a Delete.** A merge absorbs one profile into another and removes it, so a role with Edit can correct a duplicate but cannot resolve it by merging.
- **Archiving is a Delete, unarchiving is an Edit.** Archiving takes a member out of every list and can end their memberships, so it needs Delete. Bringing an archived member back needs only Edit.
- **Viewing a member does not include their memberships.** A role with member View but no membership View sees the profile - name, contact details, tags, bookings - with the memberships section empty. The expiring-memberships block on Home disappears the same way.

### Memberships

The plans and passes a member holds: subscriptions, punch cards, and their remaining allowance.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The memberships list and detail, plus the active, expiring, and stats views. |
| **Create** | Sign a member up from their profile or from the memberships list. This charges the payment, honours the plan's upfront-payment rule, and records the charge. |
| **Edit** | Extend, renew, pause, resume, top up remaining visits, apply or remove a promotion, and reconcile. Also allows selling on credit by overriding the upfront-payment requirement. |
| **Delete** | Delete a membership, **and cancel one**. |
| **Full access** | All four. |

- **Cancelling is a Delete.** It ends the membership and triggers its refunds, so a role with Edit can extend a membership but cannot call it off.
- **Selling a pass as part of a visit is not this module.** The "Add plan or pass" tab inside the booking and check-in dialogs, and selling at the till, ride along with the booking or check-in itself. Whoever may check a member in may also sell them the thing that gets them in.

### Messages

The inbox, message threads, templates, and shared email inboxes.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | Read messages and threads. At **Own**, only their own. |
| **Create** | Compose, edit a draft, delete a draft, and send. |
| **Full access** | Also the shared surfaces: create, verify, and delete organization email inboxes, and create, edit, and delete message templates. |

### Classes

The timetable: classes, their instructors, capacity, and schedule.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The class list, class detail, and booking counts. |
| **Create** | Create a class, including duplicating one on the calendar. |
| **Edit** | Change an existing class: rename, reschedule, change instructor or capacity. |
| **Delete** | Delete a class, delete in bulk, **and cancel a class**. |
| **Full access** | All four, plus overriding scheduling conflicts when a class clashes with another booking. |

- **Cancelling is a Delete.** Cancelling a class cancels every member booking on it and triggers their refunds, so a role with Edit can reschedule and rename a class but cannot call it off.
- **Own for a class write means sole instructor.** A user at Own may only change a class they teach alone, before and after the change, and a class they create can name only themselves. **Own** on View is looser: they see any class they teach, including one co-taught with a colleague, while the write actions disappear on it.
- **Viewing a class does not include its attendees.** The roster hanging off a class is Bookings: View.

### Bookings

Bookings and waitlists, for classes and for area reservations.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The booking list and detail, plus the attendee roster on a class. |
| **Create** | Make a booking, and add someone to a waitlist. At **Extended**, also add a booking to a class that has already finished or a slot whose end time has passed. |
| **Edit** | Edit or reschedule a booking, confirm it, add or remove a participant, process a waitlist, and change the entrance method of a booking that has not been checked in yet. |
| **Delete** | Cancel a booking, and remove someone from a waitlist. At **Extended**, also delete a booking outright (see below). |
| **Export** | Export bookings from [Export](/settings/export). **Extended** only. |
| **Full access** | View, Create, Edit and Delete, plus overriding booking rules: booking into a blocked area or an already-taken area or coach (the latter with a note). |

- **Delete means cancel.** Cancelling carries refunds, notifications, and a knock-on to any order it belongs to. Deleting a booking outright, with its charges and check-ins, needs Delete at **Extended**, which only the Administrator role holds by default. It is offered through the [platform API](/api-reference/introduction), [MCP](/mcp/tools) and the AI assistant, and is refused while any of its charges carries money.
- **Whoever may cancel decides about the money.** When a staff member cancels a paid booking, they choose whether to refund it in full or apply the cancellation policy. No extra permission is needed for the full refund.
- **Booking outside a coach's schedule needs no override.** Every staff member who may book can go past a coach's working hours, time off, or break after a warning. Only a blocked area or an area or coach that is already taken needs **Full access**.
- **Changing the entrance method is limited to the booking's day.** At **Own** or **Standard** you can change it until the end of the booking's day. At **Extended** you can change it any time.
- **Own is not "bookings I made".** It is bookings assigned to the user, plus every booking on a class they teach. Unlike class writes there is no sole-instructor rule, so a co-taught class's bookings belong to all of its instructors. Editing a whole recurring series additionally requires that no later occurrence belongs to another instructor.
- **Cancelling a booking that is already checked in** also needs Check-ins: **Delete** for each of its check-ins (see below), on top of Bookings: Delete. Front Desk can cancel bookings by default but not ones already checked in.
- Bookings also get cancelled by neighbouring operations under their own permissions: cancelling a class, cancelling an order, cancelling an event, and resolving an instructor time-off conflict.

### Check-ins

Recording and undoing attendance.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The attendance log and check-in detail. |
| **Create** | Record a check-in for today, up to an hour ahead of the session. At **Extended**, also check someone in outside that window (a booking days ahead, or a day that has already closed), with a warning, and recover a no-show. |
| **Edit** | Change an existing check-in, in practice the entrance method used for a visit. At **Own** or **Standard** only until the end of the activity's day. At **Extended** any time. |
| **Delete** | Remove attendance. At **Own**, your own attendance records from today. At **Standard**, any attendance from today. At **Extended**, attendance from any date. |
| **Full access** | All four at their widest level: Create, Edit and Delete at **Extended**, which includes the overrides below. |

"Today" is the activity's date in the gym's time zone, not the day the record was created or your browser's time zone.

Staff are held to the same check-in window as members checking themselves in: today, and at most an hour ahead. The overrides live behind **Extended** specifically, and they are the reason to think before raising this module's level:

- **Checking in outside the window** - Create (Extended).
- **Recovering a no-show** - Create (Extended), in the booking's gym. Checking in a booking that was already marked as a no-show can undo the no-show fee.
- **Changing the entrance method after the activity's day** - Edit (Extended).
- **Removing attendance from a past day** - Delete (Extended).

Cancelling a booking that has already been checked in needs Bookings: Delete and Check-ins: Delete for every check-in it affects, so Standard covers today's and Extended any day's. For a recurring series or a party, the whole set is checked before anything is cancelled. Removing attendance also removes the instructor's pay for it.

By default the Extended overrides land only on Administrator, owner, and 1club support. You can delegate any of them to another role by granting the action at Extended.

### Assessments

Instructors' score cards on members after a training. See [Assessments](/members/assessments).

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The cards on a session's roster and, at **Standard**, a member's whole history on their profile. |
| **Create** | Score an attendee of a session. At **Standard**, also add a card for a training that was never in 1club. |
| **Edit** | Correct a card's scores and note. |
| **Delete** | Remove a card the member may already have received. One level only. |
| **Full access** | All four. |

Two things about levels are particular to this module:

- **Own means "a session I teach" for View and Create**: the user's own class, or a booking assigned to them. A member's whole history spans sessions an instructor does not teach, so the profile view needs **Standard**. A card with no session has nothing to own, so adding one needs **Standard** too.
- **Own means "a card I wrote" for Edit**, whatever the session. The desk, which scores club-wide, can still correct its own typo without being able to rewrite an instructor's card, and an instructor can correct their own.

Defaults: Manager holds all four at **Standard**; Front Desk holds View and Create at **Standard** and Edit at **Own**; Instructor holds View, Create and Edit at **Own**. Templates (the criteria and scale) are organization configuration and live under **Settings**, not here: any assessor can read them, and only a holder of Settings edits them.

### Staff

The instructor roster and admin access for your team.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The **Staff** section: the team list, the instructor list and detail, pay-rate policies, time off, and shifts. |
| **Full access** | Create, edit, delete, merge, and reorder instructors; manage pay-rate policies; manage time off and resolve its booking conflicts; **and manage team members and admin access** - invite, edit, change club access, reset a password, and remove a user. |

Staff has one level: View and Full access are on or off. Staff records belong to every gym, so a person reaches them through the combination of their gym roles.

Staff management is bounded by a role hierarchy regardless of the permission. See [Managing who has which role](#managing-who-has-which-role).

### Billing

Charges, invoices, payments, orders, plans, and revenue configuration.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | Charges, invoices, payments, orders, plans, and packages. At **Own** it is the signed-in user's own earnings ledger, which is how an instructor sees their pay. |
| **Create** | Take a payment, create and amend an order, close or cancel an order, and create and send an invoice. At **Extended**, also issue an invoice from an order. |
| **Delete** | Void a charge, refund a charge or payment, and write off the outstanding balance when cancelling a season pass. At **Extended**, also delete a payment record. |
| **Export** | Export billing and accounting data from [Export](/settings/export). **Extended** only. |
| **Full access** | Everything above plus billing configuration: tax rates, revenue accounts, payment methods, [invoice runs](/sales/bill-runs), bulk charging of saved cards, and wallet refunds. **Extended** only. |

<Note>
**Billing's configuration sits at Extended.** The Manager default of View, Create and Delete at **Standard** takes payments, voids and refunds, but does not unlock tax rates, revenue accounts, payment methods, bulk charging, or deleting a payment record. Grant **Full access** for those. Billing entities are organization settings, not part of this module.
</Note>

<Note>
Billing includes selling. A role with Billing: Create can ring up an order whether or not it also holds Point of sale, because the register and the **Sales** > **Orders** screen drive the same operations. See the Point of sale note below.
</Note>

### Point of sale

Running the till. This module is layered on top of Billing rather than carved out of it, which makes it useful subtractively: you can grant "can work the register" without granting "can see the books".

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | Load the register: the sellable catalog and prices, the totals preview, drafts and open tabs, order and receipt lookup, the bookable timetable, the customer picker, and the rental gear still out. |
| **Create** | Transact: create, amend, execute, close, and cancel an order; record the tender; add a walk-in customer; record a check-in; and take rental gear back in. |
| **Full access** | Today this is exactly View plus Create. |

Four things to know before granting it:

- **Point of sale: View conveys member contact details and the organization's order history.** The register cannot attach a sale to anyone without the customer picker, so this is not a prices-only grant.
- **A register-only cashier still cannot do back office.** They cannot settle a customer's outstanding invoice, and they cannot reverse a sale that has already been fulfilled - only discard a draft cart.
- **To build a register-only cashier, grant Point of sale and revoke Billing.** Unchecking Point of sale on its own does not stop a role that still holds Billing: Create from selling.
- **A Point of sale grant widens timetable and roster reads to the whole gym.** An instructor at Own who is also given Point of sale will see the whole club's timetable and rosters. That follows from the grant, and it is a reason not to hand the till to instructors casually.

If your organization has the point of sale switched off in settings, the register refuses new sales regardless of permissions, while everything already on the books stays settleable. The **Point of sale** module is also hidden from the permissions editor until the point of sale is switched on. Hiding it does not remove what a role already holds, so switching the point of sale back on shows each role as it was.

### Marketing

The leads pipeline, promotions (under **Sales** > **Promotions**), automations, forms, content, media, reviews, and the website builder. Marketing: **View** also shows **Marketing** > **Network listing**, but submitting or unpublishing a network listing needs Settings access, so it stays with administrators and owners. Marketing belongs to no gym, so a person's Marketing access is the combination of their gym roles.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | See all of the above. |
| **Delete** | Delete the website. **Extended** only. Deleting is reversible for 30 days; after that the archive is purged. |
| **Full access** | Create and change all of it, including publishing the website, rolling a release back, taking the site offline, and restoring an archived site. |

<Warning>
Deleting the website is Marketing: **Delete**, which exists only at **Extended**, so the Manager's default Full access cannot reach it.
</Warning>

### Analytics

Reports and insights.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | At **Standard**: every gym-wide report in **Analytics** (clubs, members, check-ins, bookings, classes, instructors, revenue, web traffic, and marketing), plus global search. At **Own**: the **My performance** bookings and classes reports, for users linked to an instructor record. The **My performance** earnings reports need Billing: **View (Own)** instead. |
| **Full access** | The same reads. Analytics has nothing to write. |

### Products

The product catalog: products, brands, and product categories.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | Products, categories, and brands. |
| **Create** | Add a product, category, or brand. |
| **Edit** | Change any of the three, including reordering them. |
| **Delete** | Delete any of the three, including bulk delete. |
| **Full access** | All four. |

- **Stock is not part of this module.** Stock levels, ingredients, recipes, and deliveries sit on the same screens but belong to Inventory, so a catalog-only editor sees the product grid without the stock panel or recipe editor.
- **Any single product permission reveals Sales** > **Products.** The write actions do not imply View, so a role granted only Create sees the section with Products as its only tab.
- **A write action is useless without View.** Every catalog screen loads through View, so the editor ticks View for you when you check a write action.
- The register reads the catalog through Point of sale: View, so a cashier prices a sale without holding a product permission.

### Inventory

Stock, ingredients, recipes, suppliers, and deliveries.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | Stock levels, stock movements, ingredients, recipes, suppliers, delivery receipts, and product availability. |
| **Full access** | Receive and adjust stock, manage ingredients and suppliers, edit recipes, and create, scan, and edit delivery receipts. |

### Rentals

Lending gear out for a session and getting it back.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The rentals list, the counts, and what a given member still has out. |
| **Create** | Check gear out. |
| **Edit** | Take gear back in. |
| **Delete** | Write off gear that is not coming back. |
| **Full access** | All four. |

This split is deliberate: the desk can lend and take back without being able to write off missing gear. The register also sees what is still out and takes gear back through Point of sale, but lending gear, including a rental line in a sale, still needs Rentals: **Create**.

Like Point of sale, the **Rentals** module only appears in the permissions editor once your organization switches rentals on.

### Cash

The cash drawer, cash movements, and expenses.

| Action | Allows |
|--------|--------|
| **View** | The cash ledger, the drawer, the overview, and expense categories. |
| **Create** | Record a cash movement or expense, scan an expense receipt, and count the drawer. |
| **Full access** | Also correct or reverse a ledger entry and manage expense categories. |

### Permissions

Administering role permissions - the editor described below.

This module is **locked to Administrator**. It shows in the editor with a lock and an **Admin only** tooltip, an administrator always holds it, and no other role can be granted it. That is what stops a role from widening its own access.

### AI assistants (MCP)

Whether someone may connect an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude that acts as them over [MCP](/mcp/overview). It is a personal permission with one level: on or off.

Only administrators hold it by default, and owners always pass. A connection acts across the organization, so granting it to a gym-level role lets that person's assistant reach every gym they work in, with what their role allows in each. Taking it off a role stops that role's existing connections in your organization on their next call. See [Connect an assistant](/mcp/connect).

### Modules that are not in the editor

Three modules are enforced by 1club but are not per-role customizable. They always resolve from the role defaults.

| Module | Covers | Why it is not customizable |
|--------|--------|----------------------------|
| **Operations** | Areas, opening hours, pricing sheets, events, the instructor roster, and time-off reads. | Not something organizations customize per role. Classes, Bookings, and Check-ins were split out of it precisely so they could be. |
| **Settings** | Organization settings and configuration: users, notification policies, integrations, API tokens, domains, and locales. | Too broad to hand out a piece at a time. In practice this is administrator and owner only. |
| **Website** | Retired. The website builder is governed by Marketing. | Deprecated. |

## Default permissions by role

This is what each role starts with out of the box, before any customization. Administrator holds everything at its highest level and is not shown. Member holds nothing. A dash means no access; a level is shown where the action has more than one.

Worth noticing in that table:

- **Front Desk can book, amend, cancel, and check in, but not undo a check-in.** It holds Bookings: Delete, so it cancels reservations and removes waitlist entries, but no Check-ins: Delete, so a booking that is already checked in stays with managers.
- **Front Desk takes money but does not hand it back.** Voids and refunds are Billing: **Delete**, which Manager holds and Front Desk does not.
- **Front Desk has no analytics by default.** Instructors get **My performance** through their Own grants.
- **Front Desk reads members but does not amend them**, and signs a member up to a plan without being able to extend or delete that membership.
- **Instructors see every member by name, but no contact details.** Members: View (Limited) lets a coach find and book anyone; grant **View (Standard)** to give them full profiles.
- **Instructors read their timetable but do not write it.** A class is scheduled *for* an instructor, so Classes is View only, while the bookings and attendance on that timetable are theirs to run.
- **Manager runs the gym but does not configure it.** Settings is not one of the modules the editor can grant, so organization configuration stays with administrators and owners.
- **Sales sells but does not keep the books.** It adds and edits contacts and leads, books trials, and signs leads up to a membership, taking its payment as the desk does, but holds no Billing or Point of sale, so no transactions, refunds, invoices or till. It deletes nothing by default.
- **Neither desk role maintains the product catalog** - both read it.
- **Only administrators connect AI assistants by default.** Grant AI assistants (MCP) to another role to let its staff connect ChatGPT or Claude.

Organizations that customized a role before a default changed keep their saved set: for example, a Front Desk role customized before it gained Bookings: Delete stays without it until you grant it.

## The Permissions page

**Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions** is where an administrator changes what each role can do. It is the whole permission model in one screen, and every control on it maps to a real check in the product.

Only users holding the Permissions module reach it, which in practice means administrators and owners.

### Reading the page

**Across the top, one tab per staff role:** Administrator, Manager, Front Desk, Sales, and Instructor. Member has no admin access, so it is not listed. Above the table, each non-admin role says **Default** if it still matches the built-in bundle, or **Customized** if your organization has changed it.

**Below, that role's permissions as one table.** Each row is a module, grouped under **Members**, **Schedule**, **Sales**, **Marketing** and **Organization**; hover a module's name for what it covers. Each column is an action: **View**, **Create**, **Edit**, **Delete**, **Export** and **Full access**. A cell is empty where the module doesn't offer that action.

Every cell shows a green check for a grant or a red cross for **No access**. Where the action has several levels, the check is followed by the level (**Own**, **Limited**, **Standard** or **Extended**).

Selecting **Administrator** shows the whole table granted and read-only, with a note that administrators have full access to everything. The Administrator role cannot be customized, by design - it is the role that always has a way back in.

### Changing a permission

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the role">
Select its tab. Changes apply to that role, and therefore to everyone holding it, in every gym where they hold it.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the module and action">
Find the module's row and the action's column.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the grant">
Open the cell's dropdown and choose **No access** or a grant. Where the action has several levels, the grants are listed from the narrowest (**Own**) to the widest (**Extended**). In a module with no Full access of its own, the **Full access** cell grants or clears every action at its widest level in one step.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
A save bar appears as soon as anything is different. It saves every role you edited, not just the one on screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Rules the editor enforces for you

The editor prevents the combinations that would produce a role that half-works.

- **Granting a write action also grants View.** A role with Edit but not View passes the write check and then fails on every list and detail screen it needs to aim that edit at. Taking View away clears the write actions that depended on it.
- **Full access locks the actions it implies.** You cannot take away View while Full access is granting it. Take Full access away first.
- **Only real permissions are offered.** The actions and levels are derived from the checks the product actually performs, so there is no control that quietly does nothing.
- **A role cannot be left with nothing.** Clearing every permission is indistinguishable from reverting the role, so the save is refused with a message pointing you at **Reset to default** instead.
- **The Permissions module cannot be granted to another role.** It shows a lock and **Admin only**, with no working control.

### When a change takes effect

Permission changes are not instant. They apply the next time an affected user's session refreshes, within about 15 minutes, or immediately if they sign out and back in. If you are testing a change on your own account, sign out and back in rather than waiting.

### Resetting a role

**Reset to default** on a role deletes your organization's customization for that role and puts it back on the built-in bundle. It is enabled only when the role is actually customized, it asks for confirmation, and it warns you if you have unsaved edits it will also discard. There is no history to restore a customization from afterwards, so treat it as final.

### What the editor cannot do

- **Create a role.** The role names are fixed. Pick the closest one and tailor its permissions.
- **Customize Administrator.** Administrators always hold everything.
- **Customize Operations or Settings.** Those modules always resolve from the role defaults.
- **Give one person different permissions from their role.** There is no per-person editor: give them a different role in the gym instead, from **Club access**.
- **Choose gyms.** Which gyms a person reaches is set per person in **Staff** > **Team** > **Club access**, not here.

## Reading the roles and permissions table

There is also a read-only view of the whole matrix, for when you are assigning a role rather than editing one. Invite or edit a user from **Settings** > **Organization** > **Users**, **Staff** > **Team**, or **Staff** > **Instructors**, and click the question-mark icon next to **User information**. It opens **Roles & permissions**. Managers can open it too, since it needs only Staff access.

It shows every role against every module, with the actions each role holds and the level where it matters. It reflects **your organization's** permissions, including any customization, not the generic defaults. Roles with no permissions - Member - are left out, and so is the admin-only Permissions module.

If the table cannot load your organization's real sets it says so and falls back to the built-in defaults rather than showing nothing.

## Managing who has which role

- **Administrators and owners** manage every user, in every gym, and can assign any role, including Administrator and the owner flag.
- **Managers**, through Staff: Full access, can invite, edit, reset the password of, and remove users whose role is **below** Manager - Front Desk, Sales, Instructor, and Member - and can promote someone up to Manager, in the gyms they manage. A Manager cannot modify themselves, another Manager, or an Administrator, can't change someone's access in a gym they don't manage, and can never grant the Administrator role or account ownership.
- A person's gyms and their role in each are chosen when they are invited and can be changed later from **Club access**. The owner flag is separate and is preserved.

This hierarchy holds regardless of permissions, on both the screen and the server. Administrators and owners manage users under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Users**. Managers have no Settings access, so they manage the team under **Staff** > **Team** and **Staff** > **Instructors**. See [Users](/settings/admin-users).

## Worked examples

### Hiring a receptionist at a racket-sports venue

Invite them as **Front Desk**. Out of the box they look members up, sign them up to a plan, book areas, check people in, take payment at the desk, lend rackets, and answer the inbox. They cannot change organization settings, see staff pay, or maintain the product catalog.

Two adjustments are common. If your desk amends member records, grant Members: **Edit**. If your desk should also undo check-ins, grant Check-ins: **Delete** at **Standard** (today's attendance).

### Onboarding a coach at a jiu-jitsu academy

Invite them as **Instructor** in the gyms they teach at. They see their own timetable, run the bookings and attendance on it, see members by first name and last initial, read their own earnings, and send their own messages. They cannot open organization billing or settings.

If you do not want instructors cancelling bookings on their own classes, remove Bookings: **Delete**. If you want them amending their own classes, grant Classes: **Edit (Own)** - it is off by default because a timetable is set *for* an instructor. Avoid granting them Point of sale: it widens their timetable and roster reads to the whole gym.

### Promoting a studio lead to run a location

Give a trusted senior staffer **Manager** in the gym they will run, and keep their other gyms as they were. They run members and memberships, the timetable, bookings, check-ins, marketing, analytics, inventory, rentals, cash, and the till, and they manage Front Desk, Sales and Instructor access in that gym themselves, so day-to-day team changes no longer need an administrator. They take payments but do not configure billing, and they cannot reach organization settings.

### Building a register-only cashier

Start from **Front Desk**, then grant **Point of sale: View and Create** and **revoke Billing entirely**. Revoking Billing is the part that matters - leaving Billing: Create in place keeps them able to sell through the Orders screen, so unchecking Point of sale alone changes nothing. The result is a role that rings up sales but cannot see the books, settle an invoice, or reverse a fulfilled sale.

### Adding a co-owner

Give them **Administrator** and mark them as **owner**. Owners bypass every permission check and are the only users who can delete the organization. Reserve this for the people who actually own the business.

## Tips and best practices

- **Assign the lowest role that fits, then add.** Start at Front Desk or Instructor and grant the one permission that is missing, rather than defaulting people to Administrator.
- **Grant the narrowest action that does the job.** Prefer View, Create, Edit, or Delete over Full access when only one of them is needed. Full access absorbs anything added to that module later.
- **Be deliberate about Extended.** Standard is the right default for staff. Extended is what unlocks the sensitive overrides described above.
- **Assign only the gyms someone works in.** A role in a gym gives access to its members' activity and money there; a gym with no assignment stays closed to them.
- **Remember that Delete often means cancel.** On classes, bookings, and memberships, Delete is the destructive customer-facing action that triggers refunds and notifications. Withholding it is a real control.
- **Keep owners few.** Owners bypass every check and can delete the organization.
- **Read the role, not the label.** What a user can do is the sum of their permissions after customization, not the name of their role.
- **Check the Customized badge before debugging.** A role that behaves differently from this page's defaults has probably been customized in your organization.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: I changed a role's permissions but the user still has the old access.

**Cause**: Permission changes apply when the user's session refreshes.

**Fix**: Ask them to sign out and back in, or wait up to about 15 minutes.

**Issue**: A Front Desk user can look a member up but not edit them.

**Cause**: Front Desk holds Members: View by default, not Edit.

**Fix**: Grant Members: **Edit** in **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**.

**Issue**: A Front Desk user cannot cancel a booking that is already checked in.

**Cause**: Cancelling a checked-in booking also removes its attendance, which needs Check-ins: Delete. Front Desk doesn't hold it by default.

**Fix**: Grant Check-ins: **Delete** at **Standard** (today's attendance), or route those cancellations to a Manager. A role customized before Front Desk gained Bookings: Delete may also need Bookings: **Delete**.

**Issue**: A staff member can't see a gym, or it's missing from their club switcher.

**Cause**: They have no role in that gym. Only administrators reach every gym automatically, including new ones.

**Fix**: Open **Staff** > **Team**, choose **Club access** for them, and pick a role for that gym.

**Issue**: A manager sees front desk options only.

**Cause**: They hold a different role in the gym that's selected. With one gym selected, that gym's role decides.

**Fix**: Switch to the gym they manage, or **All clubs**, from the breadcrumb.

**Issue**: A Manager or Front Desk user cannot check a member in for a later day or a day that has passed.

**Cause**: Staff check-ins are limited to today, up to an hour ahead of the session. Only Check-ins: **Create** at **Extended** reaches past that window.

**Fix**: Check the member in on the day, or grant Check-ins: **Create (Extended)** to the role that needs it.

**Issue**: I unchecked Point of sale but the role can still sell.

**Cause**: The register and the **Sales** > **Orders** screen drive the same operations, and the role still holds Billing: Create.

**Fix**: Revoke Billing from that role as well. See [Building a register-only cashier](#building-a-register-only-cashier).

**Issue**: I granted a write action and the screen loads empty or errors.

**Cause**: Write actions need the View on the same module to load the records they act on.

**Fix**: The editor ticks View for you when you check a write action. If the role was configured some other way, add View back.

**Issue**: I cannot uncheck View on a module.

**Cause**: Full access is granting it through the hierarchy, so the row is ticked and locked.

**Fix**: Uncheck **Full access** first, then pick the individual actions you want.

**Issue**: My save was refused with a message about keeping at least one permission.

**Cause**: You cleared every permission for the role, which is indistinguishable from reverting it.

**Fix**: Use **Reset to default** if that is what you meant, or leave at least one permission checked.

**Issue**: An Instructor cannot see organization billing or settings.

**Cause**: Instructor permissions are at Own by design.

**Fix**: This is expected. If the person needs that access, make them a Manager rather than widening the Instructor role. Settings still requires Administrator.

**Issue**: A Manager cannot change another Manager's or an Administrator's access.

**Cause**: Managers only manage roles below Manager, and can never grant Administrator or ownership.

**Fix**: Have an administrator or owner make the change.

**Issue**: Point of sale or Rentals is missing from the permissions editor.

**Cause**: Those modules only appear once your organization switches the point of sale or rentals on.

**Fix**: Switch the feature on in **Settings** > **Sales** > **Point of Sale** or **Settings** > **Sales** > **Rentals**, then reload the Permissions page.

**Issue**: I want a brand-new role with a custom name.

**Cause**: 1club has fixed roles. Custom named roles are not supported.

**Fix**: Pick the closest role and tailor its permissions in **Settings** > **Organization** > **Permissions**.
