A gift card is money with a code, sold like any other product. A buyer picks an amount in your online shop, on your website or at the desk, and once it is paid they get the code and a printable card to hand over. Whoever redeems it gets the whole amount in their [wallet](/sales/wallet) and spends it on anything you sell. Owners set gift cards up, the front desk sells and redeems them, and members buy and redeem them online.

## When to use it

- **Sell a present before the holidays** - a customer buys a 50 card in your online shop, adds a message for a friend, and has it emailed to them on their birthday.
- **Sell at the desk** - a walk-in picks an amount at the till, pays cash or card, and leaves with a printed card with a QR code.
- **Make up for a bad experience** - give a member a complimentary gift card instead of changing a price after the fact.
- **Move from another system** - import the cards you already issued, with their codes and what is left on them, so holders keep using them.

## How it works

1. **Create it.** A gift card is a product with the amounts a buyer can pick. It has no price or tax rate of its own and is listed in the shop as **From** its lowest amount.
2. **Sell it.** The buyer adds it to the cart like any product, picks an amount, and can say who it is for, add a message, and give that person's email and a day to send it.
3. **Issue it.** A card is issued only once it is paid. An order left to pay at the desk issues nothing until the payment is recorded. Each card gets its own code.
4. **Deliver it.** The buyer gets an email with the code and a printable PDF with a QR code, and the card appears under **Gift cards** in their account. When they gave the recipient's email, the recipient gets it on the chosen day.
5. **Redeem it.** The whole amount moves into the redeemer's wallet at once. Each code works once.
6. **Spend it.** The wallet pays for anything, as usual. The card keeps track of every purchase it paid for and what is left.

A gift card cannot be paid for from the wallet.

### Gift cards and discounts

A gift card adds money to a wallet. A [discount](/sales/discounts) takes a percentage or an amount off a purchase. Use a gift card when someone should hold money with you, and a discount code when many people should get money off.

### Money and tax

- **Revenue** is counted when the card is sold, on the sale's invoice. Spending the card later is not counted as revenue again.
- **Tax** is charged when the card is spent, on what it pays for. That is why a gift card product has no tax rate.
- To keep gift cards apart in your books, file the gift card product under its own [revenue account](/settings/billing-general#revenue-accounts) and use the **Unspent** and **Expired** totals on **Sold cards**.

### Expiry

A card's validity counts from when it is given: the sale, or the send date the buyer chose. The holder gets a reminder email 30 days before it expires. At expiry, a card nobody redeemed lapses, and a redeemed card has what is left of it taken out of the wallet. Other wallet money is not touched.

Some countries set a minimum. In Germany a card lasts at least to the end of the third year after it is given, and in Austria and Spain it never expires, whatever the product says.

## Create a gift card

Go to **Sales** > **Gift cards** and click **New gift card**.

- **Name**, **Photo** and **Description** - what buyers see in your shop and on your website.
- **Amounts** - the amounts a buyer picks from. New gift cards start with 25, 50 and 100.
- **Let the buyer type an amount** - lets the buyer enter their own amount between the **Lowest amount** and the **Highest amount**.
- **Valid for (months)** - how long a card lasts. The default is 36 months. Leave it empty for cards that never expire.
- **Revenue account** - where the sales are filed.

The **Gift cards** tab lists every gift card you sell. Click one to edit it.

## Sell a gift card

### Online

Members buy gift cards in your online shop in the member portal and on your website, next to your other products. They pick an amount, and can enter who it is for, a message, the recipient's email under **Also email it to them**, and **When to send**: now or on a day within the next six months. The gift card sits in the cart as a normal product and is paid with everything else in it.

### At the desk

- **Point of sale** - add the gift card to the sale and pick the amount. See [Point of sale](/pos/point-of-sale).
- **Sell gift card** - from **Sales** > **Gift cards**, or from the **Add** (+) menu on a member's profile. Pick the buyer, the gift card and its amount, the delivery details and how the customer pays.

Selling at the desk goes through the point of sale, so it needs the point of sale turned on. Cards sold at the desk are issued as soon as the payment is recorded.

### Give a complimentary gift card

On **Sold cards**, click **Give a gift card** to give one away, for example as a goodwill gesture. Pick the member, the amount and how long it lasts, and add a message if you like. The full code is shown once, when you give it. A complimentary card is not a sale, so it brings in no revenue and is left out of the **Unspent** total.

## Redeem a gift card

The code works in any of these places:

- **A promo code field** - at checkout in the portal or on your website. The member sees what the card adds to their wallet and clicks **Add to wallet**.
- **The wallet** - members click **Add gift card** in their wallet on the **Billing** tab of their account.
- **The gift link** - the QR code on the PDF and the link in the email open the `/gift` page of your website or member portal. It shows the card and, after the holder signs in, adds it to their wallet.
- **At the desk** - staff use **Redeem gift card** from the **Add** (+) menu or the **Due now** tile on a member's profile, or **Redeem** on **Sold cards**.

Codes ignore upper and lower case and hyphens. After too many wrong codes in a short time, further attempts are blocked for a while. A member is never told why a code failed, only that it can't be used.

## Sold cards

**Sales** > **Gift cards** > **Sold cards** lists every card, newest first, with its code end, gift card, buyer, recipient, value, what is left, status, issue date and expiry date.

- **Unspent** - what you still owe on gift cards today: cards nobody redeemed in full, plus what redeemed cards have left in wallets. Complimentary cards are not included.
- **Expired** - what lapsed when cards expired.

Search by the end of a code, a name or an email, filter by status with the tabs, and click **Export** for a CSV.

| Status    | Meaning                                                                          |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scheduled | Paid, waiting for its send date                                                  |
| Sent      | Delivered and not redeemed yet                                                   |
| Used      | Redeemed into a wallet. **Remaining** shows what is left                         |
| Expired   | Passed its expiry date                                                           |
| Void      | Cancelled by staff or by a refund                                                |

Staff see only the end of a code, for example `••••-••••-2HDP`. The code is the money, so it is never shown in full after it is issued.

### A card's details

Click a card to open it. It shows the code end, the buyer, the recipient and message, when it was sent, the expiry, who redeemed it, every purchase it paid for under **Usage**, and what is left. From there you can:

- **Send again** - email the card again to the buyer or the recipient.
- **Edit delivery** - change who it is for, the message, the recipient's email or the send date.
- **Extend expiry** - give the card a later expiry date.
- **Void** - cancel a card nobody has redeemed, with a reason. The reason is shown on the card.

## Refunds

A gift card sale is refunded in full, and only while none of its cards was redeemed. Refunding voids the cards. Once a card is redeemed, its money is in the member's wallet and can't be returned as money.

## Import gift cards from another system

Go to **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Import** and choose **Gift cards**. Fill one row per card:

| Column             | Required | Notes                                                                  |
| ------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `code`             | Yes      | Kept as printed, so codes already handed out keep working              |
| `remainingBalance` | Yes      | What is left on the card. A card with nothing left is not imported     |
| `originalAmount`   | No       | The amount the card was sold for                                       |
| `issueDate`        | No       | When it was sold                                                       |
| `expiryDate`       | No       | When empty, 36 months after the issue date                             |
| `recipientEmail`   | No       | Used for the expiry reminder                                           |
| `conditions`       | No       | Free text shown to staff on the card                                   |

Each row becomes a card worth its remaining balance. No sale is recorded, because the money was taken in the other system, and the card counts in **Unspent** from the day you import it. A code that is already there is skipped, so you can run the same file again safely. Imported cards are not emailed.

## Permissions

| Action | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| See and edit the gift cards you sell | Products (read, create, update) |
| See **Sold cards** and a card's details | **billing.read.standard** |
| Export sold cards | **billing.read.extended** |
| Sell at the desk or the till | **billing.create.standard** or **pos.create.standard** |
| Redeem a card for a member | **billing.create.standard** |
| Send again, edit delivery | **billing.update.standard** |
| Extend expiry, give a complimentary card, void a card | **billing.update.extended** |

## Troubleshooting

- **The code is refused.** It was already used, has expired, was voided, or was typed wrong. Search for the end of the code on **Sold cards** to see which.
- **The recipient did not get the email.** Open the card, check the recipient's email with **Edit delivery**, and use **Send again**.
- **A card bought online has no code yet.** It is waiting for its payment. A card is issued once the order is paid.
- **A card can't be voided.** It was already redeemed. What is left is in the member's wallet.
- **A buyer can't pay with their wallet.** Expected. A gift card can't be paid for from the wallet.
