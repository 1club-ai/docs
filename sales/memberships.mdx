A membership is a [plan](/sales/plans) sold to a specific contact. The plan defines the price, billing frequency, minimum term, and session limits; the membership copies those at the moment of sale and tracks that member's sessions and billing from then on. The front desk sells memberships, managers pause, renew, and cancel them, and members use them to book and check in.

## When to use it

The Memberships page is where you manage every membership in the org and see at a glance how many sessions each one has left. Editing the plan later does not rewrite memberships already sold, so each member keeps the terms they bought. The same contact can hold several memberships at once (for example a monthly plan plus a 10-class pack).

Typical uses:

- **An unlimited monthly plan** - a yoga studio's members book as many classes as they like, and each renewal charges the card on file.
- **A class pack that counts down** - a reformer studio sells a 10-class pack, each booking takes one visit, and a cancelled booking gives it back.
- **Area hours** - a padel gym sells 20 hours a month, and a 90-minute booking takes 1.5 hours.
- **A hold for an injury or a holiday** - a jiu-jitsu member tears a ligament, so you pause the membership and they lose none of the time they paid for.
- **A goodwill session** - a class was cancelled at short notice, so you give each affected member one extra session with **Adjust sessions**.
- **A 12-month contract** - a strength gym sells a monthly plan with a 12-month minimum term. A member who leaves after five months owes an early cancellation charge, which the front desk can waive.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how each of these is set up.

A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) appears here too, sold from the schedule rather than from this page. It behaves differently in a few places, each noted below: editing, pausing, its sessions, its cancel dialog, and its renewal.

<Note>
  A membership and a booking share the same payment, pricing, and session code.
  A membership used at check-in behaves exactly as it does when used through a
  booking - there is no separate "membership check-in" logic. See [Check-in
  models](/reference/check-in-models).
</Note>

## Opening the page

Go to **Sales** > **Memberships** (`/billing/plans/memberships`). Memberships sits between **Promotions** and **Orders** in the Sales section. Old links to `/members/memberships` still open this page.

Viewing the page needs the **memberships.read.club** permission. See [Permissions](#permissions) for the rest.

## Tabs and counts

Tabs filter by status, with a count on each:

- **All** - everything.
- **Active** - the default tab. Currently valid memberships.
- **Paused** - billing and access on hold.
- **Pending** - the start date is in the future.
- **Used** - every session spent, but the membership has not been cancelled.
- **Cancelled** - ended immediately by a cancellation.
- **Expired** - past the end date without renewal. A scheduled cancellation ends here on its end date.

A membership waiting for its first payment - a plan sold to be paid at the gym, a member's checkout not yet paid, or an unpaid season pass - is **Pending payment** (or **Awaiting balance** once part has been paid). It has no tab of its own and shows under **All**. It becomes **Active** once the payment settles.

The **Frequency** filter narrows by billing frequency: **Recurring** (any renewing cadence), **One-time**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Annually**, or **Custom**.

## Grid columns

Each row shows:

- **Contact** - the member who holds the membership.
- **Plan** - the plan name, with its kind underneath: **Membership**, **Pass**, or **Drop-In**.
- **Period** - the current period's dates ("Ongoing" for a renewing membership with no end). Underneath: **Auto-renew: Yes/No** on a renewing membership, and **Minimum term until** a date while the term is still ahead.
- **Price** - the price at sale, with the billing frequency underneath.
- **Usage** - one line per session limit, labelled by what it covers, for example "Yoga: 3 / 5 Visits". **Unlimited** when the plan has no cap. See [How sessions are counted](#how-sessions-are-counted).
- **Weekly hours** - a season pass's weekly slots; "-" for anything else.
- **Status** - the status chip.
- **Payment Status** - the status of the membership's latest charge, for its sale or its last renewal: for example paid, unpaid, overdue, partially paid, or failed.

**Shared with account** is hidden by default. Add it from the column chooser to see which memberships everyone on the holder's household or company account can use.

Click a row to open the [membership detail page](#the-membership-detail-page). The row menu offers **View**, **Edit**, and what applies to the current status: **Renew**, **Extend**, **Reconcile bookings**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel Membership**, **Add Payment** (while the latest charge is unpaid), and **Delete**.

### Reconcile bookings

**Reconcile bookings** covers a member's eligible bookings in the current period with the membership, up to its sessions left. Use it when bookings were paid some other way before the member bought a capped membership that should cover them. Bookings beyond what is left stay on their existing payment. It is offered on active memberships with a session cap.

To reconcile several at once, select rows and choose **Reconcile bookings** from the bulk actions. Memberships that aren't active are skipped. Any that fail stay selected so you can retry them.

## Adding a membership

Click **Add** in the page header and choose **Add Membership**. In the dialog:

1. **Pick the contact.** On an existing membership this field is locked - a membership can't be moved to a different member.
2. **Pick the plan.** The price, billing frequency, signup fee, minimum term, sharing, and session limits fill in from the plan. A plan sold at one gym only fixes **Club** to that gym ("This plan is only sold at this club.").
3. **Set the start date.** The **End Date** is worked out from the plan when you save; a renewing membership has none. To add days on top of it, use [Extend](#extend).
4. **Set auto-renew.** Only for renewing plans; defaults from the plan. If the member already has an auto-renewing membership, the dialog warns you.
5. **Check the minimum term.** On a renewing plan, **Minimum term** defaults to the plan's term, marked "(plan)". Change it to give one member a different commitment, or pick **None**. Under the field you see the date the term is met and how many payments it commits the member to. See [Membership policies](/settings/membership-policies#the-minimum-term-is-set-on-the-plan).
6. **Check sharing.** **Shared with account** starts from the plan. Turn it on or off for this member: on, everyone on their household or company account can use the membership, all drawing on the same sessions.
7. **Check the sessions.** The session limits table starts from the plan. You can change what each row covers and how many visits it allows, or add one with **Add session limit**. On a plan with one count you can also set **Uses Remaining**.
8. **Apply a discount (optional).** Enter a promotion code or pick a discount. The discount lowers the saved price, so renewals keep the reduced amount. Only discount promotions apply here, not vouchers.
9. **Take payment (optional).** The summary shows the **Total due**. When your organization has a [billing day](/sales/membership-billing#billing-day), it also shows the **First period** with its dates and "Then {amount} from {date}", so you see the shorter first charge before anything is taken. If the plan requires payment upfront, the payment section opens automatically. You can still save without a payment: the amount stays owed, to collect from the member's profile. The signup fee is its own charge and is not reduced by a discount.

## The membership detail page

Click a row to open a membership. It shows:

- A **Membership Details** card with the plan, status, price, signup fee, **Discount**, billing frequency, auto-renew, **Start Date**, **End Date** (or **Next Billing Date**), sharing, and payment status. Days added with Extend show under the date, for example "14 day(s) - club closed for repairs". When they apply it also shows:
  - **Next charge** - "{amount} on {date}", for the period it pays for. A chip flags a renewal that won't be charged automatically: **No card on file**, **Owed - no online payments**, **Sent as a payment link**, or **Last charge failed**. See [Membership billing](/sales/membership-billing).
  - **Minimum term** - the term and the date it is met, plus any days added by pauses.
  - **Paused At**, **Resume On** ("Indefinite (manual resume)" when no date is set), and the pause reason.
  - **Booked pause**, for a pause that starts on a later day.
  - **Ends on (cancellation scheduled)**, the **Cancellation reason**, and any **Early cancellation charge** (marked "waived" when staff waived it), for a scheduled cancellation. **Cancelled At** for one that has taken effect.
- **Usage** - each session limit with what it **Covers**, used and left, the plan's **Hours** window when it has one, and **Check-ins** ("3, last on 12 Oct", or "None yet").
- A **Payment** card with the **Total**, what is **Paid**, and what is **Still owed**, shown only while something is owed.
- A **Member** card linking to the holder.
- Tabs for the membership's **Charges**, **Bookings**, and **Check-in History**. A tab shows a count once it has something in it. The Bookings tab also lists cancelled bookings, but counts only live ones.

You can change the **Discount** right on the page: apply one, swap it, or remove it.

While a charge on the membership is unpaid, **Add Payment** leads the header so you can collect it in one click. **Edit** sits beside it. The more-actions menu holds **Renew**, **Extend**, **Adjust sessions**, **Reconcile bookings**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Call off booked pause**, **Withdraw cancellation**, **Cancel**, and **Delete**, each shown only when it applies.

### Editing a membership

**Edit** opens the same dialog. Because a plan can change after a membership was sold, the edit form also lets you repair the membership's own copy:

- the session limits table, with a **Used** column - change what each row covers and how many it allows. Rows can only be added or removed when the membership is sold.
- **Billing Frequency** and, for a custom plan, its period.
- **Next Billing Date** - leave it empty to recompute it from the billing frequency.
- **Status** - with a warning when the status you pick doesn't match the dates.
- **Extend** - a switch with **Extra days** and **Reason**, the same as the [Extend](#extend) action.

To change the sessions left, use [Adjust sessions](#adjust-sessions), not the edit form.

Changing the price opens **Review price change**. Under **Periods to change**, choose **Current period only**, **Next renewal onward**, or **Current period and future renewals**. When the current period is already paid at the old price and the new one is lower, choose whether the difference goes to the member's wallet (**Add to wallet**) or the charge is simply updated (**Update in-place**). When the current period and the renewal are priced differently, the dialog says so ("Current period: X. Renewal price: Y.").

Editing a season pass opens its weekly hours instead. See [Season passes](/schedule/season-passes).

<Warning>
  To pause or resume, use the **Pause** and **Resume** actions, not the edit
  dialog. Pausing has side effects (voiding scheduled charges, moving the
  renewal date) that the edit form deliberately does not perform.
</Warning>

## The lifecycle

The actions on each row reflect the membership's status.

### Pause and resume

Use **Pause** (only from **Active**) to put a membership on hold. Billing stops, access is blocked, and a pending renewal charge is voided so the member is not billed during the freeze. The renewal clock stops too.

The **Pause Membership** dialog offers:

- **Start the pause on a later day** - turn it on to book the pause for a **Pause starts on** date. It starts as that day begins, and until then the membership runs as normal. Leave it off to pause today.
- **Schedule an auto-resume date** - turn it on to set a **Resume On** date; the membership resumes on its own that day. Leave it off for a pause you end by hand.
- **Reason (optional)** - a note such as holiday, injury, or medical leave, up to 500 characters.

If the pause goes against the membership's [policy](/settings/membership-policies) - longer than its **Longest pause**, or past its **Pauses per year** - the dialog warns you before you confirm. The pause still goes through.

When you set a resume date, the renewal date, end date, minimum term, and any scheduled cancellation move forward by the pause length as soon as the pause starts. For a pause with no resume date, they move when you **Resume** (only from **Paused**). Either way the member gets back exactly the time they paid for. The resume dialog previews the new **Next Billing Date** and **End Date** before you confirm. Resuming creates no charge by itself; the next renewal is charged from the moved date.

A booked pause shows on the detail page as **Booked pause**. Use **Call off booked pause** to cancel it before it starts.

A season pass can't be paused.

Members can pause their own membership too, today or from a later day, when their membership policy allows it. They can end a pause they started early, or call off one they booked. See [How members pause](/settings/membership-policies#how-members-pause).

### Renew

**Renew** adds a new billing period by hand. It is offered on active, pending, used, and expired memberships, but not on paused, cancelled, or one-time ones.

- If the membership is still within its current period, the new period starts where the current one ends.
- If it has expired or its end date has passed, the new period starts today.

The dialog previews the **New billing period** and the price. You can renew now and collect later. If the plan requires payment upfront, the dialog says so, and without a payment the new period stays owed. A one-time membership can't be renewed - sell a new one instead.

### Extend

**Extend** adds days on top of the period the plan sets, for example to make up for a closure. Enter the **Extra days** and a **Reason**. The dialog shows the new end or billing date. Use a negative number to take back days granted by mistake. The extension and its reason show on the detail page.

Extend is offered on any membership that has an end or renewal date and is not cancelled. If the membership has already ended, the extra days bring it back to active.

### Adjust sessions

**Adjust sessions** gives or takes sessions on a membership with a session cap, for example a goodwill session after a cancelled class, or a visit that happened outside the system. It is in the more-actions menu of the detail page while the membership is active, used, pending, or paused.

1. If the membership has more than one session limit, pick the count under **Sessions**.
2. Enter a number under **Add or remove**. A negative number takes sessions back. The dialog shows what is **Left now** and **Left after**.
3. Enter a **Reason** (required), for example "goodwill session".
4. Click **Adjust sessions**.

You can't take back more than the member has left. The change holds for the current period and is recorded with your reason; the next period starts from the plan's sessions again. A spent membership that gets sessions back returns to **Active**.

### Cancel

**Cancel** is offered on any membership that is not already cancelled or expired. The dialog asks two things:

- **Why is the membership ending?** - pick a reason: **Moving away**, **Too expensive**, **Not using it**, **Injury or health**, **Switching to another club**, **Unhappy with the service**, **Club's decision**, or **Other**. **Other** needs a note. You can add a **Note (optional)** to any reason.
- **Membership ends** - when it ends:
  - **Today (ends immediately)** - the status changes to cancelled, the end date is set to today, and access ends at the end of the gym's day.
  - **A coming period end** - the end of the current period is preselected: the membership stops renewing and stays active until then. Pick a later period end and it keeps renewing until that date, then stops; the detail page shows the date under **Ends on (cancellation scheduled)**.

A membership that does not auto-renew (including one-time packs) can only end today - there is no future period to cancel against.

**Season passes** open their own dialog instead. Ending one has to release the areas and close the pass together, so the generic cancel is refused for them. See [Cancelling a season pass](/schedule/season-passes#cancelling-a-season-pass).

Either way, any pending renewal charges after the end date are voided so the member is not billed again.

#### Minimum terms and the early cancellation charge

When a membership has a minimum term that the chosen date doesn't meet, its [membership policy](/settings/membership-policies) decides what leaving early costs. A date that carries a charge shows it in the list, for example "1 July 2027 - 150.00 early cancellation charge", and the dialog explains how many committed payments ending then leaves unmade. Turn on **Waive the charge** to record the cancellation without it. Otherwise the charge is added to what the member owes, as a charge on the membership. Ending today counts the same remaining payments as ending when the current period does, because the rest of that period is already paid.

You are offered every date, even ones the member could not choose online. When a date goes against the policy - it ends before the minimum term is met, or sooner than the notice period allows - the dialog warns you, and the cancellation still goes through. See [What staff see](/settings/membership-policies#what-staff-see).

#### Changing or withdrawing a cancellation

To move a scheduled cancellation to another date, cancel again and pick the new date. The new cancellation replaces the old one, and a charge the member has already paid counts towards the new one, so they are never charged twice.

To take a cancellation back, choose **Withdraw cancellation** from the more-actions menu on the detail page. The membership renews again, and an unpaid early cancellation charge is voided. A charge that was already paid stays paid, for you to refund or keep.

Members can cancel their own memberships when their policy allows it: they choose the end date, give a reason, and can withdraw the cancellation until that date. See [How members cancel](/settings/membership-policies#how-members-cancel). You are notified when they do.

### Delete

Delete removes a membership created by mistake, together with its unpaid charges. It is refused once any of its charges has been invoiced or had a payment attempt, even a failed one, and while check-ins still point at it. Cancel the membership instead: that stops billing and access and keeps the financial record. A membership that is already cancelled can't be deleted for the same reason - leave it as the record, and refund or void its charges if money has to go back.

## How sessions are counted

A membership counts sessions only when its plan sets a cap. An uncapped membership shows **Unlimited** (**Unlimited visits** or **Unlimited hours** on the detail page) and is never debited. Each session limit counts in its own unit:

- **Visits** - each booking, or each check-in with no booking behind it, takes one.
- **Hours** - each booking takes its length, so a 90-minute booking takes 1.5 hours.

When and how a session is taken:

- **Booked in advance** - the session is taken when the booking is made. Cancelling the booking gives it back (never above the cap), and a **Used** membership that gets a session back returns to **Active**.
- **Used at check-in** - a member checked in against the membership with no booking takes a session at check-in.
- **No double count** - a check-in for a booking takes nothing more, because the booking already used the session.

With [several session limits](/sales/plans#session-limits), each booking and check-in takes from the one count that covers it. The membership moves to **Used** only once every count is spent, and never if one of them is unlimited. A pending, paused, or unpaid membership never moves to **Used**.

When a membership starts on a [billing day](/sales/membership-billing#billing-day) and its first period is shorter, it gets a matching share of each count's sessions, rounded up.

<Note>
  A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) reads its sessions the other way
  round. The sale spends the season's hours up front on the weeks it books, so a
  new pass shows its hours fully committed here (`27 / 27 hours`), and `0 of 27
  hours left` on member-facing surfaces. It stays there for the whole season, is
  never marked **Used** at zero, and the counter rises only when a cancelled
  week credits a makeup hour back. Its two real ends are the season's last day
  and cancelling the pass.
</Note>

<Note>
  A spent or no-longer-active membership still honors bookings made against it
  while it was active. The booking took its session at booking time, so check-in
  for that booking goes through even if the membership has since run out or
  ended. See [Attendance](/schedule/attendance).
</Note>

## What a membership covers

Each session limit covers classes, areas, instructors, or **Any visit**, copied from the plan at sale. When a booking is being made, only memberships with a count that covers the chosen class, area, or instructor are offered as a way to pay.

If the plan has its own operating hours (for example an off-peak plan valid 20:00 - 23:00 on weekdays), a booking outside that window - or across its edge - is not covered by the membership.

You can change what a membership covers from **Edit** if its copy of the plan is out of date.

## Auto-renew

A renewing membership with auto-renew on is renewed by the renewal engine, which raises each renewal charge and collects it automatically - from a saved card, or by emailing the member a payment link, depending on how the gym takes payments. The charge is taken a few days before the period starts (the plan's **Renewal Lead Time**), or on the billing day when your organization has one. Each membership's **Next charge** shows exactly when and how much. See [Membership billing](/sales/membership-billing).

A declined renewal never pauses or ends the membership. The charge is marked failed, the member keeps their access, and the next period is raised as normal. Whether to cancel a member who doesn't pay is your decision. See [When a renewal fails](/sales/membership-billing#when-a-renewal-fails).

A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) never auto-renews. It is paid once for a fixed period, and the next season is a new sale at the new season's prices.

## Worked examples

### Unlimited yoga membership

A studio sells an "Unlimited Monthly Yoga" plan: monthly, auto-renew on, one session limit covering the yoga class types with no cap.

The member books as many yoga classes as they like, and the usage column shows **Unlimited**. Each renewal charges the card on file. If they go on holiday, **Pause** with a resume date freezes billing and moves the renewal date out by the pause length.

### 10-class reformer pack

A studio sells a "10-Class Reformer Pack": one-time, 10 visits covering the reformer class type.

Each reformer booking takes one visit, so the usage counts up from 0 / 10 to 10 / 10. When the tenth is used the membership moves to the **Used** tab. Because it is one-time, **Renew** is not offered - sell a fresh pack. A cancelled booking gives the visit back, and a **Used** pack returns to **Active**.

### 5 Yoga + 4 Pilates

A member holds a monthly plan with two session limits. The usage column shows "Yoga: 2 / 5 Visits" and "Pilates: 4 / 4 Visits". The Pilates count is spent, so the member can't book another Pilates class this month, but the membership stays **Active** because Yoga sessions are left. After a cancelled Pilates class, use **Adjust sessions**, pick the Pilates count under **Sessions**, and add 1 with a reason.

### Area hours

A padel gym sells "20 Hours Monthly": monthly, auto-renew on, 20 hours covering the padel area type.

A 90-minute booking takes 1.5 hours, a 60-minute booking takes 1. When the member runs out before the month ends, the membership shows **Used**; the renewal refills it for the new period.

### A hold for an injured athlete

A jiu-jitsu gym runs an "Unlimited Monthly" plan. A member tears a ligament and needs six weeks off.

- Open the membership and click **Pause**.
- Enter "injury" as the reason, and either set a resume date six weeks out or leave it open.
- Billing stops and the pending renewal charge is voided. The renewal date moves forward by the exact pause length, so the member loses none of the time they paid for.

### 12-month contract with an early cancellation charge

A gym sells "Monthly Membership" at 50 a month with a 12-month minimum term.

- On the [plan](/sales/plans#pricing), set **Minimum term** to 12 months. Every membership sold on it carries the term from its start date.
- Under [Membership policies](/settings/membership-policies), set **Ending before the commitment** to **Charge by months left**, with rules `6 months / 50%` and `3 months / 25%`.
- A member who joined on 1 January and cancels to end on 1 July has 6 payments left, so they owe 6 x 50 x 50% = 150. Ending on 1 November, with 2 left, is free.
- If a member is moving abroad and the gym agrees to let them go, staff cancel from the detail page and turn on **Waive the charge**.

## Tips & best practices

- **Let the plan carry the defaults.** Set the price, session limits, sharing, and auto-renew on the [plan](/sales/plans) so every sale copies consistent terms. Override on the membership only for genuine exceptions.
- **Prefer cancel over delete.** Delete is refused once a charge has been invoiced or paid against, and cancelling keeps the financial trail. Keep delete for memberships created in error.
- **Use pause, not cancel, for temporary absences.** Pause keeps paid-for time and the member's history; cancelling and selling again does not.
- **Adjust sessions instead of editing them.** **Adjust sessions** records who changed what and why, and the next period starts clean.
- **Watch the Used tab for packs.** Members on the **Used** tab have spent their sessions and, on a one-time pack, need a new membership rather than a renewal.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A membership I sold shows an amount owed.

**Cause**: It was saved without a payment. "Require payment upfront" binds members buying for themselves; staff can sell on credit, and the amount stays owed.

**Fix**: Collect it with **Add Payment**, or take the payment in the Add Membership dialog next time.

**Issue**: I can't find **Renew** on a membership.

**Cause**: Renew is hidden for paused, cancelled, and one-time memberships.

**Fix**: Resume a paused membership first. For a one-time pack or a cancelled membership, sell a new one.

**Issue**: A member's booking went through even though their pack shows no sessions left.

**Cause**: The booking was made while the membership still had a session, which was taken at booking time.

**Fix**: Nothing to do. The pre-booked session is already paid for and check-in proceeds. New bookings are blocked once the sessions run out.

**Issue**: I paused a membership but the member was still charged.

**Cause**: The charge was taken before the pause, or the membership was edited rather than paused.

**Fix**: Always use **Pause** - it voids pending renewal charges. If a charge was already paid, refund it from [Charges](/sales/charges).

**Issue**: A member's renewal was declined, but their membership is still active.

**Cause**: By design, a declined renewal never ends or pauses a membership.

**Fix**: Follow up with the member, or cancel the membership if you choose to. [Membership billing](/sales/membership-billing) lists every renewal whose last charge failed.

**Issue**: A member says they can't cancel or pause online.

**Cause**: Their [membership policy](/settings/membership-policies) only lets staff do it, the membership has a minimum term that is not yet met, or the member has used all their pauses for the year.

**Fix**: Cancel or pause it for them from the detail page. You are warned about the policy, and the change still goes through.

**Issue**: **Delete** fails.

**Cause**: A charge on the membership has been invoiced or had a payment attempt, or check-ins still point at the membership.

**Fix**: Cancel the membership instead to keep its financial records.

**Issue**: A membership isn't offered as a way to pay when booking a class.

**Cause**: None of its session limits covers that class, area, or instructor, the count that covers it is spent, or the booking falls outside the plan's hours.

**Fix**: Open the membership and check its usage and what each count covers. Widen it with **Edit** if its copy of the plan is out of date.
