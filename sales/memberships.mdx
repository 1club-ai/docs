A membership is an instance of a [plan](/sales/plans) assigned to a specific contact. The plan defines price, billing frequency, scope, and use limits; the membership snapshots those values at the moment of sale and tracks that member's usage and billing schedule from then on. The front desk sells memberships, managers pause, renew, and cancel them, and members use them to book and check in.

## When to use it

The Memberships page is where you manage the full lifecycle of every membership in the org, and see at a glance how many sessions each capped one has left. Editing the plan later does not rewrite memberships already sold, so each member keeps the terms they bought. The same contact can hold more than one membership at the same time (for example a monthly main plan plus a 10-class add-on pack).

Typical uses:

- **An unlimited monthly plan** - a yoga studio's members book as many classes as they like, and each renewal charges the card on file.
- **A class pack that counts down** - a reformer studio sells a 10-class pack, each booking takes one use, and a cancelled booking gives it back.
- **Court hours** - a padel gym sells 20 court hours a month, and a 90-minute booking takes 1.5 hours off the allowance.
- **A hold for an injury or a holiday** - a jiu-jitsu member tears a ligament, so you pause the membership and they lose none of the time they paid for.
- **A 12-month contract** - a strength gym sells a monthly plan with a 12-month minimum term. A member who leaves after five months owes an early cancellation charge, which the front desk can waive.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how each of these is set up.

A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) appears here too, sold from the schedule rather than from this page. It behaves differently in three places, each noted below: its allowance, its cancel dialog, and its renewal.

<Note>
  A membership and a booking share the same payment, pricing, and usage-consumption code. A membership consumed at check-in behaves exactly as it does when consumed through a booking - there is no separate "membership check-in" logic. See [Check-in models](/reference/check-in-models).
</Note>

## Opening the page

The Memberships page lists every membership in the org, regardless of status, with status tabs, a billing-frequency filter, and search by member or plan.

Go to **Sales** > **Memberships** (`/billing/plans/memberships`). Memberships sits between **Promotions** and **Orders** in the Sales section, next to the [Plans](/sales/plans) they are sold from. Old links to `/members/memberships` still open this page. You need permission to view memberships to see the tab.

## Tabs and counts

Tabs filter by status, with server-side counts in each badge:

- **All** - everything.
- **Active** - the default tab. Currently valid memberships.
- **Paused** - billing and access on hold.
- **Pending** - start date is in the future.
- **Used** - all available sessions consumed but the membership has not been cancelled.
- **Cancelled** - cancelled either immediately or at end of period.
- **Expired** - past the end date without renewal.

<Tip>
  A membership whose renewal charge is declined can be moved to an internal "pending payment" state by the billing engine until the payment settles. It is not one of the tabs above; it reappears as **Active** once the outstanding charge is paid.
</Tip>

The frequency filter in the drawer narrows by billing frequency: **recurring** (any recurring cadence), **one-time**, **weekly**, **monthly**, **quarterly**, **annually**, or **custom**.

## Grid columns

Each row shows:

- **Contact** - the member who holds the membership.
- **Plan** - the plan the membership was sold under.
- **Period** - the current period's start and end dates ("Ongoing" for recurring memberships with no fixed end).
- **Price** - the snapshotted price, with the billing frequency underneath.
- **Auto Renew** - Yes/No (shown as "-" for one-time memberships).
- **Shared with account** - **Yes** when everyone on the holder's household or company account can use the membership, otherwise "-".
- **Usage** - the allowance bar (see [How usage is counted](#how-usage-is-counted)). Shows **Unlimited** when the plan has no use cap.
- **Status** - the status chip.
- **Payment Status** - whether the membership's charges are paid, pending, or overdue.

Click a row to open the [membership detail page](#the-membership-detail-page). The row action menu exposes **View**, **Edit**, and the actions that apply to the current status: **Renew**, **Extend**, **Reconcile bookings**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel Membership**, **Add Payment** (while something is unpaid), and **Delete**.

### Reconcile bookings

**Reconcile bookings** covers a member's eligible bookings in the current period with the membership, up to its remaining allowance. Use it when bookings were paid some other way before the member bought a capped membership that should cover them. Bookings beyond the allowance stay on their existing payment. It is offered on active memberships with a use cap.

To reconcile several at once, select rows with the checkboxes and choose **Reconcile bookings** from the bulk actions. Non-active memberships are skipped. If some fail, they stay selected so you can retry them.

## Adding a membership

Click **Add Membership**. The dialog lets you:

1. **Pick the contact.** On an existing membership this field is locked - a membership can't be reassigned to a different member.
2. **Pick the plan.** Fields auto-populate from the plan: price, billing frequency, signup fee, max uses, and scope (which classes, areas, or instructors the membership covers).
3. **Adjust the start date.** The end date and renewal date are computed from the billing frequency, but you can override them.
4. **Set auto-renew.** Only available for recurring frequencies; defaults from the plan.
5. **Check the minimum term.** On a recurring plan, **Minimum term** defaults to the plan's term, marked "(plan)". Change it to give one member a different commitment, or pick **None**. Under the field you see the date the term is met and how many payments it commits the member to. See [Membership policies](/settings/membership-policies#the-minimum-term-is-set-on-the-plan).
6. **Apply a discount (optional).** Enter a promotion code or pick a discount in the price section. The discount lowers the saved price, so recurring renewals inherit the reduced amount going forward. Only discount-type promotions apply here, not wallet vouchers.
7. **Take payment (optional).** If the plan requires payment upfront, the payment section opens automatically. You can still save without a payment: the dialog notes that the plan is normally paid when sold, and the amount stays as an outstanding balance to collect from the member's profile. The signup fee is charged as a separate charge and is not reduced by a discount.

## The membership detail page

Click a row to open a membership's detail page. It shows:

- A **Membership Details** card with the plan, status, price, signup fee, any discount, billing frequency, auto-renew, sharing, usage, and payment status, plus pause details and any days it was **Extended** by, with the reason. When they apply, it also shows the **Minimum term** and the date it is met (with any days added by pauses), a **Booked pause**, and for a scheduled cancellation the **Ends on (cancellation scheduled)** date, the **Cancellation reason**, and any **Early cancellation charge** (marked "waived" when staff waived it).
- A **Payment** card with the **Total**, what is **Paid**, and what is **Still owed**.
- A **Member** card linking to the holder.
- Tabs for the membership's **Charges**, **Bookings**, and **Check-in History**, each with a count.

While a charge on the membership is unpaid, **Add Payment** leads the header so you can collect it in one click. **Edit** sits beside it. The more-actions menu holds **Renew**, **Extend**, **Reconcile bookings**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Call off booked pause**, **Withdraw cancellation**, **Cancel**, and **Delete**, each shown only when it applies.

### Editing a membership

Editing an existing membership uses the same dialog. Because a plan can be edited after a membership was sold, the edit form also exposes the snapshot fields - **renewal date**, **billing frequency**, **max uses**, **uses remaining**, **session unit**, and **scope** - so you can repair a stale snapshot directly on the membership without touching the plan.

<Warning>
  To enter or leave the paused state, use the **Pause** and **Resume** actions, not the edit dialog. Pausing has side effects (voiding scheduled charges, shifting the renewal clock) that the generic edit form deliberately does not perform.
</Warning>

## The lifecycle

Memberships move through the states below. The actions available on each row reflect the current status.

### Pause and resume

Use **Pause** (available only from **Active**) to put a membership on hold. Billing stops, access is blocked, and any pending future recurrence charge is voided so the member is not billed during the freeze. The renewal clock stops too.

The pause dialog offers:

- **Start the pause on a later day** - toggle it on to book the pause for a **Pause starts on** date. It starts as that day begins, and until then the membership runs as normal. Leave it off to pause today.
- **Schedule an auto-resume date** - toggle it on to set a **Resume On** date; the membership resumes automatically on that date. Leave it off for an indefinite pause that requires manual resumption.
- **Reason (optional)** - a free-text note (e.g. vacation, injury, medical leave), up to 500 characters.

If the pause goes against the membership's [policy](/settings/membership-policies) - longer than its **Longest pause**, or past its **Pauses per year** - the dialog warns you before you confirm. The pause still goes through.

A booked pause shows on the detail page as **Booked pause**. Use **Call off booked pause** from the more-actions menu to cancel it before it starts.

When you **Resume** (available only from **Paused**), the end date, the renewal date, and any minimum term shift forward by the pause duration so the member recovers exactly the time they paid for. The resume dialog previews the new dates before you confirm. Resuming does not itself create a new charge; the billing engine seeds the next renewal charge from the shifted renewal date once it falls due.

Members can pause their own membership too, today or from a later day, when their membership policy allows it. They can end a pause they started early, or call off one they booked. See [How members pause](/settings/membership-policies#how-members-pause).

### Renew

**Renew** appends a new billing period. It is available for active, pending, used, and expired memberships, but not for paused, cancelled, or one-time ones.

- If the membership is still within its current period, the new period stacks on top of the existing end/renewal date.
- If it has expired or its end date is in the past, the new period starts today.

The renew dialog previews the resulting **New billing period** dates and the price. You can renew now and leave the charge pending for later settlement. If the plan requires payment upfront, the dialog notes it, and without a payment the new period stays as an outstanding balance. One-time memberships show a warning and cannot be renewed - create a new membership instead.

### Extend

**Extend** adds days on top of the period the plan sets, for example to make up for a closure. Enter the **Extra days** and a **Reason**. The dialog shows the **New date** the membership ends or renews on. Use a negative number to take back days granted by mistake. The extension and its reason show on the membership detail page.

Extend is available on any membership that has an end or renewal date and is not cancelled. If the membership has already ended, the extra days bring it back to active.

### Cancel

**Cancel** is available for any membership that is not already cancelled or expired. The dialog asks two things:

- **Why is the membership ending?** - pick a reason: **Moving away**, **Too expensive**, **Not using it**, **Injury or health**, **Switching to another club**, **Unhappy with the service**, **Club's decision**, or **Other**. **Other** needs a note. You can add a **Note (optional)** to any reason.
- **Membership ends** - when it ends:
  - **Today (ends immediately)** - the status flips to cancelled, the end date is set to today, and access is revoked at end of day.
  - **A coming period end** - the end of the current period is preselected: the membership stops renewing and stays active until then. Pick a later period end and it keeps renewing until that date, then stops; the detail page shows the date under **Ends on (cancellation scheduled)**.

A membership that does not auto-renew (including one-time packs) can only end today - there is no future period to cancel against.

**Season passes** open their own dialog instead. Ending one has to release the courts and close the pass together, so the generic cancel is refused for them. See [Cancelling a season pass](/schedule/season-passes#cancelling-a-season-pass).

Either way, any pending recurrence charges after the end date are voided so the member is not billed again.

#### Minimum terms and the early cancellation charge

When a membership has a minimum term that the chosen date doesn't meet, its [membership policy](/settings/membership-policies) decides what leaving early costs. A date that carries a charge shows it in the list, for example "1 July 2027 - 150.00 early cancellation charge", and the dialog explains how many committed payments ending then leaves unmade. Turn on **Waive the charge** to record the cancellation without it. Otherwise the charge is added to what the member owes, as a charge on the membership. Ending today counts the same remaining payments as ending when the current period does, because the rest of that period is already paid.

You are offered every date, even ones the member could not choose online. When a date goes against the policy - it ends before the minimum term is met, or sooner than the notice period allows - the dialog warns you, and the cancellation still goes through. See [What staff see](/settings/membership-policies#what-staff-see).

#### Changing or withdrawing a cancellation

To move a scheduled cancellation to another date, cancel again and pick the new date. The new cancellation replaces the old one, and a charge the member has already paid counts towards the new one, so they are never charged twice.

To take a cancellation back, choose **Withdraw cancellation** from the more-actions menu on the detail page. The membership renews again, and an unpaid early cancellation charge is voided. A charge that was already paid stays paid, for you to refund or keep.

Members can cancel their own memberships when their policy allows it: they choose the end date, give a reason, and can withdraw the cancellation until that date. See [How members cancel](/settings/membership-policies#how-members-cancel). You are notified when they do.

Cancelling needs the memberships delete permission, not just update, because it ends the subscription and triggers its refunds.

### Delete

Deletion is only available to admins with the delete permission, and only when the membership has **no** charges. To remove a membership that has been paid against, cancel it instead so financial history is preserved. Attempting to delete a membership with charges is blocked with a clear error.

## How usage is counted

A membership tracks usage only when the plan sets a use cap (**Max Uses**). Uncapped memberships show **Unlimited** and are never debited. The usage bar reads as consumed / total in the plan's **session unit**:

- **Bookings** - each session counts as one unit.
- **Hours** - each session debits its duration in hours, so a 90-minute court booking debits 1.5 hours.

When and how a use is debited:

- **Booked in advance** - the use is debited when the booking is created. Cancelling the booking restores the use (never above the max), and if the membership had flipped to **Used**, restoring a use flips it back to **Active**.
- **Consumed at check-in** - a member checked in directly against the membership (no prior booking) debits a use at check-in. When the last use is consumed the membership flips to **Used**.
- **No double-debit** - a check-in linked to a booking does not debit again, because the booking already paid for the session.

<Note>
  A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) reads its allowance the other way round. The sale spends the season's hours up front on the weeks it books, so a freshly sold pass shows its allowance fully committed here (`27 / 27 hours`), and `0 of 27 hours left` on member-facing surfaces. It rests there for the whole season, is never marked **Used** at zero, and the counter rises only when a cancelled week credits a makeup hour back. Its two real ends are the season's last day and cancelling the pass.
</Note>

<Note>
  An exhausted or no-longer-active membership still honors bookings that were created against it while it was active. The booking debited a use at booking time, so check-in for that specific booking goes through even if the membership has since hit zero uses or expired. See [Attendance](/schedule/attendance) for the walk-in versus pre-booked distinction.
</Note>

## Membership scope

Memberships can be scoped to specific **classes**, **areas**, or **instructors** (the `validFor` snapshot). The scope is set on the plan and copied to the membership at sale. When a booking is being created, only memberships whose scope covers the chosen class, area, or instructor are offered as a payment option.

If the plan restricts bookable hours (for example an off-peak plan valid 20:00-23:00 on weekdays), a booking that falls outside that window - or straddles its boundary - is not covered by the membership.

You can edit the scope on an existing membership if the plan's snapshot is stale.

## Auto-renew

Recurring memberships can auto-renew. When the period ends, a renewal charge is generated and collected from the member's saved payment method. Auto-renew is only available for active, used, or pending memberships, and defaults to the plan's default-auto-renewal setting. If a renewal charge is declined, the membership is deactivated until the payment settles, then reactivates and resumes its billing cycle from the failed period.

A [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) never auto-renews. It is paid once for a fixed period, and the next season is a fresh sale at the new season's prices.

## Worked examples

### Unlimited yoga membership (Pilates/Yoga)

A studio sells an "Unlimited Monthly Yoga" plan.

- **Billing frequency** - monthly, **auto-renew** on.
- **Max Uses** - empty, so the usage column shows **Unlimited** and no session is ever debited.
- **Scope** - classes, limited to the yoga class type.

The member books as many yoga classes as they like each month. Each renewal charges the card on file. If they go on holiday, **Pause** with an auto-resume date freezes billing and pushes the renewal date out by the freeze length.

### 10-class pilates reformer pack (Pilates/Yoga)

A reformer studio sells a "10-Class Reformer Pack".

- **Billing frequency** - one-time, **auto-renew** off.
- **Max Uses** - 10, **session unit** Bookings.
- **Scope** - classes, limited to the reformer class type.

Each reformer booking debits one use; the usage bar counts down 0/10 to 10/10. When the tenth session is consumed the membership moves to the **Used** tab. Because it is one-time, **Renew** is not offered - sell the member a fresh pack. If a booking is cancelled, the use is returned and a **Used** pack flips back to **Active**.

### Racket-club court hours (Racket sports)

A padel club sells "20 Court Hours Monthly".

- **Billing frequency** - monthly, **auto-renew** on.
- **Max Uses** - 20, **session unit** Hours.
- **Scope** - areas, limited to the court area type.

A 90-minute court booking debits 1.5 hours from the allowance, a 60-minute booking debits 1. When the member runs out of hours before the month ends, the membership shows **Used**; renewing (or the auto-renewal at period end) resets the allowance for the new period.

### Membership hold for an injured athlete (Martial arts)

A jiu-jitsu gym runs an "Unlimited Monthly" plan. A member tears a ligament and needs six weeks off.

- Open the member's membership and click **Pause**.
- Enter a **Reason** of "injury" and either schedule the resume date six weeks out or leave it indefinite.
- Billing stops and the scheduled renewal charge is voided. On resume, the renewal date shifts forward by the exact pause length, so the member loses none of the time they paid for.

### 12-month contract with an early cancellation charge (Strength gym)

A gym sells "Monthly Membership" at 50 per month with a 12-month minimum term.

- On the [plan](/sales/plans#pricing), set **Minimum term** to 12 months. Every membership sold on it carries the term from its start date.
- Under [Membership policies](/settings/membership-policies), set **Ending before the commitment** to **Charge by months left**, with rules `6 months / 50%` and `3 months / 25%`.
- A member who joined on 1 January and cancels to end on 1 July has 6 payments left, so they owe 6 x 50 x 50% = 150. Ending on 1 November, with 2 left, is free.
- If a member is moving abroad and the gym agrees to let them go, staff cancel from the detail page and turn on **Waive the charge**.

## Tips & best practices

- **Let the plan carry the defaults.** Configure price, use limits, scope, and default auto-renewal on the [plan](/sales/plans) so every sale snapshots consistent terms. Only override on the membership for genuine exceptions.
- **Prefer cancel over delete.** Delete is blocked once any charge exists, and cancelling preserves the financial trail. Reserve delete for memberships created in error before any charge.
- **Use pause, not cancel, for temporary absences.** Pause preserves paid-for time and the member's history; cancelling and re-selling does not.
- **Schedule the resume date when you know it.** A scheduled auto-resume keeps a paused membership showing a coherent renewal date and reactivates without staff action.
- **Watch the Used tab for one-time packs.** Members on the **Used** tab have consumed their allowance and (for one-time packs) need a new membership, not a renewal.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A membership I sold shows an outstanding balance.

**Cause**: The plan has "require payment upfront" on, and the membership was saved without a payment. That rule binds members buying for themselves; staff can sell on credit, and the amount stays owed.

**Fix**: Collect it from the member's profile, or next time add a payment method in the payment section of the Add Membership dialog.

**Issue**: I can't find the **Renew** action on a membership.

**Cause**: Renew is hidden for paused, cancelled, and one-time memberships.

**Fix**: Resume a paused membership first. For a one-time pack, create a new membership. For cancelled memberships, sell a fresh one.

**Issue**: A member's booking went through even though their pack shows zero uses.

**Cause**: The booking was created while the membership still had a use; the use was debited at booking time.

**Fix**: This is expected. The pre-booked session is already paid for and check-in proceeds. New bookings are blocked once the allowance is exhausted.

**Issue**: I paused a membership but the member was still charged.

**Cause**: The charge was raised before the pause, or the membership was edited rather than paused.

**Fix**: Always use the **Pause** action - it voids pending recurrence charges. If a charge already settled, handle it through the refund flow in [Charges](/sales/charges).

**Issue**: A member says they can't cancel or pause online.

**Cause**: Their [membership policy](/settings/membership-policies) only lets staff do it, the membership has a minimum term that is not yet met, or the member has used all their pauses for the year.

**Fix**: Cancel or pause it for them from the detail page. You are warned about the policy, and the change still goes through.

**Issue**: The **Delete** action is missing or fails.

**Cause**: Either you lack the delete permission, or the membership has charges.

**Fix**: Cancel the membership instead to retain its financial records. Only charge-free memberships can be hard-deleted.

**Issue**: A membership isn't offered as a payment option when booking a class.

**Cause**: The membership's scope doesn't cover that class, area, or instructor - or the booking falls outside the plan's allowed hours.

**Fix**: Open the membership, check the scope section, and widen it if the snapshot is stale. Confirm the booking time falls inside any plan operating-hours window.
