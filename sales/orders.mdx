An order is a single purchase container that groups one or more line items a member is buying together - memberships, products, court and class bookings, event tickets, and packages. Each order carries its own status, an aggregate payment status, and links out to the bookings, memberships, and charges it creates when it is completed. Orders come from the front desk at the point of sale, from members checking out in the portal, and from booking flows; owners, managers, and front-desk staff then review, complete, and cancel them.

## When to use it

The **Orders** tab is the record of every basket your organization has assembled. It shows what a member bought together on one receipt, whether the money is in, and lets you finish a draft or reverse a finished purchase with everything it created.

Typical uses:

- **A court plus a racket rental** - a padel member books an hour on court and rents a racket at the desk, paid by card in one order, with each line linked to the booking and charge it produced.
- **A drop-in who changes their mind** - a jiu-jitsu visitor pays for a mat session and a bottle of water, then decides not to train, so you cancel the completed order, which voids its charges and refunds what they paid.
- **A membership with a joining fee** - a Pilates client joins a monthly reformer plan, and the order carries the membership and its signup fee as separate lines.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for more.

## What an order contains

An order is the parent; its **line items** are the children. Each line has a type that decides what completing the order creates:

- **Booking** - A court, class, instructor, or event-ticket reservation. Becomes a booking record with its own charge.
- **Membership** - A plan purchase. Becomes a membership, and its signup fee (when the plan charges one) is carried as a separate **membership signup fee** line.
- **Product** - A retail or food-and-drink item sold by quantity at a unit price (for example a drink, towel, or racket rental).
- **Package** - A bundle sold as one item; it is expanded into its component membership and product lines at order-create time.
- **Discount** - A negative line written when a promotion is applied at completion, so the line-item total matches what the member actually pays.

An order also carries a **member** (the contact it belongs to), an optional **club**, and an optional order-level **note** (for example kitchen instructions or a buzzer/pager number for a food order). A product-only order can be anonymous, but any order containing a booking or membership line must have a member attached.

## How orders relate to charges, invoices, and payments

An order is the top of the money trail:

- **Order -> charges** - Completing an order creates one charge per billable line (a booking charge, a membership charge, a product charge). The order's payment status is the roll-up of these charges' statuses. See [Charges](/sales/charges).
- **Charges -> invoices** - Charges can be grouped onto an invoice for the member. The invoice is the document; the order is the basket that produced the underlying charges. See [Invoices](/sales/invoices).
- **Charges -> payments** - A payment is recorded against one or more charges to settle them. Completing an order records a payment against the order's charges with the method you selected. See [Payments](/sales/payments).

The order detail page surfaces these links directly. Each line's **Linked to** column deep-links to the **booking**, **membership**, and **charge** it produced, and when an order has been synced to an external system (for example a POS for fiscalisation) an **Integrations** card shows the linked system and sync time.

## The order list

Use **Sales** > **Orders** to review, complete, and cancel every purchase your organization has taken.

Open **Sales** > **Orders**. The list shows every order with these columns:

- **#** - The order number.
- **Member** - The contact the order belongs to (blank for anonymous product-only sales).
- **Status** - Draft, Open, Completed, or Cancelled.
- **Payment Status** - The rolled-up payment state across the order's charges (hidden while the order is still a draft with no live charges).
- **Source** - Where the sale came from, for example the till, the member portal, or your website.
- **Items** - How many line items the order holds.
- **Total** - The order total, shown tax-exclusive in the admin grid.
- **Created at** - When the order was opened.

Tabs across the top filter by status: **All**, **Draft**, **Open**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled**, each with a live count. Sort defaults to newest first. The row action menu offers **View** and, unless the order is already cancelled, **Cancel**. The **Point of Sale** button in the top right jumps to the POS to start a new sale.

<Note>
  Orders are created from the point of sale, from member checkout in the portal, or from a booking flow - not from an "Add order" button on this tab. The Orders tab is where you review and resolve those orders after they exist.
</Note>

## Order status and the lifecycle

There are four order statuses:

- **Draft** - The basket is assembled but not yet paid or fulfilled. Nothing has been booked or charged. Draft orders can still be edited (their line items, member, club, and note replaced).
- **Open** - A running tab started at the [point of sale](/pos/point-of-sale). Items can still be added, and each one is fulfilled as soon as it is added. The tab stays open until someone closes it.
- **Completed** - The order has been executed: its lines are now real bookings, memberships, and charges, and payment has been recorded (or left pending for pay-on-arrival).
- **Cancelled** - The order has been voided. For a draft this is just a status change; for a completed order it reverses everything the order created.

The allowed transitions are:

- **Draft -> Completed** when you complete (execute) the order.
- **Open -> Completed** when you close the tab.
- **Draft -> Cancelled** when you discard a draft.
- **Open -> Cancelled** or **Completed -> Cancelled** when you reverse the order.

A cancelled order is terminal - it cannot be re-opened, and attempting to cancel it again is rejected.

## Payment status vs order status

The **payment status** is separate from the order status and is rolled up from the order's charges:

- **Paid** - Every live charge on the order is paid.
- **Partially paid** - Some charges are paid (or one is marked partially paid) while others are still outstanding.
- **Refunded** - At least one charge has been refunded.
- **Pending** - Charges exist but none are settled yet.
- **Hidden** - No live charges yet (a draft, or a fully voided order), so the chip is not shown rather than reading "pending" for something that was never billed.

Voided charges are ignored in this roll-up, so a voided line does not drag an otherwise-paid order into a misleading state.

## Completing a draft order

Completing (executing) a draft is what turns intent into records.

1. Open the draft from **Sales** > **Orders**.
2. Click **Complete order** in the top right.
3. In the **Complete order** dialog, pick a **payment method** (required when the order total is greater than zero). Add wallet credit as a secondary tender if you want a split - see [Payments](/sales/payments).
4. Confirm. The order is executed: each line becomes a booking, membership, or product charge, and payment is recorded against the resulting charges with the method you chose.

The in-app help reads: "Add a payment method and complete the order to create bookings, memberships, and charges." A zero-total order (for example a booking fully covered by a membership) completes without a payment method.

<Note>
  If you choose pay-on-arrival, the booking is confirmed but its charge is left **pending** - no wallet debit and no card charge. Collect payment on site later. The order still moves to **Completed**; only its payment status stays open.
</Note>

## Closing an open order

Open the order and click **Close order**. Any line not yet fulfilled is fulfilled first, then the order moves to **Completed**. Closing doesn't depend on payment: a closed order can still carry an outstanding balance, which you collect from its charges.

## Issuing an invoice or pro-forma for an order

From an order that has a member, you can issue a numbered document for the sale. Use **Issue invoice** or **Issue pro-forma** in the order's header. Each takes the next number in its series.

- A sale can carry one document of each type.
- **Issue pro-forma** is not offered once the order is paid, because a pro-forma asks to be paid up front.
- While an unpaid pro-forma is outstanding, **Issue invoice** is not offered. Once the pro-forma is paid, use **Convert pro-forma to invoice** to issue the invoice from it. The pro-forma is kept as sent.

Issuing documents needs the **billing.create.organization** permission. See [Invoices](/sales/invoices).

## Cancelling an order

1. Open the order and click **Cancel**, or use the **Cancel** row action in the list.
2. Confirm in the **Cancel order** dialog ("Are you sure you want to cancel this order? This action cannot be undone.").
3. If the order has been paid, pick where the refund goes under **Refund to**: **Original payment method** or **Wallet balance**. The dialog shows the **Refund amount**, and the button reads **Cancel and refund**. If only one destination is possible, it is picked for you.

What cancelling does depends on the order's state:

- **Draft** - The status flips to **Cancelled**. Nothing else happens because nothing was created.
- **Completed** - Each booking line is cancelled through the standard booking-cancellation flow, each membership line is cancelled immediately, and every related charge is voided. Then the order status becomes **Cancelled**. This reversal is idempotent, so an already-cancelled booking or already-voided charge is skipped rather than erroring.

<Note>
  A paid order can't be cancelled without refunding it, so the money never stays with your gym after the sale is erased. When a card payment can't be reversed through the card provider, the refund can only go to the wallet, and the dialog says so. When no automatic refund is available at all, the dialog tells you to refund it from the charge first and then cancel. See [Payments](/sales/payments).
</Note>

## Editing a draft

Only **draft** orders can be edited. Editing replaces the order's line items and, optionally, its member, club, and note. Once an order is completed it is immutable - you cancel and rebuild rather than edit. Attempting to edit a non-draft order is rejected with "Only draft orders can be updated".

## Worked examples

### Padel court plus racket rental in one sale

A member books an hour on court 2 and rents a racket at the desk.

- The order holds two lines: a **booking** (the court) and a **product** (racket rental).
- Complete it with **Card** as the payment method.
- Completion creates a booking charge and a product charge; the order payment status becomes **Paid**.
- The booking line's **Linked to** column points at the new booking and its charge.

### Jiu-jitsu drop-in taken at the front desk

A visitor pays for a single mat session and grabs a bottle of water.

- Built at the point of sale as a **product**-only or booking-plus-product order.
- Take **Cash**; the order completes and its charges are marked paid.
- If they later decide not to train, cancel the completed order and choose where the refund goes.

### Pilates membership with a signup fee

A new client joins on a monthly reformer plan that charges a joining fee.

- The order carries a **membership** line plus a **membership signup fee** line.
- Complete it with the client's chosen method. Completion creates the membership and its charges.
- The **Linked to** column takes you to the new membership under [Memberships](/sales/memberships).

### Climbing day pass sold with a promotion

A walk-in buys a day pass and you apply a discount code at checkout.

- The order holds the **booking** (or membership) line; applying the promotion at completion writes a negative **discount** line so the order total matches what the climber pays.
- The discount line references the promotion it came from. See [Promotions](/sales/promotions).

### Class pack sold as a package

A yoga studio sells a 10-class pass.

- The order holds a **package** line, which is expanded into its component lines when the order is created.
- Complete it as one basket; the components fulfil together. See [Packages](/sales/packages).

## Tips & best practices

- **Read status as two facts, not one.** "Completed" says the order was fulfilled; the payment-status chip says whether the money is in. A completed order can still be **Pending** if you took it pay-on-arrival.
- **Clear your Draft tab.** Drafts are abandoned or in-progress baskets. Complete the real ones and discard the stale ones so the tab reflects live work.
- **Cancel, do not edit, a finished order.** Completed orders are immutable by design. If a purchase is wrong after completion, cancel it (which reverses the bookings, memberships, and charges) and build a fresh order.
- **Use the note field for fulfilment context.** Kitchen instructions or a pager number on a food-and-drink order ride along on the order and flow to a connected POS.
- **Follow the Linked to trail for disputes.** When a member queries a charge, open the order and jump straight to the booking, membership, or charge from the item grid rather than searching each list.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: The **Complete order** button is missing.

**Solution**: It only shows for **draft** orders. An open order shows **Close order** instead, and a completed or cancelled order has nothing left to execute.

**Issue**: Completing the order fails with a message about a required payment method.

**Solution**: The order total is greater than zero, so the **Complete order** dialog needs a payment method before it can record payment. Pick one (or apply wallet credit / pay-on-arrival). A zero-total order completes without one.

**Issue**: I cannot edit an order - it says only draft orders can be updated.

**Solution**: The order is already completed or cancelled. Cancel it and build a new draft instead of editing.

**Issue**: A completed order shows **Pending** payment status.

**Solution**: The order was taken pay-on-arrival, so its charges are still open. Record the payment against those charges to move it to **Paid** - see [Payments](/sales/payments).

**Issue**: I can't cancel a paid order - the dialog says no automatic refund is available.

**Solution**: None of the order's payments can be returned automatically. Refund it from the charge first, then cancel the order. See [Charges](/sales/charges).

**Issue**: The payment-status chip is missing on a draft.

**Solution**: That is expected. A draft has no live charges yet, so there is nothing to roll up and the chip stays hidden until the order is completed.
