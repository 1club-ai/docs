**Sales** is where you set up what your gym sells and follow the money it brings in. It replaces the old **Revenue** menu and also holds **Promotions** (formerly under **Marketing**) and **Memberships** (formerly under **Members**). Owners and managers set up the catalog here, and front-desk staff and bookkeepers use it to review orders, chase what is owed, and reconcile the till.

The register itself is a separate rail item, [Point of Sale](/pos/point-of-sale). Sales is where you manage what the register sells and review what it took.

## The Sales section

Go to **Sales** in the left rail (`/sales`). It opens on the first tab you can access, usually **Plans**. Each tab appears only when your role has permission for it.

| Tab | What it holds | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Plans](/sales/plans) | Membership and pass templates | Set up pricing tiers, recurrence rules, and usage limits. |
| [Products](/sales/products) | The catalog of sellable items, with sub-tabs for packages, stock, and rentals | Define retail and add-on items sold at the point of sale and on signup forms. |
| [Promotions](/sales/promotions) | Vouchers and discounts | Run trials, intro offers, and promo codes. |
| [Memberships](/sales/memberships) | Every membership sold from your plans | Pause, renew, cancel, and track usage. |
| [Orders](/sales/orders) | Every basket bought at the desk, in the portal, or through a booking | Review, complete, or cancel a purchase and everything it created. |
| **Finance** | Money owed and collected, as tabs (see below) | Audit charges, collect payments, issue invoices, and manage wallets. |
| [Cash](/sales/cash) | The cash drawer and expenses | Count the till and record what your gym spends. Only shown when the cash module is on. |

### Products sub-tabs

**Sales** > **Products** groups the catalog with the pages that sell and stock it:

- **Products** - the [catalog](/sales/products) itself.
- **Packages** - fixed-price [bundles](/sales/packages) of plans and products.
- **Stock** and **Ingredients** - on-hand counts and recipe ingredients, when [inventory](/sales/inventory) is on.
- **Deliveries** - supplier [deliveries](/sales/deliveries) with invoice scanning, when inventory is on.
- **Rentals** - [gear lent out](/sales/rentals) and still to come back, when rentals are on or gear is still out.

### Finance

**Sales** > **Finance** (`/finance`) gathers everything about money that has moved, and the renewals about to move it, into one tab with its own sub-tabs. It opens on **Charges**.

| Sub-tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| [Charges](/sales/charges) | Everything owed: what someone owes you, one row per thing sold |
| **Membership billing** | The membership renewals coming up, and how each one will be collected |
| [Payments](/sales/payments) | Every payment: how money came in, and who recorded it |
| [Invoices](/sales/invoices) | Numbered invoice and pro-forma documents |
| [Invoice Runs](/sales/bill-runs) | Batches that sweep a period of charges into invoices |
| **Statements** | A month's statements for your members, printed for everyone at once |
| [Wallet](/sales/wallet) | Each member's store-credit balance |
| **Wallet movements** | Every credit and debit across all wallets |

The sub-tabs keep their own addresses under `/billing` (for example `/billing/charges` and `/billing/invoices`), so older bookmarks and links in emails still open the right page.

### Cash

**Sales** > **Cash** appears once you turn on the cash module under **Settings** > **Sales** > **Cash Registry & Expenses**. It has three sub-tabs: **Overview** (cash on hand and expenses by category), **Expenses**, and **Ledger**. See [Cash registry and expenses](/sales/cash).

## The money model

Sales is built on three concepts that are deliberately kept separate. Once you have the model in your head, every Finance screen lines up.

- A **charge** is what someone owes you for one thing: a booking, a membership period or signup fee, a product, a check-in, a cancellation charge, or a settlement. Charges were called transactions in earlier versions of 1club.
- A **payment** is money that came in. One charge can be paid by several payments (a deposit, then the rest at the desk), and one payment can pay several charges (a basket at checkout).
- An **invoice** is a document issued over one or more charges for a contact, with a number and a due date. Not every charge is invoiced, and invoices don't move money on their own.

A charge's `paymentStatus` tells you whether it is still owed. `pending` (shown as **Unpaid**), `partially_paid`, `overdue`, and `failed` are unresolved: the ones that have fallen due make up the contact's **Due now**, and the rest show as **Upcoming** (see [What counts as due now](/members/member-profiles#what-counts-as-due-now)). `paid`, `settled`, `refunded`, `void`, and `cancelled` are closed.

Three ways to take something back are kept apart too. A **refund** returns money from a payment. **Voiding** closes an unpaid charge that should never have been owed. A **reversal** is a negative charge that cancels all or part of another, for example when a booking is cancelled, so revenue reports stay balanced.

This separation is why you can record a payment without ever issuing an invoice (for example, taking cash at the front desk for a single booking), and why an invoice can contain charges from several different bookings or memberships.

## How a charge usually flows

The typical flow for a paid activity is:

1. A booking, membership, or signup fee is created. 1club writes a **charge** with `paymentStatus = pending` (or `paid` if the amount is zero).
2. Either the member or your staff pays it. That can happen up front (card checkout in the member portal or app, through [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe), [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos), or [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay)), at the desk (the [point of sale](/pos/point-of-sale), **Add Payment**, or a [payment terminal](/settings/payment-terminals)), at check-in if the booking policy says so, or later from an invoice.
3. When the payment succeeds, 1club creates a **payment** row, links it to the charge(s), and flips the charge's status to `paid`. Pending bookings tied to that charge are confirmed.

Invoices are optional. They get created when:

- You issue one from a charge, or straight from an [order](/sales/orders#issuing-an-invoice-or-pro-forma-for-an-order).
- You run an [invoice run](/sales/bill-runs), which takes every charge in a period that is not void and not yet invoiced, paid ones included, and groups them into invoices.
- A scheduled invoice run fires (configured under **Settings** > **Billing** > **Automated Invoicing**).

## Recurring memberships

For recurring plans, an hourly job raises each renewal charge a few days before the new period starts (three by default) and then collects it in the way the company that sold the membership takes card payments online:

| The company takes online card payments through | What happens to a renewal |
| --- | --- |
| Stripe | The member's saved card is charged. |
| myPOS, with **Charge renewals to saved cards** on | The member's saved myPOS card is charged. If it is declined or has expired, the member is emailed a payment link instead. |
| myPOS, without saved cards | The member is emailed a payment link for the renewal. |
| DatecsPay, or no online card provider | Nothing is charged. The renewal stays owed for you to collect at the desk or from an invoice. |

A failed renewal never deactivates the membership: the charge is marked failed and staff decide what to do. When the member saves a new card, 1club tries their failed renewals again with it. The same job also marks any `pending` charge whose due date has passed as `overdue`. See [Automatic membership renewals](/settings/integrations/mypos#automatic-membership-renewals) for the myPOS side, and [Plans](/sales/plans) for how a plan's fields drive renewals.

## Settings for Sales

Setup that you do once lives under **Settings**:

- **Settings** > **Sales** - **Point of Sale**, **Inventory Management**, **Rentals**, **Cash Registry & Expenses**, and **Payment terminals**. The switches here turn the matching tabs and register features on, and [**Payment terminals**](/settings/payment-terminals) connects the card readers you take payment on.
- **Settings** > **Billing** - **General** (currency, [billing entities](/settings/billing-entities), tax rates, revenue accounts, [payment methods](/settings/payment-methods), [external programs](/settings/external-programs)), [**Membership Policies**](/settings/membership-policies), and **Automated Invoicing** (scheduled [invoice runs](/sales/bill-runs#automated-invoicing)).
- **Settings** > **Bookings** - [**Pricing Rules**](/settings/pricing-rules) and [**Seasons**](/settings/seasons), which set what bookings cost.
- **Settings** > **Integrations** - the card payment providers: [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe), [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos), and [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay), plus [Hype](/settings/integrations/hype) for fiscal receipts.
