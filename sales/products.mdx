Products are the sellable items in your catalog that aren't memberships: pro-shop retail, consumable add-ons, and gear rentals. Memberships and passes live in [Plans](/sales/plans) instead. Each product has a name, a price, a revenue account for accounting, a visibility level, and can be grouped by category and brand. You maintain the catalog in the admin, the front desk rings products up at the point of sale, and members can add them to their order on signup forms.

## When to use it

Add a product for anything you charge for that isn't a membership or pass. Products are what you ring up at the point of sale, bundle into packages, and offer as add-ons on signup order forms, and each one's revenue account keeps retail, rentals, and equipment separate in your accounting. Stock tracking is optional.

Typical uses:

- **A pro shop at a padel gym** - grips, strings, and branded tees grouped in a _Pro shop_ category, scanned by barcode at the desk, and booked to a retail revenue account.
- **Drinks and towels at checkout** - a sports drink and a towel rental on the POS and as add-ons on the signup order form, with the fridge count tracked as stock.
- **Gear rental at a climbing gym** - shoes and harnesses marked as rentals, lent out alongside a day pass in one order and tracked until they come back. See [Equipment rentals](/sales/rentals).

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to set up each of these.

## The list page

You manage products under **Sales** > **Products** in the admin (`/billing/products`). The **Products** tab has its own sub-tabs, each shown only when you have access to it:

| Sub-tab | What it holds | Shown when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Products** | The catalog of sellable items, described on this page | You have any products permission |
| [**Packages**](/sales/packages) | Fixed-price bundles of plans and products | You can view billing |
| [**Stock**](/sales/inventory) | On-hand quantities per location | Inventory is on |
| [**Ingredients**](/sales/inventory#recipes-and-ingredients-for-cafe-and-bar) | Raw items that recipes draw down | Inventory is on |
| [**Deliveries**](/sales/deliveries) | Supplier deliveries and invoice scanning | Inventory is on |
| [**Rentals**](/sales/rentals) | Gear that is lent out and still to come back | Rentals is on, or gear is still out |

Products are shown in a data grid with columns for **Name**, **Price**, **Visibility**, and **Status** (Active or Inactive), plus a row menu to edit or delete. When rentals are on, a **Rented out** chip next to the name marks rental items. Click any row to open the product in the edit dialog. The dialog has its own address (`/billing/products/<id>`), so you can bookmark or share a link to it.

Use the filters to narrow the grid by **Categories** and **Brands**. Select rows with the checkboxes to **Delete** several products at once. Products still used by stock or packages are skipped and reported in a warning.

The page header has two controls:

- **Add** - a menu of everything you can create from this page: **Add Product**, and, when inventory is on and you can manage it, **Add Ingredient**, **Add Inventory**, **New delivery**, and **Add supplier**.
- The more-actions menu - **Categories** opens the category manager and **Brands** opens the brand manager (see [Categories and brands](#categories-and-brands)).

## Creating or editing a product

Choose **Add** > **Add Product** (or click an existing row) to open the product dialog.

The dialog asks for:

- **Product Name** - required.
- **Club** - which club the product belongs to. For single-club organizations the lone club is selected automatically; multi-club organizations pick one, or leave it unset to make the product available across clubs.
- **Category** - the product category, used to group products on the POS. Type to search existing categories, or type a new name to create one inline.
- **Brand** - an optional brand. Type to search, or type a new name to create one inline.
- **Barcode** - an optional code (up to 64 characters) for scan-to-sell at the point of sale. Scan the item into this field to fill it in.
- **SKU / Product code** - an optional internal product code.
- **Color** - a display color for the product.
- **Active** - inactive products are hidden from the POS and can't be added to packages or order forms, but existing sales are preserved.
- **Sale or rental** - shown when rentals are on. Set **Fulfilment** to **Rented out** to lend the product instead of selling it, then set when it is **Due back** and whether to **Return automatically**. See [Equipment rentals](/sales/rentals).
- **Price** - the sell price in your organization's currency. For a rental, it is the fee for one rental.
- **Cost price** - what you pay per unit, used for margin reporting. Optional.
- **Revenue Account** - the accounting code the sale is booked against. Leave it as **None (uses default)** to fall back to your organization's default account, or pick a specific account (shown as `code - name`).
- **Tax rate** - overrides the revenue account's default tax rate at the point of sale. Leave it as **None (uses default)** to use the revenue account's rate.
- **Visibility** - **Public** (visible to anyone), **Admin & Member** (visible to signed-in members and staff), or **Admin only** (staff-only items).
- **Description** - free text shown on public surfaces.
- **Features** - a tag list of short selling points.
- **Images** - up to five images. The hint suggests photographing on a white background and adding three or more (front, back, detail).

When you open an existing product and inventory management is on, the dialog also shows the product's **Stock** section, and a **Recipe** button appears next to the save button if you can manage inventory (see [Inventory and stock](#inventory-and-stock)).

### Categories and brands

Both categories and brands are managed from the Products page header:

- **Categories** group products on the point of sale and are sortable; drag to set the order the groups appear in. Renaming is inline. Deleting a category clears it from any products that used it (the products remain).
- **Brands** are an optional label. Deleting a brand clears it from its products.

You can also create a category or brand on the fly from the product dialog by typing a new name in the picker.

## Where products are sold

### Point of sale

Products are the first tab on the **point of sale**. They're grouped into sections by category, so your POS layout mirrors the category order you set. Uncategorized products fall into their own group. If a product has a barcode, staff can scan it to add it to the cart.

### Packages

Products can be bundled with plans into a fixed-price [package](/sales/packages). When a package sells, its fixed price is split proportionally across the component plans and products, so each product still records revenue against its own revenue account.

### Signup order forms

On a [form](/marketing/forms), you can attach products as **add-on products** - optional items a member can add to their order during signup. Attaching packages turns the signup form into an order form; add-on products then appear alongside as optional extras.

## Inventory and stock

Stock tracking is optional and off by default. Turn it on under **Settings** > **Sales** > **Inventory Management** (which enables ingredient tracking, a stock ledger, and recipe-based consumption). Once enabled, each product's edit dialog gains a **Stock** section and a **Recipe** button.

- **Direct stock** - Simple retail goods (a can of drink, a grip) are tracked directly: their on-hand count decrements on each sale.
- **Recipe-based** - A product that's assembled from ingredients (for example a mixed drink) can have a **Recipe** of ingredients; selling it consumes the ingredients instead.
- **Availability** - The Stock section shows total on-hand for the currently selected club (or the sum across clubs when "All clubs" is active), with a warning chip when it hits zero.
- **Movements ledger** - Every change is recorded as a movement (sale, adjustment, waste, receipt) with a quantity, type, and a link back to the order that caused it.
- **Not tracked** - A product with no recipe and no direct stock shows "This product is not tracked", meaning it sells without touching inventory.

Receiving and adjusting stock require the inventory manage permission; a signed adjustment requires a reason.

## Worked examples

### Pro shop for a racket club

You sell grips, overgrips, strings, and branded tees at the front desk.

- Create a **Category** called _Pro shop_ and add each item as a product in it, with a **Brand** (Wilson, Head, Babolat) where relevant.
- Set a **Barcode** on each so the desk can scan them at the POS.
- Route them to a _Retail sales_ **Revenue Account** so retail revenue is separated from membership revenue in reporting.
- If you enable inventory, track direct stock on strings and grips and receive stock when a delivery arrives.

### Drinks and towels as add-ons

You want members to grab a sports drink or rent a towel at checkout.

- Create products _Sports drink_ and _Towel_ in a _Cafe & add-ons_ category, set **Admin & Member** visibility if they're for members.
- They appear on the POS Products tab, and you can attach them as **add-on products** on your signup order form so new members can add them during signup.
- For the sports drink, enable direct stock so the fridge count stays accurate; for a mixed smoothie, define a **Recipe** so selling it draws down the ingredients.

### Gear rental at a climbing gym

You rent shoes and harnesses to drop-in climbers.

- Turn on rentals, then create products _Climbing shoes_ and _Harness_ at the rental price and set **Fulfilment** to **Rented out**.
- Lend them from the POS **Rentals** tab alongside a day pass in a single order. They stay on the rentals list until they are returned.
- Rental products cannot go in a package, so sell the day pass and the gear as separate lines rather than as a _Beginner day_ package. See [Equipment rentals](/sales/rentals).

### Trial bundle for a martial arts gym

- Create a _Uniform (gi)_ product and route it to an _Equipment_ revenue account.
- Build a package that pairs a starter membership plan with the gi at a welcome price; the package split records the membership and the gi separately.

## Tips & best practices

- **Use categories deliberately.** They drive the POS layout, so name and order them the way you want staff to find things.
- **Pick the right revenue account per product.** This is what keeps retail, rentals, and equipment revenue separated in your accounting and analytics. Leave it as default only when you genuinely want the fallback.
- **Set barcodes for anything sold by scan.** It speeds up the POS and reduces mis-rings.
- **Prefer Inactive over Delete.** Deactivating hides a product from the POS, packages, and order forms while preserving its sales history.
- **Only enable inventory when you'll maintain it.** Stock tracking is most useful for fast-moving retail and consumables; leave slow or one-off items untracked.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A product doesn't appear on the point of sale.

**Solution**: Confirm the product is **Active** and its **Visibility** isn't set to **Admin only** for a context where it shouldn't be hidden. Also check the club scope - a product tied to another club won't show for the currently selected club.

**Issue**: The product shows under the wrong group on the POS, or in an "uncategorized" group.

**Solution**: Set or change the product's **Category**. Products with no category fall into the ungrouped section. Reorder categories from the **Categories** manager to control group order.

**Issue**: I can't add a product to a package or an order form.

**Solution**: Only **Active** products can be bundled into packages or attached as add-on products. Reactivate the product first.

**Issue**: The Stock section says "This product is not tracked".

**Solution**: The product has neither direct stock nor a recipe. Receive stock to start direct tracking, or define a **Recipe** of ingredients if the product is assembled. If you don't see a Stock section at all, inventory management isn't enabled under **Settings** > **Sales** > **Inventory Management**.

**Issue**: Revenue for a product is landing in the wrong account.

**Solution**: Edit the product and set its **Revenue Account** explicitly instead of **None (uses default)**. Existing sales aren't re-booked; the change applies to future sales.

**Issue**: Some products weren't deleted in a bulk delete.

**Solution**: Products that stock or packages still use can't be deleted. Set them to inactive instead, or remove them from the packages first.
