A statement shows what a member, or a whole household, was charged and paid over a period, from what they owed going in to what they owe at the end. Front-desk staff download or email one from a member's page, owners print a whole month's statements at once for posting or handing out, and members download their own monthly statements from their account.

A statement is not an invoice. It is built from your ledger each time you open it, has no number, and is never stored, so a charge on it can still be invoiced. Use [Invoices](/sales/invoices) when someone needs a tax document.

## When to use it

- **A parent asks what they owe** - the front desk opens the family's statement for last month, which lists every child's charges and payments, and emails it to the parent.
- **Month-end letters** - a martial arts school prints every household's statement for October on the 1st of November and hands them out at class.
- **Members who don't use email** - an owner narrows the month's statements to **No email**, prints those, and posts them.
- **Self-service** - members download their own statement for any of the last twelve months from their account, without asking the front desk.

## What a statement shows

- **Previous amount due** - what was owed the day before the period started.
- Every charge on the day it fell due, every payment on the day it was made, and any refund, reversal, or correction, with a running total. A charge's line shows how much of it is tax and names its invoice, or its order. Products bought in one Point of Sale order appear as one line per day, like the till receipt.
- **Amount due** - what is owed at the end of the period. A statement ending today closes on exactly what the member's profile shows as owed.
- **Upcoming** - charges that exist but are not due yet, listed separately and not counted in the amount due.
- How old the amount due is, by the month each charge fell due: Current, 30, 60, and 90+ days.

Void charges are left out. Money spent from the member's wallet shows as a payment by Wallet. The statement is headed by the gym its charges belong to.

### Households

A household's statement covers every member's charges and payments. It is addressed to whoever pays for the household (its primary contact, or an owner), at the household's billing email, else theirs. Everyone else in the household gets their own statement when they ask for one, with only their own charges.

## A member's statement

To open one member's statement:

1. Open the member from **Members**, click the more-actions menu (**...**), and choose **Statement**. For a whole household, use **Statement** in the more-actions menu of the household's account page.
2. Pick the period. It opens on last month; **This month** and **Last month** are one click away, and you can pick any range up to a year that has already started.
3. Turn on **Whole household** to include every member of their household, when they belong to one.
4. Check the **Previous amount due**, **Amount due**, and any **Upcoming** charges.
5. Click **Download PDF**, or **Send by email**. Sending asks you to confirm the recipient - "Sends to {name} ({email})" - and goes out straight away with the PDF attached.

If the contact has no email address, you see "No email address on file, so the statement can only be downloaded."

## Printing a month's statements

**Sales** > **Finance** > **Statements** (`/billing/statements`) lists every statement one month produces, so you can print a month-end at once instead of opening each member.

1. Pick the **Month**. Only a month that has ended can be run, and the list offers the last twelve. It opens on last month.
2. Pick a gym, or all gyms, to see the statements each gym heads.
3. Check the strip at the top: how many **Statements**, how many **Has email**, and how many **No email**.
4. Every row starts ticked. Untick the exceptions. The **Owing** tab narrows the list to statements that close with something owed, and **No email** to members with no email address.
5. Click **Download {count} for printing**. You get one PDF with each statement starting on a page of its own.

Each row shows the **Name** it is addressed to (with the other household members underneath), the **Email**, and the **Amount due**. Click a row to open that statement, as on the member page.

### Who is in the month

- **One statement per household**, addressed to whoever pays for it and covering every member's charges.
- **One statement for each member in no household.** A member of a household has no statement of their own in the run, because the household's lists their charges.
- **A statement is included** when the month has a charge or payment on it, or it ends with something owed. A month paid in full is included; an account that is settled and had nothing that month is not.

Downloads are made in parts of at most 100 statements. When more are ticked, the button becomes one per part: **Download part 1 of 3 (100)**, and so on.

## What members see

Members find their statements on the **Billing** page of their account, in the member portal and on your website: this month so far and the eleven months before it, each with the **Amount due** at the end of the month and a button to download the PDF. A month appears once it has a charge or a payment. A member who pays for their household gets the household's statement; everyone else sees their own.

## Permissions

Viewing, downloading, and printing statements needs the **billing.read.club** permission. Emailing a statement needs **billing.create.club**. The gym filter on the Statements tab narrows the list; it doesn't restrict access.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                   | Why                                                                                             | What to do                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This month isn't offered on the Statements tab | Only a month that has ended can be run.                                                         | Wait for the month to end, or open one member's statement for **This month**. |
| A household member has no row of their own     | Their charges are on the household's statement.                                                 | Open the household's statement, or the member's own from their page.          |
| A member who paid everything is still listed   | The month had charges or payments on it.                                                        | Expected. Untick them if you don't want to print them.                        |
| **Send by email** isn't offered                | The contact has no email address, or you don't have permission to send.                         | Add an email address, or download the PDF instead.                            |
| The statement total differs from an invoice    | A statement covers what fell due and was paid in a period; an invoice covers the charges on it. | Use the statement for what is owed, and invoices as tax documents.            |
