The wallet is a member's balance of money held with your gym, in your gym's currency. Money reaches it from top-ups, redeemed vouchers, plan bonuses, and refunds, and members spend it at checkout alongside a card or cash. Members see and spend their balance in the portal and app, front-desk staff top it up and apply it at the point of sale, and owners use it to hold deposits and collect cancellation charges.

## When to use it

The wallet lets you give money back or add value without paying anything out: the money stays with your gym until the member spends it. Every movement is logged, and money spent from the wallet that came from a voucher or a refund is not counted as new revenue.

Typical uses:

- **Keeping a cancellation refund with the gym** - a climber cancels a booking that qualifies for a refund, the amount goes to their wallet, and it is used automatically the next time they book in the portal.
- **A "pay 100, get 120" plan** - a plan carries a wallet bonus that is added when the membership starts, and again at each renewal on a renewing plan.
- **A voucher promotion** - you share a voucher code, the member redeems it under **Account** > **Add voucher** in the portal, and the money is added immediately.
- **A deposit for area bookings** - a padel gym requires members to keep 50 in their wallet. A late cancellation is paid from it, so nobody has to chase the member.
- **Carrying over existing balances** - when you import contacts from a spreadsheet, a wallet column sets each member's opening balance.

## How it works

Every member (contact) has one wallet per organization, holding one balance in your organization's currency. A member who belongs to more than one organization has a separate wallet in each. The wallet appears the first time money is added to it; until then the balance is simply zero. There is nothing to turn on.

One unit in the wallet equals one unit of your currency. It is money, not loyalty points, and it can be spent on anything the member can pay for at checkout. It never goes below zero.

If you invoice through more than one company, the wallet belongs to your default company and can only be spent on its sales. See [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities#two-things-that-stay-whole).

### How money gets in

Each addition is a positive entry in the [wallet history](#wallet-balances-and-movements).

- **Top-ups** - the member pays money into their wallet, online or at the desk. See [Topping up a wallet](#topping-up-a-wallet). Members can top up their own wallet in the portal.
- **Vouchers** - a voucher [promotion](/sales/promotions) adds money when its code is redeemed, by the member under **Account** > **Add voucher** in the portal, or by staff from the member's page.
- **Plan bonuses** - a plan's **Wallet balance** is added when a membership on it starts, and again at each renewal of a renewing plan. It follows the start of the membership, not the payment: if the membership starts before its charge is paid and the charge later fails, the bonus is not taken back.
- **Refunds** - a refund you send to the wallet, or the part of a cancelled booking's price that was paid beyond what the cancellation costs. See [Refunds](/sales/payments#refunds).
- **Imported balances** - an opening balance from a contact import, always in your organization's currency.

### Spending it

The wallet pays alongside another payment method, or on its own when the balance covers everything.

- **Member portal and app** - the wallet balance is used automatically at checkout, up to the amount due, and the member pays only the rest by card or on arrival. The breakdown shows a **Wallet** line (for example `Wallet: -10.00`) above the total, and **Pay** shows what is left. When the wallet covers the whole order, the member pays nothing more.
- **Admin point of sale** - when you take a payment with a card, cash, or another method, tick **Also apply wallet balance ({balance} available)** and set the **Amount from wallet**. The split reads "Wallet {amount} + {remainder} via primary method". When the wallet covers the full amount, the option is hidden and the order can be paid from the wallet alone.

The amount taken from a wallet is always capped at the balance and at what is owed.

### Wallet money and revenue

Money paid into the wallet by top-up is real money; money added by a voucher, a goodwill correction, or a refund being spent again is not new money. When a sale is paid from a voucher or a refund in the wallet, that part is left out of revenue reporting, so a part-wallet, part-card payment counts the card part. Revenue figures then reflect money actually collected.

## Deposits and cancellation charges

A deposit rule asks members to keep a minimum amount in their wallet, so that what a cancelled or missed booking costs can be taken from it. Deposits start turned off; you choose when to introduce them under **Settings** > **Billing** > **General** > **Deposit rules**. See [Deposit rules](/settings/billing-general#deposit-rules) for the setup.

### What a cancelled or missed booking costs

The cancellation charge of a class, area, or instructor booking comes from the cancellation terms of its [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies), as they stood when the booking was made:

> cancellation charge = the booking's price x (100% - the refund % for when it was cancelled)

- **A missed booking (no-show)** costs the full price.
- **Payments count towards it.** Anything the member paid beyond the charge is refunded to their wallet. Anything still owed is taken from the wallet, deposit included, when the booking's gym has a deposit rule; whatever the wallet can't cover stays owed.
- **Fully covered bookings cost nothing.** A booking paid entirely by a membership or pass has no cancellation charge.
- It works the same whether the member paid upfront or planned to pay at the gym.
- Event tickets keep their own cancellation terms. A no-show on an [external program](/settings/external-programs) booking is billed to the member at the full price instead of the program's per-visit fee.

Members see the cancellation terms before they book, including "If you miss the booking, the full price is charged." Before cancelling they see exactly what it costs: the **Cancellation charge**, the **Refund**, what is **Taken from your wallet**, and anything **Still to pay**. A covered booking says "Your membership or pass fully covers this booking, so cancelling or missing it costs no money."

When **staff** cancel a booking that has been partly or fully paid, they choose what happens to the money: **Refund in full**, or **Apply the cancellation policy**.

### Seeing and waiving a cancellation charge

A cancelled or missed booking's panel shows a **Cancellation charge** (or **No-show charge**) box with the charge, what was **Taken from the wallet**, and what is **Still due**. To let the member off what is unpaid, enter a **Waiver reason** and click **Waive the unpaid charge**. What the wallet paid towards the charge goes back to it, and nothing more is owed; money the member paid upfront stays with the gym. The box then shows the waiver and its reason. Waiving needs the **billing.update.club** permission.

### Top-ups settle cancellation charges first

When a member who owes cancellation charges tops up, the top-up pays those charges first, oldest first, then refills the wallet. The top-up form shows the split, for example "Add 50.00 -> settle 10.00 in unpaid cancellation charges -> leave 40.00 in the wallet." Money above the deposit stays available for ordinary purchases.

## Wallet balances and movements

Go to **Sales** > **Finance** > **Wallet** to see every member who holds a wallet, with their **Last movement** and **Balance**. Search by name to find one member.

The **Wallet movements** tab beside it lists every entry across all wallets, newest first, with the **Date**, member **Name**, **Type**, **Description**, **Payment method**, the **Staff** member who recorded it, and the **Amount**. Tabs filter by type - **Correction**, **Voucher**, **Top up**, **Funding refund**, **Plan wallet grant**, **Refund**, **Reversal**, **Wallet addition**, and **Spend** - and only types that have entries appear. A cancellation charge paid from the wallet is a **Spend**.

Click a row on either tab to open that member's wallet, titled "Wallet - {name}". It shows the **Current balance** and the member's full history, and, depending on your permissions, tabs to top up, refund, or correct the balance. The same wallet opens from the member's profile, where a member who must hold a deposit shows **Deposit**.

### Topping up a wallet

On the **Top up** tab, enter the amount and pick a **Payment method**:

- **A manual method** (cash or a standalone card terminal) records money you already collected. It does not charge a card. Click **Record collected payment**. Recording money collected at the desk also needs the **billing.manage.organization** permission.
- **An online method** creates a checkout. Click **Create checkout**, then **Copy checkout** and send the link to the member. The wallet updates once the payment is confirmed. This does not use a card reader.

Topping up needs the **billing.create.club** permission.

### Refunding unspent money

On the **Refund unspent funding** tab (**Return deposit** when the member holds a deposit), you give back money the member paid into the wallet. What can be refunded includes anything paid in beyond the deposit, but not vouchers or goodwill additions. The money goes back through the original payment methods: Stripe refunds happen automatically, and for cash or other manual methods you confirm only after handing the money back. When part of it has to go back through a provider 1club can't refund for you, the form asks for a **Provider refund reference**: refund through that provider first, then record its reference. A **Reason** is required. If the refund leaves the member short of their deposit, the form warns you ("This refund leaves a deposit shortfall of {amount}.").

### Correcting a balance

On the **Adjust wallet balance** tab, enter an **Amount** (positive to add, negative to take away) and a **Reason**, then click **Apply correction**. It is saved to the history as a **Correction**, with your name.

Refunding and correcting both need the **billing.manage.organization** permission.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                              | Why                                                                                                                                                | What to do                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The wallet isn't offered at checkout                      | The sale is made by a company other than your default one, or the balance is zero.                                                                 | Take another payment method. The wallet belongs to your default company. |
| A member's top-up didn't all reach their wallet           | They owed cancellation charges, which a top-up settles first.                                                                                      | Expected. The wallet history shows the split.                            |
| A cancelled booking's charge wasn't taken from the wallet | The booking's gym had no deposit rule when the booking was made, the booking was fully covered, the charge was waived, or another company sold it. | Collect what is still due from [Charges](/sales/charges), or waive it.   |
| **Refund unspent funding** offers less than the balance   | Vouchers and goodwill additions can't be refunded as money.                                                                                        | Expected. Only money the member paid in can go back.                     |
