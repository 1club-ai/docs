title: "Calendar"
description: "The unified day/week calendar that drives bookings, classes, instructor time off, and check-in."
---

The calendar is the hub of the **Schedule** section. It pulls classes, bookings, and instructor time off for the visible date range and renders them as colored blocks across resource columns. The front desk runs the day from it, managers plan the week on it, and instructors see a streamlined view of their own teaching schedule.

## When to use it

Most reservations and check-ins start here. Click an empty slot and the right dialog opens pre-filled for that court, instructor, or class type, and right-click a block to check someone in, take a payment, or cancel without leaving the grid.

Typical uses:

- **Build tomorrow's class grid fast** - drop a class into each start time, then copy a popular class like your 18:00 vinyasa to the other days that run it.
- **Reschedule a padel court booking** - a member asks to move their Tuesday court an hour later, and you drag the block to the new slot.
- **Front-desk check-in during class** - as members arrive for the 09:00 class, check them in straight from the class block.
- **Declutter a multi-court venue** - a racket center with a dozen courts and several coaches shows only the courts the desk is working.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps behind each of these.

## Layout

Open **Schedule** in the left rail; the calendar is the page it lands on.

Pick a view in the calendar header: **Day**, **Timeline**, **3 days**, or **Week**. Your browser remembers the last view you used; the first time, the calendar opens in **Week**.

**Day** is a vertical time grid for one day with one column per resource: class-type columns first (A-Z), then a fallback "Classes" column, then instructor columns in the order set under **Staff** > **Instructors**, then area columns in the order set under **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Areas**. Instructor columns use the instructor's display name. The time axis is built from your operating hours, in 30-minute increments.

**Timeline** shows the same day and the same resources as **Day**, turned on its side: one row per resource, with time running across.

**3 days** and **Week** show days as columns, starting from the selected date for **3 days**. Resources are merged - you see all activities for each day in one column, not one column per resource - because a column per resource per day would be unreadably wide.

Use the arrows and **Today** to move between dates, or click the date to pick one from a calendar. The selected date is pushed to the URL as `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` so you can share a link to a specific day.

## Resources and what shows up where

The schedule reads three live data sources for the visible range:

- **Classes** - shown as blocks on their class-type column (or the fallback "Classes" column for classes without a type). Each class with assigned instructors also produces a mirror "teaching" block on each instructor's column in day view, so the instructor reads as busy.
- **Bookings** - shown on the area column if they have an area, on the instructor column if they have an instructor (both, if they have both), or on the relevant class-type column if they're class-bound. Class-bound bookings are suppressed in the **3 days** and **Week** views because the class itself already represents them.
- **Instructor time off** - shown as grey blocks on the instructor's column, alongside shift breaks and rostered days off (see [Roster and time off](/staff/roster)).
- **Area blocks** - shown as grey, dashed cards on the area's column (see [Area blocks](/schedule/area-blocks)).

Staff who can only see their own schedule still see when a court is taken by someone else's booking: the slot shows as **Occupied**, without the booking's details.

An hour that a [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) paid for carries a small season icon labelled with the season's name, so a contracted week reads as an hour of a prepaid contract rather than a casual booking. Makeup hours replayed on another date carry the same marker.

Live [events](/events/events) also render on the calendar as read-only cards: single-day events at a gym appear in their areas' lanes (or under their instructors when no areas are assigned), while multi-day and organization-wide events appear as chips in a banner above the grid. Event cards link to the event detail page and don't reserve the area they're shown in.

Active areas (`status=active`) and bookable instructors (`isBookable=true`) are the only ones included. Inactive or non-bookable resources don't get a column.

## Filtering what you see

The **Show on schedule** button in the corner of the grid lists every class type, instructor, and area, so you can toggle individual resources on or off. When some are hidden, its label tells you how many. Your choices are saved per view in browser storage.

Each column header also has an options menu: **Show only this instructor** (instructor columns), **Hide**, and **View details**, which opens the instructor's profile or the area's settings.

The organization-level defaults for what's hidden come from **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display** (the **Default visibility** card), which lets you hide entire class types, instructor types, or area types, plus uncategorized classes, instructors, and areas. Your own toggles layer on top of these defaults: if you flip a resource to a state that matches the default, the override drops so future settings changes still take effect.

### Staff filter

The **Staff** filter in the header narrows the calendar to the bookings and classes of the instructors you pick. Your own name is listed first, so a coach can show just their own schedule, or themselves plus the coach covering their evening class. Leave it empty to show all staff. The selection is kept in the URL (`?instructors=`), so you can share a filtered schedule as a link, and your browser remembers it for next time. The filter only appears when there are at least two instructors to choose from.

## Coloring

Two coloring modes can be toggled on the same **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display** page:

- **Staff coloring** - when on, a booking takes the color of the instructor assigned to it. When off, blocks use the resource's configured color (or a hash of the resource name as a fallback). The same section sets the color of **Any instructor** bookings - court bookings still waiting for a coach - so they stand out from ordinary court bookings. The card also says **Any instructor** in words, so it reads the same with staff coloring off.
- **Source coloring** - when on, a second border color is added that encodes the booking **source** (admin, portal, website, kiosk, etc.). Source colors are configurable on the same settings page.

## Creating activities

Click an empty time slot to open the create dialog. The dialog is pre-filled based on which column you clicked:

- Click a class-type column to open the class dialog with the class type and time range pre-set.
- Click an area column to open the booking dialog with the area and time range pre-set.
- Click an instructor column to open the booking dialog with the instructor and time range pre-set.

You can drag-select multiple consecutive slots to extend the duration before the dialog opens.

This applies in the **Day** and **Timeline** views. In the **3 days** and **Week** views, where columns are days, clicking a slot opens a small menu instead so you can pick **Add Booking** or **Add Class**. When the slot is tied to an instructor or an area, the menu also offers **Add Time off** or **Add Block**.

The same slot menu offers **Add Season Pass** when the date you clicked falls inside a season that is on sale at that gym, and you hold the permission to create memberships. It opens the season sale drawer with that hour pre-filled, so the whole run of weeks is sold from the slot the member asked for. See [Season passes](/schedule/season-passes).

The header has an **Add** button that opens a menu with **Booking**, **Class**, **Event**, **Checkin**, **Time off**, and **Block** (each shown only when you hold the permission for it). Next to it, the **More actions** menu links to the [Bookings](/schedule/bookings) list, [Classes](/schedule/classes) list, [Attendance](/schedule/attendance), [Areas](/settings/areas) (under **Settings** > **Clubs**, shown when you can view settings), and **Configure**, which opens the Schedule Display settings. **Event** creates a draft [event](/events/events); the events list itself lives in the **Events** section.

## Acting on existing blocks

Click a block to open it: bookings, classes, and events open their detail pages, while time-off entries, shift breaks, and area blocks open their edit dialogs.

Right-click a block (or use the mobile tap menu) for its actions. Each is shown only when you hold the permission for it:

- **Bookings** - **View**, **Edit**, **Check In**, **Add Payment** (only while there's a balance), **Message**, and **Cancel**.
- **Classes** - **View**, **Edit**, **Cancel** (or **Restore** for a cancelled class), **Add Booking**, **Check In**, **Message** (when the class has bookings), and **Delete**.
- **Time off and area blocks** - **Edit** and **Delete**.
- **Events** - **View**.

**Message** opens [Compose](/inbox/compose) addressed to the member or the class's booked participants.

**Add Payment** is smart about split balances: if the booking has one outstanding charge, you get a single-charge payment dialog with an editable amount. If it has multiple, the amount is locked to the full sum and the server settles them atomically. See [Bookings](/schedule/bookings) for the full story.

## Duplicating a class or booking

To copy a class or a booking to another time without reopening its dialog, hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) and drag the block to a new slot, then release.

While you drag with the key held, the calendar shows that you are copying rather than moving: the cursor becomes a copy cursor, the source block gets a green outline and a small copy badge, and the target slots highlight in green. Let go of the key mid-drag to fall back to a normal move.

- **A copied class** is a single one-off class at the drop time. It keeps the original's name, description, instructors, area, capacity, price, visibility, color, and class type, but never its recurrence, and it starts with no bookings or check-ins. A **Class duplicated** banner offers **Undo**, which removes it again.
- **A copied booking** is the same member on the same court or with the same coach, for the same duration, at the drop time. It's priced for the new slot, set to be paid at the desk rather than drawn from a membership, never repeats, and doesn't send a confirmation email. A **Booking duplicated** banner offers **Undo**, which cancels the copy.

The gesture works with a mouse and keyboard. Time-off blocks keep their normal drag-to-reschedule behavior. A dismissible hint in the calendar header ("Hint: Option + drag to duplicate") points it out until you close it.

## Live updates

While the calendar shows today or a future date, it checks for changes every 30 seconds and reloads when someone else adds or changes something, briefly highlighting the cards that changed. It pauses while you have a dialog, menu, or drag open. The refresh button in the bottom corner shows when the schedule was last updated and reloads it on demand.

## Bookings created with a member pre-selected

If you arrive at the schedule from a member profile with **Book a slot**, the URL carries `?contactId=X`. A toast prompts you to pick a slot, and every booking dialog you open from a slot click is pre-filled with that contact.

## Instructors on the calendar

Instructors use the same **Schedule** page. An instructor who can only see their own schedule sees their own classes and bookings, gets no actions the server would refuse, and adds their own time off from **Add** > **Time off**. An instructor who is also a manager or admin sees the full calendar and can narrow it to themselves with the [Staff filter](#staff-filter).

## Worked examples

### Build tomorrow's class grid fast

Open Day view on tomorrow, click the class-type column at each start time to drop in a class, then **Option/Alt-drag** a popular class (say your 18:00 vinyasa) to the other days that run it. Each drop is a fresh one-off class with the same name, instructor, area, and capacity but no bookings yet.

### Reschedule a padel court booking

A member asks to move their Tuesday court an hour later. Find the block on the court's column, drag it to the new slot, and confirm the old and new time in the prompt. The resource and duration stay the same; only the time shifts.

### Front-desk check-in during class

As members arrive for the 09:00 class, right-click the class block and choose **Check In**. No need to open the class detail page.

### Declutter a multi-court venue

A racket center with a dozen courts and several coaches can be noisy in Day view. Use **Show on schedule** to show only the courts you're working, saved per view so the desk keeps its layout.

## Tips & best practices

- **Day view for the desk, Week view for planning.** Day view gives you per-resource columns for live operations; Week view is better for seeing the whole week at a glance.
- **Option/Alt-drag to clone, plain drag to move.** Release the modifier mid-drag to fall back to a move; the copy cursor and green highlight tell you which mode you're in.
- **Share a day with a link.** The selected date is in the URL (`?date=YYYY-MM-DD`), so you can send a colleague a specific day.
- **Let settings own the defaults.** Set org-wide visibility under **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display**; use **Show on schedule** only for per-session tweaks, since matching the default drops your override.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A court or coach is missing from the calendar.

**Solution**: Only **active** areas and **bookable** instructors get a column. Check the area status and the instructor's **Bookable** toggle. Also confirm the resource's type isn't hidden under **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display**, or re-enable it under **Show on schedule**. If the **Staff** filter is set, only the selected instructors are shown.

**Issue**: A class booking doesn't appear in the Week or 3 days view.

**Solution**: This is by design. Class-bound bookings are suppressed in the **3 days** and **Week** views because the class block already represents them. Switch to **Day** or **Timeline** to see individual bookings.

**Issue**: Block colors aren't what I expect.

**Solution**: Coloring depends on the modes under **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display**. With **staff coloring** on, blocks use the assigned instructor's color, not the resource color, and **Any instructor** bookings use the color set for them. Turn it off to color by resource.

**Issue**: My duplicate-drag just moved the class instead of copying it.

**Solution**: Hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) for the whole drag. If you release the key before dropping, it falls back to a move.
