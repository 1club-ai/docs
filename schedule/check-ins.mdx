Attendance is the record of every member visit to your gym. Each check-in captures who came in, when, at which club, and how they entered - against a booking, a membership, an external program, or a direct payment. The front desk records visits as members arrive, and owners and managers review the trends in the [Attendance report](/analytics/schedule#check-ins).

## When to use it

The check-ins list keeps visits, membership usage, and payments in one place. Recording a check-in reuses the same pricing, payment, and membership-consumption logic as booking, so a walk-in is priced and debited exactly like a pre-booked session, and a limited plan's uses count down on their own. Every check-in shows exactly what it was recorded against.

Typical uses:

- **A member arrives for a booking** - a padel player who booked and paid online is checked in against their booking, with nothing to charge.
- **A walk-in on a pass** - a jiu-jitsu member with a 10-session pass turns up without a booking, and the visit takes one session off the pass.
- **A trial visitor with no profile** - a yoga studio in flexible check-in mode logs an anonymous visit and collects the drop-in fee.
- **Fixing how someone entered** - reception recorded a climber against their membership, but they paid at the door, so you change the entrance method and the session is restored.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps behind each of these.

<Note>
Before you start: how strict your check-in flow is depends on your booking policy and access-control settings. Read [Check-in models](/reference/check-in-models) for an overview, then configure the rules in [Booking policies](/settings/booking-policies) and [Access control](/settings/access-control).
</Note>

## The check-ins list

Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then choose **Check-ins** under **Lists** in the view picker (or press `V` on the calendar). The list opens inside the Schedule, and `/schedule/checkins` links straight to it. A check-in opens in a side panel over the page you're on. Check-ins are not under Members.

The list:

- Shows the check-ins of the period chosen in the toolbar. It starts on the calendar's period (this week when you came from **Week**, today from the other views); pick **All time** to see every check-in. The period is stored in the address, so you can bookmark or share a view.
- Is paged and searched on the server, so it stays fast however far back your history goes.
- Sorts by the day the attendance is for, newest first, so a back-dated check-in sits on its own day rather than at the top.
- Has **Scan QR** in the toolbar, shown when you can create check-ins. To record one by hand, use **Add** > **Checkin** (see below).

Each row is one check-in. The columns are:

- **Member** - Member name, avatar, and email. Rows without a member show **Anonymous**.
- **Date** - The day the check-in is for.
- **Time** - The check-in time.
- **Reference** - What the visit was recorded against: a class, a booking (class, area, or instructor), or a membership (with remaining usage). A row can show more than one, for example a booking plus the membership that covered it.
- **Entry method** - How the member entered: **Direct Payment**, **Membership**, or **External Program**. Shown as `-` when none was recorded.
- **Actions** - Open or [delete](#deleting-a-check-in) the check-in.

Click any row to open the check-in in [its panel](#the-check-in-panel). Click a member's name to jump to their profile.

Opening the list needs at least the `checkin.read.own` permission.

<Tip>
To see how busy you have been, use **Analytics** > **Check-ins** > **Attendance**. It charts check-in volume, peak times by hour, and no-show rate. See [Schedule analytics](/analytics/schedule#check-ins).
</Tip>

## Recording a check-in

Click **Add** > **Checkin** in the Schedule toolbar (on the [calendar](/schedule/calendar) or any of its lists) to open the check-in dialog. You can also use **Check In** on a booking or a class, or **Add** > **Check-in** on a [member profile](/members/member-profiles). What you see depends on the member's bookings and your booking policy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the member">
    Use the **Contact** picker to find the member. In a club that allows anonymous check-ins, this field is labelled **Contact (optional)** and you can leave it empty - see [Anonymous check-ins](#anonymous-check-ins).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a booking or an entrance method">
    If the member has a confirmed or pending booking for today, it appears as a selectable button - pick it to anchor the check-in to that booking. If there is no booking, choose an entrance method instead: **Use Membership**, **Pay Now** (direct payment), or **External Programs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect payment if required">
    When the entrance method owes money (a Pay Now walk-in, a membership with an unpaid balance, or an external program with a per-visit fee), a payment section appears. Pick how the member pays (cash, card, POS terminal, or wallet), or record the visit without a payment and settle later. If your booking policy enforces payment at check-in, the dialog says so - that rule stops members checking themselves in, not the front desk - and without a payment the amount stays owed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the check-in">
    Click the confirm button. 1club records the check-in, decrements membership usage if the plan is limited, and settles any payment you collected - all in one atomic step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Entrance methods

When a check-in is not simply anchored to an already-paid booking, you pick how the member enters. The available methods and whether one is mandatory come from the matching booking policy (see [Check-in models](/reference/check-in-models)):

- **Use Membership** - Counts the visit against one of the member's active memberships. Usage is decremented automatically if the plan has a limit. Only memberships eligible for this visit are selectable. Members can check in more than once per day - a morning and an evening session are both recorded; a quick re-scan at the door within a few minutes is treated as the same visit rather than a new one.
- **Pay Now** - The member pays for this single visit at the front desk. You can apply a discount code to a class-anchored Pay Now walk-in before collecting payment.
- **External Programs** - The member enters through a third-party program (a partner curriculum, a corporate-wellness benefit, a federation pass). If the program charges an additional per-visit fee, you collect it here. See [External programs](/settings/external-programs).

<Note>
**Pre-booked sessions honor the booking.** When a booking was created against a membership, that session was already debited from the membership at booking time. Checking the member in against that booking succeeds even if the membership has since become inactive or hit zero remaining uses - the booking is the receipt. This applies only to check-ins that select the booking. Walk-ins and entrance-method changes still require an active membership with remaining uses.
</Note>

### Check-in window

A booking can be checked in from 1 hour before it starts until the end of the booking date. Staff are held to the same window as members checking themselves in at the kiosk or in the check-in app: today, and at most an hour ahead.

Checking someone in outside the window - a booking days ahead, or a day that has already closed - needs the Check-ins **Create** permission at **Organization** scope, which only Admins hold by default. On a class page, **Check In** is disabled outside the window for everyone else, with a note saying why.

On a booking with players or a group, each person is checked in on their own place, from the **Players** section of the booking panel. See [Players and group sessions](/schedule/players-and-groups#check-in-one-person-at-a-time).

Checking in a booking that was already marked as a no-show is different: it can undo the no-show fee, so it needs **Full access** on Check-ins at **Organization** scope (Admins by default). See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions#check-ins).

After you record a check-in, a banner offers **Undo** for a few seconds. Undo removes the check-in as if it never happened. It isn't offered when the visit was paid by card, through the POS terminal, or from the wallet - refund the payment and delete the check-in instead.

## The check-in panel

Click a row in the check-ins list to open the check-in in a side panel, laid out like the [booking panel](/schedule/bookings#the-booking-panel). It shows the member (or **Anonymous**), the instructor, the **Entrance Method**, the membership's remaining usage, the class or booking it was for, the gym, and the time. It also lists the doors the visit opened (**Door access**), a walk-in's own charges, the member's review of the visit, and who recorded it and from where (**Recorded by**). A check-in for a booking opens that booking in the same panel.

From here you can:

- **Change Entrance Method** - Available on a booking-anchored check-in while the booking is still confirmed. Use it to correct how the member actually entered, for example switching a membership entry to a direct payment. Converting a paid entry to a different method triggers the appropriate refund or charge and warns you before you confirm. Once the booking's day has ended, only staff with the Check-ins **Edit** permission at **Organization** scope (Admins by default) can still change it.
- **Delete** - Remove the check-in (see below).

<Note>
There is no general "edit" for a recorded check-in beyond changing its entrance method. To correct anything else, delete the check-in and record a new one.
</Note>

## Deleting a check-in

Delete a check-in from the **Actions** column in the list, or from the **Delete** action on its panel. Deleting a check-in for today needs the `checkin.delete.standard` permission in its club; a check-in for any other day needs `checkin.delete.extended`. Confirm in the dialog to proceed.

Deleting reverses everything the check-in did, in one all-or-nothing transaction:

- Restores membership usage (credited back, capped at the plan's maximum) and reactivates a membership that had flipped to `used` or `expired`.
- Voids any pending or zero-amount charges attached to the check-in.
- Reverses any discount redemption recorded for the visit.
- Drops the external-program usage record so the program's counter stays accurate.
- Reverts a linked booking out of `checked_in` back to `confirmed`.

<Warning>
You cannot delete a check-in that has a paid charge attached. 1club blocks it with "Cannot delete a checkin with a paid charge. Refund the charge first." Refund the payment in [Payments](/sales/payments), then delete the check-in.
</Warning>

## Anonymous check-ins

When a club runs in flexible check-in mode, you can log a visit without selecting a member - useful for trial visitors and guests who do not yet have a profile. The **Contact** field becomes optional (**Contact (optional)**), and leaving it empty records the check-in as **Anonymous** in the list and on its panel.

Anonymous check-ins are controlled by the booking policy and the club's check-in mode in [Access control](/settings/access-control) - turn **Check-in Mode** to **Flexible** to allow them. See [Check-in models](/reference/check-in-models) for the full picture.

## QR code check-in

Click **Scan QR** in the toolbar of the **Check-ins** list to open the scanner. It isn't offered to an independent instructor's organization, which has no door. Scanning a member's check-in code validates it and checks the member in to the current club, so busy front desks can process arrivals without opening the full dialog.

## Worked examples

### Padel game booked and paid online

A member books and pays for a padel area in advance. When they arrive, open **Add** > **Checkin** in the Schedule, select the member, and pick their booking for today. Because payment was collected at booking, there is nothing to charge - confirm the check-in and the booking flips to attended.

### Brazilian jiu-jitsu drop-in on a class pass

A member with a 10-session BJJ pass walks in for the evening class without a booking. Select the member, choose **Use Membership**, pick the pass, and confirm. 1club decrements one session from the pass and records the visit against it.

### Trial visitor at a yoga studio

A prospective member turns up for a trial class. The studio runs flexible check-in mode, so leave the **Contact (optional)** field empty, pick the class, and choose **Pay Now** to collect the drop-in fee. The visit is logged as Anonymous.

### Corporate-wellness member at a fitness gym

A member enters through a corporate-wellness partner. Select the member, choose **External Programs**, and pick the program. If the program charges a per-visit top-up fee, collect it in the payment section; if it is fully covered, confirm without payment. See [External programs](/settings/external-programs).

### Correcting a mis-recorded entry

Reception checked a climber in against their membership, but the member actually paid at the door. Open the check-in from the **Check-ins** list, click **Change Entrance Method**, switch to **Pay Now**, and confirm. 1club restores the membership session and records the payment.

## Tips & best practices

- **Check members in promptly** - Record the visit as the member arrives so your attendance and busy-hour charts stay accurate.
- **Watch usage on limited plans** - The Reference column shows remaining allowance for membership check-ins; keep an eye on members approaching their limit.
- **Match the entrance method to reality** - Recording the true method (membership vs. pay now vs. external program) keeps revenue and usage reporting correct. If you get it wrong, use Change Entrance Method rather than deleting.
- **Refund before deleting** - If a check-in collected a payment, refund it first, then delete. The system will not let you delete a paid check-in.
- **Use QR scanning at peak times** - The Scan QR flow is faster than the full dialog when a queue forms.
- **Use the Attendance report for busy hours** - **Analytics** > **Check-ins** > **Attendance** shows peak times by hour, so you can staff the desk for the rush.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A member does not appear in the contact picker.

**Solution**: Confirm the member exists in your contacts. If you are checking in a guest without an account, the club must be in flexible mode to record an anonymous visit.

**Issue**: The system says the membership has no remaining uses.

**Solution**: The plan is limited and all sessions are consumed. If the member had already booked this session against the same membership, check them in through that booking - the session was debited at booking time and the check-in still succeeds. Otherwise select a different entrance method.

**Issue**: The check-in is blocked asking for payment.

**Solution**: The matching booking policy enforces payment at check-in (or at booking with an outstanding balance). Collect payment with a method in the payment section, confirm an active membership, or apply an external program. See [Booking policies](/settings/booking-policies).

**Issue**: Check-in is not open yet, or is closed for the day.

**Solution**: Check-in opens 1 hour before the booking starts and closes at the end of the booking date, for staff as well as members. Checking in outside that window needs the Check-ins **Create** permission at **Organization** scope (Admins by default). See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions#check-ins).

**Issue**: You cannot delete a check-in.

**Solution**: Either you lack the delete permission (`checkin.delete.standard` for today's check-ins, `checkin.delete.extended` for any other day), or the check-in has a paid charge. Refund the payment in [Payments](/sales/payments) first, then delete.

**Issue**: A new check-in is not in the list.

**Solution**: Check the period in the toolbar - the check-in must fall inside it (or pick **All time**). Rows are ordered by the date and time the check-in is for, newest first, so a back-dated check-in sits on its own day rather than at the top.
