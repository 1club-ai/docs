A booking can carry more than one person. On an area that takes several players, the member who books (the **organizer**) adds the people they are playing with. On a session with a coach and no area, the member books for a group, such as a couple sharing a lesson. Members add people while they book in the portal, on your website, in the app, and on 1club.me, and staff add or remove them from the admin.

## When to use it

Without players, a four-person game is one name on the schedule and three people the gym knows nothing about. With players, everyone on the booking gets the details and a calendar invite, sees the booking in their own account, pays for their own place or is paid for by the organizer, and is checked in on their own.

Typical uses:

- **A doubles game for four** - the organizer books a padel area and adds three friends. Each gets an email with the booking and the calendar invite, and the booking shows in their own bookings.
- **A lesson for two** - a couple books one coach for one session. The session is priced for two, and each pays for their own place unless one of them pays for both at checkout.
- **A partner pays with a program card** - one player comes in on a corporate-wellness [external program](/settings/external-programs), and the program pays their place only. The other places stay with the organizer.
- **Someone joins late** - staff add one more person to an upcoming coach session that still has room. Only the new person gets a charge.

## How it works

|                     | An area with several players                                                                                 | A coach session for a group                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| What sets the limit | The area type's **Players** setting (see [Area types](/settings/area-types#players)), counting the organizer | **People per session** on the coach's [instructor type](/settings/instructor-types), counting the organizer                          |
| Who is added        | **Players**                                                                                                  | **People** ("Who else is coming" when staff book)                                                                                    |
| Price               | The area's price, split into equal places                                                                    | The session price for each person, unless a **Number of people** [pricing rule](/settings/pricing-rules#conditions) prices the group |
| Someone added later | Plays as the organizer's guest                                                                               | Grows the group by one, up to the limit, and gets their own charge                                                                   |

The organizer is the contact the booking is for. Everyone else is a player, kept on the booking beside them.

- **Each person pays for their own place.** The organizer's place is the booking's own charge. Every other person's place is a charge in their own name, which they pay themselves or cover with their own pass or program. Nobody's place is re-priced when someone else joins or leaves. At checkout the member can also pay for everyone, which puts the whole price on the organizer.
- **A player can see the booking and leave it.** Cancelling, rescheduling, and changing who else plays stay with the organizer and with staff. A player does not see the organizer's contact details or how the organizer pays.
- **Only a single booking takes players.** A class has its own roster, and a repeating series has no roster of its own, so neither takes players.
- **Singles or doubles.** When an area type offers both, the booking records which one was booked, and that sets how many players it takes.

## Adding people as a member

While booking an area that takes several players, the member adds the people they are playing with:

- **In your portal and on your website** - search your gym's members by name, phone, or email, or add anyone by email. People the member has played with before are listed first.
- **On 1club.me** - add players by email.
- **Household members** - can be added in one tap at checkout.

For a coach whose instructor type takes more than one person, the member adds the people who are coming in the same way, and the session is booked for that group. The checkout shows the total and what it comes to per person.

Everyone added gets an email with the booking details and a calendar invite. Members can still add or remove players after booking.

## Adding people as staff

In the booking dialog, pick the **Contact** first. When the area or coach takes more than one person, a second picker appears:

- **Players** - for an area. When the area type offers singles and doubles, **Singles or doubles** comes first and sets how many places there are.
- **Who else is coming** - for a session with a coach and no area.

Under the picker, the dialog shows how many more people fit, and once someone is added, what each place costs ("Everyone pays for their own place: ..."). Players can only be added to a new single booking. A booking that repeats takes no players.

If the booking is saved but the players can't be added (for example the area filled up in the meantime), the booking stands and a warning says the players were not added.

### On the booking panel

Open the booking from the [schedule or the bookings list](/schedule/bookings#the-booking-panel). Its **Players** section (or **People**, for a coach session) lists the organizer first, then each player, then the places still open:

- **Add Player** or **Add person** puts someone on an upcoming booking. On a coach session, the person added gets their own charge for their place.
- The remove button takes someone off. Their unpaid charge is voided or their pass use is given back. Someone who has already paid for their place must be refunded first.
- **Singles or doubles** changes the format on an area that offers both. On a repeating booking it changes that booking only, not the rest of the series.
- **Edit split** is offered on a coach group's bill, and on an area bill whose players took their places at check-in, to settle the shares by hand when the price changed after it was billed.

## Check-in, one person at a time

Each person on the booking is checked in on their own place. On the booking panel, each row in **Players** has its own check-in button.

- **The organizer** pays their own place, or comes in on their pass or external program.
- **Each player** comes in on their own pass or external program, or pays their part.
- **Someone with no name** on the booking takes one of the open places as an anonymous check-in.
- The first check-in marks the booking as checked in, and the others can still come in after it.

Players can also check themselves in with their own code at the [kiosk](/settings/kiosk) and in the check-in app. See [Attendance](/schedule/attendance).

### An external program pays one place

An [external program](/settings/external-programs) pays for one person's visit. On an area with more than one place it pays **one place, never the whole booking**, whether the program was chosen at booking, by changing the entrance method, or at check-in. The places the organizer still holds stay theirs as ordinary charges, owed at the gym. They are never collected online with the booking, so a partner can still pay their own place on their own card or program at the door.

A session with a coach, and an area that seats one person, are one visit and are paid as a whole.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: There is no way to add players to an area booking.

**Solution**: The area type takes one person per booking. Set its **Players** to **Singles / doubles** or **A group** under **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Area Types**. Players also can't be added to a booking that repeats.

**Issue**: A member booking a coach can't add anyone.

**Solution**: The coach's instructor type has **People per session** set to **Just the booker**. Set it to **A group** with a **Max people** above 1 under **Settings** > **Services** > **Instructor Types**.

**Issue**: Two people paid the full session price for a lesson for two.

**Solution**: That is the default: each person pays the full session price for their own place. Add a pricing rule with the **Number of people** condition to price the group, for example 25% off for two. See [Pricing rules](/settings/pricing-rules#conditions).

**Issue**: I can't remove a player.

**Solution**: They have already paid for their place. Refund their payment first, then remove them.

**Issue**: The booking says the rest of the area is still owed after a member came in on a program.

**Solution**: That is expected. A program pays one place, and the other places stay with the organizer until they or the other players pay or take them.
