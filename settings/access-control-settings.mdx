Door access opens your gym's doors for the people who are expected: members with a booking, members who checked in, and anyone staff let in by hand. 1Club works out who may enter which area, and when, and keeps your door system in step. The doors themselves run on the door system you connect for each gym: [WebLock](/settings/integrations/weblock), [Impro](/settings/integrations/access-bridge) or [Salto KS](/settings/integrations/salto).

<Note>
  This page is about doors. [Access control](/settings/access-control) covers
  the front desk: check-in modes, walk-ins, and how members identify themselves
  when they check in. The two meet in one place: the credentials a member uses
  at the front desk (QR code, access card, PIN) are the ones sent to the door
  system.
</Note>

## When to use it

Use door access when a door should open because someone booked or arrived, without staff updating the door system by hand.

Typical uses:

- **A studio door that opens only for class** - a Pilates studio's door opens for each booked member shortly before class and closes after it, and a cancellation takes the access away.
- **A court gate for the booked slot** - a padel gym's court gate opens for the players who booked that hour, and for nobody else.
- **A walk-in on a membership** - a member without a booking checks in at the front desk or from the app, picks the area they're going to, and the door opens for the rest of the day.
- **A one-off visitor** - staff let a coach or a contractor into one area for an afternoon.

## How it works

- **Access belongs to an area.** Every door in your door system is linked to an [area](/operations/areas) in 1Club, on that system's integration page. Access is always to one area at one gym.
- **An access grant says who may enter, where and when.** Each grant names one person, one area and a time window.
- **Grants come from what happens in 1Club:**
  - a **booking** in that area,
  - a **check-in**,
  - or a grant **staff add by hand**.
- **A membership alone doesn't open a door.** A member with a membership gets access by booking or by checking in.
- **The access buffer widens the window.** Each gym sets how early people can get in before a booking and how long they keep access after it ends.
- **1Club keeps the door system in step.** A new or changed grant is sent straight away, and a full check runs every five minutes, so access that ends or is withdrawn is taken away again.

Each gym uses one door system at a time.

## Before you start

- The gym needs at least one active area. See [Areas](/operations/areas).
- Activate a door system for the gym and link its doors to your areas:
  - [WebLock](/settings/integrations/weblock)
  - [Impro](/settings/integrations/access-bridge)
  - [Salto KS](/settings/integrations/salto)
- No grants are created at a gym until its door system is on.
- Viewing grants requires the **operations.read.club** permission. Adding, revoking and restoring them requires **operations.manage.club**.

## Choose how members identify themselves

Go to **Settings > Access Control > Settings**. Each gym has its own section.

1. Turn on **Access Control** for the gym. This lets members register access cards and PINs.
2. Under **Enabled Identification Methods**, choose what the door system should accept:
   - **QR Code** - the QR in the member's app. Every member already has one, so there is nothing to issue.
   - **Access Card** - an RFID or NFC card or fob. Enroll cards on [Access cards](/members/member-cards).
   - **PIN Code** - a number the member types at a keypad.
3. Click **Save**.

Only the methods you enable are sent to the door system. When PIN is on and a member has no credential yet, 1Club creates a random 6-digit PIN for them the first time it sends them to the door system.

<Note>
  Salto KS doors open from the member's phone with a digital key instead. See
  [Salto KS](/settings/integrations/salto).
</Note>

## Access grants

Go to **Settings > Access Control > Access grants** to see every grant. The list shows:

| Column                             | What it shows                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ID**                             | The grant's number.                                                                                                                               |
| **Sync**                           | Whether the door system has the grant: synced, pending or failed. A dash means nothing needs sending, for example a grant that has already ended. |
| **Area** and **Club**              | Where the grant lets the person in.                                                                                                               |
| **Contact**                        | Who it is for.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Source**                         | **Booking**, **Check-in** or **Manual**. **Legacy** marks grants from the earlier setup.                                                          |
| **Valid from** and **Valid until** | When the door opens for them, including the access buffer.                                                                                        |
| **Status**                         | **Active**, **Expired** once the window has passed, or **Revoked**.                                                                               |

Times are shown in your device's timezone.

### From a booking

A booking in an area creates a grant for that area. It opens the access buffer before the booking starts and closes the access buffer after it ends. For example, a class ending at 14:30 with a 15-minute buffer shows **Valid until** 14:45.

If the booking moves, the grant moves with it. If the booking or its class is cancelled, the grant is revoked.

### From a check-in

A check-in for a booking or a class uses that booking's area and window. Only the buffer after the end applies, because the member is already inside.

A check-in on a membership, without a booking, asks which area the member is going to:

- With one active area, it's chosen automatically.
- With several, staff or the member choose one, or **No door access**.
- With none, the member checks in without door access.

That access lasts from check-in until midnight, gym time. This works the same at the front desk, on the kiosk, on self-service check-in, and when members check themselves in from the app or the member portal.

### Add a grant by hand

1. Click **Add access grant**.
2. Choose the **Contact**, the **Club** and the **Area**.
3. Set **Valid from** and **Valid until**.
4. Click **Add grant**.

A manual grant uses exactly the times you enter, with no access buffer.

### Revoke and restore

- Click **Revoke** on an active grant to take the access away. It stays revoked even if the booking or check-in behind it changes later.
- Click **Restore** on a grant you revoked to give the access back. You can't restore a grant whose window has already passed.

Deleting a contact or an area deletes its grants, and the door system is cleaned up after it.

<Note>
  If the door system still lets someone in after their access was withdrawn, the
  grants list warns you. It means the person is also in a group set up directly
  in the door system. Remove them from that group there.
</Note>

## Worked examples

### Pilates studio, door open only for class

- Create a **Studio** area for the gym and link the studio door to it on your door system's integration page.
- Set the access buffer to 15 minutes.
- Turn on **Access Control** for the gym and enable **QR Code**.
- A member books the 18:00 class. Their QR opens the studio door from 17:45 until 15 minutes after class. If they cancel, the access is withdrawn.

### Padel gym, court gates for the booked hour

- Create one area per court and link each court gate to its area.
- A booking for Court 2 at 19:00 opens Court 2's gate for the players in that booking, from the buffer before 19:00 until the buffer after the hour ends. The other courts stay closed to them.

### 24/7 gym, members walk in

- Create a **Gym floor** area and link the main door to it.
- Enable **Access Card** and **PIN Code**, and enroll members' fobs on [Access cards](/members/member-cards).
- A member checks in from the app when they arrive. With one area, it's chosen for them, and the main door opens for them until midnight.

## Tips & best practices

- **Link every door to an area first.** A door that isn't linked to an area never opens through 1Club.
- **Keep area names unique within a gym.** Some door systems use the area name to find the matching zone.
- **Use the buffer, not longer bookings.** The access buffer covers arriving early and leaving late, without changing the booking itself.
- **Revoke, don't delete.** Revoking keeps the record and still takes the access away. Use **Restore** if you change your mind before the window ends.
- **Watch the Sync column.** A grant stuck on pending or failed hasn't reached the door system yet. The check every five minutes retries it.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: A member with an active membership can't open the door.
**Cause / fix**: A membership alone doesn't open a door. The member needs a booking in that area or a check-in for it. Check the **Access grants** list for an active grant in the right area.

**Issue**: A booking was made but no grant appeared.
**Cause / fix**: The booking's gym needs an active door system, and the booking needs an area. Check that the door system is on for the gym, and that the area is linked on its integration page.

**Issue**: A grant is active but the door doesn't open.
**Cause / fix**: Check the **Sync** column. If it's pending or failed, the door system hasn't received it yet. Also check that the member's credential type is enabled under **Enabled Identification Methods**, and that the door is linked to the grant's area.

**Issue**: A walk-in checked in but got no door access.
**Cause / fix**: When the gym has several areas, someone has to choose one at check-in. **No door access** checks the member in without opening any door.

**Issue**: The member's card isn't recognised.
**Cause / fix**: Enroll the card on [Access cards](/members/member-cards) and make sure **Access Card** is enabled for the gym. For Impro, the card technology also has to match your readers. See [Impro](/settings/integrations/access-bridge).
