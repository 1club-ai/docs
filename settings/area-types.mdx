An area type is a category for your spaces, such as _Padel_, _Tennis_, or _Reformer studio_. Every [area](/settings/areas) can belong to one. The type sets what is played there and how bookings of those spaces work, so you configure it once instead of on every area. Owners and admins manage area types.

## When to use it

- **A multi-sport racket center** creates _Tennis_, _Padel_, and _Squash_ types, each with its own sport, so members find the right area and each area offers the right surfaces.
- **A padel gym** sets its _Padel_ type to **Singles / doubles** with only doubles ticked, so every booking is a four-player game and the organizer adds three players.
- **A small-group studio** sets **Bookings and classes at once** to 3 on its _Small group room_ type, so three bookings can share the same time slot.
- **A gym with a pool and a sauna** sets their types to **Walk-in, no booking**, so members on a plan that covers them come in without booking.
- **A gym with a members-only recovery room** sets **Who can book** on its _Recovery room_ type to a chosen audience, so only those people can book it online while staff can still book anyone in.

## Where to find it

Go to **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Area Types**. The list shows each type's **Name**, **Slug**, **How members use it**, whether it is **Bookable**, its **Price/Hour**, and its **Sport**. Types can be dragged into order.

- Click **Add** > **Add Area Type** to create one.
- Click a row, or use its **Edit** action, to change it.
- Use **Delete** to remove it. Deleting cannot be undone.

## Area type fields

### Basic Information

- **Name** - What staff and members see. Required.
- **Slug** - Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. It must be unique within your organization. Required.
- **Description** - Optional.
- **Sports** - The sports played in this type of space. The type's sports also decide which surfaces and features the area form offers. See [Areas](/settings/areas).

### Booking & Pricing

- **Available for booking** - Areas of this type can be booked by members.
- **How members use it** - **Booked by the hour** (the default) or **Walk-in, no booking**. A new area of this type starts with this setting, and changing it here applies to every area of the type. See [Booked or walk-in](/settings/areas#booked-or-walk-in).
- **Default Price per Hour** - The default hourly rate for areas of this type.
- **Revenue Account** - The revenue account bookings of these areas book their income to. **None (use default)** uses your organization's default revenue account. When an area is booked with a coach, the coach half posts to the [instructor type's](/settings/instructor-types) account instead.
- **Bookings and classes at once** - How many bookings and classes can share the same time slot on areas of this type, for example 3 for a small-group room. A new type starts at 1, which makes each booking exclusive. Leave it empty for **Unlimited**.
- **Who can book** - Leave it empty and anyone can book areas of this type. Choose an audience and only those people can book them; staff can still book anyone in.

### Players

How many people play on one booking, the person booking included:

- **Just the booker** - One person per booking. Members add no players.
- **Singles / doubles** - Tick **Singles**, **Doubles**, or both. At least one stays ticked. With both ticked, the member (or staff) picks **Singles or doubles** when booking, which sets how many players the booking takes.
- **A group** - Up to a **Max Players** number you set (at least 2).

Above one person, the organizer adds the people they're playing with, each pays for their own place, and each is checked in on their own. See [Players and group sessions](/schedule/players-and-groups).

## How area types show to members

On the booking flow, members see a booking card for each named area type. Spaces with no area type share a card named after your gym's **Name for areas** booking label. See [Organization](/settings/organization#club-fields-worth-knowing).

## Permissions

Area types live in Settings, so you need Settings access to open the page. Creating, editing, and deleting them is also checked against the Operations module at club scope. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

## Tips

- **Create area types before areas.** The type scopes the area's surfaces and features and seeds its player setup.
- **Use one type per kind of space, not per gym.** Area types are shared across all your gyms, so _Padel_ covers the padel areas at every location.
