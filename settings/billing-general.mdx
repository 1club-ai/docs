**Settings** > **Billing** > **General** is where owners and admins set up the money side of 1club once: the currency you charge in, how tax is shown and calculated, the day of the month memberships renew on, which revenue accounts your sales post to, how members can pay, and whether members must keep a deposit in their wallet. Most of it is set when you start and rarely touched again.

## When you need it

- **Getting started** - set the currency and your tax rates before you create plans and products, so every price is read correctly from day one.
- **Prices shown with VAT** - a gym in the EU shows members VAT-inclusive prices and marks its 20% rate as **Tax Included in Price**, so a 120 price is invoiced as 100 plus 20 tax.
- **Separate revenue lines for your bookkeeper** - a padel gym keeps area rentals, coaching, and bar sales on different revenue accounts, each with its own code and default tax rate.
- **Everyone billed on the 1st** - a CrossFit box sets its billing day to the 1st, so every membership renews the same day and new members pay only for the days up to it.
- **A wallet deposit for area bookings** - a gym requires members to keep at least 50 in their wallet before a new area booking is confirmed.

The page is one long form, divided into sections:

| **External Programs** | Third-party passes used as an entrance method. See [External programs](/settings/external-programs). |

The **Billing entities** and **Deposit rules** sections are shown only to staff with organization settings access.

## General

The first block on the page has no heading.

- **Currency** - the currency prices are shown and taken in. Changing it converts nothing: plan and product prices keep their numbers and are read as the new currency, while invoices and payments already recorded keep the currency they were issued in.
- **Show prices with tax included** - prices shown to members include tax. On by default.
- **Calculate instructor compensation on pre-tax revenue** - instructor pay is calculated after tax is removed. Off by default. See [Pay rates](/staff/pay-rates).

When you change a field here or under **Membership billing**, a bar appears at the bottom of the page. Save from there.

Your legal name, tax number, bank details, and invoice numbering are not here. They belong to the company you invoice through, in the **Billing entities** section. See [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities).

## Membership billing

- **Billing day** - the day of the month renewing memberships are charged on. **The day they joined** (the default) renews each membership on the anniversary of its start. Pick a day from **1st of the month** to **28th of the month** to renew every membership sold from now on that day instead. Existing memberships keep their dates.
- **First period** - shown once you pick a day. What a member who joins between two billing days pays for the days up to it: **Charged by the day** (its share of a full period, the default), **Full price**, or **Free until the billing day**. The info icon shows a worked example, such as "Joining on 15 Oct at 100 a month: 54.84 for 15 Oct - 31 Oct, then 100 on 1 Nov".

Only memberships that renew automatically and are counted in months follow the billing day; weekly plans, one-time plans, and season passes keep their own dates. See [Membership billing](/sales/membership-billing#billing-day) for how the first period, its sessions, and the renewal charges work.

## Tax rates

Click **Add Tax Rate** to create one, or the pencil icon on a row to edit it.

- **Name** - for example _VAT_.
- **Rate (%)** - the percentage, from 0 to 100.
- **Description** - optional.
- **Active** - turn off to retire a rate without deleting it.
- **Default** - marks the rate as your default.
- **Tax Included in Price** - on, the tax is taken out of the price (120 at 20% is 100 plus 20 tax). Off, it is added on top (100 plus 20% is 120 in total).

The list shows each rate with an **Incl.** or **Excl.** chip, so you can see at a glance which way it works.

## Tax report

The Tax report adds up a period's sales the way a sales tax or VAT return asks for them: sales excluding tax, the non-taxable part, the taxable part, the tax charged, and the total, grouped by revenue account, tax rate, or gym, per day or per month. It lives under **Analytics** > **Finance** > **Tax**, not on this page, and reads the tax rates and revenue accounts you set here. It can be narrowed to one billing entity or revenue account and exported as Excel or CSV, with or without every sale behind it. See [Finance analytics](/analytics/finance#tax).

## Revenue accounts

Revenue accounts are the categories your income posts to, the lines your bookkeeper reconciles against. Click **Add Revenue Account** to create one.

- **Account Code** - your accounting code, for example `4000`.
- **Account Name** - for example _Area rentals_.
- **Description** - optional.
- **Default Revenue Account** - marks the account as your default. A sale that names no account posts here, and reports count any charge stored with no account under it.
- **Default Tax Rate** - the tax rate applied to what posts to this account, or **No Tax Rate**.
- **Billing entity** - shown only when you trade through more than one company. Sells everything posted to this account through one company, whichever gym it was sold at. Leave it on **Whichever the club bills through** to let the gym decide. See [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities#by-revenue-account).

## Deposit rules

A deposit rule sets a minimum amount members must hold in their wallet. It is a shared wallet minimum, not a separate charge for each booking, and bookings fully covered by a membership or pass are exempt. Changes apply immediately.

Click **Add Deposit Rule** and fill in:

- **Name** - for example _Area deposit_.
- **Policy** - what happens when a member's wallet is below the minimum:
  - **Warn about shortfall** - shows a warning, but doesn't add the deposit to the booking checkout or stop the booking. This is the default.
  - **Follow booking payment policy** - adds the missing deposit to the booking payment. It is required before the booking is confirmed only when the [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) requires payment upfront; otherwise the member can pay on arrival.
  - **Require deposit before booking** - the missing deposit must be paid before a new booking is confirmed, even when the booking itself can be paid on arrival. This needs online card payments.
- **Minimum amount** - the wallet balance members must hold.
- **Clubs** - shown when you have more than one gym. Leave it empty to cover all gyms. A rule for specific gyms takes precedence over the all-gyms rule.

Each gym can belong to only one rule, and there can be only one all-gyms rule. With a single gym, you can have one deposit rule, and **Add Deposit Rule** is disabled once it exists. The list shows each rule's **Name**, **Policy**, **Minimum amount**, and, with more than one gym, its **Club**.

Deposits start turned off: nothing is asked of members until you add a rule. Once a gym has a rule, what a cancelled or missed booking there costs is taken from the member's wallet, deposit included. See [Deposits and cancellation charges](/sales/wallet#deposits-and-cancellation-charges).

Collecting a deposit with a booking (**Follow booking payment policy** or **Require deposit before booking**) needs every gym the rule covers to sell through your default company, because the wallet belongs to the default company. Otherwise those options are disabled, with "Collecting a deposit with a booking requires all covered clubs to sell through the default billing entity, which holds the wallet." **Require deposit before booking** is also disabled without online card payments. See [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities#two-things-that-stay-whole).

Staff see the deposit on the member's profile, where **Wallet** reads **Deposit** when a deposit is required. See [Member profiles](/members/member-profiles).

To give a deposit back, staff open the member's wallet and use **Return deposit** (shown as **Refund unspent funding** when no deposit is required). The money goes back through the original payment methods, and 1club warns you when the return leaves the member short of the minimum. See [Wallet](/sales/wallet).

## Permissions

The page opens for staff with the **billing.read.club** permission.

| Section | Who can change it |
| --- | --- |
| General and **Membership billing** | **settings.manage.organization** |
| **Billing entities** | Seen with **settings.read.organization**; changed with **settings.manage.organization** |
| **Tax Rates**, **Revenue Accounts**, **Payment Methods** | **billing.manage.organization** |
| **Deposit rules** | Seen with **settings.read.organization**; added, edited, and deleted with **settings.create**, **settings.update**, and **settings.delete.organization** |
| **External Programs** | **settings.manage.organization** |

See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
