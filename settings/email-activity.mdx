**Email activity** is the log of every email 1Club sent for your organization: booking confirmations, receipts, invitations, messages your team sends, and the notices your team receives. Each email shows who it went to, what kind it was, whether it was delivered, and whether the recipient opened or clicked it. Owners and admins use it to answer "did that member get the email?".

## When to use it

- **A member says they never got their booking confirmation.** Find the email, see that it **Bounced**, and read the reason in its details.
- **Checking whether members read a message you sent.** Filter on **Marketing** emails and **Opened** to see who engaged.
- **A coach is not getting staff notices.** Switch to the **Team** tab and check the coach's address for **Dropped** or **Spam** status.

## Where to find it

Go to **Settings** > **Data Tools** > **Email activity**. The page opens on today's emails sent to members, newest first.

Each row shows the **Date**, **Recipient**, **Subject**, **Type**, **Audience**, **Template**, **Status**, and **Engagement**.

## Filtering the list

- **Audience tabs** - **All**, **Member** (the default), or **Team** for emails to your staff.
- **Time range** - Defaults to today. Pick another range to look further back.
- **Filters** - Narrow by one value of each:
  - **Email type** - **System**, **Transactional**, **Marketing**, or **Internal**.
  - **Delivery status** - **Queued**, **Sent**, **Processed**, **Delivered**, **Deferred**, **Bounced**, **Dropped**, **Failed**, **Spam**, **Unsubscribed**, or **Skipped**.
  - **Engagement** - **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Unsubscribed**, or **Spam reported**.

## Email details

Click a row to open **Email details**: the subject, recipient, **From** address, type, audience, template, delivery status, engagement, any **Error**, and the **Created**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Opened**, and **Last event** times.

When an email was not delivered, the details also check the recipient's address and explain what is blocking it and what to do next. For example:

- The recipient marked email from you as spam, or unsubscribed from all email. Only the recipient can undo this, so ask them to add your address to their contacts and send again.
- The email provider is discarding messages to this address. Contact support to have it cleared, then send again.
- The address is excluded from marketing emails since a given date. Invitations and receipts are still sent.

A member's activity feed links straight to the details of an email sent to them.

## Permissions

Email activity needs Settings access. Without it, the page says you don't have permission to view email activity. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
