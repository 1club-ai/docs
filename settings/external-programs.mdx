An external program is a third party that pays for a member's visit outside 1club - a corporate-wellness benefit, a municipal scheme, a federation pass, or an aggregator such as MultiSport. A member uses it as their entrance method instead of one of your own memberships or a direct payment. You define programs once, and the front desk picks them at check-in and when booking. Each visit is logged against the program, which is the record you bill the program from.

## When to use it

Use external programs when something other than your own memberships gets a member through the door. Each visit is logged against the program, so you can report on usage and reconcile with the partner.

Typical uses:

- **An aggregator pass at a padel gym** - a multi-gym pass sends players to your padel areas and pays for the whole visit at an agreed rate. The front desk picks the pass at check-in, which records the visit.
- **A benefit that pays part of the price** - a municipal sports card pays 5 off every visit, and the member pays the rest at the desk.
- **Corporate wellness at a yoga studio** - a local employer's benefit covers its staff's yoga classes, and you track which visits arrived through it while the employer pays you separately.
- **Reciprocal access at a climbing gym** - members of a partner federation are waved in through **Direct check-in** with no fee.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to set these up.

## How it works

A program pays for a visit in one of two ways, set by **Program pays**:

| Program pays                 | What the member pays                                                                               | Use it when                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The whole visit**          | Only the program's **Additional Price Per Visit**, if you set one, whatever the visit is listed at | The program reimburses you per visit at a rate you agreed with it    |
| **A fixed amount per visit** | The visit's price, less the **Amount Covered Per Visit** (never below zero)                        | The program contributes a set amount and the member tops up the rest |

The fixed amount comes off the same price a direct payment would charge, tax included. What a program covered is recorded with each visit, so changing the amount later doesn't rewrite past visits.

A program also has a scope, set in its **Validity** section:

- **Restricted programs** (you pick Classes, Areas, and/or Instructors) become a selectable **entrance method** when a member books or checks in for a matching class, area, or instructor - alongside **Membership** and **Direct payment**.
- **General programs** (no scope) can't grant access to a specific booking. Instead they appear in the front-desk **Direct check-in** flow (when they carry no per-visit fee), and they factor into **dynamic booking pricing**. This lets a partner or corporate program adjust prices without being tied to one class or area.

### One person, one place

A program pays for **one person's** visit. On an area with more than one place - a padel area for four - it pays **one place, never the whole booking**, whether the program was picked at booking, by changing the entrance method, or at check-in. The booking names the program when the organizer's own place is on it. The other places stay the organizer's as ordinary charges, owed at the gym: they are never collected online with the booking, so the other players can still pay their own place on their own card or program at the door.

A session with a coach, and an area that seats one person, are one visit and are paid as a whole. See [Players and group sessions](/schedule/players-and-groups#an-external-program-pays-one-place).

A repeating booking can't be on a program, because a program pays visits one booking at a time.

## Managing external programs

You define programs in the **External Programs** section of **Settings** > **Billing** > **General**.

### Viewing all programs

1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **General** and scroll to **External Programs**.
2. The grid shows **Name**, **Code**, **Club** (or "All clubs"), **Program pays** (**The whole visit**, or the amount covered), **Additional Price Per Visit**, **Member cards**, **Visibility**, and **Status** (active or inactive).
3. Click a row to edit, or click **Add External Program** to create a new one.

When one of your own programs looks like a program 1club knows (for example its name reads like MultiSport), a note above the grid offers **Review**, so you can link it and let it read that program's member cards.

### Creating an external program

1. Click **Add External Program**.
2. Fill in the **General** section (see the fields below).
3. In **Pricing**, choose what the program pays.
4. In **Validity**, keep **General program** or choose **Restricted program** to scope it to specific class, area, or instructor types.
5. Optionally set **Operating Hours** to restrict when the program is usable.
6. Click **Save**.

### Fields

**General**

| Field            | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Program**      | Pick a program 1club knows, such as MultiSport, or **Custom program** for your own. A known program is offered only in the countries its cards work in, and only once per organization: one you already have reads "already added". |
| **Name**         | The display name staff and members see (for example, "Corporate wellness"). For a known program it's your own label, so "MultiSport" and "Multi" are the same program.                                                              |
| **Code**         | A short identifier used in reports and partner reconciliation. Lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores only. The system auto-fills it from the name as you type. Codes must be unique across your organization.        |
| **Club**         | Shown when you have more than one club. Leave empty to apply organization-wide, or pick one club to scope the program to a single location.                                                                                         |
| **Visibility**   | **Public** (the default), **Admin & Member**, or **Admin only**. Controls where the program is offered (see below).                                                                                                                 |
| **Member cards** | Which card format the program's members carry, or **No cards**. With a format set, staff can put a member's program card on their profile, and scanning it at the desk names the member and the program.                            |
| **Active**       | Toggle off to disable a program without deleting it.                                                                                                                                                                                |

**Pricing**

| Field                          | What it does                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Program pays**               | **The whole visit** or **A fixed amount per visit**.                                             |
| **Amount Covered Per Visit**   | With a fixed amount: what the program takes off the visit's price. The member pays the rest.     |
| **Additional Price Per Visit** | With the whole visit: added to what the member pays for every visit. Leave at 0 for free access. |

### Visibility

- **Public** - offered everywhere, including member-facing surfaces.
- **Admin & Member** - offered to staff and to members.
- **Admin only** - offered only on staff surfaces; for programs your front desk admits people with but members can't choose themselves.

Visibility decides **who may choose** the program, not whether it works. Staff surfaces - the admin, the instructor app, and the point of sale - offer programs of every visibility, including **Admin only**. Member-facing surfaces - the member portal, the 1club network, and the unattended kiosk - offer only **Public** and **Admin & Member** programs.

### Validity

The **Validity** section decides whether the program is a **General program** or a **Restricted program**. For a restricted program, select any combination of:

- **Classes** - the program covers the class types you list (or all class types if you list none).
- **Areas** - the program covers the area types you list.
- **Instructors** - the program covers the instructor types you list.

Choosing **General program** leaves the program unscoped (see [How it works](#how-it-works)). A general program can't grant access to a booked resource, but it drives dynamic pricing and the Direct check-in flow.

### Operating hours

Set day-of-week hours during which the program can be used. Outside those hours, a restricted program won't appear in the entrance-method picker. The editor starts from a default schedule that mirrors your club's operating hours - override individual days only when the program's availability differs.

## Using an external program at check-in

There are two paths, matching the two program shapes:

**As an entrance method for a booked resource**

1. When the front desk books or checks in a member for a class, area, or instructor, they choose the entrance method.
2. **External program** appears as an option when at least one restricted program is active, in scope for that resource, and within its operating hours. Staff see programs of every visibility; members choosing for themselves see only **Public** and **Admin & Member** programs.
3. The staff member picks the program.
4. Whatever the program leaves to the member - its **Additional Price Per Visit**, or the rest of the price after a fixed amount - is charged through the same payment pipeline as a direct-payment check-in, and the visit is recorded against the program.

On a booking with several people, each person is checked in on their own place and can come in on their own program. See [Players and group sessions](/schedule/players-and-groups#check-in-one-person-at-a-time).

**As a Direct check-in (no booking)**

At small gyms where staff just wave a member in without a class or area, the **Direct check-in** flow lists the member's eligible memberships and any **general** external programs that carry no per-visit fee, whatever their visibility. Pick the program to log the visit against it.

Either way, the visit is logged against the program so you can report on how many times it was used.

## Worked examples

### Aggregator pass at a padel gym

A multi-gym pass provider sends players to your padel areas and reimburses you per visit.

- **Program**: the known program if 1club offers it in your country, otherwise **Custom program**. **Name**: Multi-gym pass. **Code**: `multigym-pass`.
- **Program pays**: **The whole visit**. **Additional Price Per Visit**: whatever the member pays you on top, often 0.
- **Validity**: **Restricted program**, with Areas narrowed to your padel area type.
- **Visibility**: **Admin & Member**.

When a pass holder books a padel area and checks in, the front desk selects **External program** > **Multi-gym pass**. On a booking for four, the pass pays the holder's place, and the other three places stay with the organizer.

### A sports card that pays part of the visit

A municipal card pays 5 toward every class.

- **Program pays**: **A fixed amount per visit**. **Amount Covered Per Visit**: 5.
- **Validity**: **Restricted program**, with Classes left empty to cover every class type.

A member on a 12 drop-in class pays 7 at the desk, and the program is billed 5 for the visit.

### Corporate wellness for a yoga studio

A nearby employer covers unlimited yoga for its staff.

- **Name**: Acme wellness. **Code**: `acme-wellness`. **Visibility**: **Admin only**, so only the front desk can choose it.
- **Program pays**: **The whole visit**, with **Additional Price Per Visit** at 0, since the employer pays you on a separate monthly invoice.
- **Validity**: **Restricted program**, with Classes narrowed to your yoga class types.
- **Operating hours**: weekdays only, if the benefit excludes weekends.

### Federation reciprocal access at a climbing gym

Members of a partner climbing federation get reciprocal day access.

- Keep **Validity** on **General program**, so any federation member can be waved in through **Direct check-in** with no fee.
- Keep **Additional Price Per Visit** at 0 so the program stays eligible for Direct check-in.

## Tips & best practices

- **Match codes to your partner's naming.** If the aggregator calls it `summer-pass-2026`, use that as the **Code** so finance reports line up with the partner's statements.
- **Pick the known program when there is one.** Linking it lets the program read its members' cards, and stops a second copy of the same program being created.
- **Scope tightly with Validity.** A corporate program that only covers group classes should list only Classes, not Areas - this keeps the entrance-method picker clean.
- **Keep Direct check-in programs free.** A general program with a per-visit fee is excluded from the Direct check-in list, because that flow only offers no-cost entries.
- **Use Admin only for staff-managed programs.** The front desk can still admit people with them, while members can't choose them in the portal or at the kiosk.

## Troubleshooting

**A restricted external program doesn't appear at check-in**
Check, in order: the **Active** toggle is on; the program's **Club** matches the club where the check-in happens (or is empty); the class, area, or instructor matches the program's **Validity** scope; and the current time is inside **Operating Hours**. If a member is checking in themselves (portal or kiosk), **Visibility** must be **Public** or **Admin & Member**.

**A program doesn't show in the Direct check-in list**
Direct check-in only lists **general** programs that carry **no** per-visit fee. If the program is restricted or has an **Additional Price Per Visit** above 0, it won't appear there - it's meant to be used as a booked-resource entrance method instead.

**The program paid only part of an area booking**
That's expected on an area with more than one place: a program pays one person's place. The other places stay with the organizer, owed at the gym, until they or the other players pay or take them.

**A repeating booking can't be put on a program**
A program pays visits one booking at a time, so a series can't be on one. Book the occurrences one by one, or pay the series directly.

**A member sees a program they shouldn't**
Change **Visibility** to **Admin only**. Members can then no longer choose it, while staff still can.

**Saving fails with a duplicate-code error**
A program with that **Code** already exists in your organization. Codes are unique across all clubs, so pick a different code.

**A known program is missing from the Program list, or reads "already added"**
Your organization already has that program - each known program can be added once. A known program is also offered only in the countries its cards work in.

**You want to retire a program but keep historical records**
Turn the **Active** toggle off instead of deleting. Disabled programs stay in reports but can't be selected for new check-ins. Delete only programs that were never used.
