An external program is a third-party offering - a partner curriculum, a corporate-wellness benefit, a federation pass, an aggregator like a city-wide multi-gym pass - that a member can use as their entrance method at check-in instead of one of your own memberships, optionally with an extra per-visit fee. You define programs once, and they surface for the front desk at check-in and in dynamic booking pricing.

## When to use it

Use external programs when something other than your own memberships gets a member through the door. Each visit is logged against the program, so you can report on usage and reconcile with the partner, optionally with a per-visit price.

Typical uses:

- **An aggregator pass at a padel gym** - a regional or multi-gym pass sends players to your courts, and the front desk picks the pass at check-in, which records the visit and its per-visit price.
- **Corporate wellness at a yoga studio** - a local employer's benefit covers its staff's yoga classes, and you track which visits arrived through it while the employer pays you separately.
- **Reciprocal access at a climbing gym** - members of a partner federation are waved in through **Direct check-in** with no fee.
- **Partner studio drop-ins** - a sister gym's members use your facility under a partnership.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to set up the first three.

## Where external programs fit

A program behaves in one of two ways, depending on what you choose in its **Validity** section:

- **Restricted programs** (you pick Classes, Areas, and/or Instructors) become a selectable **entrance method** when a member checks in against a matching booked class, area, or instructor - alongside **Membership** and **Direct payment**. Any **Additional Price Per Visit** is charged.
- **General programs** (no scope) can't grant access to a specific booking. Instead they do two things: they appear in the front-desk **Direct check-in** flow (when they carry no per-visit fee), and they factor into **dynamic booking pricing**. This lets a partner or corporate program adjust prices without being tied to one class or area.

You don't have to choose a scope at save time - a general program is a valid, first-class program.

## Managing external programs

You define programs in the **External Programs** section of **Settings** > **Billing** > **General**.

### Viewing all programs

1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **General** and scroll to **External Programs**.
2. The grid shows **Name**, **Code**, **Club** (or "All clubs"), **Additional Price Per Visit**, **Visibility**, and **Status** (active or inactive).
3. Click a row to edit, or click **Add External Program** to create a new one.

### Creating an external program

1. Click **Add External Program**.
2. Fill in the **General** section (see the fields below).
3. In **Validity**, keep **General program** or choose **Restricted program** to scope it to specific class, area, or instructor types.
4. Optionally set **Operating Hours** to restrict when the program is usable.
5. Click **Save**.

### Fields

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The display name staff and members see (for example, "Corporate wellness"). |
| **Code** | A short identifier used in reports and partner reconciliation. Lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores only. The system auto-fills it from the name as you type; edit it any time before you rely on it. Codes must be unique across your organization. |
| **Club** | Shown when you have more than one club. Leave empty to apply organization-wide, or pick one club to scope the program to a single location. With a single club, the program is tied to that club. |
| **Additional Price Per Visit** | Optional amount charged each time the program is used as a paid entrance method at a booked resource. Leave at 0 for free access. |
| **Visibility** | **Public** (the default), **Admin & Member**, or **Admin only**. Controls where the program is offered (see below). |
| **Active** | Toggle off to disable a program without deleting it. |

### Visibility

- **Public** - offered everywhere, including member-facing surfaces.
- **Admin & Member** - offered to staff and to members.
- **Admin only** - offered only on staff surfaces; for programs your front desk admits people with but members can't choose themselves.

Visibility decides **who may choose** the program, not whether it works. Staff surfaces - the admin, the instructor app, and the point of sale - offer programs of every visibility, including **Admin only**. Member-facing surfaces - the member portal, the marketplace, and the unattended kiosk - offer only **Public** and **Admin & Member** programs.

### Validity

The **Validity** section decides whether the program is a **General program** or a **Restricted program**. For a restricted program, select any combination of:

- **Classes** - the program covers check-ins for the class types you list (or all class types if you list none).
- **Areas** - the program covers check-ins for the area types you list.
- **Instructors** - the program covers check-ins with the instructor types you list.

Choosing **General program** leaves the program unscoped (see [Where external programs fit](#where-external-programs-fit)). A general program can't grant access to a booked resource, but it drives dynamic pricing and the Direct check-in flow.

### Operating hours

Set day-of-week hours during which the program can be used. Outside those hours, a restricted program won't appear in the entrance-method picker. The editor starts from a default schedule that mirrors your club's operating hours - override individual days only when the program's availability differs.

## Using an external program at check-in

There are two paths, matching the two program shapes:

**As an entrance method for a booked resource**

1. When the front desk records a check-in against a class, area, or instructor, they choose the entrance method.
2. **External program** appears as an option when at least one restricted program is active, in scope for that resource, and within its operating hours. Staff see programs of every visibility; members choosing for themselves see only **Public** and **Admin & Member** programs.
3. The staff member picks the program.
4. If the program has an **Additional Price Per Visit**, that amount is charged through the same payment pipeline as a direct-payment check-in, and the visit is recorded against the program.

**As a Direct check-in (no booking)**

At small gyms where staff just wave a member in without a class or area, the **Direct check-in** flow lists the member's eligible memberships and any **general** external programs that carry no per-visit fee, whatever their visibility. Pick the program to log the visit against it.

Either way, the visit is logged against the program so you can report on how many times it was used.

## Worked examples

### Aggregator pass at a padel club

A multi-gym pass provider sends players to your padel courts and reimburses you per visit.

- **Name**: Multi-gym pass. **Code**: `multigym-pass`.
- **Additional Price Per Visit**: your reimbursement rate, so revenue reports stay accurate even though the player doesn't pay you directly.
- **Validity**: **Restricted program**, with Areas narrowed to your padel area type.
- **Visibility**: **Admin & Member**.

When a pass holder books a court and checks in, the front desk selects **External program** > **Multi-gym pass** and the per-visit price is recorded.

### Corporate wellness for a yoga studio

A nearby employer covers unlimited yoga for its staff.

- **Name**: Acme wellness. **Code**: `acme-wellness`. **Visibility**: **Admin only**, so only the front desk can choose it.
- **Additional Price Per Visit**: 0, since the employer pays you on a separate monthly invoice.
- **Validity**: **Restricted program**, with Classes narrowed to your yoga class types.
- **Operating hours**: weekdays only, if the benefit excludes weekends.

### Federation reciprocal access at a climbing gym

Members of a partner climbing federation get reciprocal day access.

- Keep **Validity** on **General program**, so any federation member can be waved in through **Direct check-in** with no fee.
- Keep **Additional Price Per Visit** at 0 so the program stays eligible for Direct check-in.

## Tips & best practices

- **Match codes to your partner's naming.** If the aggregator calls it `summer-pass-2026`, use that as the **Code** so finance reports line up with the partner's statements.
- **Record a per-visit price when the partner reimburses you.** Even when the member pays nothing at the door, capturing the visit price gives you an accurate revenue picture.
- **Scope tightly with Validity.** A corporate program that only covers group classes should list only Classes, not Areas - this keeps the entrance-method picker clean.
- **Keep Direct check-in programs free.** A general program with a per-visit fee is excluded from the Direct check-in list, because that flow only offers no-cost entries.
- **Use Admin only for staff-managed programs.** The front desk can still admit people with them, while members can't choose them in the portal or at the kiosk.

## Troubleshooting

**A restricted external program doesn't appear at check-in**
Check, in order: the **Active** toggle is on; the program's **Club** matches the club where the check-in happens (or is empty); the class, area, or instructor matches the program's **Validity** scope; and the current time is inside **Operating Hours**. If a member is checking in themselves (portal or kiosk), **Visibility** must be **Public** or **Admin & Member**.

**A program doesn't show in the Direct check-in list**
Direct check-in only lists **general** programs that carry **no** per-visit fee. If the program is restricted or has an **Additional Price Per Visit** above 0, it won't appear there - it's meant to be used as a booked-resource entrance method instead.

**The Additional Price Per Visit isn't charged**
Confirm the price is set above 0 and that the program is being used as an entrance method on a booked resource (not through Direct check-in, which excludes priced programs). The charge only lands when the staff member completes the check-in payment.

**A member sees a program they shouldn't**
Change **Visibility** to **Admin only**. Members can then no longer choose it, while staff still can.

**Saving fails with a duplicate-code error**
A program with that **Code** already exists in your organization. Codes are unique across all clubs, so pick a different code.

**You want to retire a program but keep historical records**
Turn the **Active** toggle off instead of deleting. Disabled programs stay in reports but can't be selected for new check-ins. Delete only programs that were never used.
