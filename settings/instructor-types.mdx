An instructor type is a category for the staff members can book directly, such as _Personal Trainer_, _Tennis Coach_, or _Yoga Instructor_. You assign a type to each [instructor](/staff/instructors), and the type sets defaults for how private sessions with that kind of instructor work. Owners and admins manage instructor types.

## When to use it

- **A gym with personal trainers and physios** keeps them as separate types, so each reports its own revenue and can be filtered on the schedule.
- **A personal trainer who runs small groups** gets a type with **Max Concurrent Bookings** set to 3, so three bookings can share one time slot.
- **A tennis academy** sets its _Tennis Coach_ type to take **A group** of up to four people per session.

## Where to find it

Go to **Settings** > **Clubs** > **Instructor Types**. The list shows each type's name and description.

- Click **Add Instructor Type** to create one.
- Click a row, or use its **Edit** action, to change it.
- Use **Delete** to remove it. Deleting cannot be undone.

## Instructor type fields

- **Name** - What staff and members see. Required.
- **Slug** - Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. Required.
- **Description** - Optional.
- **Max Concurrent Bookings** - How many bookings an instructor of this type can hold in the same time slot, for example 3 for a personal trainer with small groups. Defaults to 1.
- **Revenue Account** - The revenue account bookings with instructors of this type book their income to. **None (use default)** uses your organization's default revenue account.
- **People per session** - **Just the booker**, or **A group** up to a **Max people** number you set.

## Permissions

Instructor types live in Settings, so you need Settings access to open the page. Creating, editing, and deleting them is also checked against the Operations module at club scope. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
