Integrations link 1Club with external services so your bookings, payments, door access, and marketing work alongside the tools you already use. An owner or admin activates and configures each one, and once it is active it runs in the background. Integrations are a paid-plan feature: on the Free plan they stay locked, and during the trial only a few are available (see [Plan limits](#plan-limits)).

## When to use it

Start with a payment provider if members should pay online, then add what your setup needs: a climbing gym opening its doors for booked members through WebLock, a gym syncing area bookings with Google Calendar, or a yoga studio tracking how many bookings its Instagram "Book now" link brings in. See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for setups like these.

## The integrations page

You manage every integration from **Settings > Integrations**. The page has two lists:

- **Active Integrations** - the integrations your organization uses, with a count. **Social Booking** always comes first. Each row shows a status chip, such as **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Configured**, and a **Configure** button that opens the integration's own page.
- **Available Integrations** - everything you can still add, with a count, grouped under **Payments**, **Access control**, **Marketing**, and **Tools**. Each row has an **Activate** button.

Every row also says what the integration does for your gym and carries a **Learn more** link to its page in these docs. When your organization trades through more than one billing entity, the Stripe, myPOS, and DatecsPay rows also say how many of them the provider is connected for, for example "Connected for 1 of 2 billing entities".

Each integration keeps its own configuration and status, so you can run several at once, for example Stripe for online payments and WebLock for door access.

## How it works

Every integration follows the same lifecycle, though the details differ per provider:

1. **Activate** - click **Activate** next to an available integration. What happens next depends on the integration:
   - **Google Calendar, WebLock, Impro, Salto KS, and Hype POS** are added straight away, a confirmation appears, and their page opens so you can finish the setup.
   - **Stripe** starts your Stripe account setup in a new tab and opens its page.
   - **myPOS, DatecsPay, Google Business Profile, Google Tag Manager, WhatsApp, and Meta** open their page, where you enter credentials or sign in to the provider's account to activate them.
2. **Configure** - on the integration's own page, fill in credentials, mappings, or sync rules. Each integration has its own settings - see the pages above.
3. **Test** - most integrations let you check the setup before you rely on it, for example **Test connection** for WebLock, Impro, Salto KS, and Hype POS, **Validate configuration** for myPOS, and **Test configuration** for DatecsPay. Stripe, Google Calendar, Google Business Profile, Meta, and WhatsApp check the account while you sign in to it.
4. **Use** - once active, the integration runs in the background. It appears under **Active Integrations** with its status, and you return to its page with **Configure**.
5. **Deactivate** - click **Deactivate** at the top of the integration's own page, not on the list. The confirmation, for example **Deactivate Stripe?**, says what you lose, usually that the integration stops working and its settings are deleted. For a payment provider connected for several billing entities, it also says which one the deactivation applies to. If 1Club can't deactivate it, the reason appears in the dialog. Afterwards Stripe, myPOS, and DatecsPay stay on their page, and the others return you to **Settings > Integrations**.

Deactivating does not delete data already sent to the provider, such as events in Google Calendar or charges in Stripe. Door access works differently: WebLock has no **Deactivate** button and is switched off per gym, and Impro and Salto KS withdraw the access they granted before they are removed.

<Note>
  Opening **Settings > Integrations** requires the
  **settings.read.organization** permission. Activating, configuring, testing,
  and deactivating integrations requires **settings.manage.organization**.
</Note>

## Plan limits

- **Free plan** - every integration except Social Booking is locked. A banner says "Integrations are not available in your plan."
- **Trial** - Google Calendar, Google Business Profile, and Social Booking are available. Payments, messaging, door access, and the rest stay locked, and a banner says so.
- **Paid plans** - everything is available.

A locked integration's **Activate** or **Configure** button is greyed out. An integration you activated before the lock stays listed under **Active Integrations**, but you can't open its page until you upgrade.

## Choosing a payment provider

1Club supports **Stripe**, **myPOS**, and **DatecsPay** as payment providers. You can run Stripe alongside one of the hosted-checkout providers, but most operators pick one based on country and preferences:

- **Stripe** is the broader option - widest country coverage, digital wallets, Stripe Connect onboarding, automatic payouts, and a large dashboard ecosystem.
- **myPOS** is a fit if you already have a myPOS merchant account or operate in a region where myPOS is the established provider. Customers pay on myPOS's hosted checkout page, so card data never touches 1Club.
- **DatecsPay** is a BORICA-backed hosted checkout for merchants who already have a BORICA e-commerce terminal, typically issued by a Bulgarian bank. Like myPOS, customers pay on the provider's secure page. Unlike myPOS it has no shared sandbox - both Test and Live need your own BORICA credentials - and it does not support shareable payment links.

Every provider supports a test mode so you can run the full checkout flow before charging real cards. myPOS and DatecsPay both use hosted checkout for the same "pay by card" step, so only one of them can be active at a time for the same billing entity. You can run Stripe alongside either.

## Worked examples

**Padel gym taking online court payments (Stripe).** After upgrading to a paid plan, a racket-sports gym activates Stripe and runs a few bookings in test mode using Stripe test cards to confirm the portal checkout works. It then switches on **Live mode** and onboards its own Stripe Connect account. Court bookings and coach sessions now collect real payments at checkout.

**Boxing gym in a myPOS region.** A martial-arts gym already banks with myPOS. It activates myPOS, pastes its Configuration Pack from the myPOS Checkout Store, validates the configuration, and starts collecting membership dues through hosted checkout - no second payment processor needed.

**Climbing gym with door access.** A climbing gym activates WebLock and links each of its areas to WebLock door groups and light devices, so a booking or check-in in an area opens that area's doors. It keeps Stripe active in parallel for online payments.

**Yoga studio marketing its schedule.** A yoga studio uses the always-on Social Booking tool to generate a tagged "Book now" link for its Instagram bio, then checks **Analytics > Bookings by source** to see how many reformer bookings came from Instagram versus its website.

## Tips & best practices

- Try every payment provider in test mode first. A test payment through the real checkout flow is the only reliable proof that live credentials will work.
- Keep only the integrations you actually use active. Fewer active integrations means fewer places to reconcile and fewer moving parts when something looks off.
- Most integrations are set up once for the whole organization. Door access is the exception: WebLock, Impro, and Salto KS are set up per gym, and a gym can use only one of them at a time. Payment providers are connected per billing entity when you trade through more than one company.
- Upgrade to a paid plan before you plan to take payments. Stripe, myPOS, and DatecsPay can't be activated on the Free plan or during the trial.

## Troubleshooting

- **Every Activate button is greyed out** - your organization is on the Free plan. Upgrade to unlock integrations. Social Booking stays available regardless.
- **Only Google Calendar and Google Business Profile can be activated** - your organization is still in the trial. Upgrade to unlock payments, messaging, door access, and the rest.
- **I can see integrations but can't activate or configure them** - you are missing the **settings.manage.organization** permission. Ask an admin to grant it, or have an admin do the setup.
- **An integration I deactivated still shows external data** - deactivating stops 1Club from creating new activity, but it does not delete data already sent to the provider. Manage that from the provider's own dashboard (for example refunds in Stripe or myPOS).
- **Google Tag Manager is still under Available Integrations even though I added a container ID** - the list catches up when the Google Tag Manager page loads. Click **Activate** next to Google Tag Manager to open its page, or save the container ID again.
