DatecsPay connects 1club to Datecs and BORICA. It has three modules you switch on per company: **Online** takes card payments on BORICA's hosted checkout, **POS Terminals** charges cards on Datecs terminals at your desk, and **Fiscal** prints fiscal receipts on Datecs fiscal devices. An owner or admin sets it up once; any one module can run on its own.

<Note>
  If your organization trades through more than one company, each company
  connects its own BORICA terminal. Pick the company under **Billing entity** at
  the top of the DatecsPay page before you save or deactivate anything. The line
  under the picker reminds you that payments for that company's gyms settle to
  the account you are setting up. The picker only appears when you have more
  than one billing entity. See [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities).
</Note>

## When to use it

DatecsPay suits a gym in Bulgaria that banks with BORICA, uses Datecs hardware at the desk, or both. Card data never touches 1club servers, and every request is signed with your own RSA key. Payment status comes back on its own: through the cardholder's browser on return, a signed BORICA notification, and a reconciliation sweep that catches anyone who closes the tab early.

Unlike Stripe and myPOS, DatecsPay has no free shared sandbox. Both the **Test** and **Live** environments run against BORICA with your own credentials. Only the terminal and gateway differ between them.

Typical uses:

- **A gym already banking with BORICA** - take card payments online through the BORICA terminal your bank issued, with no Stripe or myPOS account.
- **A BlueCash-50 at reception** - charge cards on the Datecs terminal from the till, and print each sale's fiscal receipt on the same box. Turn on **POS Terminals** and **Fiscal**.
- **Fiscal receipts beside a Stripe reader** - keep taking cards on Stripe and use a BlueCash-50 only to print fiscal receipts for cash and card sales. Turn on **Fiscal** alone, with no BORICA credentials.
- **Rehearsing checkout before going live** - run the full checkout against BORICA's test gateway with your test terminal, where no real money moves, then switch to **Live**.
- **A member who closes the tab mid-payment** - the membership still settles, because the reconciliation job confirms the charge with BORICA within a few minutes, with no staff action.

## Modules

Each module is a checkbox on the DatecsPay page, switched on per company. Any one can be on alone.

| Module            | What it does                                                                                                                                                       | What it needs                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Online**        | Members pay by card on BORICA's hosted checkout, wherever they buy online.                                                                                         | Your BORICA credentials, entered under the checkbox. No other online card provider for the company.                                                    |
| **POS Terminals** | Staff charge cards on Datecs terminals, such as the BlueCash-50, from the till. The terminals close their card batch once a day.                                    | A **Daily card batch close** time. You pair the terminals under [Payment terminals](/settings/payment-terminals#datecs-terminals).                       |
| **Fiscal**        | Datecs fiscal devices print the fiscal receipt for every cash and card sale at the desk, whichever provider's reader took the card.                                | Nothing on this page. You add fiscal rules and fiscal devices under [Fiscal receipts](/settings/fiscal).                                                 |

Datecs terminals and fiscal devices have no cloud connection of their own. They reach 1club through the **Payment Bridge** app that runs on the device, which you pair with a one-time code.

## Before you start

- **A paid plan.** DatecsPay is a paid-plan integration. On the Free plan and during the free trial, **Activate** is greyed out until you upgrade.
- **For Online: BORICA credentials for the environment you want.** Both Test and Live need a **Terminal ID (TID)**, a **Merchant ID (MID)**, your **private key**, and **BORICA's certificate**. See [Get your BORICA files](#get-your-borica-files) for which file is which. BORICA issues a separate set for each environment, so keep the two sets apart. POS Terminals and Fiscal need none of these.
- **For Online: no other online card provider for the company.** Each company takes online card payments through one provider at a time: Stripe, myPOS, or DatecsPay. While Stripe or myPOS takes them, the **Online** checkbox is locked and says which provider holds it. Untick **Online** under **Use Stripe for** on the [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe#use-stripe-for) page, or untick **Online** on the [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos#one-online-card-provider-per-company) page, first. Both can keep running card readers beside DatecsPay, and POS Terminals and Fiscal aren't affected.
- **For Fiscal: the company's country.** Fiscal receipts print only for a company whose [billing entity](/settings/billing-entities) is in Bulgaria.

<Note>
  Configuring DatecsPay requires the **settings.manage.organization**
  permission. Viewing its settings requires **settings.read.organization**.
</Note>

## Get your BORICA files

You need these only for the **Online** module. They come from **BORICA** (through Datecs or your bank), not from 1club, and BORICA issues a separate set for **Test** and for **Live**.

Setting up a terminal leaves you with several files that look alike. Only two of them go into 1club:

| File                                                                           | Goes into 1club? | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your private key**, named like `V12345678_20261119_P_private.key`             | Yes              | Saved on your computer when the certificate request was made with BORICA's key tool. `P` is for Live and `T` for Test. It may be protected with a passphrase you chose at the time. |
| **BORICA's certificate**, `MPI OW APGW` (a `.cer`, or a `.pem` public key)       | Yes              | BORICA's own certificate. It's the same for every merchant, and differs only between Test and Live.                                                                                       |
| **Your terminal's certificate**, named like `V12345678-yourgym.bg-P.cer`        | No               | The certificate BORICA issued for your terminal. BORICA keeps its own copy.                                                                                                                |
| **Your terminal's public key**, named like `V12345678-yourgym.bg-P.pub`         | No               | The public half of your private key. BORICA keeps its own copy.                                                                                                                            |
| **The certificate request**, a `.csr`                                           | No               | What was sent to BORICA to get your terminal's certificate.                                                                                                                                |

You also need your **Terminal ID (TID)**, such as `V12345678`, and your **Merchant ID (MID)**. Both are in the letter or email that came with your terminal, and the TID is also in your file names.

<Warning>
  Only the private key made together with the request BORICA signed works. BORICA's key tool makes a new key every time it runs, so if the request was made more than once, use the key saved with the last one sent. A lost private key can't be recovered: make a new request with BORICA's key tool and ask Datecs for a new certificate.
</Warning>

## Activate DatecsPay

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**.
2. Under **Payments**, click **Activate** next to DatecsPay. 1club opens the DatecsPay page. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.
3. Tick the modules you want: **Online**, **POS Terminals**, **Fiscal**. **Online** starts ticked, unless another provider already takes the company's online card payments.
4. If **Online** is ticked:
   1. Choose an **Environment**: **Test** runs against BORICA's test gateway, and **Live** processes real payments against BORICA production. BORICA gives you different credentials for each, so enter the set that matches.
   2. Enter your **Terminal ID (TID)** and **Merchant ID (MID)**.
   3. Check the **Merchant name** and **Merchant website**. 1club pre-fills these from your organization's name and website, but you can edit them if BORICA registered a different name or URL.
   4. Paste your private key file into **Private key (..._private.key)** and BORICA's certificate into **BORICA certificate (MPI OW APGW)**. Open each file in a text editor and copy everything, from `-----BEGIN` to `-----END`. If your key file is protected with a passphrase, enter it in **Private key passphrase**. 1club uses it once to open the key and never stores it. **Where to get these files** above the fields opens [Get your BORICA files](#get-your-borica-files).
5. If **POS Terminals** is ticked, set the **Daily card batch close** time. See [Daily card batch close](#daily-card-batch-close).
6. Click **Save**. The page shows **Active** next to the title and, with Online on, the environment, **Test** or **Live**. **Save** stays greyed out until at least one module is ticked.
7. With Online on, click **Test connection**. 1club asks BORICA with your saved key and terminal, and checks BORICA's reply with your saved certificate. See [Test connection](#test-connection).
8. Pair your Datecs terminals under [Payment terminals](/settings/payment-terminals#datecs-terminals), and add fiscal rules and fiscal devices under [Fiscal receipts](/settings/fiscal).

<Note>
  The private key and certificate are stored encrypted and shown back to you as
  masked fields (`•••••• (saved - leave blank to keep)`). When you edit the
  configuration later, leave them blank to keep the existing values. Only paste
  a value if you are replacing it.
</Note>

Your country and time-zone offset are not part of this form. 1club derives them automatically from your organization settings and sends them to BORICA at checkout.

## Configuration fields

The BORICA fields apply only while **Online** is ticked. With Online off, none of them is required.

| Field                               | Required                                       | Notes                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Environment**                     | Yes                                            | **Test** or **Live**. Your BORICA credentials differ between the two.                                                                                 |
| **Terminal ID (TID)**               | With Online                                    | Your BORICA terminal identifier; differs per environment.                                                                                             |
| **Merchant ID (MID)**               | With Online                                    | Your BORICA merchant identifier.                                                                                                                      |
| **Merchant name**                   | No                                             | Pre-filled from your organization name. Editable.                                                                                                     |
| **Merchant website**                | No                                             | Pre-filled from your organization website. Editable.                                                                                                  |
| **Private key (..._private.key)**   | On first save, and when you change environment | Your private key, used to sign requests to BORICA. Stored encrypted. Leave blank when editing to keep the current key.                                |
| **Private key passphrase**          | No                                             | Only if your key file is passphrase-protected. Used once to decrypt the key and never stored.                                                         |
| **BORICA certificate (MPI OW APGW)** | On first save, and when you change environment | BORICA's own certificate, used to check BORICA's replies. Differs between Test and Live. Leave blank when editing to keep the current certificate. |
| **Daily card batch close**          | With POS Terminals                             | When the company's Datecs terminals close their card batch each day, in each terminal's gym's local time. Defaults to 03:00.                          |

## Daily card batch close

With **POS Terminals** on, every Datecs terminal of the company closes its card batch once a day at the time you set. The Payment Bridge app on the terminal runs it, so you don't close the batch by hand. Pick a time when the desk is closed. If a terminal must close its batch before it can take a card and that doesn't go through, the card panel says so and nothing is charged; try again.

The card batch close is not the fiscal day: 1club doesn't run X or Z reports on your fiscal devices.

## Switch a module off

Untick the module and click **Save**:

- **Online** off - members can no longer pay by card through BORICA for that company.
- **POS Terminals** off - the company's Datecs terminals are retired from [Payment terminals](/settings/payment-terminals). This is refused while one of them has a card charge still being resolved.
- **Fiscal** off - the company's fiscal devices are removed, and its desk sales tell staff to issue the receipt by hand. This is refused while a fiscal device still owes a receipt: wait for it to print, or [waive it](/settings/fiscal#waive-a-fiscal-receipt) once you have issued it by hand.

A BlueCash-50 that is both a terminal and a fiscal device stays paired while either module still uses it.

## Test vs. live environment

The environment applies to the **Online** module only. Datecs terminals and fiscal devices work the same way in either.


- **Test** runs against BORICA's test gateway with your test terminal. No real money moves, so you can rehearse the full checkout flow.
- **Live** runs against BORICA production with your live terminal. Real payments are processed and settled to your account.

There is no credential-free sandbox. Both environments need a full credential set, and the TID, key, and certificate are different for each.

DatecsPay keeps one credential set per company, for the environment you saved. When you switch **Environment**, the page clears the **Terminal ID (TID)**, key, passphrase, and certificate fields. To save, enter the TID, private key, and BORICA certificate issued for the new environment. If you leave the key or certificate blank, the page asks for the one issued for that environment. Your saved set for the old environment is replaced, so keep a copy if you plan to switch back.

## Test connection

The **Test connection** button asks BORICA about your latest card payment of the day (or a made-up one, if there isn't one yet), using your saved private key and TID, and checks BORICA's signed reply with your saved BORICA certificate. It charges nothing and changes nothing at BORICA, so it's safe to run in **Live**. On success it shows "BORICA accepted the key and certificate" and records the time.

When it fails, it says which part is wrong:

| Message                                                    | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **BORICA refused the private key for terminal ...**         | The saved private key isn't the one BORICA holds for this terminal, or the TID belongs to the other environment. Save the private key made with this terminal's certificate request. See [Get your BORICA files](#get-your-borica-files). |
| **BORICA's reply does not verify with the saved BORICA certificate** | The certificate field holds something other than BORICA's own certificate, or the one for the other environment. Save BORICA's `MPI OW APGW` certificate for the environment you selected.               |
| **BORICA did not answer**                                   | BORICA's gateway didn't respond. Try again in a minute.                                                                                                                                                      |

Run it after every change to the key, the certificate or the TID. A setup that fails it can't take payments: BORICA may still charge the card, but 1club can't confirm the payment.

## What syncs online

- Payments created in 1club open a BORICA hosted-checkout page that the customer is redirected to.
- After payment, the status returns to 1club through up to three paths, so a payment settles even if one path fails:
  1. **Browser return** - BORICA posts the signed result back through the cardholder's browser when they return to 1club, and 1club verifies the signature and settles the payment.
  2. **Signed notification** - BORICA can also notify 1club server to server. The notification's signature is verified against your BORICA certificate before it is processed.
  3. **Reconciliation** - a background job runs every 5 minutes and confirms any payment still pending (for example because the customer closed the tab before returning) with a direct status check to BORICA.
- The payment status and BORICA transaction references are recorded on the 1club payment, and any linked charges and bookings are settled automatically.
- Every path is matched to the same payment by a unique reference, so a redelivered notification or an overlapping reconciliation never double-charges or double-settles.

## Where members pay by card

Members pay through BORICA's checkout page wherever they buy online: bookings, classes, events, memberships, the [shop](/sales/products#member-portal-shop), signup forms, and outstanding balances in the member portal and on your website. In the member app, the checkout page opens inside the app and the member returns to the app with the result. If the confirmation takes a moment longer, the app says so rather than reporting a failure, so nobody pays twice.

## Membership renewals

DatecsPay doesn't collect renewals on its own: BORICA's checkout needs the member at the page, and there is no saved card to charge. When DatecsPay takes a company's online card payments, each renewal of an auto-renewing membership is raised as a charge and left owed. Collect it at the desk or from an invoice. See [Recurring memberships](/sales/overview#recurring-memberships).

## Payment links

DatecsPay has no shareable payment link. The **Send myPOS payment link** action on a charge works with myPOS only, so on DatecsPay a member pays through the checkout in the portal, the app, or your website rather than from a link you send them. If sending a pay-by-link is central to how you collect, myPOS is the better fit.

## Refunds

1club does not push refunds to BORICA. Refund a DatecsPay payment from your BORICA/acquirer side, then reconcile the charge in 1club.

## Deactivate

1. Pick the company under **Billing entity**, if you have more than one.
2. Click **Deactivate** at the top of the DatecsPay page.
3. Confirm in the **Deactivate DatecsPay?** dialog. It says DatecsPay stops working and its settings are deleted. With more than one billing entity, it also says the change applies to the selected company only.

Deactivating switches off all three modules at once: the company's Datecs terminals are retired and its fiscal devices are removed. You stay on the DatecsPay page afterwards, with the form reset. Your stored credentials for that company are cleared, so activating again means entering them again. If 1club can't deactivate DatecsPay, for example because a fiscal device still owes a receipt, the reason shows in the dialog.

## Troubleshooting

- **Activate is greyed out** - your organization is on the Free plan or the free trial. Upgrade to a paid plan to activate payments.
- **Test connection fails** - the message says whether BORICA refused the key or its reply didn't verify with the certificate. See [Test connection](#test-connection).
- **Save says the private key is a certificate or a public key** - you pasted your terminal's `.cer` or `.pub` into **Private key**. Paste the `..._private.key` file instead. See [Get your BORICA files](#get-your-borica-files).
- **Save says the certificate is your own public key** - you pasted your terminal's `.cer` or `.pub` into **BORICA certificate**. Paste BORICA's `MPI OW APGW` certificate instead.
- **Save says the private key is passphrase-protected, or the passphrase is incorrect** - enter the passphrase chosen when the key was made in **Private key passphrase**.
- **Save asks for the key and certificate again** - you changed **Environment**. BORICA issues a separate set per environment, so paste the key and certificate for the environment you selected.
- **Customers see a signature error on the checkout page** - the private key on file isn't the one BORICA holds for your terminal, or the TID belongs to the other environment. Run **Test connection**, which says which.
- **Payment succeeded on BORICA but the charge stays unpaid in 1club** - this usually resolves on its own: the reconciliation job confirms pending checkouts within a few minutes. If it persists, run **Test connection**: 1club can confirm a payment only while BORICA accepts your key and its reply verifies with your certificate. BORICA keeps a payment for about a day, so fix the setup within that time, or record the payment by hand.
- **Live payments fail but test works** - confirm you are in the **Live** environment and entered live credentials (a production TID and the production BORICA certificate, not the test ones).
- **The Online checkbox is locked, or saving says another provider "already takes online card payments"** - only one online card provider can be active per company. Untick **Online** on the Stripe or myPOS page for that company, then tick **Online** on DatecsPay again.
- **Save is greyed out** - no module is ticked. Tick at least one, or click **Deactivate** to switch DatecsPay off.
- **Switching Fiscal off is refused** - a fiscal device still owes a receipt. Wait for it to print, or waive it on the payment once you have issued it by hand, then save again.

## Worked examples

**Bulgarian gym already banking with BORICA.** A fitness gym whose bank issued it a BORICA e-commerce terminal makes its key and certificate request with BORICA's key tool, receives its terminal's certificate, and downloads BORICA's production certificate. It activates DatecsPay, selects **Live**, enters its TID and MID, pastes its `..._P_private.key` and BORICA's `MPI OW APGW` certificate, saves, and runs **Test connection**. One real area booking confirms hosted checkout settles the charge.

**A member who closes the tab mid-payment.** A member pays for a membership on BORICA's page but closes the browser before returning to 1club, so the payment briefly shows as pending. Within a few minutes the reconciliation job checks the status with BORICA, confirms the charge, and settles the membership, with no staff action needed.

## Tips & best practices

- Keep your test and production credential sets clearly separated. The TID, key, and BORICA certificate differ per environment, and mixing them is the most common cause of signature errors.
- Run **Test connection** after every change to the key, certificate or TID, before members pay.
- Keep your private key files somewhere safe. BORICA never sees them, so nobody can send you a lost one.
- Leave the masked private key and certificate fields blank when editing later. Only paste a value when you are deliberately replacing it.
- Decide up front whether each company takes online card payments through DatecsPay, myPOS, or Stripe. Only one online card provider can run per company.
- If you sell monthly memberships, plan how you will collect renewals. DatecsPay leaves each renewal owed, while Stripe and myPOS can collect them automatically.
