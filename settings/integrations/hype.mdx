Hype is a point-of-sale and kitchen system. The Hype integration links 1Club to your Hype account through the Hype Exchange Service (HES). You take payment in 1Club as usual, and every paid order is sent to Hype for a compliant fiscal receipt and the kitchen display. Your Hype menu (categories and products) syncs back into 1Club. An owner or admin activates it once, with an API token from Hype support.

## When to use it

Hype is for gyms that run a cafe, bar, or kitchen alongside bookings and memberships and need one fiscalization path for everything they sell. Every 1Club order line - bookings, memberships, signup fees, and products - is billed against one **Service item** in Hype's catalog, so Hype can fiscalize it without a matching catalog item. The receipt still prints the real line name.

Typical uses:

- **A cafe next to the courts** - court bookings and cafe sales both go to Hype for compliant receipts, and the cafe's drinks and snacks sync into 1Club as products.
- **One desk for passes and coffee** - staff ring up a day pass and a coffee in 1Club and take the payment by cash or card reader. Hype receives the sale already paid, with the payment methods that paid it, and fiscalizes it.
- **Memberships on the same fiscal receipts as the shop** - a yearly membership and its signup fee fiscalize through the service item, and the receipt prints "Membership - Yearly plan" rather than the placeholder name.

## Before you start

- Hype is a **paid-plan** feature. On the Free plan and during the trial, its **Activate** and **Configure** buttons stay greyed out until you upgrade.
- Configuring Hype requires the **settings.manage.organization** permission. Viewing its settings and status requires **settings.read.organization**.
- You need a Hype account and an integration provisioned by **Hype support**. When they provision it, they give you an **API token** for the Hype Exchange Service. Without a provisioned integration there is nothing for the token to link to.

<Note>
  Hype support starts the initial sync when they provision the integration. You
  can't start or restart it from 1Club. 1Club only works through the tasks Hype
  queues and shows the status Hype reports.
</Note>

## Activate Hype

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and click **Activate** on the **Hype POS** card, under **Tools**. The **Hype POS** page opens.
2. Enter your **API token** - the token Hype support gave you.
3. Leave **HES base URL** at its default, `https://es.hype-software.com` (production). Only change it if Hype told you to use another host on `hype-software.com`, such as the dev environment `https://es.dev.hype-software.com`.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Click **Test connection**. 1Club reads the status Hype reports for your integration and shows it on the status chip. Automatic syncing runs while the status is **Syncing** or **Active**.

The API token is stored encrypted and is never shown back to you. Once a token is saved, the field shows a masked placeholder, and leaving it blank when you save keeps the current token.

## Configuration fields

| Field                                   | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **API token**                           | The Hype Exchange Service token Hype support gives you when they provision the integration. Stored encrypted. Leave it blank when you save to keep the current token.                     |
| **HES base URL**                        | Defaults to `https://es.hype-software.com`. Must be an `https://` address on `hype-software.com`. Use `https://es.dev.hype-software.com` only if Hype directs you to the dev environment. |
| **Service item**                        | The Hype product every 1Club sale is recorded under for fiscal receipts. You can only pick it once products have synced from Hype. See [Set the service item](#set-the-service-item).     |
| **Send open tabs to Hype as they grow** | Off by default. Sends each round on an open tab to Hype as it is added. See [Send open tabs to Hype as they grow](#send-open-tabs-to-hype-as-they-grow).                                  |

Changes to these fields take effect when you click **Save**.

## Connection status

The status chip on the **Hype POS Configuration** card shows what Hype reports:

- **Not activated** - Hype is not activated yet.
- **Inactive** - activated, but Hype reports the integration isn't running, or 1Club hasn't read its status yet. Click **Test connection** to read it.
- **Syncing** - Hype's initial sync is running. While it runs, the page refreshes on its own every couple of seconds.
- **Active** - Hype reports the integration is ready. Paid orders go to Hype only while the status is **Active**.
- **Error** - Hype aborted the sync or reported an error, or Hype rejected the API token. The most recent error is shown under the form, and syncing pauses. If the token was rejected, enter a new one and click **Save** to resume.

The last successful sync is shown under the form as **Last sync**.

## Test connection

**Test connection** checks that your saved token works against the HES base URL and updates the status chip with what Hype reports. A toast says **Token works** or **Token rejected by Hype**, and the **Hype status** panel shows, for each integration Hype has linked to the token:

- **Integration** - the Hype-side integration ID.
- **Status** - Hype's lifecycle status for it.
- **Pipeline** - the sync pipeline status and, where available, the step (for example "step 4/11").
- **Failed queue jobs** - any jobs Hype couldn't process, with the entity, method, and Hype's error message. These usually point at the exact missing mapping.

If the token works but no integration is linked yet, the panel says so. Only Hype can fix this: contact Hype support and ask them to provision an integration for your account.

If the token is rejected, the status changes to **Error** and syncing pauses until you save a new token. If the token now belongs to a different Hype integration than before, 1Club clears the **Service item**, because it belongs to the old catalog. Pick it again.

## Sync now

Hype drives the sync by queueing tasks (create this category, create this product, this order changed) that 1Club works through. 1Club checks the queues every 15 seconds while the status is **Syncing** or **Active**. **Sync now** works through every queue straight away and then refreshes the status from Hype. Use it after Hype support tells you provisioning has advanced, or when you want new menu items to appear without waiting.

## What syncs

**From Hype into 1Club (the menu):**

- **Categories** sync into 1Club product categories.
- **Products** sync into 1Club products.
- Both appear in the **Synced mappings** panel, on the **Categories** and **Products** tabs, each with the time it synced.

**From 1Club to Hype (sales):**

- An order goes to Hype once it is closed and fully paid. Each line - product, booking, membership, or signup fee - references your **Service item**, but carries the real name of what was sold (for example "Membership - Yearly plan" or "Booking - Court 2") and its price including tax, so the Hype receipt shows what was actually sold.
- The order also carries the payment methods that paid it. 1Club shares its payment methods with Hype during the sync, and Hype matches each one to its own payment types.
- Discounts on the order go to Hype as an order-level discount.
- If you cancel an order in 1Club that Hype already received, 1Club marks it **Cancelled** in Hype.
- If sending a paid order fails, for example during a Hype outage, 1Club tries again for paid orders closed in the last hour.

**From Hype back to 1Club:**

- Order status changes in Hype don't change anything in 1Club. The money is collected in 1Club, so 1Club stays the record of what was paid.
- If Hype cancels a sale, the payment in 1Club is left as it is. If the sale is void, refund it in 1Club.

## Set the service item

Hype fiscalizes against its own catalog, but 1Club sells bookings, memberships, and products that don't exist in Hype's catalog. The **Service item** solves this. It is a placeholder product in Hype that every 1Club sale is recorded under for the fiscal receipt. Hype still receives the real line name and price, so the receipt shows what was actually sold.

1. Make sure products have synced from Hype. If the picker is empty and greyed out, click **Sync now**.
2. Choose the placeholder product in the **Service item** dropdown.
3. Click **Save**.

### If you have no placeholder product yet

The picker only offers products that exist in Hype. If none of them is a good placeholder, or nothing has synced yet:

1. In Hype's POS, add a product to use as the placeholder (for example "1Club sale").
2. On the **Hype POS** page in 1Club, click **Sync now** so the new product syncs.
3. Pick it in the **Service item** dropdown and click **Save**.

<Warning>
  Until a service item is set, no order reaches Hype. That means no fiscal
  receipt and no kitchen ticket, even while the status reads **Active**. The
  page shows an "Orders are not being sent to Hype" warning until you pick one
  and save. 1Club only catches up on paid orders from the last hour, so older
  orders never reach Hype.
</Warning>

## Send open tabs to Hype as they grow

By default, a tab rung up at the till reaches Hype once, when it is closed and paid. Turn on **Send open tabs to Hype as they grow** and click **Save** to send each round to Hype as soon as it is added, so the kitchen sees it straight away. Other open orders, such as an online checkout waiting for its card payment, still go to Hype only when they are closed and paid.

<Warning>
  Before you turn this on, open Hype's back office and match Hype's accepted
  order status to 1Club's **Accepted** status. 1Club relies on that status to
  know when Hype stops accepting changes to an order, so it can send later
  rounds as a new order instead.
</Warning>

## Deactivate

1. On the **Hype POS** page, click **Deactivate** at the top of the page.
2. Confirm in the **Deactivate Hype POS?** dialog. It warns that the integration stops working and its settings are deleted.

1Club removes the API token and settings, and the links between your synced categories and products and their Hype items. The categories and products themselves stay in 1Club. You return to **Settings** > **Integrations**, and Hype moves back to the available integrations. If deactivation fails, the reason is shown inside the dialog.

## Worked examples

**Padel gym with a cafe.** A padel gym runs a cafe next to its courts. Hype support provisions the integration and sends an API token. The gym activates Hype, saves the token, clicks **Test connection**, and waits for the menu to sync. It clicks **Sync now** to speed it up, and its drinks and snacks appear under **Synced mappings** > **Products**. It picks one of those products as the **Service item**. Court bookings and cafe sales now both go to Hype for compliant receipts.

**Climbing gym with one desk.** A climbing gym sells day passes and coffee at one desk. Staff add both to an order in 1Club and take the payment on the card reader. When the order closes paid, Hype receives it with the card payment attached and fiscalizes it. There is no second step in Hype.

**Martial-arts gym selling memberships through Hype.** A jiu-jitsu gym wants membership signups on the same fiscal receipts as its shop. Because every line routes through the **Service item**, the yearly membership and the signup fee both fiscalize, and the Hype receipt still prints "Membership - Yearly plan" rather than the placeholder name.

## Tips and best practices

- Set the **Service item** as soon as products have synced. Until one is chosen, orders never reach Hype.
- Use **Test connection** to read Hype's pipeline step and failed jobs before assuming a sync is stuck. The error messages point at the exact missing mapping.
- Click **Sync now** after Hype support tells you provisioning advanced, rather than waiting for the next automatic check.
- Leave the **API token** field blank when you edit other settings. Only paste a value when you are deliberately replacing the token.
- Readiness is whatever Hype reports. If the status shows **Inactive** or **Syncing** for a long time, that is Hype's pipeline, not a 1Club setting you can change.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Service item dropdown is empty** - products haven't synced from Hype yet. Click **Sync now**, or add a placeholder product in Hype's POS and sync again (see [If you have no placeholder product yet](#if-you-have-no-placeholder-product-yet)).
- **Orders aren't appearing in Hype** - check that a **Service item** is set, the status is **Active**, and the order is closed and fully paid. Open orders wait until they close, unless they are till tabs and **Send open tabs to Hype as they grow** is on.
- **The status stays Inactive after you saved the token** - click **Test connection** so 1Club reads the status from Hype. If Hype still reports it inactive, contact Hype support.
- **The status shows Error** - read the error under the form. If Hype rejected the token, enter a new one and click **Save**.
- **Test connection says the token works but no integration is linked** - the token works, but Hype hasn't provisioned an integration for it. Contact Hype support.
- **The status is stuck on Syncing** - this reflects Hype's provisioning pipeline. Use **Test connection** to read the pipeline step and any failed queue jobs, and send those details to Hype support.
- **The Service item was cleared** - the token now belongs to a different Hype integration. Pick the placeholder product from the new catalog and click **Save**.
- **A sale was cancelled in Hype** - the payment in 1Club is unchanged. If the sale is void, refund it in 1Club.
