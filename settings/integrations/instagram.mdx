Connect your gym's Instagram to 1club, one account for each gym. Your newest posts appear on your website and member portal, your best reviews go to Instagram in a click, and an independent coach can fill their profile from their own account. You set it up once; after that it keeps itself up to date.

## Why connect it

- **Show visitors your gym is alive.** A climbing gym adds the Instagram section to its home page, and every new route it posts appears there without anyone touching the website.
- **Keep members in the loop.** Members opening the portal see this week's posts on its home page, next to their bookings.
- **Turn praise into posts.** A Pilates studio gets a five-star review and posts it to its Instagram story straight from the review, without downloading anything.
- **Set up a coach profile in minutes.** An independent personal trainer brings their picture, bio, photos and videos over from Instagram instead of uploading them again.

## How it works

Your posts are read live from Instagram, so a post you add, edit or delete on Instagram changes on your website and portal within a few minutes. 1club keeps no copy of them. The only pictures 1club keeps are the ones you choose: a review you post to Instagram, and the photos and videos a coach imports to their profile.

Connecting is separate from the Instagram link in your gym's **Social profiles**. The link works without connecting, for any account. When you connect, the link is set to the account you signed in with, so the two always match.

## Before you start

- Your Instagram must be a **Business** or **Creator** account. A personal account can switch for free in the Instagram app, under **Settings and privacy** > **Account type and tools**.
- Connecting needs the **settings.update.extended** permission. Posting a review needs **marketing.update.standard**, the same as replying to a review.

## Connect Instagram

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and open **Instagram**.
2. Next to the gym you want to connect, click **Connect Instagram**.
3. Sign in to Instagram and allow 1club to read your account and publish to it.
4. You come back to 1club, and the gym shows **Active** with your Instagram username.

You can also connect from the gym itself: in **Settings** > **Clubs**, open the gym, and click **Connect Instagram** in its **Social profiles**.

## Show your posts on your website

1. Open your website in the editor and add the **Instagram** section.
2. Place it low on the page, near the footer, where it shows your gym is active without pulling visitors away from booking.
3. Choose how many posts it shows, from 4 to 6, and publish.

The section shows one row of your newest posts, each opening on Instagram. It hides itself when your newest post is more than 90 days old, so a quiet account never makes the gym look quiet.

## Your posts on the member portal

Nothing to set up. Once you connect, the portal's home page shows your newest posts in one row, above your opening hours and location.

## Post a review to Instagram

1. Go to **Marketing** > **Reviews** and open a review.
2. Click **Post on Social**.
3. Under **Social media**, choose **Instagram Feed** or **Instagram Stories**. Change the **Background** if you like.
4. For the feed, check the **Caption**. It starts with the review and the reviewer's public name.
5. Click **Post to Instagram**. When it is posted, click **View post** to see it on Instagram.

Only your gym's own public reviews can be posted. Reviews from Google are never republished.

## Fill a coach profile from Instagram

Independent coaches can bring their Instagram onto their 1club profile.

1. Open your coach profile and, on the **Bio** tab, click **Import from Instagram**. If Instagram isn't connected yet, you connect it first and come straight back.
2. Choose your Instagram picture, your bio, and the photos and videos you want.
3. Click **Import**. Nothing is added until you choose it, and importing the same post twice adds it once.

## Disconnect

On the **Instagram** page, click **Deactivate** next to the gym. Your posts leave your website and portal, and your Instagram link stays in **Social profiles**. To remove 1club's access on Instagram's side too, do it in the Instagram app under **Settings and privacy** > **Website permissions** > **Apps and websites**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Instagram won't connect** - check the account is a Business or Creator account, not a personal one.
- **"This connection has ended"** - Instagram stopped accepting the connection, for example after you removed 1club's access or changed the account type. Click **Reconnect**.
- **The Instagram section doesn't appear on your website** - the gym isn't connected, or its newest post is more than 90 days old.
- **A reel shows only its cover** - Instagram doesn't share the video file for reels with licensed music. The cover still opens the reel on Instagram.
- **There is no Post to Instagram button** - the gym isn't connected, **Facebook** or **LinkedIn** is chosen under **Social media**, or your role can't post reviews.
- **"Reconnect Instagram to let 1club post to it"** - publishing wasn't allowed when you connected. Click **Reconnect** and allow it.
