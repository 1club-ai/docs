The Salto KS integration connects 1club to Salto KS, Salto's cloud access control system. Each area in a gym becomes a Salto access group holding the locks you choose, and a booking or check-in puts the member in that group for its time window. Members open the lock with their phone, using a digital key they activate by signing in with their 1club account. An owner or admin adds the Salto account 1club signs in as, then sets up each gym.

## When to use it

Use it when your gym's doors run on Salto KS locks and you want access to follow what members book, with no cards to hand out.

Typical uses:

- **Studio doors that open only for class time** - a yoga studio's room opens for each booked member shortly before class and closes again after, from the member's phone.
- **Courts with no front desk** - a padel gym's court gates open for the players who booked the slot, and the gym's opening hours stay enforced even if the internet goes down.
- **A large membership on a small Salto subscription** - only the members inside their booking window take a Salto seat, so thousands of members can share a subscription sized for the people actually in the building.

## How it works

- **1club signs in as your Salto account.** You add a Salto KS account, a system user that administers your sites, and 1club manages access with it. 1club's own connection to Salto is already set up, so the account is all you enter.
- **Areas become access groups.** **Sync areas** creates a Salto access group for each area, holding the locks you choose.
- **Access follows the schedule.** A booking or a check-in puts the member in the area's group for its own window plus the access buffer.
- **Doors open from the phone.** Members activate a digital key in the 1club mobile app by signing in with their 1club account, then open the lock over Bluetooth.
- **Seats follow who is inside.** A member takes a Salto subscription seat only while their access is open.

### The access window

The **Access buffer (minutes)** sets how early a member can get in before a booking starts, and how long they keep access after it ends. It can be 15-120 minutes.

A check-in grants access immediately and keeps it until the end of the booking plus the buffer.

### Opening hours backstop

With **Limit access to opening hours** on, 1club sends the gym's opening hours to Salto. The doors then refuse anyone outside those hours, even if 1club can't be reached. It is on by default. The hours are sent when you click **Sync areas**, so sync again after you change the gym's opening hours or this switch.

### Seats

Salto counts everyone who has access right now against your subscription. Members outside their booking window don't take a seat, so a seat comes free as soon as a member's window closes.

1club only manages the Salto users it created itself. Other integrators' users on the same Salto site are left alone.

## Before you start

- Salto KS is a **paid-plan** feature. On the Free plan and during the trial, its **Activate** and **Configure** buttons stay greyed out until you upgrade.
- Configuring Salto KS requires the **settings.manage.organization** permission. Viewing its settings requires **settings.read.organization**.
- Your gym's locks must be on a site in **Salto KS production**. 1club connects to Salto production only.
- You need a Salto KS account for 1club to sign in as. See [Create a dedicated Salto account for 1club](#create-a-dedicated-salto-account-for-1club).
- Each gym needs at least one active area. See [Areas](/settings/areas).
- A gym can use only one door system at a time. If a gym already uses another provider, such as [Impro](/settings/integrations/access-bridge) or [WebLock](/settings/integrations/weblock), turn that one off for the gym first.
- Members need a login email on their 1club account. Salto links each member by that email, so a member without one can't be given access.

## Create a dedicated Salto account for 1club

1club manages access as a **system user**: a Salto KS account that is an administrator of your sites. Create a new account just for 1club rather than using a staff member's own Salto login.

1. Pick an email your business controls and uses for nothing else, for example `salto-1club@yourgym.com`. A shared mailbox works well, because Salto sends the invitation and any password reset there.
2. Don't use an email that a member or staff member signs in to 1club with. Salto matches people by email, so a shared address mixes the integration up with that person.
3. In Salto KS, have a site administrator invite that email as an **administrator** of every site whose locks 1club should open.
4. Accept the invitation from the mailbox and set a password.

A dedicated account keeps door access working when staff change. If 1club signed in as a person's account, door access would stop syncing as soon as they left, lost their administrator rights, or changed their password. It also makes changes in Salto's activity log easy to trace back to 1club.

If a gym's site belongs to a different Salto customer, for example a landlord or another franchise, ask that site's administrator to invite the same email. If they won't, add their own system user as a second account. See [More than one Salto account](#more-than-one-salto-account).

## Add the account to 1club

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and click **Activate** next to Salto KS, under **Access control**. 1club opens its page. Once it is added, you reach the page from **Configure** under **Active Integrations**.
2. On the **Salto KS accounts** card, click **Add account**.
3. Enter the **System user email** and **System user password** of the account you created.
4. Click **Save account**.

**Save account** tests the account with Salto before keeping it. Salto KS becomes active once an account passes. If Salto refuses the account, nothing is saved and the dialog shows what to fix. See [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

The card shows the Salto environment 1club connects to, each account, and which gyms use it. To change an account's password, click its pencil icon and enter the new one. Leave the password blank to keep the saved one.

## Set up a gym

Pick the gym under **Clubs**. Until it is live, a **Before you turn it on** checklist shows what is left.

1. Under **Connection**, choose the **Site**: the Salto site whose locks are this gym's doors. Sites are listed under the account that administers them, so choosing a site also chooses the account.
2. Set the **Access buffer (minutes)** (15-120).
3. Leave **Limit access to opening hours** on unless you have a reason to turn it off.
4. Click **Save**. The **Locks** lists load once the site is saved.
5. Under **Areas**, choose the **Locks** each area opens, and save.
6. Click **Sync areas**.
7. Turn on **Door access through Salto KS** and confirm with **Turn on**. Members with a booking or check-in get door access within a few minutes.

Each area shows **Access group ready** once it has been synced, or **Not synced** until then. An area opens no doors until you sync areas.

Lock choices reach Salto only when you sync areas. Click **Sync areas** again whenever you change an area's locks, add or rename an area, or change the gym's opening hours.

Use **Test connection** to check the gym's connection. **Test connection** and **Sync areas** are available once the gym is saved, and only while there are no unsaved changes.

## Activity

The **Activity** section shows:

- **Members with access** - members who can open the gym's doors right now.
- **Pending updates** - changes 1club still has to send to Salto.
- **Last door update** - when 1club last changed access in Salto.
- Any members whose access couldn't be updated, with the reason. These retry automatically.

Access syncs every 5 minutes. Click **Sync now** to update the gym straight away.

If Salto refuses a member because the subscription has no free seat, free a seat or ask your installer to enlarge the subscription.

## More than one Salto account

A gym's site can only be reached by a system user that administers it. If your gyms' sites are administered by different Salto customers, add one account for each with **Add account**. The **Site** list then shows every account's sites, grouped by account. If one account can't list its sites, the other accounts' sites still show, with a warning for the one that failed.

Each system user can be added once per organization.

## Turn a gym off or move it to another site

Turning off **Door access through Salto KS** and confirming with **Turn off** withdraws the gym's access in Salto within a few minutes. Your settings stay saved, so you can turn it back on later.

To move a gym to a different site or account, choose the new **Site** and save. If members have access right now, 1club asks you to confirm with **Move club**. It removes everyone from the old site first, then moves the gym. Choose locks and click **Sync areas** for the new site afterwards.

## Delete an account

Click the bin icon next to an account and confirm. An account no gym uses is removed straight away.

If gyms use the account, 1club first removes their members from Salto, then sets those gyms back to not set up, then deletes the account. This can't be undone. If Salto can't be reached or a member can't be removed, nothing is deleted and 1club says which gym held it up.

## Deactivate

Click **Deactivate** at the top of the Salto KS page. The confirmation, **Deactivate Salto KS?**, says that Salto KS stops working and its settings are deleted, so you'd need to set it up again to use it. Click **Deactivate** to confirm.

Deactivating withdraws every gym's access in Salto before it removes the accounts and every gym's setup. If Salto can't be reached, or a member can't be removed, deactivation is refused and you can try again later.

Once it is deactivated, you return to **Settings** > **Integrations**.

## Troubleshooting

When Salto refuses a connection, 1club says what failed and how to fix it:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Salto did not accept the system user's email or password.** | Check both, and that the account exists in Salto production. Accounts don't carry over between Salto's test and production environments, so a site administrator has to invite the email in production. |
| **The system user is not an administrator of this site.** | Ask a site administrator in Salto KS to invite the system user's email as an administrator of that site, then test again. |
| **Salto does not know this site.** | The site may have been removed, or it belongs to Salto's test environment. Choose the site again. |
| **Salto KS could not be reached.** | Salto didn't answer or had an error on its side. Try again in a few minutes. |
| **Salto did not accept 1club's connection for this environment.** | The problem is on 1club's side, not your account. Contact 1club support. |
| **Salto does not let 1club sign in as a system user in this environment.** | The problem is on 1club's side, not your account. Contact 1club support. |
| **1club is not yet connected to Salto KS production.** | Contact 1club support. |

Other problems:

- **The page asks you to create an area first** - the gym has no active area. Use **Manage areas** to add one.
- **The Site list is empty** - no account has been added yet, or the account doesn't administer any site. Add the account, or have a site administrator invite it to the site.
- **A site is missing from the list** - the system user isn't an administrator of that site. Have a site administrator invite it.
- **An area opens no doors** - check that it shows **Access group ready**. If it shows **Not synced**, click **Sync areas**.
- **The switch can't be turned on** - the gym already uses another door system, or the checklist isn't finished. Turn the other provider off for this gym, or finish the steps.
- **Door access stopped after a password change** - edit the account and enter the new password.
- **A member can't be given access** - make sure the member has a login email on their 1club account.
