The WhatsApp integration connects your gym's own WhatsApp Business number to 1Club. Once connected, members can message you on WhatsApp and staff reply from the [inbox](/messaging/message-list), booking notifications go out over WhatsApp automatically, and [automations](/marketing/automations) can reach members on the channel they check most. You connect it through Meta's embedded signup - a guided Facebook popup where you pick or create a number and authorize 1Club - so there are no tokens or API keys to copy by hand.

## Why connect WhatsApp

Members read WhatsApp far more reliably than email, so booking confirmations, reminders, changes, and cancellations land in the app members already have open, with no extra setup per message. 1Club handles the parts WhatsApp is strict about: it creates the message templates Meta requires in your WhatsApp Business account when you connect, tracks opt-in, honors STOP automatically, and only sends business-initiated messages to members who have agreed. That protects your number's quality rating with Meta. The connection is stored securely, and 1Club never shows the access token back to you.

Typical uses:

- **Answering trial enquiries** - prospects message your number about a beginner class, the front desk replies from the inbox inside the 24-hour window and books them in, and because they are now an opted-in contact, their booking confirmation goes out over WhatsApp too.
- **Telling members about a schedule change** - when a class moves rooms and more than 24 hours have passed since most members last messaged, 1Club sends the approved booking-change template to affected members automatically, and staff handle the replies.
- **One number for the whole front desk** - every conversation lands in the shared inbox, where any staff member can reply, rather than on one person's phone.

## Before you start

- WhatsApp is a **paid-plan** feature. On the Free plan and during the trial, its **Activate** and **Configure** buttons stay greyed out until you upgrade.
- Connecting and managing templates requires the **settings.manage.organization** permission. Viewing the status requires **settings.read.organization**.
- You need a Facebook Business account and a phone number you can verify for WhatsApp. You can create a new number during the embedded signup.

<Note>
  WhatsApp embedded signup must be enabled on the 1Club server for your
  deployment. If it isn't, the page shows "WhatsApp isn't available on this
  server yet." instead of the connect button. Contact 1Club support if you
  expect it to be available.
</Note>

## Connect WhatsApp

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations** and click **Activate** on the **WhatsApp** card, under **Tools**. The **WhatsApp** page opens.
2. Under **Connect WhatsApp**, click **Connect with WhatsApp**.
3. A Facebook popup opens. Sign in to your Facebook Business account and follow Meta's steps to select or create a WhatsApp Business account and phone number, then authorize 1Club.
4. When you finish the popup, 1Club completes the connection: it subscribes to your WhatsApp Business account so inbound messages reach your inbox, registers the phone number if it isn't registered yet, and creates the standard message templates in your account.
5. A **WhatsApp connected** message appears, the status chip changes to **Active**, and the **Message templates** card appears below.

<Note>
  If you close the popup or deny access, you see **Connection cancelled** and
  nothing changes. If Meta reports a problem, you see **Couldn't connect
  WhatsApp** with Meta's reason. Click **Connect with WhatsApp** again to retry.
</Note>

## Connection status

The status chip on the **Connect WhatsApp** card shows one of:

- **Not activated** - no WhatsApp connection exists yet.
- **Inactive** - a connection exists but isn't active. Use **Connect with WhatsApp** to authorize the number again.
- **Active** - your number is connected and can send and receive messages from the inbox.

Once connected, the button changes to **Reconnect**, which reruns the embedded signup so you can refresh the connection or switch to a different number. 1Club renews the connection's access on its own, so you rarely need to reconnect unless you are changing numbers.

## Two-way messaging and the 24-hour window

- Members who message your WhatsApp Business number arrive in the [inbox](/messaging/message-list). If the sender isn't already a contact, 1Club creates one from their WhatsApp profile name and number, so the conversation is attached to a contact record.
- WhatsApp allows **free-form replies only within a 24-hour customer-service window** that opens each time someone messages you. Inside that window, staff can reply with any text - no template needed.
- Outside the window, WhatsApp only allows approved templates. 1Club handles this for you: when you send from the inbox, through an automation, or with an AI-drafted reply outside the window, it sends the approved general-message template carrying your text.

## Automatic booking notifications

Once WhatsApp is connected, these member notifications are delivered over WhatsApp, using 1Club's approved templates, to members who are reachable on WhatsApp and have opted in:

- **Booking confirmation** - sent when a booking is made.
- **Booking reminder** - sent ahead of the class or session.
- **Booking change** - sent when a booking's details change, such as its time or place.
- **Booking cancellation** - sent when a booking is cancelled.

More templates (waitlist spot available, membership expiration reminder, review request, and a general message) are used by the waitlist, [automations](/marketing/automations), and other flows. Members choose which notification channels they receive in their notification settings, and turning on the WhatsApp channel counts as opting in.

## Message templates

WhatsApp requires any message you _start_ (rather than reply to within the 24-hour window) to use a template Meta has reviewed and approved. That covers booking notifications and automations. **1Club creates and manages this template set for you** - you don't write them, and you can't edit them in 1Club.

When you connect, 1Club creates its standard template set in your WhatsApp Business account: booking confirmation, cancellation, reminder, and change, waitlist spot available, membership expiration reminder, review request, and a general message. Each one is created in English, plus each language your gym uses. Meta then reviews each template and language separately, usually within a few hours. A notification uses a template only once Meta has approved it.

The **Message templates** card is a read-only status view. It lists each template with its **Notification**, **Template** name, **Language**, and **Status**:

- **Approved** - ready to send.
- **Pending Meta review** - created, waiting for Meta's approval.
- **Rejected by Meta** - Meta didn't approve it, so it isn't used.
- **Not set up yet** - the template doesn't exist in your WhatsApp account. Use **Re-provision** to create it.
- **Doesn't match 1club's template** - a template with the same name exists in your WhatsApp account, but its content differs from the one 1Club manages, so 1Club won't use it. Delete it in WhatsApp Manager, then click **Re-provision** to create 1Club's version.

1Club refreshes these statuses from Meta once a day. Two buttons let you act straight away:

- **Sync status** - refreshes the approval statuses from Meta now. If Meta doesn't return a template's content, 1Club can't check it and leaves it as it was, so try again shortly. If the refresh itself fails, the statuses shown may be out of date until you run **Sync status** again.
- **Re-provision** - creates every template from 1Club's standard set that is missing from your WhatsApp account, then refreshes the statuses. Each new template goes to Meta for review before it can be used. Run it after you add a language to your gym, so the templates for that language are created. It is safe to run repeatedly, and it never changes a template that already exists under the same name.

Because **Re-provision** only creates missing templates, it doesn't resubmit a template Meta rejected or replace one that doesn't match. Delete that template in WhatsApp Manager first, then click **Re-provision** to create it again.

<Note>
  These WhatsApp templates are separate from [Templates](/messaging/templates)
  under **Settings > Communication > Templates**, which holds your custom and
  system message content for email, SMS, and inbox sends. The WhatsApp templates
  here are the Meta-approved formats that let business-initiated messages go out
  after the 24-hour window, and they aren't editable.
</Note>

## Consent and opt-out

WhatsApp penalizes businesses that message people who didn't agree to it, so 1Club gates every **business-initiated** send (notifications, automations, and messages sent outside the 24-hour window) on the member's consent, and honors opt-out automatically.

**How a member opts in:**

- They message your WhatsApp Business number. Any inbound message counts as an opt-in under WhatsApp's rules.
- They send a re-subscribe keyword such as **START**.
- They turn on the WhatsApp channel in their notification settings.
- They agree to WhatsApp messages when they enter their own phone number while signing up.

**How a member opts out:**

- They send a standard opt-out keyword as their whole message - **STOP**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, **CANCEL**, and similar. 1Club records the opt-out and blocks all business-initiated sends to them, even inside the 24-hour window, until they opt back in with **START**. An ordinary message after an opt-out doesn't opt them back in.

Replies inside the 24-hour window are never blocked by this gate. If a member messaged you, you can always reply.

### Who is reachable on WhatsApp

1Club learns which members are actually on WhatsApp from real signals: an inbound message from them, or a delivered or read outbound message. Contacts confirmed reachable show a small **WhatsApp badge** in the inbox and contact lists, so staff can see at a glance who can be reached on the channel. If Meta reports that a number isn't on WhatsApp, 1Club clears the signal. Business-initiated notifications only go to members who are both reachable and opted in.

## Automations and AI replies

- **Automations** can send on WhatsApp as one of their message channels. Outside the 24-hour window they use the approved general-message template automatically.
- **AI-drafted inbox replies** ([Generate with AI](/messaging/message-list)) work on WhatsApp threads like any other channel, and the same window and template rules apply.

## Deactivate

1. On the **WhatsApp** page, click **Deactivate** at the top of the page.
2. Confirm in the **Deactivate WhatsApp?** dialog. It warns that the integration stops working and its settings are deleted.

1Club removes the connection. New inbound messages no longer reach your inbox, and staff can't send from your number until you connect again. The message templates stay in your WhatsApp Business account. You return to **Settings > Integrations**. If deactivation fails, the reason is shown inside the dialog.

## Worked examples

**Martial-arts gym fielding trial enquiries.** A muay thai gym connects its WhatsApp Business number and shares it on its social profiles. Prospects message asking about the beginner class. Because that message opens the 24-hour window, the front desk replies from the inbox and books them in. The prospect is now an opted-in contact, so their booking confirmation goes out over WhatsApp too.

**Yoga studio confirming a schedule change.** A studio's morning reformer class moves rooms. More than 24 hours have passed since most members last messaged, so 1Club sends the approved booking-change template to affected members automatically, and staff handle the replies as they come in.

**Padel gym running one number across the team.** A padel gym connects a single WhatsApp Business number, so several front-desk staff pick up member conversations from the shared inbox rather than messages landing on one person's phone.

## Tips and best practices

- Reply inside the 24-hour window whenever you can. Free-form replies feel more personal and don't depend on template approval.
- Share the same WhatsApp Business number on your website and social profiles, so messages land in the inbox and members opt in naturally.
- After connecting, give Meta time to approve the templates (usually a few hours). Use **Sync status** to check. Business-initiated sends only work once the relevant template is **Approved**.
- Use **Reconnect** rather than deactivating and connecting again when you switch numbers or refresh access.
- After you add a language to your gym, click **Re-provision** so members who use it get templates in their language.

## Troubleshooting

- **"WhatsApp isn't available on this server yet."** - embedded signup isn't enabled for your deployment. Contact 1Club support.
- **The Activate button is greyed out** - WhatsApp is a paid-plan feature. Upgrade from the Free plan or the trial.
- **"WhatsApp connect isn't ready yet"** - the Facebook popup hasn't finished loading. Wait a moment and click **Connect with WhatsApp** again.
- **"Authorized, but the popup didn't return your WhatsApp account details"** - the Facebook popup finished without sending back the account information. Click **Connect with WhatsApp** and complete the flow again.
- **Connection cancelled** - you closed or denied the popup. Click **Connect with WhatsApp** to retry.
- **Members message but nothing reaches the inbox** - check that the status is **Active**. If it shows **Inactive**, click **Connect with WhatsApp** to authorize the number again.
- **Notifications aren't going out over WhatsApp** - check that the relevant template shows **Approved** (click **Sync status**), that the member is reachable (WhatsApp badge) and opted in, and that they haven't sent STOP.
- **A template shows Rejected by Meta** - **Re-provision** doesn't resubmit it. Delete it in WhatsApp Manager, then click **Re-provision** to create it again.
- **A template shows Doesn't match 1club's template** - the template in your WhatsApp account isn't the one 1Club manages, so it won't be used. Delete it in WhatsApp Manager, then click **Re-provision**.
- **"Setup isn't finished, so templates can't be managed. Reconnect WhatsApp."** - the connection is missing its WhatsApp Business account details. Click **Reconnect** to finish setting it up.
