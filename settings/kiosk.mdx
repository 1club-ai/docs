1club has two self-service screens for a tablet at your gym. The **Check-in App** is a touch-first check-in screen for the door: members find themselves by name, QR code or PIN and are checked in, with a clear result for each. The **kiosk** does check-in too, and also lets visitors browse and book classes, areas and plans. An owner or admin turns each one on per gym, a staff member signs the device in once, and it stays on that one gym.

## When to use which

Both take routine arrivals off the front desk. A member never signs in to an account on the shared device, and cases that can't be completed unattended send the member to the front desk, so staff only handle the exceptions.

Typical uses:

- **Self check-in at a busy entrance** - a CrossFit box puts the Check-in App on a tablet by the door. Members hold up the QR code from their 1club App and are checked in without touching the screen. Staff step in only when a payment is due.
- **Class check-in in the studio** - a yoga studio runs the Check-in App with **Find class** first: members tap the class starting next, then their name on the class list.
- **An open gym with no classes** - a weights gym uses **Open Gym** mode, so members check in on their membership with no class list at all.
- **Booking an area on the spot** - at a padel gym, the kiosk shows an area tile where a member picks an area and time slot, then scans a QR code to finish the booking on their phone.

| | Check-in App | Kiosk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it's for | Self check-in at the door | Check-in plus browsing and booking |
| Runs on | The **1club Check-in** app for iPad and Android tablets, or a browser | A browser |
| Members identify by | Name (always), QR code from the 1club App, PIN | QR code, access card, PIN, name search, class roster |
| Booking and buying | Not on the device | Classes, areas and plans, finished on the member's phone |
| Settings | **Settings** > **Check-in & Access** > **Check-in App** | **Settings** > **Check-in & Access** > **Kiosk App** |

Each is turned on separately per gym, so a gym can run either or both. The rest of this page covers the kiosk first, then the [Check-in App](#check-in-app).

## What the kiosk does

The kiosk home screen shows a tile for each enabled capability:

- **Check in** - a member identifies themselves and the kiosk records their check-in.
- **Classes** - browse the day's schedule and check in to a class.
- **Plans** - browse plans and subscribe on-site.
- **Products** - browse your product catalog.
- **Area tiles** - one tile per area type you choose to show, leading to area booking.

A member never signs in on the kiosk. Booking and subscription flows finish on the member's own phone by scanning a QR code the kiosk displays, which deep-links into their member portal.

## Setting up a device

The kiosk lives at your organization's kiosk address (for example, `your-org.kiosk.1club.ai`). When only one gym has the kiosk enabled, the address resolves to it automatically; with several enabled, the URL includes the gym's slug.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the kiosk for a gym">
    Go to **Settings** > **Check-in & Access** > **Kiosk App**
    (`/settings/member-experience/kiosk`) and turn on **Enable Kiosk** for the
    gym. Use **Copy** to grab that gym's kiosk URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the URL on the device">
    Open the kiosk URL in the device's browser. Chrome kiosk mode or iPad Guided
    Access keep the device locked to the kiosk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in once">
    Sign in with a staff account. Only users with admin-portal access for that
    organization can set up a kiosk: admin, manager, front desk, instructor, or
    the owner. The device stays signed in afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a gym if prompted">
    When more than one gym has the kiosk enabled, choose the gym after signing
    in. A single enabled gym skips the picker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To switch the device to a different gym later, long-press the logo on the kiosk to re-enter setup.

<Note>
  The person who signs in only provisions the device. Members and visitors use
  the kiosk without any account of their own.
</Note>

## Member check-in

From the **Check in** tile (or a class's check-in screen), a member identifies themselves. The kiosk previews what the check-in would use, shows a confirmation screen with the member's name and the entry it will record, and commits it only after staff or the member confirms. An abandoned confirmation clears itself after 30 seconds.

### Identification methods

Which methods appear depends on your [Access control](/settings/access-control) identification methods and the kiosk toggles below.

| Method          | How it works                                                                                           | Enabled by                                           |
| :-------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| **QR code**     | The kiosk camera or a connected scanner reads the member's QR code.                                    | Access control identification method **QR Code**     |
| **Access card** | The member taps or scans a physical card.                                                              | Access control identification method **Access Card** |
| **PIN code**    | The member enters the last 4 digits of their PIN on the on-screen keypad.                              | Access control identification method **PIN Code**    |
| **Phone QR**    | The kiosk displays a static QR that the member scans with their phone to open their portal.            | Kiosk toggle **Enable check-in with QR**             |
| **Search**      | The member types their name and picks themselves from the results.                                     | Kiosk toggle **Enable search to check in**           |
| **List**        | On a class check-in screen, the class's booked, not-yet-checked-in attendees are listed and the member taps their name. | Kiosk toggle **Enable class roster check-in** |

See [Access cards](/members/member-cards) for the QR, card, and PIN credentials members present.

### What a check-in resolves to

The kiosk mirrors the admin check-in rules and resolves the member to one of these entries:

- **Booking** - the member has a confirmed booking whose check-in window is open at this gym.
- **Membership** - no booking, but the member holds an eligible membership for general entry. If the plan limits sessions, the confirmation screen shows uses remaining and the visit decrements it.
- **Free class walk-in** - the member walks in to a free class.
- **Membership class walk-in** - the member walks in to a class covered by an eligible membership.

If the member is already checked in for that booking or class, the kiosk shows a warning instead of recording a duplicate.

### When the front desk is required

Some cases cannot be completed unattended and the kiosk stops the member with a front-desk-required result. Staff handle these from the admin. This happens when:

- The member has an active membership, but it has an unpaid balance, a scope that does not apply, or no uses left.
- The only way in is a pay-per-visit (direct payment) or an [external program](/settings/external-programs), which needs operator validation.
- A booking has an outstanding balance that must be paid first.

For the full matrix of when a member gets in, what payment is enforced, and how walk-ins are handled, see [Check-in models](/reference/check-in-models).

## Self-enrolling a credential

When credential enrollment is enabled in [Access control](/settings/access-control), a member can register their own access card at the kiosk. The member first authenticates (for example by scanning their phone QR), then taps or scans the new card to enroll it. If enrollment is turned off, the kiosk tells the member it is not available.

## Browsing and booking

### Classes

The **Classes** tile shows the schedule by day. A member taps a class to see its details, then starts check-in from there.

### Areas

Each area type you choose to show appears as its own tile on the home screen. A member picks the area and time slot, reviews the booking summary, and scans the displayed QR code with their phone to complete the booking in their portal.

### Plans

The **Plans** tile lists the plans available at that gym with pricing and session details. A member taps a plan, reviews it, and scans the displayed QR code to subscribe from their phone.

### Products

The **Products** tile displays your product catalog, grouped by category with prices, for members to browse on-screen.

## Kiosk settings

Configure the kiosk per gym under **Settings** > **Check-in & Access** > **Kiosk App** (`/settings/member-experience/kiosk`). A live preview on the left reflects each change. Enable **Enable Kiosk** for a gym first to reveal the rest:

| Setting                            | What it controls                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Enable check-in on home screen** | Show the **Check in** tile. On by default.                                        |
| **Enable check-in with QR**        | Show the static phone QR members scan to open their portal. Off by default.       |
| **Enable search to check in**      | Let members find themselves by typing their name. Off by default.                 |
| **Enable class roster check-in**   | Show the **List** of booked attendees on a class check-in screen. Off by default. |
| **Show classes**                   | Show the **Classes** tile. On by default.                                         |
| **Show plans**                     | Show the **Plans** tile. On by default.                                           |
| **Show products**                  | Show the **Products** tile. Off by default.                                       |
| **Show full member names**         | When off, names are shortened for privacy, for example "John K.". Off by default. |
| **Area types to show**             | The area types that appear as home-screen tiles. Empty means none.                |
| **Instructor types to show**       | The instructor types the kiosk surfaces. Empty means none.                        |
| **Background image or video**      | Media shown full-screen behind the home screen, with a darkening overlay.         |

## Check-in App

The **Check-in App** is a self-service check-in screen for a tablet at your gym's door. A member identifies themselves and is checked in on the spot, and the screen shows the result in a colour anyone can read from a distance:

- **Green** - they're checked in ("You're in").
- **Amber** - they're in, but a payment is still due ("One quick stop - payment required"). The visit is recorded as unpaid for the front desk to collect.
- **Red** - the tablet couldn't check them in and sends them to the front desk.

### How a check-in works

1. The member identifies themselves: by typing their name (always available), by holding the QR code from their [1club App](/settings/mobile-app) up to the tablet camera, or by entering their PIN.
2. A member with an upcoming booking at the gym is checked in the moment they identify themselves.
3. Without a booking, the member picks from the classes starting in the next hour (check-in opens 1 hour before a class). Your [booking policy](/settings/booking-policies) decides whether their membership covers it, or an unpaid payment is recorded for the front desk.
4. In **Open Gym** mode, the member checks in on their general membership with no class list at all.

With **Find class** first in the user journey, the member taps a class and then their name on its **Class list** instead. A member who is already checked in for the day is told so rather than checked in twice.

If your gym requires a [waiver](/settings/waivers) that the member hasn't signed, the tablet shows it and they accept it to check in. Signing on the tablet needs their QR code or full PIN; a member identified by name only, or a minor whose guardian must sign, is sent to the front desk. A **Help** button on the screen explains the steps to members.

### Set up a tablet

Go to **Settings** > **Check-in & Access** > **Check-in App**. The page lists your gyms with how many are live, and a **Set up a tablet** guide at the top.

1. Turn on **Enable Check-in App** for the gym.
2. Install **1club Check-in** on the tablet. It is available for iPad and Android tablets: scan the code in the guide with the tablet's camera, or open the App Store or Google Play.
3. Open the app and enter your **Organization short name**, shown in the guide with a copy button. If your organization has no short name yet, the guide says so; set one in [organization settings](/settings/organization).
4. Sign in once with a staff account. The tablet then stays on the check-in screen. Only admins, managers, front desk staff and instructors (and the owner) can set up a tablet.

Prefer a browser? Click **Copy check-in link** next to the gym, open the link on the tablet, and sign in with a staff account. Chrome kiosk mode or iPad Guided Access keep the tablet on the check-in screen.

To sign a tablet out, delete and reinstall 1club Check-in: the check-in screen has no sign-out button, so members can't sign it out. Removing the staff member's access to your organization also signs the tablet out the next time it opens. In a browser, open the gym's link and sign in with another staff account.

### Settings

Click **Show settings** on a live gym to configure it. A live preview beside the settings shows the app for that gym.

| Setting | What it controls |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Check-in modes** | What members can check in to: **Classes**, **Open Gym**, or both. With both, members first pick **Gym** or **Classes**. At least one is required. |
| **Reset after inactivity (seconds)** | How long the screen waits with no interaction before it returns to the start. 10 to 600 seconds. |
| **Show splash screen** | A branded attract screen between visitors ("Tap anywhere to check in"), with an optional **Background image or video**. Video plays muted, on a loop. |
| **Show full member names** | When off, names are shortened for privacy, for example "John K.". |
| **Identification methods** | Name search is always on. Turn on **Check in with QR code** (members hold their 1club App QR code up to the tablet camera) and **Check in with PIN** as needed. The camera runs while the app is open, so QR works best with the splash screen off. |
| **Method order** | With both QR code and PIN on, drag to choose which members see first. |
| **User journey** | With classes offered, drag **User authenticate** and **Find class** to choose whether members identify themselves or pick a class first. |

### Troubleshooting the Check-in App

- **"Check-in isn't available here"** - the Check-in App is off for that gym. Turn on **Enable Check-in App** for it.
- **"Club not found"** - the short name typed into the app doesn't match your organization. Check it in the **Set up a tablet** guide.
- **"No classes in the next hour"** - classes appear an hour before they start. Turn on **Open Gym** if members should be able to check in without a class.
- **QR scanning doesn't start** - the tablet's camera is blocked or missing. Allow camera access for the app, or turn the splash screen off so the camera stays on.
