The 1club member app is the phone app your members use to book, buy, and check in at your gym. It is live on the App Store and Google Play, so there is nothing for you to build or publish. Owners and front-desk staff use this settings page to see what the app offers and to share the download links with members.

## Why share it

Members who have the app book and pay on their own, and check in with a digital QR code instead of asking at the desk.

Typical uses:

- **A QR code at reception** - a climbing gym prints the app's QR code next to the front desk, so new members install the app while they sign up.
- **Links in the welcome email** - a yoga studio pastes the store links into its welcome email template.
- **Faster check-in** - a padel gym asks members to install the app so they can show their QR code at the door. See [Access cards](/members/member-cards).

## Open the page

Go to **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Mobile App**. A phone preview sits beside two sections.

**What members can do** lists what the app offers:

- Browse the schedule and book or cancel classes.
- Buy and renew memberships, packs, and drop-ins.
- Check in with a digital QR code.
- Get push notifications for bookings and updates.
- Manage their profile, payment methods, and history.
- Ask the built-in AI assistant for help.

**Share the app with your members** gives you a scannable QR code and direct links to the App Store and Google Play. Add them anywhere: your website, the reception desk, emails, or social channels.

There is nothing to switch on or save on this page.
