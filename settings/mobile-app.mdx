The 1club App is the free phone app your members use to book, pay, check in and keep track of their training at your gym. It is live on the App Store and Google Play, so there is nothing for you to build, publish or switch on: every gym on 1club is in it for its own members. Owners and administrators use **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Mobile App** to see what the app offers and to share the download links.

## Why share it

Members who have the app book and pay on their own, check in with one code instead of asking at the desk, and get your messages and booking updates as push notifications. The app also gives them a reason to come back each week: a weekly streak, a monthly visit goal and their progress at your gym.

Typical uses:

- **A QR code at reception** - a climbing gym prints the app's QR code next to the front desk, so new members install the app while they sign up.
- **Links in the welcome email** - a yoga studio pastes the store links into its welcome email template.
- **Faster check-in** - a padel gym asks members to install the app so they can show their QR code at the desk or hold it up to the [Check-in App](/settings/kiosk#check-in-app). See [Member cards](/members/member-cards).
- **A private members' gym** - a members-only club that isn't on the 1club network still gives every member the app: they sign in and find the gym waiting for them.

## What members do in the app

The app has five tabs.

| Tab          | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **For you**  | Your gym first. It greets the member by name with one clear next step, such as booking a first visit or keeping their weekly streak going, with your next free slot or class one tap away. Their next booking sits under it, with who teaches it. Then their week (the days they came and their streak), what's coming up at your gym, and **Recommended for you**. |
| **Book**     | Your gym's schedule: live class times with who teaches and how many spots are left, and your areas and coaches to book.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Activity** | Their bookings and memberships, at every gym they use.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Explore**  | A map and list of gyms listed on the 1club network, with search and filters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Profile**  | Their access code (**Access**), messages, wallet, documents, notification settings, language and account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

### For you and the member's gym

For you leads with one gym, the member's own (shown at the top with its name and logo). A member of several gyms, or of a gym with several locations, switches it with **Switch club**. Until they pick one, it is the gym of their soonest booking, otherwise their latest past one. It is only a display choice: every booking still belongs to the gym it was made at, and **Activity** shows them all.

Someone with no gym yet sees the 1club network feed instead: events and places to train near them. Other gyms stay out of a new member's way: For you only suggests training elsewhere once the member is settled with you, or asks for it.

**Recommended for you** only lists what is missing for that member, for example:

- **Sign** documents your gym requires (see [Waivers](/settings/waivers)).
- **Set a monthly goal** of visits, and **Aim higher** once it is met.
- **Booking reminders**, to turn on notifications before each booking.

A member can hide a suggestion they don't want.

### Booking and paying

Booking takes two taps: pick a time on the **Book** tab, then confirm in the booking sheet. Your [booking policies](/settings/booking-policies) and prices apply exactly as on your website and member portal, and the member can add the booking to their phone's calendar afterwards.

How the member pays depends on your payment provider:

| Provider                                                                               | In the app                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe)                                                | The booking sheet takes Apple Pay, Google Pay or a saved card.                                                                   |
| [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos) or [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay) | The member taps **Pay** and your provider's secure card page opens inside the app, then returns them to the app with the result. |

This covers everything members buy in the app: class, area and coach bookings, event tickets, memberships and plans, and paying an outstanding balance on a booking they already have. If the provider takes longer than usual to confirm, the app says so plainly instead of reporting a failure, so members aren't nudged into paying twice.

### Progress

The **Progress** screen (tap their week on For you, or the progress row in **Activity**) shows the month against last month, six months of visits, the current and best weekly streak, achievements (1, 10, 50 and 100 visits), and one more block: their belt or level when they are on a [progression track](/settings/progression), otherwise hours played at a gym that only rents areas, otherwise the sports they did this month.

A week counts toward the streak when the member visits at least once by Sunday.

### Check-in and doors

Members check in with one code: their access QR, under **Profile**, works at every gym they belong to. Front-desk staff scan it, or the member holds it up to the [Check-in App](/settings/kiosk#check-in-app) camera. Where you use [door access](/settings/access-control), the same screen shows their PIN, and with a supported smart lock they can open doors with their phone (**Digital key**).

### Push notifications

When a member allows notifications, their phone alerts them when your gym sends them a message, when a waitlist spot opens up and when their schedule changes. Members choose which kinds they get under **Profile** > **Notifications**. Messages you send from the [Inbox](/inbox/overview) and from [automations](/marketing/automations) reach the app as well as email or SMS, depending on the channels you pick.

## How members find your gym

What a member sees depends on your gym's [network level](/marketing/marketplace#private-link-only-or-listed), which you set in **Marketing** > **Network listing**:

- **Your existing members** see your gym as soon as they sign in with the email address or phone number you already hold for them. Nothing to invite, at any level.
- **People you invite from the admin** see your gym as soon as they are invited, and accept the invitation before they buy.
- **New people** at a **Listed** gym find you in **Explore**, open your gym's page (photos, sports, amenities, opening hours, location) and tap **Book now**. Signing in is one email field: someone who already has a 1club account carries straight on, and a first-time visitor goes through your own signup, with your branding, signup documents and required fields. They arrive as a real member, with a contact record your automations and lists expect.
- **New people** at a **Private** gym can't find it in Explore. Share your gym's QR code or link instead: they book a first visit on your website or member portal, and the gym is in their app from then on. See [Bring people in with your gym's QR code](/marketing/marketplace#bring-people-in-with-your-gyms-qr-code).

To send the link to someone already on your member list, open their profile, click **...** and choose **Send invite link**.

<Note>
  A gym only receives new members from Explore while it is listed on the
  1club network. Unpublishing your listing stops new people from finding you
  there, but your own members keep the app.
</Note>

## The Mobile App settings page

Go to **Settings** > **Member Apps** > **Mobile App**. A phone preview sits beside two sections.

- **What members can do** - a summary of the app, for staff who are asked about it at the desk.
- **Share the app with your members** - a scannable QR code (**Scan to download**) and direct links to the App Store and Google Play. Print the code for reception, or add the links to your website, emails and social channels.

There is nothing to switch on or save on this page. To change what members see in the app, change it at the source: your [plans](/sales/plans), [classes](/schedule/classes), [booking policies](/settings/booking-policies), [Brand Book](/settings/organization#brand-book) and [network listing](/marketing/marketplace).

## Troubleshooting

**A member signed in but doesn't see the gym.** They probably signed in with a different email or phone number from the one on their member record. Check their profile in **Members**, or send them your gym's link with **Send invite link** so they book with you once.

**A member can't pay by card in the app.** Card payments need [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe), [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos) or [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay) connected for the gym. Apple Pay and Google Pay are offered with Stripe only.

**A member doesn't get push notifications.** They need to allow notifications for 1club in the phone's settings, and keep the kind of notification on under **Profile** > **Notifications** in the app.

**Someone can't find our gym in Explore.** Explore shows listed gyms only. A private or link-only gym doesn't appear there; share your QR code or link instead.
