Notification settings control which categories of notifications you personally receive and on which channels they reach you. A **notification** is the system's record of something that happened, addressed to a person ("a member booked a court", "a renewal payment failed"). It is delivered through one or more **channels** - App, email, or SMS - and which channels fire for you, per category, is exactly what this page governs.

These preferences are per user: each staff member sets their own, starting from the defaults an admin sets for their role (see [Role defaults](#role-defaults)). They do not change what the system generates, only how it reaches you.

## When to use it

Tune these so the alerts that matter reach you on the channel you actually check, and the rest stay quiet. A studio owner might keep new memberships and signups on while leaving every class booking off, an instructor keeps the alerts about their own classes, and a manager who prefers email switches categories to **Email** only. See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for these setups.

## Managing your preferences

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My Notifications**. The dialog has two tabs: **Notifications**, your notification feed, and **Configure**, where you set your preferences.

On **Configure** you see:

1. **A note** that you are not notified about bookings, cancellations, and other changes you make yourself. Everything someone else does still reaches you.
2. **Browser notifications** - a switch that shows alerts on this device even when 1Club is not the active tab. It applies to the current browser only. If the browser blocks notifications for the site, allow them in the browser settings first.
3. **One row per category**, with the category label, a short description, a switch to turn the category on or off for you, and a **Channels** selector (shown only when the category is on) where you pick the delivery channels.

Changes save immediately when you toggle a switch or change channels - there is no separate save button. A confirmation toast appears on success. Removing every channel from a category turns the category off.

## Notification categories

The categories fall into two groups: your own personal activity, and staff alerts about what members do.

### Personal categories (always available)

These concern your own account and are offered to every staff member:

| Category | Label | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `message` | My Inbox | A message arrives in a conversation you are part of. |
| `task` | My Tasks | A task is assigned to you, or one of yours falls due. |
| `booking` | My Bookings | A booking of your own is made, moved, or cancelled. |
| `payment` | Payments | Payments of your own, for example a payment confirmation receipt. |
| `progression` | Progression | You reach a new level or earn an award. |

### Staff-alert categories (shown when relevant to you)

These notify staff about member actions. You only see a category if it can actually route to you (see [Which categories you see](#which-categories-you-see)):

| Category | Label | Fires when | Routes to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `billing` | Bill Runs | A scheduled bill run finishes, with what it collected and what it could not | Admins, managers |
| `user_billing` | User Billing | A member's renewal payment fails | Admins, managers |
| `user_booking` | User Bookings | A member books or cancels | Admins, managers |
| `class_instructor` | Class Bookings | A member books or cancels a class | The class's instructors |
| `user_membership` | User Memberships | A member buys a membership | Admins, managers |
| `user_message` | User Messages | A member sends an inbound message to the gym | The location's managers, otherwise admins, managers, and front desk |
| `user_review` | User Reviews | A member leaves a review | Admins and managers; the reviewed session's instructors |
| `user_signup` | User Signups | A new member signs up themselves | Admins, managers |
| `user_checkin` | User Check-ins | A member's check-in fails | Admins, managers, front desk |

Instructors no longer receive **User Messages** alerts. An instructor hears about a message only when a member writes to them directly.

<Note>
Per-event booking alerts for a specific event are gated by that event's own "notify staff" setting, not by a toggle here. When an organizer turns that on, the alert always reaches admins and managers regardless of their personal **User Bookings** preference, so it does not appear as a separate switch on this page. Bookings made through the 1Club network work the same way: they always notify the gym's admins and managers (or the location's managers, when it has them), whatever their **User Bookings** setting.
</Note>

### Which categories you see

The list is filtered to what you can receive:

- **Personal categories** (My Inbox, My Tasks, My Bookings, Payments, Progression) always appear.
- **Role-based staff categories** appear when your role is in the alert's recipient roles. An admin or manager sees Bill Runs, User Billing, User Bookings, User Memberships, User Signups, and so on; front desk sees User Messages and User Check-ins.
- **Instructor categories** (Class Bookings, and instructor reviews) appear when you have an instructor profile, so you're notified about your own classes and sessions.
- **Locked categories** are hidden. If an admin turned off **Allow users to change** for a category in your role's defaults, it no longer appears on your list and you always get the role's setting.

Owners are treated as admins for relevance, so they see every category.

## Channels

A notification can be delivered on any of these channels:

- **App** - Reaches you inside 1Club: the notification center in the dashboard, plus an alert on any phone or browser where you have allowed notifications. These are one channel, not two. Which of your devices light up depends on what you have connected, not on anything you pick here.
- **Email** - Sent through SendGrid, using your organization's branding.
- **SMS** - A text message from your gym's own [SMS sender](/settings/sms) to the phone number on your profile.

In **My Notifications** you select **App** and **Email** per category. SMS is not offered there: an admin can add it as a channel for a role under [Role defaults](#role-defaults), and it then reaches users of that role who have a phone number on their profile.

<Note>
**App** was previously two separate channels, **In-app** and **Push**. They were merged because they never really were separate: switching off In-app did not stop the phone alert. Anything you had set to In-app is now set to **App**, so you may start seeing device alerts you did not get before. To stop browser alerts while keeping the notification center, turn off **Browser notifications** on the **Configure** tab (it applies to that browser only). On a phone, turn off notifications for 1Club in the phone's settings.
</Note>

### How channels resolve

For each category, the effective channels are the ones you selected, intersected with the channels that category actually supports - you never receive a channel you didn't ask for. Beyond that:

- **Category off** - You receive nothing for that category, on any channel.
- **On-by-default categories with no saved preference** - You receive them on their default channels (App and Email).
- **Opt-in categories** (User Bookings, My Bookings, and User Check-ins) - You receive nothing until you enable them; they start off by design to avoid noise from high-volume booking and check-in activity.

<Note>
Deleted, invited (invitation not yet accepted), and inactive accounts are never notified on any channel, regardless of preferences. This is a structural safeguard, not something you configure.
</Note>

## Role defaults

An admin sets the starting point for everyone in a role under **Settings** > **Organization** > **Notifications**. Pick a role from the **Roles** list (each shows its user count and whether it is **Default** or **Customized**), and for each notification category set:

- **The switch** - whether the category is on for the role.
- **Channels** - which channels it uses: **App**, **Email**, or **SMS**. A category that is on needs at least one channel.
- **Allow users to change** - when off, the category is **Locked**: users of that role no longer see it on their own **My Notifications** list and always get the role's setting.

Save your changes before you use the other actions:

- **Apply to all** - next to each category, replaces that category's setting for everyone in the role, including people who changed it themselves. It asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.
- **Reset to Default** - removes your configuration for the role and returns it to the built-in defaults. Users who changed a setting themselves keep their own choice.

Role defaults only reach users who have not changed that setting themselves. A note under a category says how many users have their own setting and will not get the default; use **Apply to all** to replace theirs.

## Staff notification routing

Staff alerts about member actions are resolved and delivered automatically. Understanding the routing helps you set expectations for your team:

- **By role** - Alerts like New booking, New membership, and New signup go to every active user whose role matches (admins and managers for most; front desk is also included for inbound messages and failed check-ins).
- **By location** - An inbound message to a specific location goes to that location's managers when it has any, and otherwise to the organization's admins, managers, and front desk.
- **By instructor** - Class-booking and instructor-review alerts resolve to the instructors of the specific class or session involved: the directly assigned instructor for one-to-one sessions, plus the class's instructors for class bookings. Only active instructors with an active linked account are reachable.
- **Not about yourself** - The person who made a change is left out of the alert about it. An admin who cancels a class is not told they cancelled it, while everyone else on the alert still is.
- **Deduplicated** - If one person matches more than one rule for the same event (for example an admin who also teaches the booked class), they receive a single notification, not one per rule.
- **Per-recipient channels** - Each recipient's own notification preferences decide which channels fire for them. One person may get a new booking in the app while another gets it by email.

Delivery per channel is independent: an email failure doesn't suppress the App notification, and vice versa.

## Worked examples

### Yoga studio owner who wants signal, not noise

You run a busy pilates and yoga studio and don't want a ping for every class booking.

- Leave **User Bookings** off (it starts off anyway).
- Keep **User Memberships** and **User Signups** on with **App** and **Email** so you see new members and new revenue.
- Keep **My Inbox** on so member replies reach you.

### Instructor at a climbing gym

You teach classes and want to know about your own sessions only.

- **Class Bookings** appears because you have an instructor profile - keep it on to be told when someone books your class.
- **User Reviews** appears too - keep it on to see reviews of your sessions.
- You won't see admin-only categories like User Signups, because they never route to you.

### Front desk at a racket-sports gym

You triage member enquiries at reception.

- **User Messages** appears for your role - keep it on with **App** so inbound member emails and messages surface immediately at the desk. A member's email reply to a message you sent alerts in the app only, never by email, because your gym's mailbox already has the reply.
- Turn on **User Bookings** if reception also tracks new court bookings; leave it off if that's noise for you.
- Turn on **User Check-ins** if you want to hear when a member's check-in fails, so you can walk over and help.

### Studio manager who prefers email over app notifications

You'd rather everything arrive by email.

- For each staff category you care about, open its **Channels** selector and choose **Email** only, removing **App**.
- Those categories then skip the notification center and your devices, and land in your inbox instead.

## Tips & best practices

- **Start from the defaults.** Most categories are on with App and Email out of the box. Turn things off rather than trying to enable everything.
- **Opt in to booking and check-in alerts deliberately.** User Bookings, My Bookings, and User Check-ins are off by design; enable them only if you actually want per-booking or per-check-in pings.
- **Use channels to match how you work.** Front-desk staff often prefer App; owners who live in email can drop App for staff categories.
- **Remember routing is role-based.** If a colleague isn't getting an alert, check their role and instructor profile before assuming a preference is wrong - some categories simply never route to some roles.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: I'm not receiving a category I expect.

**Cause / fix**: Either the category is toggled off, or it's an opt-in category (User Bookings, My Bookings, User Check-ins) you haven't enabled. You're also never notified about changes you made yourself. Turn the switch on and confirm at least one channel is selected.

**Issue**: A category I want isn't even listed on the page.

**Cause / fix**: The list only shows categories that can route to you. Admin-only categories won't appear for a front-desk or instructor account, and instructor categories require an instructor profile. A category an admin locked for your role is hidden too. Adjust the person's role or instructor profile, or the role defaults, if they genuinely need it.

**Issue**: I enabled a category but still get nothing.

**Cause / fix**: Check the **Channels** selector for that row and confirm at least one channel is selected. Also confirm the account isn't invited-but-not-accepted or inactive, which never receive notifications.

**Issue**: A staff member gets duplicate alerts for the same booking.

**Cause / fix**: They shouldn't - matching multiple routing rules for one event is deduplicated to a single notification. If you see genuine duplicates, confirm they aren't two different categories (for example a class instructor also being an admin who enabled User Bookings), which are distinct notifications by design.

**Issue**: Turning off App didn't stop the email (or vice versa).

**Cause / fix**: Channels are independent. Removing **App** stops both the notification-center entry and the device alert; you must also remove **Email** from that category's channels to stop the email.

**Issue**: I started getting alerts on my phone for a category I had set to In-app.

**Cause / fix**: In-app and Push are now a single **App** channel, so a category you had on In-app now also alerts your devices. Either remove **App** from that category here, which also drops it from the notification center, or leave it on and turn off **Browser notifications** on the **Configure** tab (or notifications for 1Club in your phone's settings) to keep the notification center without the interruption.
