Payment methods are the ways your organization can take money - cash, card via Stripe, in-app wallet credit, and any custom manual method you define. You configure them, control where each one is visible, and choose which method members see preselected when they book in the portal. The front desk picks from them at the point of sale, and members see the ones you make visible to them at checkout.

## When to use it

Set up a method for every way money actually reaches you, so each payment is recorded against the right one and your reports show how members paid. Visibility keeps desk-only methods out of the member portal.

Typical uses:

- **Cash at the front desk** - staff select the private **Cash** method at the point of sale, while members never see it in the portal.
- **Card payments in the portal** - a studio connects Stripe and preselects the **Stripe** method, so members booking a class check out with fewer clicks.
- **Bank transfer for invoiced customers** - a gym records transfers from corporate accounts and families against a private "Bank transfer" method when reconciling paid invoices.
- **Wallet credit for drop-ins** - a climbing gym lets members top up a wallet and spend the credit on drop-ins, while the desk records cash on the **Cash** method.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for the steps behind each of these.

## Channels

Every method belongs to a channel, which determines how a payment is processed:

- **Manual** - recorded outside any gateway (cash, bank transfer). This is the channel for every method you create.
- **Stripe** - card processing through your connected Stripe account. Provided as a system method.
- **Wallet** - in-app wallet credit. Provided as a system method.

A method's channel is fixed. To take card payments you don't create a Stripe method - you use the system **Stripe** method and [connect Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe) so it can take payments.

Card readers add their own system method too. **Stripe Terminal** and **myPOS terminal** appear in the list only once you have registered an active reader for that provider under [Payment terminals](/settings/payment-terminals).

## The payment methods list

Payment methods are configured in the **Payment Methods** section of **Settings** > **Billing** > **General**. The list combines two sources:

- **System methods** - provided by 1Club, marked with a **System** chip: **Stripe**, **Wallet**, and a terminal method for each provider you have an active card reader for. You can't create, edit, or delete these from this page.
- **Organization methods** - manual methods for money you collect outside a payment gateway. Every organization starts with two, **Cash** and **POS Terminal** (for card payments taken on a machine that isn't connected to 1Club, recorded by hand), both **Admin only**. Add your own for anything else, such as bank transfer.

Each row shows the method's **Name**, **Code**, **Visibility**, and a **Preferred** toggle (for methods available to member bookings). System methods sort to the top of the list.

## Adding a manual payment method

1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **General** and scroll to **Payment Methods**.
2. Click **Add Payment Method**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** - what the method is called in the admin and at checkout (for example, "Cash" or "Bank transfer").
   - **Code** - a short identifier used in reports and integrations (for example, `cash`, `venmo`, `square`).
   - **Visibility** - **Admin only** (staff only), **Admin & Member** (also shown to members in the portal and app), or **Public**. New methods default to **Admin only**.
4. Click **Save**.

Manual methods record a payment without going through a gateway - useful for in-person collection or any flow you settle outside Stripe. Every manual method you create belongs to the **Manual** channel automatically; there's no channel picker.

## Editing or deleting a method

- **Edit** - open an organization method to change its **Name** or **Visibility**. The **Code** is locked after creation, because reports and integrations reference it.
- **Delete** - only methods that aren't referenced by any existing payment can be deleted. If a method has been used, change its **Visibility** to hide it instead - this keeps historical payments intact.
- **System methods** can't be edited or deleted here. For **Stripe**, the row's action links to **Configure Stripe integration** so you manage it under [Integrations](/settings/integrations/stripe).

Creating, editing, and deleting methods needs full billing access, and choosing the preferred method needs organization settings access. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

## Preferred method for member bookings

Methods that are available to member bookings - those with **Public** or **Admin & Member** visibility that are paid online - show a **Preferred** toggle in the list. Turn it on for one method to preselect it when a member books a class in the member portal, reducing checkout friction. Turning on a different method's toggle moves the preference; turn the current one off to leave members with no preselection so they choose explicitly.

## Worked examples

### Accept cash at the front desk

1. Check that the **Cash** method, which every organization starts with, has **Visibility** set to **Admin only**.
2. Front-desk staff can select it on point-of-sale and walk-in payments, while members never see it in the portal.

### Let members pay by card in the portal

1. Connect Stripe under [Integrations](/settings/integrations).
2. The system **Stripe** method is already in the **Payment Methods** list and is visible to members.
3. Turn on its **Preferred** toggle so it's preselected for bookings.

### Add bank transfer for invoiced billing

1. Add a manual method named "Bank transfer" with **Visibility** set to **Admin only**.
2. Use it when reconciling paid invoices for corporate accounts or families that settle by transfer.

### Sell class packs on a wallet at a climbing gym

If members top up an in-app wallet and spend it on drop-ins, payments made from wallet credit are recorded against the system **Wallet** method, and the desk keeps using the **Admin only** **Cash** method for cash. You don't need to configure the **Wallet** method. Wallet credit belongs to your default company - see [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities#two-things-that-stay-whole).

## Tips & best practices

- **Keep codes stable.** They're referenced in reports and integrations, and can't be changed after a method is created.
- **Hide, don't delete.** Changing a deprecated method's visibility keeps historical payments intact; deletion is blocked once a method has been used.
- **Pick one preferred method.** Only one method is preselected for member bookings - choose the one most members use.
- **Set visibility deliberately.** Anything set to **Public** or **Admin & Member** shows in the member portal and app; use **Admin only** for desk-only methods like cash.

## Troubleshooting

**A method won't delete**
It's referenced by at least one existing payment. Set its **Visibility** to **Admin only** to keep it out of the member portal instead of deleting it.

**Members can't pay by card in the portal**
Connect Stripe under [Integrations](/settings/integrations/stripe). The system **Stripe** method is visible to members by default and can't be edited.

**The Preferred toggle isn't shown for a method**
Only methods available to member bookings show the toggle. Check that the method's visibility makes it available to members.

**A member sees a method they shouldn't at checkout**
Set that method's **Visibility** to **Admin only** so only staff see it.
