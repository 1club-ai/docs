A payment terminal is a card reader at one of your gyms that staff can send a sale to. The customer taps, inserts, or swipes their card on the reader, and 1club records the payment automatically. Owners and admins register the readers here; front-desk staff then pick a reader when they take a card payment.

## Why use it

Without a connected reader, the front desk types the amount into a standalone card machine and then records the payment by hand, which invites typos and payments recorded against the wrong sale. A connected reader receives the exact amount from 1club and settles the sale on its own.

Typical uses:

- **A reader at reception** - a padel gym registers one Stripe reader at the front desk, and staff charge area bookings and day passes on it from the till.
- **Two providers in one building** - a gym takes cards at reception on a Stripe reader and runs a myPOS device at the bar. Both appear in the same list, each named for where it sits.
- **Spotting a dead reader before the rush** - the front desk checks the list in the morning, sees a reader marked **Offline**, and fixes its power or network before members arrive.

## How it works

Readers come from your payment providers. 1club supports **Stripe Terminal**, **myPOS terminal**, and **Datecs terminal** readers, and you can mix them across your gyms. Before you can add a reader, the provider has to be connected for the company that bills for that gym:

- **Stripe** - connect it under [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe) and tick **Terminal** under **Use Stripe for**. Stripe sells readers only in some countries, and in some of them only for one currency.
- **myPOS** - connect it under [myPOS](/settings/integrations/mypos) and turn on its **Terminal** capability.
- **Datecs** - activate [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay#modules) and tick its **POS Terminals** module. See [Datecs terminals](#datecs-terminals).

Each reader belongs to one gym, and it charges through the payment account of the company that bills for that gym. If you trade through more than one company, see [Billing entities](/settings/billing-entities#connect-each-companys-payment-accounts).

Card payments taken on a registered reader are recorded against that provider's terminal payment method, which appears in your [payment methods](/settings/payment-methods) list once you have an active reader for it.

Readers are separate from online card payments. Each company takes online card payments through one provider, but it can run readers from both Stripe and myPOS, whichever provider handles its online payments.

## Open the list

Go to **Settings** > **Sales** > **Payment terminals**. The Stripe and myPOS integration pages link here with a **Manage** button.

If no connected provider can take in-person payments yet, the page says so and offers **Set up an integration**, which opens **Settings** > **Integrations**. If a provider is connected but can't be used for readers, for example because Stripe does not sell readers in your country or currency, a warning names the provider and the reason.

The list shows each reader's:

- **Name** - with its serial number underneath, when known.
- **Provider** - Stripe Terminal, myPOS terminal, or Datecs terminal.
- **Club** - the gym the reader sits at.
- **Billing entity** - the company its card payments settle to. Shown only when you have more than one company.
- **Device** - the reader model, when the provider reports it.
- **Status** - **Online**, **Offline**, or **Unknown**. Hover it to see when the reader was last seen.

Status is not refreshed on its own. Click **Refresh status** to ask the providers for the latest state of every reader.

## Add a reader

1. Click **Add payment terminal**.
2. Give it a **Name** that tells staff where it is, such as _Front desk_ or _Bar_.
3. Choose the **Provider**.
4. Choose the **Club** the reader sits at. Once you pick a gym, only the providers connected for the company that bills for it are offered.
5. Enter the **Pairing code or serial number**:
   - For **Stripe Terminal**, the pairing code shown on the reader's screen, or the serial number on the box.
   - For **myPOS terminal**, the terminal ID (TID) or serial number printed on the device. The device must already be on your myPOS account.
6. Click **Register**.

If the provider refuses the reader, for example because a pairing code expired or the gym is missing an address, the reason is shown so you can fix it and try again.

## Datecs terminals

A Datecs terminal, such as the BlueCash-50, has no cloud connection of its own. It reaches 1club through the **Payment Bridge** app running on it, so you pair it the other way round: 1club gives you a code, and you enter it on the terminal.

1. Click **Add payment terminal**.
2. Give it a **Name**, choose **Datecs terminal** as the **Provider**, and choose the **Club** it sits at.
3. Click **Get a pairing code**.
4. Enter the code in the Payment Bridge app on the terminal. The code works once, until the time shown. The dialog shows when the terminal has paired.

If the code expires before you use it, close the dialog and get a new one. If the device still asks for a code after the dialog says it paired, someone else used your code: remove what it added and get a new code.

A BlueCash-50 is also a fiscal device. Pairing it here adds only its card side. To print fiscal receipts on it, add it under [Fiscal receipts](/settings/fiscal#add-a-fiscal-device) as **A payment terminal**, with no second pairing. It works the other way too: when a BlueCash-50 is already one of your fiscal devices, **Add payment terminal** offers it under **Add** as _name (BlueCash-50 fiscal device)_. Pick it to turn on its card side without pairing again.

Card charges on a BlueCash-50 print their fiscal receipt on the same box, as part of the charge. Each Datecs terminal closes its card batch once a day at the time set on the [DatecsPay](/settings/integrations/datecspay#daily-card-batch-close) page.

## Retire a reader

Open the row's menu, choose **Delete**, and confirm with **Retire**. The reader stops accepting payments, and the payments it already took keep their history, so you can still tell which device took a payment months later.

Retire every reader at a gym before you point that gym at a different company. A reader belongs to one payment account, so 1club refuses to move a gym to another company while it still has readers registered. Register them again afterwards.

## Take a payment on a reader

Staff send a sale to a reader wherever they take a payment at the desk: the [point of sale](/pos/point-of-sale#payment-methods), **Complete order** on an [order](/sales/orders), and **Add Payment** on a charge, booking, check-in, or membership.

1. Choose the provider's terminal method, such as **Stripe Terminal** or **myPOS terminal**, as the payment method. It is offered only at a gym that has an active reader for that provider.
2. Pick the reader on your counter, then click **Charge card**. The amount appears on its screen. If the computer is set to a [till](/settings/tills), only that till's readers are offered.
3. Ask the customer to tap, insert, or swipe their card. The dialog follows the payment and stays open until the reader answers. Click **Cancel** to stop a charge the customer hasn't paid yet.
4. When the payment is approved, it is recorded against what it paid for: the booking is confirmed, the membership starts, or the order completes.

If the card is declined, the dialog shows the provider's reason and the sale stays open, so the customer can **Try again** with another card or pay another way. Nothing is lost if you close the tab, lose the connection, or lock the tablet after the customer taps: 1club keeps checking with the provider and records the payment against the right sale a few minutes later.

On a Stripe test reader, a **Simulate tap** button lets you rehearse the whole flow without a card.

If your company prints [fiscal receipts](/settings/fiscal), a card taken on any reader gets its receipt too: on the BlueCash-50 itself, or on the till's fiscal device for other readers. The payment shows a **Fiscal receipt** line with whether it printed.

## Permissions

Adding, refreshing, and retiring readers needs permission to manage organization settings. Taking a payment on a reader needs the same access as any other payment at the desk. Writing off a terminal payment that can't be resolved needs full billing access, which only admins and owners hold by default. Staff who can read settings, billing, or the point of sale can see the list, which is how the till offers a reader to send a sale to. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The page says no provider supports in-person terminals | No provider's terminals are set up for your organization. | Connect Stripe, connect myPOS and turn on its **Terminal** capability, or activate DatecsPay and tick **POS Terminals**. |
| A Datecs charge says the fiscal device didn't answer or is busy | A BlueCash-50 prints the fiscal receipt as part of the charge, and its fiscal side couldn't. Nothing was charged. | Check that the device is on and not printing another receipt, then try again. |
| A provider you connected isn't offered when you add a reader | It isn't connected for the company that bills for the gym you picked. | Connect the provider for that company, or pick a gym that bills through a connected company. |
| A reader shows **Offline** | The provider can't reach it, usually power or network. | Check the reader's power and connection, then click **Refresh status**. |
| A reader shows **Unknown** | Its status hasn't been refreshed, or the provider reported a state 1club doesn't recognize. | Click **Refresh status**. |
| You can't change which company a gym bills through | The gym still has readers registered. | Retire its readers, change the company, then register them again. |
| Stripe isn't offered as a provider | **Terminal** isn't ticked under **Use Stripe for** on the [Stripe](/settings/integrations/stripe) page. | Tick **Terminal** for the company that bills for the gym. |
| A payment says it couldn't confirm the charge reached the terminal | The connection to the provider broke at the moment the amount was sent. | Look at the reader's screen. It may already have taken the card. Don't take a second payment for the sale until this one resolves. |
| A terminal payment stays **Pending** for more than a day | Neither the provider nor 1club can tell any more whether the card was charged. | Check the provider's statement. Then an admin can open the payment's actions, choose **Write off this payment**, and record what was checked. This cannot be undone. |
