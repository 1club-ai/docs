A progression track is a named ladder of skill levels that a member advances through over time: the belt system in a jiu-jitsu academy, the graded levels in a kids swim program, or the skill tiers in a tennis gym. Each level can set what it takes to reach it - sessions, days at the level below, and a curriculum of skills - so you and your members can see who is ready. Owners and managers set up the tracks once; instructors and the front desk promote members from their profile, from a class roster after a grading, or in bulk.

## When to use it

Use progression tracks when your members work through a graded syllabus and you want each person's current level, and how they got there, in 1club instead of in a coach's notebook.

Typical uses:

- **Belts and stripes** - a jiu-jitsu academy tracks White to Black belt with up to four stripes per belt, sets 20 sessions per stripe, and sees on the class roster who has earned their next stripe.
- **Grading day** - after a belt test, the head coach opens the class, ticks off who passed on the **Eligible** tab, and moves them all up in one confirmation. Each member gets a promotion notice.
- **A curriculum per belt** - a karate school lists the katas and techniques each belt requires, with a video link for each, and ticks them off on the member's progression as they learn them.
- **Kids moving to the adult ladder** - a BJJ academy maps its kids belts onto the adult track, and 1club moves each child across on their 16th birthday.
- **Programs members join** - a martial-arts school groups its classes into Kids BJJ (7-12), Adult BJJ and Muay Thai, and new students pick their program on the signup form.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to set up each of these.

## How it works

- **A program is what a student joins.** It groups class types (Fundamentals, No-Gi, Open Mat) and carries the track their sessions count toward. See [Programs](#programs).
- **A track is the ladder, a level is a rung.** Levels are ordered; a member's current level on a track is their latest entry on it. A track can also award **stripes** within each level.
- **Each level can set a promotion requirement**: the **Sessions** a member needs at the level below, the **Days** they must hold it, and a **Curriculum** of skills to learn. Stripes can have their own sessions and days. See [Promotion requirements](#promotion-requirements).
- **Eligible is a signal, not a promotion.** When a member meets every requirement the next level (or stripe) sets, 1club shows them as eligible. Nothing changes until someone promotes them.
- **Promoting appends to the member's history** and, for a real promotion or a new stripe, tells the member. See [Promotion notices](#promotion-notices).

## Programs

A **program** is what a student actually signs up for, for example "Kids BJJ (7-12)", "Adult BJJ" or "Muay Thai". It groups class types and carries the progression track their sessions count toward. Programs also show on your website and signup forms.

Go to **Settings** > **Services** > **Programs**. Click **Add** > **Add Program**, or click a program to edit it. Drag the handle to set the order programs are listed in on your website. Each program shows how many class types it holds.

A program has:

- **Name** and **Description** - what members see on your website and on the signup form.
- **Youngest age** and **Oldest age** - optional, shown on the website as "Ages 7-12", "Ages 16+" or "Up to age 12". They are for display only: who may book a class is set on the class type.
- **Progression track** - the track the sessions of this program's class types count toward, or **No track**.
- **Color** and **Image** - used on the website's Programs section.

You put a class type into a program on the class type itself, with its **Program** field (see [Class types](/settings/class-types#counting-toward-a-progression-track)). The **Program** field only appears once you have at least one program. Several programs can share one track, for example two kids programs that climb the same kids belts.

Deleting a program keeps its class types, without a program. Plans still cover class types, never a program, so a program changes nothing about what a plan includes.

### Where members meet programs

- **Website** - add a **Programs** section to show each program's image, ages and description, with **View timetable** linking to the timetable filtered to that program. See [Website](/marketing/website).
- **Signup forms** - choose **Programs on this form** in a signup [form](/marketing/forms), and the form asks new students to **Pick a program** (you can rename that step with **Program selection title**). They land on that program's timetable when they finish.
- **Member profile** - the program a member picked at signup shows as **Chosen program** in their contact information, so staff know what they came for when choosing their plan and first class.

## Tracks and levels

Go to **Settings** > **Services** > **Progression Levels**. The page shows every track visible to your organization as a card.

A **track** has:

- **Track name** - what members and staff see, for example "Adult BJJ Belts" or "Learn to Swim".
- **Sport** - a short tag used to group tracks (for example `jiu-jitsu`, `swimming`, `tennis`).
- **Description** - optional context shown on the track card.
- **Stripes per level** - the maximum number of stripes a member can earn within a single level, from 0 to 10. Set it to 0 to turn stripes off; set it to 4 for the classic jiu-jitsu belt system.

A **level** has:

- **Skill** - the primary label for the rung, for example "White belt", "Level 2", or "Intermediate".
- **Level** - an optional secondary label shown as a colored chip next to the skill (a degree, grade number, or short code).
- **Color** - used for the level's chip and the belt or line drawn on member cards.
- **Sessions** and **Days** - the [promotion requirement](#promotion-requirements) to reach this level. The first level has neither, because there is no level below it.
- **Each stripe** - on a track with stripes, the sessions and days each stripe at this level takes.
- **Curriculum** - the skills to learn for this level, opened from the level's checklist icon.

Each enabled track's card shows which programs count toward it ("Counts sessions of: Kids BJJ, Little Champs"), or "All sessions count toward this track" when no program with a class type points at it.

### Global templates and your own tracks

- **Global templates** are platform-wide ladders, read-only and shared across organizations. Each global card has an enable **toggle** and a **Customize** action. You can still write a curriculum for a template's levels without customizing it.
- **Custom tracks** belong only to your organization. They carry a **Custom** chip, are fully editable, and can be deleted. You get one by creating a track or by customizing a template.

The Brazilian Jiu Jitsu template comes with requirements: sessions for each stripe and each belt, and the IBJJF minimum time at each belt and black belt degree. A gym that enables it sees who is eligible straight away. Customize the track to set your own numbers.

**Enable a template** by flipping its toggle. To turn it off, flip it again and confirm with **Remove**. A track that any member has a progression entry on cannot be disabled ("Cannot disable track: there are active member assignments"). Programs that point at a disabled track keep the link, so turning it back on restores the same counting.

**Customize a template** with **Customize**. The dialog says "Editing a copy of this template. Nothing is saved until you click Save." When you save, 1club:

1. Creates a custom copy of the track and all its levels.
2. Disables the original template for your organization, so you don't end up with two parallel ladders.
3. Moves existing member assignments onto the matching levels of the copy.
4. Moves the programs that pointed at the template over to the copy, so the same sessions keep counting.

### Create a track

1. Go to **Settings** > **Services** > **Progression Levels** and click **Add** > **Create track**.
2. Fill in **Track name** and **Sport** (both required), an optional **Description**, and **Stripes per level**.
3. Save. The levels editor opens so you can author the ladder.

### Author levels

Open a custom track (or a template's **Customize** view) to edit its levels.

- Click **Add level** to append a row, and fill in **Skill**, an optional **Level** label, and a **Color**.
- Enter **Sessions** and **Days** on any level after the first, and **Each stripe** on a track with stripes. Leave a field empty for no requirement.
- Drag the handle to reorder rows - order is the ladder.
- Use the delete icon to remove a row. A level cannot be deleted while any member's progression history uses it ("Level is currently assigned to one or more members").
- Click **Save** to persist all changes together.

A stripe cannot require as much as the next level: the editor refuses it, because the next level would always come first.

## Promotion requirements

A level's requirement is everything it asks for. A member is **eligible** for it once they meet every part that is set:

| Requirement    | What it means                                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sessions**   | Sessions attended at the level below, for example 20 sessions at Orange before Blue. The count starts again at every new level. |
| **Days**       | Calendar days the member has held the level below, on your gym's clock. Earned yesterday counts as 1.                           |
| **Curriculum** | Every skill in the level's curriculum is ticked off for the member.                                                             |

**Each stripe** works the same way within a level: the sessions and days the next stripe takes, counted from the member's last stripe, or from the level when they have none yet. Awarding a stripe never restarts the count toward the next level.

### Sessions to the next level

- **What counts as a session.** A check-in, or a completed booking - the same count as the member's week streak on their account page, so the two never disagree. Cancelled bookings and no-shows never count. If your gym does not require check-in, a booking counts once the day is over even when the member did not show up, unless you mark it a no-show. Require check-in for an accurate grading count.
- **When counting starts.** At midnight on your gym's clock, the day after the member earned their current level. The session where a belt is awarded belongs to the level it was earned at. Backdating a level, or correcting its date, recounts automatically.
- **Which sessions count.** Only sessions of class types in a program that points at the track. When no program with a class type points at the track, every session counts, so a gym that teaches two disciplines without programs counts a session in one toward both. Private lessons and area bookings have no class type, so they don't count toward a track that programs point at.

### Curriculum

Open a level's **Curriculum** (the checklist icon on its row) and click **Add skill**. Each skill has a **Skill** name, a **Description**, and a **Video link**. Drag skills into teaching order. A new level must be saved before you can add skills to it ("Save the level first").

Deleting a skill asks you to confirm, because members who learned it lose that record.

Staff tick skills off on the member's progression (see [Promote one member](#promote-one-member)), under **Curriculum**: "Skills for Blue belt: 3 / 5 learned". Members see what is left to learn for their next level next to their progress bar in their account.

### Where eligibility shows

- **Member profile** - the progress tile shows each requirement, for example "13 / 20 sessions to Blue belt", "45 / 90 days at current level" and "3 / 5 skills learned", or **Eligible for promotion to Blue belt** once every one is met. The next stripe shows as **Eligible for stripe 2 at Blue belt**.
- **Class page** - a green award next to a member's name on the roster. See [Promote a class after grading](#promote-a-class-after-grading).
- **Member account (portal and website)** - the **Your progression** card on **My account** > **Overview**, next to the week streak, and the progression section of the member's **Profile** show the same count and bar.
- **Progression report** - **Analytics** > **Members** > **Progression Report** lists every member's sessions toward the next level, days at their level, and a status (**Eligible**, **On track**, **Stalled** or **No requirement**). See [Members analytics](/analytics/members#progression-report).
- **Smart tags** - turn on the **Progression** tags to tag who is eligible, close, stalled or recently promoted. See [Smart tagging](/settings/smart-tagging#progression).

## Promoting members

Recording a level needs the Members **Edit** permission. A member does not need a portal login to hold a level. Only tracks enabled for your organization can be assigned.

### Promote one member

1. Open the member's profile. The progress tile (titled **Progression Levels**, or **Progress** when assessments are also on) shows their current levels.
2. Click a level on the tile to open the member's progression. Each track shows its history, with a **Promote** button that moves the member up one level, and the **Curriculum** for their next level, where you tick off skills they have learned.
3. To record any other level, use the plus icon on the tile and choose **Assign Level**, then:
   - Pick a **Track** (skipped when only one is enabled) and a **Level**.
   - If the track has stripes, choose the number of **Stripes**.
   - Set the **Date earned**. It defaults to today and can't be in the future. Backdate it to record a grading that already happened.
   - Add optional **Notes** (for example "Promoted at winter grading").
   - Leave **Email the member about this promotion** (or **Email the member about this stripe**) ticked to send the [notice](#promotion-notices). It doesn't appear for a member's first level, or when the date earned is before their current level.
4. **Save**.

### Promote a class after grading

Open a class from the schedule. A green award next to a member's name means they meet every requirement for their next level or their next stripe. Hover it to see which, with their sessions (for example "27 / 25 sessions").

When anyone on the roster is eligible, an **Eligible** tab sits next to **Active**. It lists each of them with where they are, their **Next step** (**New level** or **Stripe**), their sessions and days, and whether they are checked in.

1. Open the **Eligible** tab. Everyone starts ticked: untick whoever did not pass.
2. Click **Promote** (or **Move up** when the selection includes stripes).
3. The confirmation lists each member's change. The change is recorded on the class's day unless you pick another date (a class still to come records today).
4. Leave **Tell each member about their new level or stripe** ticked to send each one a notice, and confirm.

The **Eligible** tab needs Members **View** with full profiles, and ticking members needs Members **Edit**. It is not shown in the instructor-only class view.

### Promote a selection of members

1. Go to **Members** and filter by the **Eligible for Promotion** smart tag, or by **Level**.
2. Select the members and click **Promote** in the bulk action bar. It appears for staff with Members **Edit** when at least one track is enabled, and not on the **Leads** tab.
3. Choose the track if you have more than one. Each selected member moves up one level, earned today, with stripes back to zero.
4. Leave **Tell each member about their promotion** ticked, and click **Promote**.

Anyone with no level on the track, already at the top, or with a later level or stripe already recorded is left as they are, and the result says how many. The [Progression report](/analytics/members#progression-report) offers the same promotion for one member or a ticked selection.

### Progression history

Every save appends a dated entry to the member's progression timeline - level changes, stripe-only changes, and note edits all create a history row. A member's current level on a track is their most recent entry on it.

The history shows each entry's date, level (with belt or line and stripes), and notes, newest first. Use an entry's pencil icon to correct its **Date earned**. Deleting an entry needs Members **Delete**; if you delete the most recent one, the previous entry becomes the member's current level.

## Promotion notices

When you promote a member to a new level, they hear about it in the app and by email, in their own language, without any automation. The message names the track, the level they reached and the one they came from, and links to their progress in their account. A new stripe sends its own, shorter notice.

You control the wording: **Promotion** and **New stripe** are editable [templates](/settings/templates), so you can add whatever your gym says at a grading.

Only real promotions and new stripes send anything. These stay silent:

- recording a member's **first** level on a track, so entering your existing ranks doesn't email the whole roster,
- moving a member **down**, which is usually a correction,
- re-saving a note or the same level,
- a date earned **before** the member's current level,
- any promotion where you untick the notice box.

Members switch promotion notices off under **Progression** in their notification settings, and you can turn them off for everyone in your [notification policy](/settings/notifications). Assessment cards ride the same category.

## Ageing out

Kids ladders often end at an age: IBJJF juvenile belts end at 16. On a custom track, click **Ageing out** on its card to set:

- **Continue on** - the track members move to, or **Never**.
- **At age** - the age they move at.
- **Each level continues at** - for each level on this track, the level they get on the next track. Unmapped levels continue at **The first level**.

1club checks every day and moves each member once they reach the age: they get the mapped level on the next track, earned on the birthday they reached it (never dated before the level they hold now). It is a first level on that track, so no notice is sent. A member who already holds any level on the next track is left alone, and so is an archived contact or a member with no date of birth. Nobody is moved onto a track your organization has disabled. Moving the member's plan to the adult program stays your decision.

The card then reads **Ages out at 16**. Templates have no **Ageing out** action: customize one first.

## Levels in audiences

The levels of a track work as a condition elsewhere. Wherever you pick **Levels** in an audience, you can choose a level **and up**, such as **Blue belt and up**:

- **Who can book and see prices** on a [class type](/settings/class-types), to keep an Advanced class for blue belts and up,
- [booking policies](/settings/booking-policies),
- [promotions](/sales/promotions),
- message audiences in the [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

The Member list also filters by **Level**.

## Worked examples

### Adult jiu-jitsu belt system (martial arts)

Enable the Brazilian Jiu Jitsu template: it already has four stripes per belt, sessions for each stripe and belt, and the IBJJF minimum days at each belt. Create a program "Adult BJJ" pointing at the track, and put your Fundamentals, No-Gi and Open Mat class types in it, so a strength class doesn't move anyone toward a belt. Require check-in so only attended classes count.

After each class, the roster's award shows who has earned a stripe. On grading day, open the grading class's **Eligible** tab, untick anyone who did not pass, and move the rest up together.

### Kids BJJ moving to the adult ladder

Customize the kids belt template, then use **Ageing out** on the custom track: **Continue on** the adult BJJ track **At age** 16, with each kids belt mapped to the adult belt it continues at. Each child moves across on their 16th birthday, earned that day. Move their plan to the adult program yourself.

### Karate curriculum (martial arts)

On each belt of your karate track, open **Curriculum** and add the katas and techniques the belt requires, in teaching order, with a video link each. Instructors tick skills off on the student's progression. A student is eligible only once every skill is learned and any sessions and days are met, and sees what is left to learn in their account.

### Learn to swim, kids levels (swimming)

Create a track "Learn to Swim" with sport `swimming` and **Stripes per level** `0`. Author sequential levels such as Level 1 (Water confidence) to Level 5 (Endurance). Create a "Learn to swim" program with ages 4-10 pointing at it, add a Programs section to your website, and assign each child their current level.

### Tennis skill tiers (racket sports)

Create a track "Tennis skill tiers" with sport `tennis` and no stripes, with tiers like Beginner, Improver, Intermediate, Advanced, Competition. Use **Levels** and **Intermediate and up** in a class type's **Who can book and see prices** to keep your advanced clinic for the right players, and pair it with the 1club Rating on [Matches](/events/matches).

## Tips & best practices

- **Prefer enable over customize.** If a template matches your syllabus, toggle it on. You can add a curriculum to a template without customizing it. Customize only to rename, recolor, reorder, change requirements, or add and remove levels.
- **Order is the ladder.** Drag levels into true progression sequence before you start promoting.
- **Use programs to scope counting.** If your gym runs more than one discipline, put each discipline's class types in a program pointing at its track.
- **Require check-in where sessions drive grading**, so the count reflects real attendance.
- **Write promotion notes.** They make the history a real grading record.
- **Add your gym's words to the Promotion template** - when to bow in, what comes next.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                                     | Cause                                                                                                                        | Fix                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A template's levels cannot be edited                        | Templates are read-only and shared                                                                                           | Click **Customize** to make an editable copy                                         |
| "Cannot disable track: there are active member assignments" | A member has a level or history entry on the track                                                                           | Delete those entries from the members' progression history, then turn the track off  |
| A level will not delete                                     | A member's progression history uses it                                                                                       | Delete those history entries first                                                   |
| A member's session count dropped or jumped                  | A class type moved into or out of a program on the track, or a program's track changed, which recounts every member          | Expected. Put every class type that trains for the track in a program pointing at it |
| No **Eligible** tab on a class                              | Nobody on the roster meets their next requirement, or you lack full Members **View**                                         | Check the members' progress on their profile                                         |
| The member sees no progress bar                             | They have no current level, hold the top level, or the next level sets no requirement                                        | Set **Sessions**, **Days** or a **Curriculum** on the next level                     |
| A member has the sessions but isn't eligible                | Another requirement on the next level (days or skills) isn't met yet                                                         | Check the progress tile: every set requirement must be met                           |
| A booked member who did not show up was counted             | Your gym does not require check-in                                                                                           | Mark the booking a no-show, or require check-in                                      |
| A member didn't get a promotion notice                      | It was their first level, a correction, a backdated save, the box was unticked, or they turned progression notifications off | Expected for those cases                                                             |
| Bulk promotion left some members as they were               | They have no level on the track, are at the top, or already have a later level or stripe                                     | Expected. Promote them individually if needed                                        |
| **Ageing out** missing on a track card                      | The track is a template                                                                                                      | Customize it first                                                                   |
| Deleting a history entry changed the member's current level | The current level is always the most recent entry                                                                            | Add a new assignment to restore the intended level                                   |
