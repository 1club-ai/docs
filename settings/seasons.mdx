A season is the fixed period a [season pass](/operations/season-passes) is sold for: a winter, a term, a league. It carries a name your members recognize, a first and last day, and the volume discount that rewards holding more hours each week. Everything else about the price comes from your [pricing rules](/settings/pricing-rules).

Open **Settings > Revenue > Seasons** (route `/settings/billing/seasons`). It sits directly after **Pricing rules**, because they are the same task: a season's price is its hourly rates from there, multiplied by its weeks, with the volume discount from here applied once.

## Overview

- **A period, not a product** - a season is the window; what you sell against it is a season pass, sold from the [schedule](/operations/schedule).
- **One gym per season** - a season belongs to a single gym, so two venues with different price lists run their own seasons.
- **Volume discount ladder** - hold more hours a week, earn a bigger discount. Steps are keyed on weekly hours, and the highest step reached is the one that applies.
- **Rates live in pricing rules** - a season carries no price of its own, so a published price table stays in one place.
- **Readiness at a glance** - each row says whether the season is on sale, not priced yet, or needs fixing, with the exact reason on hover.
- **On sale toggle** - stop selling a season without touching the passes already sold.
- **Whole weeks warning** - a range that is not a whole number of weeks is allowed but flagged, because it charges two members differently for the same weekly hour.

## The list page

The table has these columns:

- **Name** - what you called the season. This is the name members see everywhere.
- **Dates** - first and last day.
- **Length** - the season's length in weeks.
- **Setup** - a chip reading **On sale**, **Pricing not set up**, **Pricing needs fixing**, or **Not on sale**. Hover it to read the exact issue.
- **Passes** - how many season passes have been sold against this season.
- **Status** - whether the season is upcoming, running, or finished.

Use **New season** to create one, or the row menu to edit or delete. When you have no seasons yet, the page explains what a season is and offers the same button.

## Creating a season

Click **New season**. The dialog has four sections.

### Season

- **Name** (required) - whatever your members call it, for example _Winter 2027_. This name appears on the schedule, on the member's pass, and on the sale.
- **Club** - the gym the season runs at. Once a pass has been sold against the season this is locked, because the courts it holds belong to that venue.

### Dates

- **First day** and **Last day** - the season's range, inclusive. The subtitle shows the resulting length in weeks as you pick.
- **Existing subscribers claim by** - an optional date you record for your own renewal communication with last season's holders. The platform does not enforce it: nothing is reserved and no reminder is sent.

A range that runs backwards is refused. A range that is not a whole number of weeks is allowed but warns you, because some weekdays then occur more often than others: two members buying one weekly hour would be charged different totals depending on the day they picked.

### Pricing

- **Revenue account** - where the season's income posts. Optional, exactly like any other plan; leave it empty to use your organization default.
- **Volume discount** - the ladder. Each step is **From (hours/week)** plus a **Discount**, read as "hold this many hours a week, get this discount".

A new season starts with a sensible default ladder:

| From (hours/week) | Discount |
| ----------------- | -------- |
| 1                 | 3%       |
| 2                 | 5%       |
| 3                 | 7%       |
| 4                 | 10%      |

The highest step a member reaches is the one that applies, and the last step is the cap, so someone holding five or six hours a week simply gets the top discount. Below the first threshold there is no discount. Steps are keyed on **hours**, not on the number of weekly slots, so a single 90-minute slot lands on the one-hour step rather than falling into a gap.

Two steps cannot start at the same number of hours, hours must be between 0 and 168, and a discount must be between 0% and 100%. Without any step at all, every season pass sells at full price.

<Note>
  A season plan carries no price field. The pass costs the sum of the hours it books, priced by your [pricing rules](/settings/pricing-rules), with one volume discount applied to that total. Set an hourly rate per time band in Pricing rules and the season prices itself from them.
</Note>

### Availability

- **On sale** - turn this off to stop selling the season. Passes already sold are untouched; the sale option simply stops being offered on the schedule.

## What saving a season creates

Saving writes two things: the season itself, and the plan the pass is sold through. You never create that plan by hand, and it never appears in a plan catalogue - not in the member portal, not on the marketplace, not in POS, and not in the plan picker on a booking - because a season pass can only be sold from the schedule, where the courts are.

If the season saves but its pricing does not, the dialog tells you so and keeps your input: fix the field it points at and save again. You will not end up with a duplicate season.

## Readiness states

The **Setup** chip answers one question: can you sell this season right now?

| Chip                    | What it means                                                        |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **On sale**             | Priced and available. The schedule offers the sale inside its dates.  |
| **Pricing not set up**  | The season has no plan to sell yet, so nothing can be priced.         |
| **Pricing needs fixing**| The plan exists but is misconfigured. Hover the chip for the reason.  |
| **Not on sale**         | Configured correctly, but the **On sale** toggle is off.              |

The reasons behind **Pricing needs fixing** are specific, and each names the field to change:

- A season is paid once, not on a repeating cycle.
- The season needs to belong to one gym.
- The pricing belongs to a different gym than the season.
- A season counts sessions in hours.
- No volume discount is set.

## Editing and deleting

Editing a season updates its name, dates, discount ladder, revenue account, and availability. Two things are protected:

- **The gym is locked once a pass has been sold**, because the sold courts belong to that venue.
- **Deleting is only possible while nothing has been sold** against the season. Once a pass exists, the season is part of a paid contract; turn **On sale** off instead. Deleting is permanent and asks for confirmation.

## Worked example: an indoor tennis winter

A tennis venue sells its indoor winter as a season, October to April.

1. **Pricing rules first.** Author one rule per time band with the **Absolute price** type, matching the printed price table: off-peak morning 41, day 39, peak 47, late 41, weekend 41. Add a second rule per band scoped to the season's plan with the member rate: 37, 34, 42, 37, 37. See [Pricing rules](/settings/pricing-rules).
2. **Create the season.** Name it _Winter 2026/27_, pick the gym, set the first and last day so the range is a whole number of weeks, and record your claim-by date for last season's holders.
3. **Set the ladder.** The default 1/2/3/4-hour steps at 3/5/7/10% match how most venues publish theirs. Adjust the percentages to your own table.
4. **Turn On sale on**, then sell from the schedule. A member holding a Tuesday 19:00 court and a Thursday 10:00 court holds two hours a week, so they land on the 5% step, and each week is priced by the band its hour falls in.

## Current limits

- **Renewal is not automated.** A season pass never auto-renews, and the **Existing subscribers claim by** date is a note to yourself, not an enforced hold. Next season is a fresh sale.
- **One gym per season.** A multi-venue organization creates one season per venue.
- **No self-serve purchase.** Members cannot buy a season pass themselves yet; the sale happens in the admin, on the schedule.
- **The ladder is per season**, not per plan or per member. Two different discount structures need two seasons.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: The schedule does not offer **Add Season Pass** on a date inside my season.

**Solution**: The option appears only when the season is actually sellable on that date. Check the **Setup** chip: a season that is switched off, or never finished pricing, still sits in its own date range but cannot be sold. Also confirm you hold the permission to create memberships.

**Issue**: Every season pass sells at full price.

**Solution**: The volume discount ladder has no steps. Add at least one.

**Issue**: The dialog warns that my range is not a whole number of weeks.

**Solution**: That is a warning, not an error, and the season saves. Adjust the last day so the range covers whole weeks if you want every weekday to occur the same number of times; otherwise expect a Monday hour and a Saturday hour to total differently.

**Issue**: I cannot change the gym on a season.

**Solution**: A season that has sold a pass is locked to its gym, because the courts held for that pass belong to it. Create a new season for the other venue.

**Issue**: I cannot delete a season.

**Solution**: A season with a pass sold against it cannot be deleted, so financial history and the booked courts stay intact. Turn **On sale** off to retire it.
