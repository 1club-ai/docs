Smart tagging applies tags to your contacts automatically, from rules you switch on once. 1club reads how often each person comes, what they pay and owe, where they are in their membership, how they progress and answer your messages, and simple facts such as their age and birthday, and keeps every contact's tags up to date. Owners and managers set the rules; everyone else uses the tags to filter the member list, target promotions and messages, and start automations. You can't add or remove a smart tag on one profile: you change the rule instead.

## When to use it

Use smart tags when you want segments that maintain themselves - who is slipping away, whose membership is about to expire, who plays padel - and then act on them with promotions, automations and reports.

Typical uses:

- **Catch members drifting away** - a gym turns on **At Risk** and starts the **At-risk member win-back** automation, and checks the [At-risk customers](/analytics/members#at-risk-customers) report each Monday for the members tagged **At Risk** or **Declining**.
- **Members who paid and never came** - a studio messages everyone tagged **Not Started** a week after they bought their first plan.
- **Marketing by sport** - a padel and tennis gym targets a padel-night promotion at the padel **Interest** tag only.
- **Recovering payments** - a Pilates studio builds an automation that messages members tagged **Overdue**.
- **A birthday offer** - an academy sends a free guest pass to everyone tagged **Birthday**, a week before the day.

## How it differs from manual tags

|                              | Manual tags                                                                | Smart tags                                       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where they come from**     | You create them and apply them to specific contacts                        | 1club applies them from rules                    |
| **Who decides who has them** | You, per contact                                                           | The rule, from the contact's current data        |
| **Configured in**            | **Settings** > **Members** > **Manual Tags**, or any tag chip on a contact | **Settings** > **Members** > **Smart Tags**      |
| **Editable per contact**     | Yes                                                                        | No - change the rule, not the tag on the contact |
| **Examples**                 | "VIP", "Family of John", "Yoga teacher"                                    | "Regular", "Overdue", "Expiring Soon", "Adult"   |

Both kinds behave the same when you filter or segment. A promotion that targets "Overdue" works the same whether that tag is manual or smart.

## Who gets smart tags

- **Leads, members, drop-ins and lapsed members** are eligible for every category that is on.
- **Plain contacts** (never booked, checked in or held a plan, and not in your [pipeline](/members/leads)) only receive the categories that don't depend on activity: **Contact stages**, the lead tags (**Source**, **Stale Lead**, **Engaged**, **Unresponsive**), **Payment method**, **Age**, **Birthday**, **Relationship** and **Language**.
- **Staff** (instructors, managers, admins) never receive smart tags. Any smart tag they had before becoming staff is removed on the next evaluation; their manual tags are kept.
- **Archived contacts** are frozen: their tags stay as a snapshot and are not recomputed.

| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |

| **Membership**       | [Lifecycle Tags](#lifecycle), [Membership Tags](#membership), [Sessions Left Tags](#sessions-left)                                                                                 | Lifecycle, Membership                 |
| **Attendance**       | [Attendance Tags](#attendance), [Trend Tags](#declining), [At Risk Tag](#at-risk), [Loyalty Tags](#loyalty), [Interest Tags](#interest), [Engagement Scoring](#engagement-scoring) | Attendance, Trend, Engagement Scoring |
| **Payments**         | [Financial Tags](#financial), [Spending Tags](#top-spender), [Payment Method Tags](#payment-method)                                                                                | Financial, Spending                   |
| **Messaging**        | [Engaged Tag](#lead-tags), [Unresponsive Tag](#lead-tags)                                                                                                                          | None                                  |
| **Progression**      | [Progression Tags](#progression), [Assessment Tags](#assessment)                                                                                                                   | None                                  |
| **Personal details** | [Age Tags](#age), [Birthday Tags](#birthday), [Relationship Tags](#relationship), [Language Tags](#language)                                                                       | Age, Birthday, Relationship           |

Four categories only appear once they could tag someone, so the page doesn't offer what your gym can't use yet: **Language** (your organization uses more than one language), **Interest** (your gyms offer more than one sport), **Progression** (a [progression track](/settings/progression) is enabled), and **Assessment** (you have an [assessment template](/members/assessments)). A category that is already on always shows.

### Change a category

1. Use the switch on a card to turn the category on or off. It saves at once.
2. Click the card to open its panel. The table lists each **Tag** with its **Rule** and **Threshold**.
3. Rename or recolor a tag, change its threshold, add a tag with **Add Tag** (where the category allows more than its fixed rows), or delete one.
4. Click **Save**.

Tag names must be unique within a category, and thresholds are validated (for example, minimum visits can't be negative). Changing smart tag settings needs the Members **Edit** permission.

After you save, 1club updates the tags themselves right away: a renamed tag is renamed on every contact that has it, and a deleted one comes off every contact. Saving doesn't re-check who qualifies - that happens on the next sync. To apply a new threshold or a newly enabled category now, click **Sync tags** at the top of the page.

## Categories

Within a category a contact gets at most one tag, the highest that matches, unless the category says otherwise below.

### What counts as a visit

The attendance categories, the engagement score and the progression counts all read **visits**: a check-in, or a completed booking. A gym whose members book an area and play without scanning in is read the same as one with a turnstile. Cancelled bookings and no-shows never count.

### Contact stages

Tags contacts by how recently they were added, for spotting fresh leads. The field is **Max Days Since Created**. Default: **New** (within 14 days).

### Lead tags

Four categories for segmenting and re-engaging leads, each with its own switch and each off until you turn it on.

- **Source Tags** - tags each person with the [source](/members/leads#sources) they came from, one tag per source, named and colored like the source. The category has no rows of its own: add, rename or recolor sources under **Settings** > **Members** > **Pipeline**. A contact keeps their source's tag after they convert.
- **Stale Lead Tag** - a lead in an open pipeline stage with no booking, form, stage move or conversation for **Days without activity** (default 7). A message only counts when it was exchanged with the person, so campaigns and automated messages don't keep a lead warm. A lead in **Lost**, or out of the pipeline, never carries it.
- **Engaged Tag** - people who recently clicked or replied to an email or WhatsApp message, or read one on WhatsApp, within **Days since they last responded** (default 30). An email open alone doesn't count, because some mail apps open every email they deliver.
- **Unresponsive Tag** - people who received at least **Marketing messages, at least** (default 3) marketing emails or WhatsApp messages within **Period (Days)** (default 60) and clicked, replied to or read none of them. SMS reports no responses, so someone reached only by SMS is never Unresponsive.

Engaged and Unresponsive apply to anyone you message, not only leads. All four are re-checked overnight, when a message or form arrives from the person, when their stage changes, and when their source is set. The [Re-engage stale leads](/marketing/automations#suggested-blueprints) automation starts on the Stale Lead tag.

### Lifecycle

Where each person is in their journey. A contact gets the first of these that applies:

- **Pipeline stage** - a contact in your [pipeline](/members/leads) carries their stage as their Lifecycle tag (for example "New lead"). The panel lists one row per stage, with **Edit in Pipeline settings**.
- **Frozen** - every membership or [season pass](/schedule/season-passes) the member holds is paused. A paused plan next to an active one isn't Frozen. This row can be renamed and recolored, not deleted.
- **Tenure** - how long the member has been with you, counted from the start of their first plan, while they hold an active or pending plan. Any plan starts the count, a day pass or class pack included, and so does a plan shared with them on an account. Someone who visited before buying their first plan doesn't get **Trial**, but gets the later tiers.

Tenure defaults: **Trial** (day 1+), **Probation** (day 15+), **Active** (day 46+). The field is **Min Days Since First Membership**.

### Membership

Tags members around three dates: when their membership ends, when a pause ends, and its yearly anniversary.

| Tag                    | Rule                        | Days                                                             | Default            |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Expiring**           | Days Before Expiry          | Positive means days _before_ expiry, negative means days _after_ | 7 days before      |
| **Expiring Soon**      | Days Before Expiry          | As above                                                         | 3 days before      |
| **Expired**            | Days Before Expiry          | As above                                                         | 7 days after       |
| **Freeze Ending Soon** | Days before the pause ends  | 0 to 60                                                          | Empty: tags nobody |
| **Member Anniversary** | Days before the anniversary | 0 to 60                                                          | Empty: tags nobody |

- **Before-expiry** tags only match active memberships that are **not auto-renewing**, because an auto-renewing one isn't going to lapse.
- **After-expiry** tags only apply when the member has **no** active membership.
- **Freeze Ending Soon** and **Member Anniversary** apply alongside an expiry tag, from their number of days ahead through the day itself. They can't be deleted.
- **Freeze Ending Soon** - a pause on one of the member's memberships, or one shared with them, ends within the window. Pair it with **Frozen** to welcome a member back.
- **Member Anniversary** - the yearly return of the day the member's current stretch with you began, while they hold an active recurring membership or season pass. A member who left and came back counts from their return. A stretch begun on 29 February comes round on 28 February.

### Sessions Left

Tags members whose pass or membership is almost out of sessions, or ran out recently and hasn't been bought again. Off by default.

| Tag                   | Applies when                                                                                                    | Field                      | Default |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------- |
| **Few Sessions Left** | An active plan has a limited allowance with at least 1 and at most this many sessions (or hours) left           | **Sessions left, at most** | 2       |
| **No Sessions Left**  | A pass was used up within this many days, and the member has no active pass of the same plan with sessions left | **Days since it ran out**  | 30      |

A member can carry both. Both rows are fixed. A pass past its end date doesn't count for either.

### Attendance

Groups members by how often they visit over one period. Every tier counts over the category's **Period (Days)** (default 30), and each tier sets **Minimum Visits**.

| Tag              | Rule                         | Default |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- | ------- |
| **Not Started**  | Has a plan, never visited    | -       |
| **All-Star**     | Minimum visits in the period | 16      |
| **Regular**      | Minimum visits in the period | 8       |
| **Casual**       | Minimum visits in the period | 4       |
| **Low Activity** | Minimum visits in the period | 1       |
| **Inactive**     | No visits in the period      | 0       |

**Inactive** and **Not Started** are only for someone with an active plan: **Not Started** when they have never visited at all, **Inactive** otherwise. A plan that hasn't started yet doesn't count, since its holder couldn't have come. A contact with nothing active isn't inactive but lapsed.

### Declining

The **Trend Tags** card holds one tag, **Declining**: a member with an active plan who came at least **Min Visits Before** times (default 4) in the attendance period before this one, and at most **Now At Most** (default 50%) of that in this one. It reads a falling count, not a low one: someone who always comes twice a month is never Declining, someone who drops from twenty visits to nine is. It uses the attendance period, and can sit beside an attendance tag. On by default.

### At Risk

The **At Risk Tag** card tags a paying member who has stopped coming. Off by default. A member carries **At Risk** while all of these hold:

- they hold a live membership or season pass that is not paused, so a Frozen member never is,
- they have been with you for at least 14 days without a break,
- they owe nothing,
- they have had no visit in **Days without a visit** (default 10),
- and nothing is booked for the next 3 days.

Booking, visiting, pausing or a failed payment takes the tag off. It is re-checked on bookings, check-ins, memberships and payments, and overnight. The **At-risk member win-back** automation [blueprint](/marketing/automations#suggested-blueprints) starts on it, and is suggested only while the category is on.

The [At-risk customers](/analytics/members#at-risk-customers) report under **Analytics** > **Members** lists everyone tagged **At Risk** or **Declining** by the thresholds you set here, so it shows exactly whom the member card shows. Turn both on to fill it.

### Loyalty

Ranks members by their total visits, booked or checked in, over all time. Use it for long-term recognition, where Attendance measures recent habits. Off by default.

Defaults: **Legend** (50+), **Champion** (25+), **Pro** (10+), **Rising Star** (5+), **Beginner** (fewer than 5). The field is **Minimum Activity**.

### Interest

Sport-by-sport tags for members who booked the same sport at least **Minimum Bookings per Sport** times (default 3). One tag per sport that crosses the threshold, so a member can hold several. Off by default, and only offered when your gyms offer more than one sport.

### Engagement scoring

Not a tag category: **Calculate engagement scores** gives each member holding an active plan a score from 0 to 100, read from their visits alone over the attendance period:

- half from how often they come compared with your other scored members,
- a quarter from keeping up with the period before,
- a quarter from how recently they came, fading to nothing a period after their last visit.

A member who pays every month but never comes scores low. Scores are recalculated nightly, and a member whose plan ends has their score removed. The levels are **Low**, **Medium**, **High** and **Rockstar**, at 25, 50 and 75. The score shows on the member's profile and on the [engagement dashboard](/analytics/engagement). On by default; turning it on calculates every score straight away.

### Financial

Flags members with unpaid or overdue payments. Each past-due tier sets **Min Days Overdue**.

- **Unpaid** - owes money, but nothing is past due yet. This row is fixed.
- **Past Due** - a payment is 1+ day overdue.
- **Overdue** - a payment is 7+ days overdue.

### Top Spender

The **Spending Tags** card holds one tag, **Top Spender**: a contact in the **Top Share** (default 10%) of your paying contacts by what they paid over **Period (Days)** (default 365). Paid charges count at their total, so a refund takes the spend back off. A share rather than an amount, so the same setting works in any currency. On by default.

### Payment method

Tags contacts by the kinds of payment method they have saved in Stripe and can still be charged to. An expired card doesn't count. There's one fixed row per kind: **Card**, **ACH Debit**, **SEPA Debit**, **Bacs Debit**, **Link**, and **Other Payment Method**, and you can only rename and recolor them. Off by default.

A contact gets one tag per kind they have saved. The tags update when a payment method is saved or removed, not in the nightly run. Turning the category back on rebuilds the tags from Stripe in the background. A gym that doesn't use Stripe sees none of these tags.

### Progression

Tags members by their levels on your [progression tracks](/settings/progression). The four tags are fixed: you can edit each one's name, color and threshold. A member who holds several tracks gets a tag if any track qualifies, and can carry more than one.

- **Eligible for Promotion** - meets every requirement the next level sets (see [Promotion requirements](/settings/progression#promotion-requirements)).
- **Close to Promotion** - not eligible yet, but has reached **Sessions reached** (default 80%) of what is needed.
- **Stalled at Rank** - holds a level but hasn't attended a session counting toward that track in **Days without a session** (default 30).
- **Recently Promoted** - was promoted within **Days since the promotion** (default 14). A first level, a stripe or a correction never counts.

Filter the member list by **Eligible for Promotion** and promote everyone at once (see [Promote a selection of members](/settings/progression#promote-a-selection-of-members)).

### Assessment

Tags members by their [assessments](/members/assessments). The three tags are fixed, like Progression.

- **Not Assessed Recently** - attended at least one session within **Days since assessed** (default 30) but has no assessment in that time. Members who stopped coming aren't flagged.
- **Top Performer** - the average of their last 3 assessments is at least **Average score at least** (default 80%) of the scale.
- **Needs Attention** - that average is at most **Average score at most** (default 50%).

### Age

Tags contacts by age group from their date of birth, each with a **Min Age** and **Max Age**. Defaults: **Infant** (0-4), **Kid** (5-12), **Junior** (13-17), **Adult** (18-64), **Senior** (65+), the same bands a [class type's](/settings/class-types#age-group-and-difficulty) age group uses. Editing a tag's bounds changes only the tag.

### Birthday

Tags contacts from **Days Before** their birthday (default 7) through the day itself, on your gym's clock. A 29 February birthday falls on 28 February in a common year. You can add more rows with their own window, such as "Birthday Today" with 0 days (0 to 31): they are not a ladder, so a contact carries every row whose window includes today. The default **Birthday** row can't be deleted. Leads carry it too.

### Relationship

Tags contacts by their household role: owners and adults are tagged **Parent**, children **Child**. You can only rename and recolor the two rows. A contact can carry both, for example as an adult in one household and a child in another.

### Language

Tags members by the preferred language on their account: **English**, **Español**, **Français**, **Deutsch**, **Български**, **Čeština**. You can only rename and recolor the rows. A contact with no account has no known language and gets no tag.

## How and when tags are kept in sync

Smart tags are recomputed:

- **On events** that could change a tag - a contact is created or changes pipeline stage, a membership is created, cancelled, paused, resumed or expires, a payment succeeds or fails, a booking is created, cancelled, marked no-show, checked in or completed, a payment method is saved or removed, a member is promoted or a level is corrected, an assessment is added, edited or removed. The affected contact is re-evaluated right away.
- **Nightly**, for every contact, which catches time-based changes such as a membership entering its expiry window or a birthday coming up.
- **On demand**, with **Sync tags**, which re-evaluates everyone and reports how many members were updated.

A sync only touches smart tags. Manual tags are never removed by it.

## Where smart tags show up

- **Member list** - filter by tag. See [Member list](/members/member-list#search-and-filter).
- **Member profile** - smart and manual tags appear together next to the member's name.
- **[Promotions](/sales/promotions)** - restrict a voucher or discount to contacts with specific tags.
- **[Automations](/marketing/automations)** - start on a tag being added, or use tags in entry conditions.
- **Messages** - send to an audience of tags.
- **Reports** - the [At-risk customers](/analytics/members#at-risk-customers) report reads the At Risk and Declining tags.

Turning a category off removes its tags from everyone on the next sync, and promotion and automation filters that referenced them simply stop matching anyone.

## Manual tags

Manual tags are the ones you apply by hand. Manage them under **Settings** > **Members** > **Manual Tags**: click **Add Tag**, enter a **Tag Name**, and optionally pick a **Color**. Click a tag to edit it. Deleting a tag removes it from every member who has it. You add and remove manual tags on one contact with the tag button next to their name on their profile.

To flag one person in a way a rule can't, use a manual tag rather than trying to change their smart tags.

## Worked examples

### Keep members from drifting away

Turn on **At Risk** (leave **Days without a visit** at 10) and keep **Declining** on. Start the **At-risk member win-back** automation blueprint, which reaches out over three weeks and stops when the tag comes off. Each week, work through the [At-risk customers](/analytics/members#at-risk-customers) report, which also shows each member's monthly value.

### Racket-sports gym: fill areas and chase renewals

- Turn on **Interest** so regulars pick up padel and tennis tags, and target a padel-night promotion at the padel tag.
- Use **Attendance** to separate regulars (**Regular**, **All-Star**) from members who are slipping (**Low Activity**, **Inactive**).
- Keep **Membership** on and pair **Expiring Soon** with a renewal discount.

### Martial-arts school with a kids' programme

- Keep **Age** and **Relationship** on to tag kids and their parents, so you can message parents about their children.
- Turn on **Progression** to see who is **Eligible for Promotion** before a grading, and nudge members who are **Stalled at Rank**.
- Turn on **Assessment** so coaches see who is **Not Assessed Recently**.

### Studio: welcome new members who never came

Keep **Attendance** on and start an automation on the **Not Started** tag that invites the member to book their first class.

## Tips & best practices

- **Read the visit-based tags together.** Attendance says how often someone comes now, Declining says they come less than before, At Risk says they stopped.
- **Use Loyalty for recognition, Attendance for short-term campaigns.**
- **Don't duplicate smart tags with manual ones.** If you are hand-tagging members "Overdue", adjust the Financial thresholds instead.
- **Remember auto-renewing memberships never show "Expiring".**
- **Click Sync tags after changing a threshold** to see the result straight away.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                                                    | Cause                                                                                                          | Fix                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| A member isn't getting an activity tag (Attendance, Financial, Loyalty...) | They are a plain contact with no bookings, check-ins or plans and not in your pipeline, or the category is off | Confirm the contact has activity and the category is on |
| A category is missing from the page                                        | It can't tag anyone yet: one language, one sport, no progression track enabled, or no assessment template      | Set up what it reads first                              |
| A member who never comes isn't **Inactive**                                | They have no active plan (they are lapsed), or they never visited, which makes them **Not Started**            | Expected                                                |
| The At-risk customers report is empty                                      | **At Risk** and **Trend** are off, or nobody meets their rules                                                 | Turn them on and click **Sync tags**                    |
| A member isn't tagged "Expiring" though their membership ends soon         | The membership auto-renews                                                                                     | Expected                                                |
| A member has no Language tag                                               | They have no account with a preferred language, or the category is off                                         | Expected for contacts without an account                |
| Staff have no smart tags                                                   | Staff are excluded from smart tagging                                                                          | Expected                                                |
| I changed a threshold but tags didn't update                               | Saving doesn't re-check who qualifies                                                                          | Click **Sync tags**, or wait for the nightly run        |
| I can't switch a category                                                  | You lack the Members **Edit** permission                                                                       | Ask an admin                                            |
| A lead shows a pipeline stage as their Lifecycle tag instead of Trial      | While a contact is in your pipeline, their stage is their Lifecycle tag                                        | Expected                                                |
