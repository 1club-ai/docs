A till is one checkout position at a gym, such as reception or the bar. It groups the payment terminals at that counter and the fiscal device that prints its receipts. The computer at each counter remembers its till, so staff there are offered only that counter's terminals, and cash receipts print on the right device.

## When to use it

Tills are optional. A gym with one card reader and one fiscal device doesn't need them. Add tills when a gym has more than one counter and you want each one to stay on its own devices.

Typical uses:

- **Reception and a bar** - a padel gym has a Stripe reader and a BlueCash-50 at reception, and a myPOS reader and a second BlueCash-50 at the bar. With a till at each, a coffee sold at the bar is charged on the bar's reader and its receipt prints at the bar.
- **Two computers sharing one fiscal printer** - two front-desk computers each have their own card reader but share one fiscal device. Both tills point at the same fiscal device.
- **Stopping sales on the wrong reader** - staff at a busy reception keep sending card payments to the reader at the other end of the building. A till on each computer offers only the reader in front of them.

## How it works

- **A till belongs to one gym.** It lists the payment terminals at that gym it uses, and at most one fiscal device. Several tills can share a terminal or a fiscal device.
- **The browser remembers its till.** The choice is stored on the computer at the counter, not on the staff member, so whoever signs in there works on that till.
- **Payment screens follow the till.** With a till chosen, the card payment screens offer only its terminals. With none chosen, they offer every terminal at the sale's gym, as without tills.
- **Receipts follow the till.** Cash, and cards taken on a reader other than a BlueCash-50, print their [fiscal receipt](/settings/fiscal) on the till's fiscal device. A card charged on a BlueCash-50 always prints on that terminal.
- **A till is a filter, not a permission.** It narrows what the screen offers. Whether a staff member may take a payment on a terminal still depends on their access to the terminal's gym.

Without a till, cash receipts print on the gym's fiscal device when it has exactly one. A gym with more than one fiscal device needs a till to say which one prints, or the desk is told to issue the receipt by hand.

## Open the Tills settings

Go to **Settings** > **Sales** > **Tills**.

The **Tills** item appears once a gym has two active payment terminals or two fiscal devices to choose between, or once a till exists. Before then, there is nothing for a till to choose.

<Note>
  Viewing the Tills page needs the settings **View** permission at
  **Extended**. Adding, editing, and deleting tills needs the matching
  **Create**, **Edit**, or **Delete** permission on settings at Extended. See
  [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-permissions).
</Note>

## Add a till

1. Click **Add** (or **Add till** while the list is empty).
2. Enter a **Name** staff will recognize, such as _Till A_ or _Bar_.
3. Choose the **Club** the till is at.
4. Under **Payment terminals**, pick the active terminals at that gym this counter uses.
5. Under **Fiscal device**, pick the gym's fiscal device that prints this counter's receipts, or leave **No fiscal device**. This field appears only when [fiscal receipts](/settings/fiscal) are on.
6. Click **Save**.

If you change the **Club**, the terminals and fiscal device you picked are cleared, because they belong to the old gym.

The list shows each till's **Name**, **Club**, **Payment terminals**, and **Fiscal device**. Open a till's row menu to **Edit** or **Delete** it. When you delete a till, computers set to it show all of their gym's terminals again.

## Choose the till at the counter

Each computer picks its till once:

- **At the point of sale** - pick the till from the **Till** field at the top of the [point of sale](/pos/point-of-sale). Only the current gym's tills are offered.
- **When completing an order** - if the computer has no till yet and there is more than one, the **Complete order** dialog asks for one, because the receipt prints on that till's fiscal device.

The browser remembers the choice until you change it. Choose **No till** to go back to every terminal at the gym. If the till you picked is later deleted, the computer goes back to no till on its own.
