Instructors are the coaches, trainers, and class leads who teach classes or take one-on-one bookings. Owners and managers set up their profiles, rates, and availability, members book instructors directly, every published instructor gets a profile page on your website, member portal and 1club.me, and instructors who sign in see their own timetable and earnings.

## When to use it

An instructor profile decides whether members can book that person directly, when they are available, and how they are paid. The same profile fits a coach who sells private lessons, a guest who only leads classes, and part-time staff whose hours change week to week.

Typical uses:

- **A padel coach who takes private lessons** - members book one-on-one lessons from the portal at the coach's hourly rate, only within their working hours.
- **A class-only martial-arts lead** - a visiting black belt leads the Saturday BJJ class but takes no private bookings, so **Bookable by** is set to **No one**.
- **A coach members can find** - a tennis coach with a bio, certifications and photos gets a profile page that search engines index, with a button straight to their booking calendar.
- **A Pilates studio on rotating shifts** - part-time instructors follow a weekly rotation that sets their bookable availability, with one-off swaps edited on the roster.
- **Pay only for paid drop-ins** - coaches earn on memberships, fully-paid bookings, and paid-and-checked-in external-program visits, never on no-shows or unpaid seats.

See [Worked examples](#worked-examples) for how to set up each of these.

## The instructor list

Instructors live under **Staff** > **Instructors**. The [calendar](/schedule/calendar) and the booking dialog both surface them.

The list shows one row per instructor with a drag handle and these columns: **Instructor** (name and avatar), **Display name**, **Type**, **Status**, **System status**, **Sports** (up to three with a `+N` overflow), **Club**, and **Rate** (the hourly rate).

Drag a row to reorder; the new order is what shows on the schedule and in selectors. The drag handle is disabled while a reorder request is in flight. If the server rejects the reorder, the list snaps back to the server order with a toast.

Click a row to open the instructor page, or **Add Instructor** to create one. The profile has **Bio**, **Bookings**, **Classes**, **Reviews**, **Pay rates**, **Time off**, and **Roster** tabs, so you can manage one instructor's work, pay, absences, and shifts in one place. Its **Add** menu attaches a **Pay rate** or records **Time off** for them.

Each row's menu (and the profile's header) offers, depending on your permissions:

- **Edit** - open the instructor dialog.
- **Invite to Admin** or **Revoke Admin Access** - give the instructor a login, or take it away. Revoking keeps the instructor profile; they just can no longer sign in.
- **Merge duplicate instructor** (list only) - move a duplicate's upcoming classes, recurring series, and future pay into this instructor, then deactivate the duplicate.
- **Delete** - remove the instructor. If they still teach upcoming classes or recurring series, you're first asked to hand those to another instructor or leave them unassigned. An instructor who has taught classes can't be deleted without losing that history, so you're offered **Archive** instead: the profile leaves the instructor list and can no longer be booked, and past classes keep their record. Archiving needs permission to delete members.

## Creating or editing an instructor

The user dialog handles both inviting a new instructor (which creates the contact, user, and instructor profile in one shot) and editing an existing one. The instructor section asks for:

- **Display name** - shown on the schedule and to members instead of the full name, for example "Alex M.". Leave it blank to fill it in automatically.
- **Instructor type** - managed under **Settings** > **Services** > **Instructor Types**. Picking a type can default the max concurrent bookings, and sets how many people a one-to-one session takes.
- **Hourly rate** - in your organization's currency. Used when a booking is created against this instructor; duration multiplied by this rate seeds the line total.
- **Sports** - tag-style list. Used by the [public marketplace](https://1club.me) for filtering and on the list row.
- **Experience** and **Certifications** - shown on the instructor's profile page.
- **Media** - photos for the profile page's gallery.
- **Price includes area price** - the hourly rate already covers the area, so a lesson booked on an area isn't charged for the area again.
- **Visibility** - where the instructor appears for members: **Public** (your website, portal, the 1club App and 1club.me), **Admin & Member** (signed-in members only), or **Admin only** (named nowhere a member can read, including the classes they teach).
- **Bookable by** - who can book them directly: **Public**, **Admin & Member**, **Admin only** (staff book them, members don't), or **No one** (they lead classes but take no direct bookings). It can't be wider than **Visibility**: members can't book a coach they can't see.
- **Max concurrent bookings** - how many overlapping one-on-one bookings the instructor accepts.
- **Active Instructor** toggle - inactive instructors stay on past bookings but can't be assigned.
- **Working hours** - per-day hours used by the booking availability checks.
- **Give them instructor access** - when on, the user is invited with the instructor role and can sign in, see their own schedule on [Home](/home/overview) and the calendar, and see their earnings under **Analytics** > **My performance**.

Per-instructor display colors used on the schedule are configured separately under **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display**, not on the instructor dialog itself.

On the instructor page itself, the **Bio** tab holds the display name and bio in each language your organization offers, and the page also sets the **Slug**, the readable part of the instructor's profile address.

## Instructor profile pages

Every **Public** instructor gets a profile page on your [website](/marketing/website), your [member portal](/settings/member-portal) and, when your gym is listed, on [1club.me](/marketing/marketplace). It shows their photo, type and sports, their bio, experience and certifications, the photo gallery, and the classes they teach next. A card beside it sends visitors to that instructor's booking calendar or, when members can't book them one-to-one online, lets visitors message your gym about training with them.

The pages are built for search engines too:

- Each page has its own title and description in every language your site supports.
- The address is readable and uses the instructor's **Slug** (their name by default). If you change the slug, the old address redirects to the new one, so links you shared keep working.
- Instructors with a bio or other details are added to your sitemap automatically. **Admin & Member** and **Admin only** instructors stay out of search.

The instructor lists on your website and the team on your 1club.me gym page link to these profiles, so a visitor can read about a coach before booking.

## Independent professionals

An [independent professional](/marketing/marketplace#independent-professionals) - a coach who signs up as themselves, without a venue - is the one instructor in their own organization. **Staff** > **Instructors** opens straight on their instructor page, which also holds **Where do you teach?**: the gyms, their own address, the client's place within a radius (with travel time), or online (with a meeting link). Their classes name one of those places instead of a gym. Pay rates, the roster, inviting and archiving are hidden for them, because they are the owner.

## How instructors show up on the schedule

The [Schedule](/schedule/calendar) page reads bookable instructors and renders one column per instructor in day view. A class assigned to an instructor produces a "teaching" mirror event on the instructor's column, so the instructor reads as busy during their classes (in addition to the class itself appearing under its class-type column).

Instructor time off (added from the calendar with **Add** > **Time off**, or under **Staff** > **Time off**) renders as grey blocks on the instructor's column. When schedule **staff coloring** is enabled, blocks use the assigner's profile color instead of the resource color. The toggles are under **Settings** > **Bookings** > **Schedule Display**.

## Shifts and time off

For staff whose hours change week to week, drive an instructor's availability from a **shift roster** instead of fixed working hours: build reusable shifts and weekly rotations under **Staff** > **Roster** and assign instructors to them. Record absences under **Staff** > **Time off**. See [Roster and time off](/staff/roster).

## Rescheduling on the calendar

On the **Schedule** calendar you can drag a booking or class to a new time. The move keeps the same resource (area, instructor, or class type) and the same duration - only the start and end time shift. Every drop asks you to confirm the old and new time, and for recurring events you choose whether the change applies to just that occurrence or to all future ones.

## Pay rates and earnings

What an instructor earns is set by pay rate policies under **Staff** > **Pay rates**, not by the **Hourly rate** (which is what members pay). Policies can pay per class, per attendee, per session, or a share of revenue, and can hold pay back until the booking is paid. Instructors who sign in see the result under **Analytics** > **My performance** > **Earnings Overview**. See [Pay rates](/staff/pay-rates#instructor-earnings).

## Mixed-role accounts

A user who is both an instructor and an admin or manager sees the full [Schedule](/schedule/calendar) and can narrow it to their own classes and bookings with the **Staff** filter in the calendar header. Their own numbers are under **Analytics** > **My performance** (see [Schedule analytics](/analytics/schedule)).

## Worked examples

### Padel coach who takes private lessons

Create the coach, set their **Instructor type** to _Coach_, add padel and tennis to **Sports**, set the **Hourly rate**, set **Visibility** and **Bookable by** to **Public**, and set **Max concurrent bookings** to 1 for one-on-one lessons. Their working hours (or shift roster) then gate when members can book them from the portal.

### Class-only martial-arts lead

A visiting black belt leads the Saturday BJJ class but never takes private bookings. Add them, assign them to the class, and set **Bookable by** to **No one**. They appear on the class without being offered for one-on-one bookings.

### Pilates studio on rotating shifts

A studio with part-time instructors puts them on rotating shifts: it builds a "Morning 06:00-14:00" shift with a lunch break and assigns each instructor to a weekly rotation. The generated roster becomes their bookable availability; a one-off swap is edited directly on the roster and sticks as a manual override.

### Pay only for paid drop-ins

A gym that runs paid drop-in classes sets a pay rate policy with **Compensate for unpaid bookings** left off, so coaches earn on memberships, fully-paid bookings, and paid-and-checked-in external-program visits - never on no-shows or unpaid seats.

## Tips & best practices

- **Set Bookable by to No one for guest and class-only leads.** It keeps the booking selector focused on people members can actually book.
- **Write a bio and add photos.** They fill the instructor's profile page, and an instructor with something to say is added to your sitemap.
- **Use a shift roster when hours vary week to week.** Fixed working hours are simpler for steady schedules; rosters suit part-time and rotating staff.
- **Gate pay on payment to avoid overpaying.** The paid-booking toggle stops payouts on unpaid or no-show visits, and each recurring occurrence is gated on its own charge.
- **Set sports accurately.** They drive marketplace filtering, so a member searching for a padel coach only sees the right people.
- **Deactivate, don't delete, departing staff.** Inactive instructors drop out of selectors but keep their history on past bookings.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue**: An instructor isn't in the booking dialog's selector.

**Solution**: Only active instructors that can be booked appear. Check **Bookable by** (not **No one**) and the **Active Instructor** toggle on their profile. Members also need **Visibility** and **Bookable by** at **Admin & Member** or **Public** to book them online.

**Issue**: An instructor wasn't paid for a booking they delivered.

**Solution**: Their pay rate policy likely has **Compensate for unpaid bookings** off, and the visit is unpaid or a no-show. Pay is recorded once the charge settles; for recurring bookings, each occurrence pays out only when it's charged.

**Issue**: The instructor can't sign in to their dashboard.

**Solution**: Access is only granted when **Give them instructor access** was on at invite. Use **Invite to Admin** from the instructor's row menu to send the invitation.

**Issue**: A bookable instructor shows no availability.

**Solution**: If the instructor is rostered for that date, availability comes from their **roster**, not their working hours - and a rostered day with no shift is a day off. Assign them to a rotation or add roster shifts under **Staff** > **Roster** (see [Roster and time off](/staff/roster)).
