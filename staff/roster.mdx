The **Roster** and **Time off** tabs of the **Staff** section decide when an instructor is available. The roster sets regular working hours from reusable shifts and weekly rotations; time off records absences. Owners and managers maintain both, and members can only book an instructor inside the hours they leave open.

## When to use it

Fixed operating hours on the instructor profile suit coaches with a steady week. When hours change week to week, a roster saves you editing each profile, and time off blocks bookings for a holiday or sick day without touching the regular pattern.

Typical uses:

- **A Pilates studio on rotating shifts** - part-time instructors alternate mornings and evenings on a two-week rotation, and their bookable availability follows it.
- **A lunch break nobody can book over** - a "Morning 06:00-14:00" shift carries a 12:00-13:00 break, so members can't book the coach over lunch.
- **A coach's holiday** - you add a week of time off, and the dialog lists the lessons and classes it clashes with so you can reassign or cancel them in one go.

## How it works

There's no global on/off switch. An instructor uses their roster on any date they're rostered - a date covered by a rotation assignment, or one with a shift placed directly on it - and falls back to their [operating hours](/staff/instructors#creating-or-editing-an-instructor) on every other date. A rostered day with no shift reads as a day off.

Time off sits on top of either: during a time-off entry the instructor can't be booked, and the entry shows as a grey block on their column on the [calendar](/schedule/calendar).

## The Roster page

Open **Staff** > **Roster**. The page has two parts: the **Scheduled shifts** grid at the top (one row per instructor, one column per day, with week navigation) and the **Shifts & rotations** panel below it, where you build the reusable pieces. Viewing needs **staff.read.organization**; creating and editing shifts, rotations, and assignments needs **staff.manage.organization**.

## The building blocks

- **Shifts** - reusable named time windows like "Morning 06:00-14:00", each with a color and optional standard **breaks** (e.g. lunch). Managed in the **Shifts** card.
- **Rotations** - a weekly pattern that picks a shift for each day, repeating over a cycle of 1-12 weeks. Managed in the **Rotations** card.
- **Assignments** - attach an instructor to a rotation for a date range; this is what generates their roster.
- **Roster** - the generated list of individual shifts, shown in the Scheduled shifts grid and editable shift by shift. An assignment with an **Effective until** date generates the whole roster up to that date; an **Ongoing** one uses a rolling 8-week window. See [Repeating schedules](/reference/repeating-schedules).

## Set up a rotating schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your shifts">
    In the **Shifts** card, choose **Add shift**. Give it a name, a start and
    end time, and a color, then add any standard **breaks** (name, start, end) -
    for example a 12:00-13:00 lunch. Breaks must fall inside the shift window.
    Repeat for each distinct pattern you run (e.g. "Morning", "Evening").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a rotation">
    In the **Rotations** card, choose **Add rotation**. Set a **Cycle length**
    (1-12 weeks) and a **Cycle start date** (the date week 1 begins). Then, for
    each day of each week, pick one of your shifts or leave it **Off**. A
    two-week cycle that alternates mornings and evenings, for instance, captures
    a typical part-time pattern.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an instructor">
    In the **Scheduled shifts** grid, on the instructor's row, click their
    rotation cell (**Set regular shifts**). Pick the rotation, the **Starting
    week** (which week of the cycle they begin on), and the **Effective from** /
    **Effective until** dates - leave the end open for an **Ongoing**
    assignment. Their roster generates immediately: to **Effective until** when
    you set one, so a coach contracted through June has their whole roster on the
    grid, or a rolling 8 weeks when the assignment is ongoing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the roster">
    The grid now shows each instructor's generated shifts with their breaks.
    Click any day cell to add a one-off shift or edit an existing one. Editing a
    generated shift marks it as a **manual override**, so rotation regeneration
    leaves it untouched; use **Cancel shift** to drop a single occurrence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An assignment with an **Effective until** date generates its roster all the way
  to that date. An **Ongoing** assignment is generated for a rolling 8-week
  window and extended automatically. Either way, manual overrides and one-off
  shifts survive regeneration; unedited rotation shifts are replaced when you
  change the rotation.
</Note>

## Breaks

Breaks are the unavailable windows inside a working shift - most commonly lunch. Define them on a shift (in the **Shifts** card) as a name plus a start and end time within the shift window.

Once a shift is on the roster, its breaks:

- **Block bookings** during their window, so nobody can book the instructor over lunch.
- **Show on the roster grid** under each shift, as a `Break 12:00-13:00` line.
- **Show on the Schedule calendar** as a muted, non-interactive card in the instructor's column, labeled with the break name.
- **Are editable per shift** - open any shift from the roster grid to add, edit, or remove its breaks. One-off shifts can carry their own breaks too.

## Working hours on the instructor profile

On an instructor's details page, a read-only **Working hours** block shows the hours derived from their roster, with a **Manage shifts** link back to **Staff** > **Roster**.

## Time off

**Staff** > **Time off** lists every instructor time-off entry with **Instructor**, **Start time**, **End time**, **Status** (upcoming, active, or past), and **Note**, newest first. You can also add time off from the calendar (**Add** > **Time off**) and from an instructor's details page.

To add an entry, click **Add time off** and fill in:

- **Instructor** - who is away.
- **Start time** and **End time** - the window. For a new entry it defaults to tomorrow's opening hours.
- **Note** - optional, for example "Vacation".

As soon as the instructor and window are set, the dialog checks for bookings and classes that overlap it and lists them under **Resolve scheduling conflicts**. For each one, pick an action, or use **Apply to all**:

- **Keep as is** - leave it with this instructor.
- **Reassign instructor** - pick a replacement in **Reassign to**.
- **Leave unassigned** - remove the instructor and keep the booking or class.
- **Cancel booking** or **Cancel class** - cancel it. Cancelling a booking waives the member's cancellation charge unless you turn on **Keep the cancellation charge**; cancelled classes follow their own cancellation flow.

Click a row to edit an entry, or delete it from the row actions. Managing time off needs the **staff.manage.organization** permission.
